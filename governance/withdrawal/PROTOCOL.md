## Rules for activity beginning or resuming now

Read the current Research Home and relevant R17 Work Events before acting. Match
the exact object, source version, hypothesis/measure/domain/quantifiers, active
lease and output head. A stale cached instruction or branch name is not authority.
Use the rules below on new work without relabeling historical rows. A workflow
pause or closed scheduling task is never discharge of its mathematical target.

1. Separate object kind, source evidential status, present admissibility,
   scheduling state and review provenance. HOLD is unresolved; suspension is
   protective; invalid proof, refuted contract and closed strategy are different.
   Avoid an unqualified KILLED label. Cost, time and an unknown next step justify
   scheduling decisions, not claims of falsity or impossibility.
2. A documented material defect can immediately suspend affected use through an
   additive, scope-specific event. Preserve the source. Final negative verdicts
   require a separate evidence-bound nonauthor review and the existing operator
   decision path. Distinct providers do not by themselves prove independence;
   record coauthorship, shared evidence and prior exposure. Never invent review.
3. Withdrawal disables whole proof routes. It does not erase their requirements.
   Requirements within a route are AND; alternative valid routes are OR. The
   parent loses support only if no admissible route remains. Loss of support
   does not establish the parent's falsity. For a pure withdrawal, recorded
   support after must be a subset of recorded support before.
4. Preserve every node, source, failed route and refuting artifact. Keep the
   search terms, exact failed assumptions, explanation and conditions for a
   materially different retry. Scheduling shutdown is not a memory deletion.
5. A qualitative survivor needs its own supported statement and quantifiers.
   Lost decimal certification proves neither qualitative falsity nor survival.
   Reuse existing valid lemma IDs; newly extracted statements require explicit
   contracts, dependency lists and warrants. Do not silently change measures,
   dimensions, intervals or all-small-radius quantifiers under the same ID.
6. Restoring an old pointer requires revalidation of that exact old route. A
   historical predecessor may share the defect. Unknown validity routes to a
   named open obligation, not to the previous allegedly good version.
7. Keep dashboard target populations fixed within a withdrawal. Closing two
   workstreams must not silently change eight-of-ten scientific completion to
   eight-of-eight. A changed reporting basis is a separate explicit event.
8. Freeze the affected graph slice and assess dependency coverage. A checker
   cannot find dependencies never entered. PARTIAL/UNASSESSED coverage supports
   investigation, not an assertion that all effects have been reconciled.

## Transition record and publication

Before a substantive withdrawal, pin: event/object IDs; contract and typed scope;
old/new snapshot digests; reason and exact evidence; author; every changed item;
affected dependents; named surviving routes or unresolved obligations; and review
requirements. Attach this payload to an R17 event. Generate impact views from it.
Do not independently maintain competing status truth in README, lane and graph
files. Preserve the original frozen carrier; use overlays or numbered successors.

Use PREPARED -> EXECUTED -> RECORDED -> ACCEPTED as distinct publication concepts.
GitHub execution and Drive recording are not an atomic distributed transaction.
Bind the same transition ID, payload digest and exact source/commit identities on
both surfaces; read back both; only then record publication. A missing or mismatched
side is a hold on that transition, not a freeze of unrelated research. Identical
retries reuse an event identity; conflicting retries remain separately visible
for reconciliation. Never rewrite an old receipt to make a retry appear original.
Owner acceptance and scientific review remain separate from implementation/tests.

## Executable v1 boundary

`tools/withdrawal_check.py check` validates loss-only candidate snapshots and
explicit scheduling changes. It preserves contracts, scopes, route requirements,
artifact identities and dashboard membership. Support is derived as a least fixed
point of named grounds and proof routes, not from status labels or empty lists.
It reports lost support, surviving witnesses and disabled routes. It has no write
or network capability. JSON unknown fields, unknown versions, duplicate keys,
non-finite values, missing coverage and stale payload bindings fail closed.

The checker **does not** adjudicate final falsity, accept new evidence, restore a
route, migrate scientific records, authenticate a review or verify Drive/GitHub
receipt references over the network. It will reject these unsupported operations
rather than pretend they are implemented. Its success is STRUCTURALLY_VALID with
scientific_acceptance=false, even for a structurally complete RECORDED payload.
Grounds are externally admitted warrants, not raw numerical run receipts.

`inventory` is a read-only enrolment aid. It hashes individual source records,
exposes declared dependencies and marks every item NOT_MIGRATED / NOT_ASSESSED.
It does not convert existing depends_on lists into complete proof routes, invent
unconditional grounds, reconcile frozen/current-note statuses or move a gate.

Synthetic examples carry SYNTHETIC identifiers and placeholder review/artifact
hashes. They are tests, not scientific evidence or authentic review records.
