## Operational meaning

1. **Personal information never enters a place others can see.** That covers
   dates of birth, family members' names and other private individuals, legal
   names beyond the public "Dylan Roy", licence, ID, account or policy numbers,
   personal email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses and precise locations,
   social media handles and profiles, health, finances, employment history and
   private correspondence. Places others can see include every GitHub repository,
   branch, pull request, issue and comment, and the shared Drive folders that
   models read. Share links that carry access keys (for example Dropbox `rlkey=`
   or Google `resourcekey=`) are treated the same way.
2. **Unrelated material stays out of project spaces.** The project is the
   Universal Law research program described in [README.md](../README.md) and
   [docs/RESEARCH_INDEX.md](../docs/RESEARCH_INDEX.md), together with its
   prize-problem track and its own multi-model operations. The owner's other
   research lines, personal documents and third-party material are not
   brought into its repositories or shared folders.
3. **When either is found, purge it.** Remove it from current trees by a
   reviewable change. Do not repeat the value in commit messages, PR bodies,
   issues or logs; describe only its kind and location. Drive copies in shared
   folders are trashed or moved out. The owner's private Dropbox keeps its
   originals. A value that remains in Git history, in pull-request refs or in
   caches is reported to the owner as a residual exposure; it is not declared
   gone. Removing it from those needs a history rewrite or GitHub Support.
4. **Publish what belongs.** Material related to the project is published to
   GitHub in the repository whose role fits it (see the cross-repository
   contract in `governance-`), with provenance and without status promotion.
5. **Check before publishing.** Before any import from Drive, Dropbox or another
   private source, screen for the items above. Any doubt means the item is held
   privately; holding is always recoverable.

This rule narrows what may be published. It does not reduce the autonomy granted in
[OP-AUTONOMY-20260923-v2.1](OP-AUTONOMY-20260923-v2.1.md) for project work.
