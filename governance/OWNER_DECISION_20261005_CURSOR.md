## Decision

1. [The September 27 stop](OWNER_STOP_20260927.md) no longer applies in any
   repository. Its text stays unchanged as historical evidence.
2. Cursor agents work on request. Any lane, including the Grok Bot Chief of
   Staff, may summon one Cursor agent for one bounded task: a nonauthor read, a
   reproduction, an experiment, or a fix on the summoning lane's own branch. The
   summoner states the scope and checks the output. Cursor agents disclose their
   provider, model and session. They do not self-assign repository changes, and
   they arm no permanent windows, timers or loops. This agrees with the `Math-`
   agent entry, which admits Cursor agents as support at a lane's request.
3. The Grok Bot fleet keeps its reading, routing and integration role under the
   Chief of Staff.
4. While hosted runners are scarce, the Chief of Staff may integrate disjoint,
   fully reviewed, additive packets as one train with a single tested merge.
   Each packet's landed bytes must equal its reviewed bytes.
5. One claim per object: check the coordination thread for an active claim
   before authoring.

organizational-independence credit. Required hosted checks apply at the tested
commit. Integration uses merge commits only, never by the author, and a head
stays frozen while a read is open. Scientific acceptance conditions,
`formal/` and register custody, and [OP-PRIVACY](OP-PRIVACY-20260927.md) keep
their meaning. Nothing here changes a scientific status, flag, premise, prize or
