OP-GDN-002 — COUPLED MATHEMATICAL–ARCHITECTURAL ADVANCEMENT AND REQUIRED SAFEGUARDS

Operator: Dylan Roy
Issued: July 21, 2026
Authority: Binding research-governance guidance
Canonical impact: Governance only. Mathematical truth remains evidence-governed.

1. COUPLED ADVANCEMENT INVARIANT

The mathematics and research architecture are one coupled system. Every material mathematical transition must produce either a corresponding architectural change or a complete no-change certificate demonstrating that the existing architecture already represented, tested, and governed the transition. Silence is not evidence of sufficiency.

Every material architectural change must cite the concrete mathematical failure mode, coordination defect, provenance defect, recurring ambiguity, measurable resource leak, or anticipated adversarial risk that it addresses. Architecture must not grow merely because more structure is possible.

A no-change certificate must identify the exact object, exact statement, evidence class, law or probability measure, regime, normalization, coordinate system, dependencies affected and unaffected, existing controls, why no schema/verifier/register/status/governance change is required, and the failure that would reveal the judgment was wrong.

2. UNEXPLAINED ADVANCEMENT ALARMS

Mathematics advancing without an architectural response or no-change certificate is an alarm. It may indicate an untracked assumption, new object class, measure mismatch, conditioning mismatch, coordinate dependence, planar-versus-torus conflation, limiting-versus-finite conflation, local-to-global drift, untracked dependency, or verifier/claim misalignment.

Architecture expanding without measurable reduction in mathematical uncertainty is also an alarm. It may indicate bureaucratic overgrowth, duplicated registers, review-of-review loops, status proliferation, governance displacing proof work, or coordination activity that does not close, kill, falsify, or sharpen an item.

3. SYNCHRONIZED-CALIBRATION RISK

Mathematics and architecture can share the same hidden error. A rigorous proof of the wrong object, perfectly tracked by a rigorous system, remains a failure. Periodic outside-lineage challenges are mandatory: blind reconstruction, independent notation, alternative formalization, planted controls, mutated fixtures, intended-object challenges, specialist review, and literature comparison after internal results are frozen.

Outside review must ask both whether the proof is internally correct and whether the formally defined object answers the original scientific question.

4. FALSE-INDEPENDENCE CONTROL

Model agreement is not automatically independent evidence. Every independent-review claim must disclose sources read, whether the original derivation was visible, whether reconstruction was independent, whether code or fixtures were reused, inherited assumptions, the independent route, and whether the object definition or only internal algebra was checked.

Agreement is weighted by methodological independence, not model count.

5. CONSENSUS-LAUNDERING CONTROL

A candidate claim does not gain authority through repeated summaries, register copies, citations, ballots, or descriptions of acceptance. Primary derivations, executable receipts, and raw evidence must remain identifiable. Summaries are not independent verification. Ballots govern project action, not mathematical truth. Any reviewer unable to inspect primary evidence must record that limitation.

6. CLOSURE-INFLATION CONTROL

Every closure package must state the exact object, exact statement, regime, coordinate and normalization dependence, evidence class, exact/asymptotic/numerical/conditional/instrument status, broader claims remaining open, forbidden extrapolations, and reopening condition. Narrow closure may reduce uncertainty but may not silently widen its scope.

Required distinctions include canonical normal form versus physical coefficient; planar coalesced versus exact finite-torus law; instrument versus theorem verification; typed pair-Palm versus adjacency-conditioned probability; exact finite-r versus limiting-jet statement; graph versus continuum persistence; and candidate versus canonical theorem.

7. GLOBAL-OBJECT AUDIT

Significant proof chains must periodically answer: What scientific question is being answered? What exact theorem would answer it? Is the current chain proving that theorem or a surrogate? Have measure, conditioning, pairing rule, domain, finite/limiting object, coordinate invariance, proxy, or active machine roots changed?

Correct local lemmas do not compensate for target drift.

8. MATHEMATICAL ERRORS AS ARCHITECTURAL TEST CASES

For every serious error, determine what allowed it to survive and whether a reusable control is justified: schema field, object-card distinction, verifier rule, negative test, preregistration requirement, closure restriction, provenance rule, machine-state constraint, named failure class, or reviewer instruction. Not every typo requires architecture; structural error classes do.

9. ARCHITECTURAL FAILURES AS MATHEMATICAL RISKS

Duplicate evidence, identifier collisions, stale summaries, partial refreshes, unreadable files, instruction injection, candidate/canonical confusion, and premature closure can alter the evidence seen by models. Every architecture incident requires downstream mathematical-contamination assessment and, where necessary, quarantine and re-review.

10. REQUIRED TRANSITION RECORD

Every material advance records: previous state; new state; exact object; exact claim or rule; evidence added; evidence removed, killed, or superseded; independent-review status; dependencies affected; dependencies unaffected; mathematical change; architectural change; no-change certificate where applicable; new failure modes; detection tests; uncertainty permanently removed; remaining uncertainty; required human decision; and reopening condition.

11. OUTCOME-BASED ARCHITECTURE METRICS

The system evaluates itself by uncertainty retired, recurring error classes converted into tests, duplicate work reduced, stale contradictions reduced, easy-closure time, primary-definition reconstructions, object-mismatch promotion blocks, justified architectural changes, mathematical-advance/coordination ratio, reopened closures, human-review burden per settled result, and active claims with complete object cards and dependency maps.

Increasing coordination activity without reducing uncertainty is not progress.

12. OBSERVER INDEPENDENCE

Gemini and other observers activated by Dylan are not automatically absorbed into internal consensus. Their distance may be preserved through blind objective review, primitive-only source access, independent notation, omitted-alternative search, path-dependence review, and frozen-package outside-referee work.

13. FINAL OPERATING PRINCIPLE

The objective is not an error-free system. The objective is a coupled system in which unsupported progress increasingly creates detectable inconsistencies elsewhere. Mathematical claims cannot change materially without traceable status, dependency, evidence, and architectural response. Architectural mechanisms cannot grow materially without demonstrating the uncertainty or failure risk they remove.
