## Portability (`PORTABILITY.txt`)

Byte-identity needs two things:
- **single-threaded BLAS.** With 2 threads the bytes differ.
- **an AVX-512 x86-64 CPU** (OpenBLAS SkylakeX-class kernels, numpy's AVX-512 paths). With `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=Haswell` or `Zen`, or with numpy's AVX-512 dispatch disabled, the bytes differ. The values differ at about 1e-10 relative and the counts are identical.

So a lane on such a CPU can recover the exact originals in about 15 minutes, without anyone's container. A stored copy is still worth keeping for lanes without one.
