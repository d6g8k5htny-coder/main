Exploratory packet: zeta-zero heat-flow folds and a CUE short-bar control

Adds experiments/zeta_fold_exploration/ only. It checks an external note,
forwarded by the project owner on 12 October 2026, that links zeta zeros
to this project's fold and short-bar law. It records what is classical,
what is corrected, and what is useful to the project. Every claim is
paraphrased, never quoted.

- dbn/: three independent H_t implementations. Two de Bruijn-Newman zero
  collisions are computed to about 40 digits, both at t < 0. The fold law
  r^2 = 8 tau (1 + O(tau)) and lobe area (|A|/6) r^3 (1 + O(tau)) hold
  near the collision.
  Proposition 1 covers nondegenerate double zeros only.
- theta/: theta, Mellin, Poisson and Epstein identities, plus the
  project-normalizer checks (periodization invisible locally at L = 24).
- stats/: Riemann-Siegel zeros, Gaussian and CUE controls, barcodes and
  finite-tail exponent estimates. For the Hardy-Z primitive (t in
  [1e5, 1e6], cut 0.01) the Hill estimate of the short-bar density
  exponent is -0.020 [-0.041, +0.001] (sampling-only). Finite-tail
  estimates for the Hardy-Z primitive are substantially above -1/3 and
  near 0; sampling-only intervals exclude -1/3; no limiting exponent or
  calibrated total-error rejection. -1/3 and 0 are theoretical
  expectations under stated conditions, not calibrated control answers.
  The data were inspected first, so this is exploration, not confirmation.
- README.md, CLAIMS.md, MANIFEST.json (SHA-256 of every file), pinned
  requirements, stdlib exact tests and numeric smoke tests.
