## Current mathematical fronts

**Gaussian persistence:** global elder-pairing and short-lifetime proof candidates, explicit SIDE24 coefficient enclosures, and an unrestricted bounded-remainder candidate. [Reading order](docs/RESEARCH_INDEX.md#gaussian-persistence).

**RN counting:** exact probability-to-count implications and counterexamples. The original expected-count integral and 24-jet numerical obligations are separate unfinished work. [Read the interface](docs/RESEARCH_INDEX.md#rn-counting).

**P15 combinatorics:** actual-coordinate obstruction covers, the price-extension counterexample, and a restricted transformed-price successor that retains the 816-label example. [Read the construction and boundary](docs/RESEARCH_INDEX.md#p15-combinatorics).

Positive theorem claims remain author-side candidates until their source-bound mathematical reviews establish otherwise. A commit, hash, merge or passing test is not an independent proof review. The guide distinguishes supplied results, failed extensions and remaining work rather than presenting a completion badge.
