\section{Exact scope}\label{sec:scope}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is restricted to:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item ambient dimension $3$;
\item torus side $24$;
\item the normalized periodized Bargmann--Fock covariance \eqref{eq:K24};
\item finite nonessential superlevel $H_0$ bars;
\item the first-moment lifetime density as $\ell\downarrow0$;
\item the ordered maximum--index-two-saddle, directed-$S^2$ and Palm conventions used above.
\end{itemize}
It does not imply:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item a theorem for other side lengths;
\item a theorem for arbitrary isotropic or stationary Gaussian fields;
\item a result for $H_k$, $k>0$;
\item an interchange of the limits $L\to\infty$ and $\ell\downarrow0$;
\item an infinite-volume persistence process;
\item an explicit range of $\ell$ on which \eqref{eq:main} or \eqref{eq:three-term} holds to a stated accuracy: every remainder constant in this paper is existential;
\item a numerical value of $\varepsilon_{24}$, or a directed-rounding certificate for the nonlinear integral \eqref{eq:c} beyond the relative bound $|c_{3,24}/c_{3,\infty}-1|<10^{-106}$ \rec{v1coef}. Such a certificate is not used in the analytic estimate \eqref{eq:correction}.
\end{itemize}
The records behind Theorem~\ref{thm:three-term} and Remarks~\ref{rem:beyond}--\ref{rem:cand} hold in the dimensions and sides stated in each record, at their stated scopes, and the numerical enclosures are specific to the sides stated. This paper states them only for $d=3$ and $L=24$.
