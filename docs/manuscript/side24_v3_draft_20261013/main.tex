\title[Short-lifetime $H_0$ persistence on the side-24 torus]{Short-lifetime $H_0$ persistence for the three-dimensional side-24 periodized Bargmann--Fock field}
\author{Dylan Roy}
\date{V3 draft, 13 October 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 60G60; Secondary 60G15, 55N31, 60D05}
\keywords{Gaussian random fields, persistent homology, persistence diagrams, Kac--Rice formula, critical points, elder rule}

\begin{abstract}
Let $f_{24}$ be the normalized periodization to the cubic torus $\T_{24}^3$ of the real Bargmann--Fock field with covariance $\exp(-|x-y|^2/2)$. We consider finite, nonessential superlevel-set $H_0$ persistence bars and their first-moment lifetime density per unit volume. We prove the short-lifetime law
\[
\nu_{3,24}(\ell)=c_{3,24}\,\ell^{-1/3}\bigl(1+O(\ell^{1/3})\bigr),\qquad \ell\downarrow0,
\]
where $c_{3,24}>0$ is given by an explicit finite-dimensional Gaussian-cone integral. The proof identifies the dimension-cancelling local fold mechanism, proves that, for compact positive marks, elder-rule selection changes the all-typed local contact count by only $O(r^3)$ in Palm probability, and fixes all ordered-pair, directed-sphere, Kac--Rice and lifetime-Jacobian normalizations. The coefficient is related to its value for the Bargmann--Fock field on $\R^3$ by
\[
\frac{c_{3,24}}{c_{3,\infty}}-1=-\frac{620813376}{35}e^{-288}+\varepsilon_{24},\qquad |\varepsilon_{24}|<10^{-216},
\]
with $c_{3,\infty}=2^{-23/6}3^{-8/3}(29\sqrt6-36)\,\Gamma(1/6)\,\pi^{-5/2}$.
We also state the three-term expansion $\nu_{3,24}(\ell)=c_{3,24}\ell^{-1/3}+c_1\ell^{1/4}+c_2\ell^{1/3}+O(\ell^{3/7})$, with explicit constants $c_1<0$ and $c_2$, and describe the cusp and fold mechanisms that produce the corrections.
\end{abstract}
