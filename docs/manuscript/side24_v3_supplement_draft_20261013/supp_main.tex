\title[Supplement: short-lifetime $H_0$ persistence on the side-24 torus]{Supplementary material for ``Short-lifetime $H_0$ persistence for the three-dimensional side-24 periodized Bargmann--Fock field''}
\author{Dylan Roy}
\date{Draft, \today}

\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains the proofs that the main text cites. Part~I proves the leading short-lifetime law, its rate and the side-24 correction: the setting and pair coordinates (\S\ref{sec:setting}), the contact measure (\S\ref{sec:contact}), the finite-side coefficient (\S\ref{sec:coefficient}), the side-24 correction (\S\ref{sec:correction}), elder selection (\S\ref{sec:selection}), the limit (\S\ref{sec:limit}) and the bounded remainder (\S\ref{sec:rate}). Part~II states the three-term law and the results behind it, with their proofs (\S\S\ref{sec:remainder}--\ref{sec:ctwo}).
\end{abstract}
