## Addendum 2026-10-14 — distinguish universality statements

The preceding paragraphs preserve the September 25/27 search record, not a current theorem-status inventory.

[Heo, arXiv:2610.08257v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08257v1), submitted 6 October 2026, is a preprint reporting different centered persistence-ratio limit laws for independent uniform samples in dimensions two and three, with the Čech filtration and homological degree one fixed (Theorem 1.1). It also excludes the proposed left-skewed Gumbel family (Corollary 1.4). These are attributed preprint results, not an independent verification of the complete proof here.

[Bobrowski–Skraba, arXiv:2406.05553v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.05553v3), Section 2.2 and Theorems 3.1–3.2, concern a different universality statement: at fixed dimension, positive homological degree and filtration, the ratio limit is independent of the sampling density under their stated density and death-radius cutoff hypotheses. It may still depend on those fixed indices. A strong law of large numbers is not the stronger common-law conjecture across dimensions.

Neither result directly proves or refutes the project's additive Gaussian H0 lifetime law. Point sampling and smooth random-field filtrations, ratio and additive-gap observables, and H1 and H0 must not be conflated. A common leading exponent does not establish a common full distribution. Keep the novelty comparison specific to the declared Gaussian H0 exponent, coefficient and elder-selection control; do not broaden it to all analytic near-diagonal persistence asymptotics.
