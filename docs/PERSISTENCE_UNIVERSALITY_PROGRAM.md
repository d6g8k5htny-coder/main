the probability law transverse to its discriminant, and the intensity of actual
persistence bars. Audit the core theorem and its interfaces first; then prioritize
universality and joint limiting laws. Additional lemmas earn priority only when
they discharge a named interface, distinguish competing mechanisms, or enable a
predeclared falsification test.

This program implements the owner's October 8 objective. It is a research design
and execution route, not a new scientific-status register or an acceptance vote.
The full objective includes actual proofs, critical Lean interfaces, fifty blinded
model tests, five papers and external scientific uptake; completion of the
software accompanying this document does not complete that objective.

## State the law and its sampling measure

For finite ordinary superlevel H0 bars, write ell=birth-death>0. Exclude the
essential class and zero-length pairs. For the reference field at fixed d,L,
the lifetime density per unit volume is nu(ell)~c[d,L] ell^(-1/3). Therefore
the cumulative expected short-bar count per unit volume is
(3/2)c[d,L] t^(2/3). An inverse-lifetime moment uses a third convention and
has threshold 2/3. Every figure, abstract and benchmark must identify its estimand.

The parent's ordered maximum/saddle radial ledger has factors

    r^(d-1) × r^3 × 12 r^(-(d+3)) × r^2 = 12 r.

These are spatial polar measure, height-mark Jacobian, pin-density
desingularization and endpoint determinant weights. Assigned maximum and saddle
roles introduce no extra division by two. With ell=k r^3,

    r dr = ell^(-1/3) d ell / (3 k^(2/3)).

The dimension cancels in this ledger. A cubic fold supplies the gap exponent;
the radial counting law supplies the rest. A change of sampling, determinant
tilt or selection can change the persistence exponent without changing the fold.
The [exact executable controls](../experiments/universality/fold_controls.py)
check this distinction by rational arithmetic. They are toy pushforward
calculations, not a random-field counterexample or Lean execution.

### The first conditional universality statement

Use this as a precisely stated transfer target. Let q=(b,k,u), k>0, with the
declared birth/gap/orientation measure dq. Suppose the relevant local pair
intensity is r A_r(q) dr dq, its lifetime is exactly k r^3, and actual-bar
selection is p_r(q). Require:

1. A_r tends to A_0 almost everywhere, with a finite, strictly positive integral
   of k^(-2/3) A_0.
2. p_r tends to one for candidate/actual-bar coefficient equality. A positive
   limiting p_0 can preserve the exponent while changing the coefficient.
3. A single integrable envelope bounds k^(-2/3) A_(ell/k)^(1/3)(q) over the
   small-lifetime regime. When the local chart ends, extend its contribution by
   zero; prove the escaping/small-k/orientation tails instead of assuming them.
4. The complement contributes o(ell^(-1/3)) in lifetime density.
5. The counting identity is measurable and uses each actual finite bar once,
   with precisely the Kac–Rice/Palm law and determinant weights being consumed.

Then the change of variables and dominated convergence give

    nu_bar(ell) ~ c ell^(-1/3),
    c = (1/3) integral k^(-2/3) A_0(q) dq.

With limiting selection p_0, insert p_0 inside the integral. This elementary
conditional deduction identifies what a general-field proof must establish;
it does not verify those five hypotheses for any new field.

For an asymptotic gap rather than exact height marking, additionally prove a
uniform invertible lifetime map and its derivative asymptotics. Pointwise
ell~k r^3 alone is insufficient to justify pushing a density forward.

A first extension class worth testing is stationary Gaussian torus fields with
strictly positive Fourier variances and sufficiently summable weighted square
roots of those variances at every derivative order needed by the argument.
Use actual contact ranks and conditional covariance, with positive transverse
discriminant density. General non-Gaussian fields require a new density/moment
and conditional-law argument. The fifty-model catalog does not establish it.

### Singularity taxonomy with probability hypotheses

If dI~C r^alpha dr and ell=k r^p, then

    density exponent = (alpha+1)/p - 1,
    cumulative exponent = (alpha+1)/p,
    coefficient = (C/p) k^(-(alpha+1)/p).

A universality table must list both the geometric gap and the sampling law.
For the protected local fold f_mu(x,y)=b+mu*x-x^3/3-|y|^2/2,
the separation is 2 sqrt(mu) and ell=(4/3)mu^(3/2). A sampling density
rho(mu)~C mu^beta, beta>-1, gives lifetime-density exponent (2 beta-1)/3.
Thus a smooth density vanishing quadratically at the discriminant gives +1
instead of -1/3. This attacks the geometry-only inference; constructing such
sampling inside an admissible stationary field class remains a separate task.

| Local model | Additional sampling hypothesis | Gap scaling | Conditional density exponent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Generic fold, cubic gap | Positive smooth density in unfolding mu; equivalently radial alpha=1 | mu^(3/2), or separation^3 | -1/3 |
| Same fold | Density vanishes as mu^2; radial alpha=5 | Same cubic geometry | +1 |
| One-parameter odd quintic contact x^5/5-mu*x | Positive smooth density in mu; radial alpha=3 | mu^(5/4), or separation^5 | -1/5 |
| One-parameter odd septic contact x^7/7-mu*x | Positive smooth density in mu; radial alpha=5 | mu^(7/6), or separation^7 | -1/7 |
| Multiple-control/corank strata | Specify unfolding path, codimension, probability density and counting weights | Depends on path and contact | No single exponent assigned here |

The last three rows are conditional local calculations, not accepted persistence
universality classes. Higher-codimension events need probability/tail estimates
and actual persistence pairing before entering a random-field theorem. Holding
alpha=1 artificially while changing p gives a different table; it must not be
presented as the natural sampling law of every singularity.

## Global correspondence and higher homology

There are three different obligations:

- **Global ordinary H0 semantics:** under Morse/distinct-value and topology
  hypotheses, each finite bar has its birth maximum and first actual elder
  merging saddle. Prove Borel identification and once-counting for the actual
  source and its conditional law. The existing scoped results should be consumed
  with their reviewed hypotheses, not replaced by a new graph.
- **Dominance of local folds:** local fold pairs account for the leading
  ell^(-1/3) density. Quantify the nonlocal, degenerate, boundary and exceptional
  contribution. Far/nonlocal short bars may survive with bounded density.
- **Asymptotic matching:** specify whether unmatched counts are negligible in
  mean, probability or a marked-process metric. The soft-fold result's positive
  finite-radius rejection rate rules out an exact candidate/bar bijection.

For Hk, k>0, define the coefficient field, ordinary/relative/extended persistence,
boundary convention, critical-index pairing and handle cancellation mechanism.
For superlevel persistence, the relevant handle indices change with degree;
max/elder-merging-saddle language is specific to H0. A general Hk theorem requires
the persistence boundary-map/rank interface and its own selection/exhaustion
argument. Do not infer it from a local H0 chart or a homology software output.

## Coefficient as a local-jet functional

The transfer formula isolates a universal change-of-variables factor 1/3.
A_0 still contains actual conditional jet densities, covariance, determinant/cone
moments, marks and orientation measure. Prove coordinate/metric invariance of
the complete expression before naming a local jet invariant. In finite-period
models, replacing actual directional covariance by an isotropic/GOE law is invalid.

The factorization target is a source-defined local-jet functional integrated
against a specified universal geometric kernel. A scalar product
"one model invariant × one universal constant" is a stronger claim and needs
its own factorization proof. Retain d, geometry, volume and field-amplitude units;
amplitude scaling F -> aF sends c -> a^(-2/3)c at this density convention.
Alternative spectra/non-Gaussian laws never inherit the reference SIDE24 digits.

## Marked process: choose the nontrivial limit

On a fixed torus, the mean count below t is asymptotic to
L^d (3/2)c[d,L] t^(2/3), hence tends to zero. Markov's inequality gives the empty
unconditioned process in probability. The fixed-domain occurrence/Palm results
are meaningful conditioning laws, not an increasing-volume Poisson theorem.

A proposed nontrivial spatial process can instead use domains with volume V_t,
rescale positions to a fixed domain and lifetime by t, and require
V_t t^(2/3) -> lambda. Prove existence of a limiting coefficient in that regime,
uniform approximation/asymptotic estimates, marked factorial/Laplace convergence,
tightness and anti-clustering or an explicit cluster law. Identify the mark space,
topology and normalization before claiming convergence. Ordinary Poisson,
compound Poisson and more general clustered limits are competing hypotheses.

The [C6 cluster packet](https://github.com/d6g8k5htny-coder/Math-/blob/13af1089fd7991105a3d8828539bdd9a41ca87c3/frontiers/c6_rare_cluster_laws_20260929/PROOF.md)
already records conditional compound-Poisson approximations and an ordinary-Poisson
obstruction for its count interface; its limiting cluster law is unidentified.
That Palm/critical-count object must not be silently substituted for the ordinary
bar process. Separated-fold multiplicity also means that expected counts and
occurrence probabilities need separate higher-order expansions.

## Fifty models and predictive falsification

The [implemented suite](../experiments/universality/README.md) supplies fifty
distinct planar finite Fourier law candidates, ten positive symmetric spectral
mechanisms times five circular coefficient-vector laws. Gaussian and non-Gaussian
laws with the same covariance are different models, not different seeds. An
executable exploratory pilot checks every quantized-grid barcode independently.
The [blinding protocol](../experiments/universality/PROTOCOL.md) specifies the
actual confirmation inputs; none are inferred from successful pilot execution.

Required falsification dimensions are:

1. Change the transverse sampling weight while retaining cubic fold geometry.
2. Expose covariance/jet-rank degeneration, missing regularity and higher-contact
   strata; declare these boundary cases before seeing results.
3. Retain candidate/elder rejection and nonlocal short bars instead of removing
   inconvenient observations from the estimated intensity.
4. Attack geometry, boundary conditions, anisotropy and amplitude units.
5. Compare predeclared density and cumulative bin laws, with resolution,
   truncation, sampling and analytic remainder uncertainty accounted for.
6. Test pair/triple marked counts and clustering in a declared process regime;
   a fitted mean slope cannot select a joint limiting law.

Consume original failed campaign predicates and immutable outcomes alongside
new results. A model outside the theorem class is a falsification of an overly
broad class claim, not necessarily of the narrower reference theorem. An
unresolved numerical certificate gives an indeterminate comparison, not a pass.
