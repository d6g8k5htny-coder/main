# P15 is not part of the Gaussian-field paper

Scientific effect: NONE.

The full-price theorem for a realized disjoint capacity/clutter family, with demands at least 2 and sharp factor 1/(3 - log(3e - 2)), is a combinatorics note. Demand one is an obstruction to the unrestricted extension. It is not a solution of an unrestricted prize problem, and it is not a Gaussian-field theorem.

Keep the proofs. Remove P15 from the GRF reading path. Do not cite it next to SIDE24.
