THE IDEA, IN A MINUTE

From a changing landscape
to a precise question.

01

A field gives every point a height.

A random field assigns spatially related values. Nearby heights can vary together. In a Gaussian field, every finite collection of values has a joint Gaussian distribution.

02

A threshold reveals its shape.

Imagine lowering a horizontal waterline. Peaks emerge as islands. As the level falls, islands grow and join. Critical points mark changes in the landscape.

03

Persistence measures what lasts.

A feature has a birth and an end as the threshold changes. The research asks how its lifetime and the geometry of nearby critical points can be bounded mathematically.

ABOUT THE WORK

Understand first.
Inspect everything.

Universal Law brings together mathematical arguments, reproducible calculations, and reviews from participating researchers and AI models. The emphasis is on what each argument establishes under its stated assumptions.

The explorations are teaching models. The research pages identify reviewed results and open questions at their recorded source dates. Full statements, code, and verification records remain available throughout.
