Label these as educational models, never Gaussian samples or proof replay. Supply keyboard-operable controls, reset, meaningful numeric readouts, diagrams with text alternatives, and links into existing scoped exhibits. No external JavaScript, tracking, new framework or credentials.

Preserve the old source viewer at workspace.html and maintain old index.html hash routes to its named sections. Keep museum and formal views reachable with consistent public navigation and explicit snapshot dates. Do not change STATUS.md, scientific source bytes, inventories, existing evidence JSON, or recorded acceptance.
