## Evidence actually supplied and remaining predicates

nonzero endpoint Hessian determinants. The proof constructs endpoint
normalizers without choosing eigenvectors, gives a common positive radius,
and proves spatial `C^2` bounds and a common inverse ball by contraction.

`r^(3/2)` and the second derivatives of `E_i` scale as `r^(-3/2)`. The actual
physical ellipsoid has axial scale `r` and transverse scale `r^(3/2)`. Ordinary physical Morse charts therefore
retain the shrinking-domain boundary as contact becomes degenerate. The
theorem gives neither a uniform physical radius through contact nor an
ordinary Morse chart at the exact zero-determinant contact point.

input family on the stated common domains. Spatial `C^2` estimates do not
imply joint `C^2` dependence in separation `r` through zero. Replacing `r`
with its square root does not itself prove that missing input regularity.
