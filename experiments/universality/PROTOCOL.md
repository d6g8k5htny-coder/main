# A falsification protocol before a blind confirmation

Use [the research program](../../docs/PERSISTENCE_UNIVERSALITY_PROGRAM.md) as the
mathematical target. The catalog supplies fifty finite Fourier candidate laws;
the small pilot is an exploratory software exercise. It consumes only its named
exploratory seed namespace. It is not held out, a slope estimate or a continuum
certificate.

## The observable and experimental unit

For each declared model m, domain/volume V and bin [a,b), define
mu_m([a,b)) = E N_m([a,b))/V for actual positive finite ordinary superlevel H0
bars. Essential and zero bars are excluded. The independent statistical unit is
one entire field realization. Bars, grids, cutoffs and algorithms for the same
field are coupled observations.

The proposed leading bin law is (3/2)c_m(b^(2/3)-a^(2/3)). Density exponent -1/3
and cumulative exponent 2/3 are distinct. c_m must be derived for model m with
its volume, amplitude, covariance, marks and geometry. Reference SIDE24 digits
are not predictions for the other forty-nine laws. For Hk and other filtrations,
freeze a different observable and theory contract.

## Inputs required before consuming confirmation samples

Freeze and archive all of the following, with source identity and substantive
review appropriate to each mathematical input:

1. Each law's complete definition, dimension, topology, covariance or higher law,
   units and actual theorem-admission predicates. Finite-cutoff candidates do not
   automatically have an ideal infinite-field extension or contact rank.
2. An implementation-to-ideal-law coupling or a deliberately finite-law estimand;
   PRNG seed labels and floating coefficients do not prove an IID ideal input law.
3. Truncation/spatial/nodal/barcode/count-transfer bounds valid at every retained
   sample, with exceptional-event count moments. Exact integer H0 certifies the
   quantized grid only.
4. A predeclared lifetime window and exact bin endpoints justified by the numeric
   asymptotic remainder/cutoff and approximation budget, independent of fits.
5. Coefficient enclosures and complete null prediction bands, including model,
   sampling, analytic remainder and numerical uncertainty.
6. Fixed sample size per model with field-level simultaneous error control,
   predeclared discrepancy and indeterminate rules, multiplicity across models
   and bins, and zero/empty/failed-certificate policy. No bar-level pseudoreplicates.
7. Separate calibration and holdout custody: a distinct custodian freezes model
   labels, seeds and analysis code before generation; the analyst sees masked
   model IDs. Commitments bind configuration, algorithm version, sample plan,
   bin endpoints, reference predictions, uncertainty and decision rule. Reveal
   labels only after the fixed analysis outputs are frozen. A hash alone does
   not make an author-run experiment blind.
8. An independent source-aware audit of the actual coupling/window/analysis and
   a retained record of all exclusions, failures, amendments and reveals.

Missing inputs keep that model's continuum confirmation unexecuted. Do not fill
the gaps with fitted coefficients or a favorable pilot slope. The current runner
has no confirmation mode and creates no holdout or blinding receipt.

## Predictive attacks and decision policy

Freeze boundary/control predictions before the new data. Include vanishing
discriminant density, altered pair-count tilt, rank-degenerate spectra, insufficient
smoothness, higher-order contacts, boundary/geometry changes and nonlocal pairing.
The model catalog's non-Gaussian and alternative-spectrum rows are candidate
stress laws; they have not been proved to violate or satisfy an admission predicate.
Add deterministic counterexamples with actual stated scopes where appropriate.

All valid fields enter the field-level count analysis; empty bins are observations.
All invalid/unresolved numerical rows retain a reason and cannot become passes.
Distinguish a rejection inside the proved class from a failure of a proposed
broader admission class, and from an indeterminate numerical comparison. Do not
refit the interval or discard spectra/rows after reading outcomes.

For marked processes, freeze a conditioning or volume-growth regime, position and
lifetime rescaling, mark space, candidate limiting laws and joint statistics.
Retain pair/triple counts and clustering. On a fixed torus unconditioned short-bar
processes approach the empty process; a mean slope does not establish a Poisson law.

## What can run now

Run the exact toy controls and the tiny fifty-law pilot to verify mechanisms and
software. Their results demonstrate implementation coverage only. The existing
[periodic-H0 readiness contract](../periodic_h0/CONFIRMATION_READINESS.md) remains
the analytic boundary for its own model, and older failed campaigns remain
evidence. Prospective dimension-three and geometry adapters need their own
barcode/count/error certificates; the planar cap is not a dimension-free cap.
