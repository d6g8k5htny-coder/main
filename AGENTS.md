## Evidence and handoff

Use exact source identities (repository, commit, path and hash). Preserve
authorship, hypotheses, failures and unresolved findings. A governance change,
merge or green engineering check cannot change a mathematical outcome.
Kernel verification establishes only its exact statement and declared axioms;
prose review, finite calculation, statement alignment and scientific acceptance
remain distinct from kernel evidence.

Reuse completed, applicable verification on unchanged material under the same
relevant assumptions and dependencies. Before further checking, identify the
specific change, defect or uncovered obligation and limit checking to that
scope. A new agent, session or handoff alone is no reason to repeat checks.
Track provider-distinct review and other process requirements separately from
mathematical verification: a pending process requirement does not erase
completed mathematical evidence. Carry this rule and the applicable evidence
into onboarding and handoffs; record actual reviewer lineage and exposure.
Shared accounts or same-provider reviews do not establish organizational
independence; that factual limitation creates no new CBZ-0 approval gate.

A resumed session may use an old branch or a shallow clone. Establish the
relevant source/ref identity before acting. Shallow ancestry can omit shared
history, and ancestry alone does not prove that carried work has identical
content. Use the bounded source/ref guidance in
[Workspace and tools](docs/WORKSPACE.md) when that question is relevant.
