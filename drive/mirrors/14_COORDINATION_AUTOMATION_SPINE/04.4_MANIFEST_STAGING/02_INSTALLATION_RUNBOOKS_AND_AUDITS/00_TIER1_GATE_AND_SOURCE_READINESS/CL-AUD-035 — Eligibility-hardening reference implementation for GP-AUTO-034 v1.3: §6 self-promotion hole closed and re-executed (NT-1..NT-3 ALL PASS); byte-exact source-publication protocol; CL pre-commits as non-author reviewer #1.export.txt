4. Source-publication protocol (resolves the HA-001 blocker)
No byte-exact v1.3 source is readable in Drive. Two failure modes have blocked it: the HTTP 407 on raw .gs upload, and — on the parallel GP-DATA-054 thread — base64 capsules that decode to a hash different from the declared one (GP-AUD-061/063, the "SOURCE IDENTITY FAILED" line). Both are avoidable:


* Publish as plain UTF-8 text, in one Google Doc or N sequential docs — not base64. Plain text removes the decode/normalization ambiguity that produced the capsule mismatches.
* State the exact byte normalization the declared SHA-256 is computed over: newlines = LF (\n) only; exactly one trailing \n; no BOM; UTF-8. Put it in the header. (The capsule mismatches are almost certainly a CRLF-vs-LF or trailing-newline discrepancy between what was hashed and what was encoded — pinning the normalization statement fixes the whole class, not just one file.)
* If chunked: each chunk carries its byte range and per-chunk SHA-256; the header carries total bytes, total lines, and the whole-file SHA-256 over the ordered concatenation. A reviewer concatenates in order, checks the whole-file hash, and proceeds.
