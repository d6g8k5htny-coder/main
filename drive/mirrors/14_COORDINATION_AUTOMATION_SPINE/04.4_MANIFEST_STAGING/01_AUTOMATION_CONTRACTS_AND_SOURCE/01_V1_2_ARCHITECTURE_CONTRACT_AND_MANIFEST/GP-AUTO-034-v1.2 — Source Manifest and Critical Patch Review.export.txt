COUPLED ADVANCEMENT RULE


For mathematical transition types, the validator requires evidence class, law or measure, regime, normalization, coordinate system, independent-review status, affected dependencies, unaffected dependencies, remaining uncertainty, and reopening condition. It then requires either a nonempty architectural change or a valid linked no-change certificate.


For architectural transition types, the validator requires architecture failure mode, uncertainty permanently removed, and detection tests.


For serious mathematical error transitions, it requires failure class, architecture response, and recurrence control.


For architecture incidents, it requires downstream-contamination assessment, affected mathematical objects, and quarantine status.


PRIMARY-EVIDENCE RULE


Evidence marked summary-only, or assigned SUMMARY, NAVIGATION, BALLOT, REGISTER_COPY, or REVIEW_OF_REVIEW roles, cannot satisfy primary evidence. Admissible primary roles are PRIMARY_DERIVATION, EXECUTABLE_RECEIPT, RAW_DATA, FORMAL_PROOF, and INDEPENDENT_RECONSTRUCTION.


INDEPENDENCE RULE


The deterministic score starts at 1.0 and applies penalties for original-derivation visibility, same-code reuse, same-fixture reuse, inherited assumptions, lack of independent reconstruction, failure to check object definition, and algebra-only review. Independent notation and properly preserved blind-observer conditions supply limited positive weight. High independence begins at 0.75 and also requires independent reconstruction, object-definition checking, and non-algebra-only review.


REFEREE-READY RULE


An object is REFEREE-READY only when verified terminal closure, operator human decision, approved canonical impact, non-null authority, transition PASS, admissible primary evidence, high-independent review, global-object audit PASS, dependency and evidence chains, complete object card, valid routing, and zero validation errors all hold.
