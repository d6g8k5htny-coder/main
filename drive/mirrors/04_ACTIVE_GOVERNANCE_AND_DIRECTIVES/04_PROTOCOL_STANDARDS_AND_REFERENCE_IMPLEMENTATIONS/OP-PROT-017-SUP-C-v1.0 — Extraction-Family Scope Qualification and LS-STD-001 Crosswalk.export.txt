=====================================================================
0. WHY THIS SUPPLEMENT EXISTS
=====================================================================


OP-PROT-017-v1.0 section 4 opens: "The following is the sole authorized frozen-body extraction
procedure for this Drive." Its steps N1-N8 contain NO step for a carrier-declared internal
blank-line transform. Read literally, that sentence forbids the BRH-1 conditional blank-run halving
on the carriers that declare it — which contradicts LS-STD-001, the extraction-family classification
standard that OP-PROT-017's own trigger diagnostic (CL-AUD-283 Finding G1) produced.


OP-PROT-017 is a frozen, receipted object (body 1,751 B / SHA-256
ef55e58ed1d74c54286ec2f9f4ea43f91be1dced98725b14dc60e183aa780267; receipt
CL-REC-OPPROT017-20260727-01). Under the successor-only rule its body is not edited in place. This
supplement qualifies the scope additively. Every normative statement of OP-PROT-017 survives
unchanged, which is itself the main result.


=====================================================================
1. THE QUALIFICATION
=====================================================================


  1. OP-PROT-017 REMAINS the binding transport, decoding, marker-resolution, canonicalization, and
     hashing protocol. Nothing here weakens N1-N8: same-run native text/plain export by exact Drive
     ID or raw-file read; BOM discarded on decode; CRLF and lone CR normalized to LF; markers matched
     as UNIQUE FULL LINES with fail-closed multiplicity; region strictly between the marker lines;
     leading whitespace and leading LF preserved; all trailing LF removed and exactly one appended;
     UTF-8 without BOM; identity is the (length, digest) pair.


  2. OP-PROT-017's executable N1-N8 path IS THE LMC-1 REALIZATION — the Literal Marker
     Canonicalization of LS-STD-001, in which no internal blank-run transform is applied. This is
     what OP-PROT-017 already does; naming it removes the ambiguity.


  3. A BRH-1 CARRIER uses the SAME OP-PROT-017 transport and marker logic, and ADDITIONALLY applies
     its carrier-declared conditional blank-run halving (LS-STD-001 section 3) BETWEEN N6 and N7 —
     that is, after region extraction and interior preservation, before trailing-LF
     canonicalization. This is one inserted step, not a different protocol.


  4. EXTRACTION-FAMILY SELECTION IS GOVERNED BY LS-STD-001, precedence A (declared family
     identifier) > B (declared prose policy, read in full) > C (incorporated versioned manifest) >
     D (never inferred from project custom).


  5. THE CARRIER DETERMINES THE FAMILY. The advertised hash NEVER determines the family. Trying
     alternate rules and selecting the one that matches the advertised hash is a provenance failure
     (LS-STD-001 control R6).


  6. NO PREVIOUSLY REPRODUCED IDENTITY IS CHANGED. OP-PROT-017-SUP-A measured 21 of 21
     marker-extracted frozen objects reproducing under N1-N8, and GP-AUD-DQ055 measured 36 of 37
     active rows. Every one of those carriers is an LMC-1 carrier (no declared blank-run transform),
     so the LMC-1 realization is exactly what reproduced them. The BRH-1 carriers named by CL-AUD-283
     G1 and LS-STD-001 section 8 — GP-DATA-208/212/214 — are NOT in either swept set. The two
     families operate on disjoint carrier sets on this Drive; there is no measured collision.


  7. THE 21-OF-21 AND ALL OTHER REPRODUCTION RECEIPTS REMAIN VALID within their carrier families.
     They were, and remain, LMC-1 reproductions. This supplement reclassifies none of them; it only
     records that they are LMC-1 and that a BRH-1 carrier would take the additional inserted step.
