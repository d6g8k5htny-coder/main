### (d) Reading copies are not the objects

A row with `exact: false` is a `text/plain` export of a native Google Doc. Its `sha256` and `bytes`
are the **export's**, first computed in this repository; the Doc carries no digest in
`drive/inventory.jsonl`, a later export may differ, and the export is not the Doc's bytes. A PDF
rendering is likewise not a frozen body and carries no marker-delimited identity — `HA-007 V2.pdf`
is indexed, not stored, for that reason among others. `CL-COR-227-v1.0`'s own rule applies to this
directory: a rendering can confirm presence, never absence.

A row with `exact: true` means only that the bytes fetched from the Drive hashed equal to the
2026-09-17 inventory digest at the inventory byte count. That is identity of bytes. Nothing was
stored whose digest did not match; no `HASH_MISMATCH` arose in this port.
