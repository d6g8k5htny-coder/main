The failure this prevents
Search the Drive for apoha, psi, topological, Reflexive, Lambda, or HLV and you get one hit — a single December 2025 report. It is easy to read that as "this work isn't here" or, worse, "this work doesn't exist."


It exists. It is on the other surface. The two surfaces hold different things, and neither is a superset of the other.

Three consequences worth internalizing
1. The ancestor/descendant pairs straddle the boundary. Three active project-knowledge programs have their visible ancestor sitting in the Drive legacy stratum:


* HLV / ECM / RH ← Drive's Complexity Minimization… (2025-12-15)
* Lambda Loop ← Drive's Evaluating the Λ-Dialectic Framework (2025-12-14)
* RFT ← Drive's Did you verify all of those… (2026-01-04), which is the negative finding


Tracing provenance for any of these means crossing surfaces. The Drive side is older and, in the RFT case, more skeptical than the descendant manuscripts.


2. q0 appears on both sides, at different stages. Drive holds it consolidated and current; project knowledge holds the working files it was consolidated from. When they disagree, the Drive consolidation governs — its authority order is stated in Q0_MASTER and reproduced in _Q0 DRIVE INDEX. Do not resolve a conflict by preferring whichever file you found first.


3. The decisive open test is project-knowledge-side. Applying the topological fingerprint to actual Quijote snapshots is the verification the cosmological claims turn on. All of its pipeline is in project knowledge; none of it is in Drive.


________________


Practical rule
Looking for the current q0 theorem, its ledger, or its verifier → Drive. Looking for anything else, or for q0's working history → project knowledge. Looking for where a secondary program came from → check the Drive legacy stratum for its ancestor. An empty Drive search is not evidence of absence. Check the other surface before concluding anything is missing.
