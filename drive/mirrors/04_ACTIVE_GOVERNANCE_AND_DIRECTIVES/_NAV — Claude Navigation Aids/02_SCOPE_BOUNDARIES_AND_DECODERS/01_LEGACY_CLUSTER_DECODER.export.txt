Method and its limits. Identifications were made on 2026-07-20 from each file's opening content as returned by Drive search, plus full reads of the two short documents. Seventeen Drive files predate the q0 uploads. Three of the six distinct documents are titled Text2, Text3, and Texts. Those names identify nothing. Until now, the only way to learn what they held was to open a 1.84 MB file and read.


Canon status of this entire stratum. The Research Carry-Forward Canon (§3) directs that RUFL/RUFT, Magneto Harmonics, HLV, and FIM claims are not to be treated as established theories — they are historical research branches containing hypotheses, partial models, failed derivations, and idea-generating material. Every identification below inherits that status. Nothing in this stratum is live.

Copy reconciliation summary
17 files → 6 documents → 11 redundant copies.


The pattern is consistent and explains itself: each source document was uploaded as .docx and separately converted to a Google Doc, and in three cases the conversion ran twice. The two exact-size pairs (Λ-Dialectic Google Docs at 19,855; Request Refined docx at 40,915) are the strongest duplicate candidates.


Not acted on. Canon §7 holds that original Drive files remain untouched absent an explicit instruction, and prescribes a manifest-and-hash procedure before any archival move. Deduplication is exactly such a move. Recommended sequence if you choose to proceed: hash all 17 with SHA-256 → confirm the suspected pairs are byte-identical rather than merely same-sized → retain the .docx originals as authoritative and the Google Doc conversions as readable → record the result in the next Canon version. Do not reverse that order.
