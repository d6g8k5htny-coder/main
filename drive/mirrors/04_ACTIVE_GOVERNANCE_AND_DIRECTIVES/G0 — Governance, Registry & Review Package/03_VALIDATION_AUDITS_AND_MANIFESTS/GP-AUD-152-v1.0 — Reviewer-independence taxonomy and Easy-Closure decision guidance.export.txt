2. THREE LEVELS OF INDEPENDENCE


Future artifacts should distinguish the following rather than using the single word “independent” without qualification.


A. Execution independence
A fresh execution, isolated run, separate tool path, or byte-exact re-computation. This can detect transcription, arithmetic, implementation, and mutation failures even when performed by the same model family.


B. Line independence
A review performed under a separately designated project line, role, or session, such as CL versus AO48 versus CW/C047R under the current roster. This can reduce immediate context contamination but does not establish provider-level diversity.


C. Organizational independence
A review from a different provider/model family or a qualified human/proof-assistant channel with genuinely disjoint provenance. This is the strongest ordinary cross-model standard presently available.


Every gate should state which of A, B, or C is required. Copy count, session count, or renamed lineage must never be represented as organizational independence.
