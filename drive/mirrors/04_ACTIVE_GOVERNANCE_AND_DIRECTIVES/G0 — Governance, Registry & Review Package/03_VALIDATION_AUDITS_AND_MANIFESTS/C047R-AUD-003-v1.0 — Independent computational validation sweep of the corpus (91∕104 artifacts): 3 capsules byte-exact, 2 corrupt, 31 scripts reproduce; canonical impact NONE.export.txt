1. SCOPE & METHOD
Every Python script (58) and every capsule-candidate Doc (46) in the shared drive was
handed to an independent validator running in a clean sandbox (Python 3.9; numpy 2.0.2,
scipy 1.13.1, sympy 1.14, mpmath). Each artifact was: materialized to exact bytes;
for capsules, checked against its OWN declared gzip/source byte-counts and SHA-256s;
safety-scanned (network / file-deletion / shell-out); and, if clean, executed with a
300s timeout and compared to its declared receipt/headline. 91 of 104 completed here;
13 were deferred (see §5). No canonical state was changed; nothing was closed or deleted.


2. RESULTS (91 completed)
  Capsule-candidate Docs (40 of 46 processed):
    - 5 are genuine byte-exact capsules; 35 are audit/narrative/blank docs (no embedded
      source) — correctly identified, nothing to run.
    - Of the 5 real capsules: 3 verified BYTE-EXACT (all four hash/length checks pass) and
      re-ran to their declared verdict; 2 are CORRUPT in the Drive (see §3).
  Scripts (51 of 58 processed):
    - 31 executed. Many reproduce an explicit PASS/verdict (see §4).
    - 20 are not standalone-runnable: they import co-located project modules or read
      hardcoded inputs (e.g. c019_flow, jet15_meta.pkl, C104_FREEZE.json, /mnt/data/*)
      not present in isolation — recorded honestly as "requires external input", NOT failures.
    - 5 were correctly NOT run by the safety scanner because they shell out via subprocess
      (validate_gate_kernel_v2_0, joint sim, build_q0_c093_release, C096_GATE_KERNEL_V1_2_REDTEAM,
      audit_q0_c093_release) — benign build/validation drivers, but flagged per policy.

  * GP-DATA-114-v1.0 ("Uniform-r Fixed-q Degree-Four Corridor Box"): CORRUPT capsule.
    Stored SOURCE gzip has 16 illegal embedded spaces, wrong length (3650 vs 3600B) and bad
    padding; RECEIPT gzip truncated (849 of 1149B). Every declared SHA-256 mismatches;
    neither block decompresses. Confirmed stable across 4 independent fetches -> the
    corruption is in the Drive Doc, not transcription. The Doc's own status line already
    says "FRESH DRIVE RECONSTRUCTION REQUIRED BEFORE USE". Recommend re-publishing it.
  * "FAILED STAGING — GP-DATA-072-v1.0": CORRUPT gzip (6194 vs declared 7149B). Its title
    already marks it a failed staging shell; superseded by GP-DATA-072-v1.2. No action beyond
    confirming it should not be used.
  * C047R_verification_battery_v2.py: source SHA-256 MISMATCH vs its self-declared authoring
    hash (whitespace/line-ending lineage variant) — but all 10 symbolic tests + 9 controls
    PASS. Consistent with the earlier CL note; recommend re-stamping the self-hash.
  No OTHER integrity mismatch was found: the 3 verified capsules and every self-hashed
  script that carried a hash matched exactly.
