2. FIRST-PRINCIPLES DERIVATION


Write Q = μ + σZ with μ=-b, σ=√2, Z~N(0,1), and α=(0-μ)/σ=b/√2. For a Gaussian X~N(μ,σ²), direct integration by parts gives


    E[X² 1{X≤a}]
      = (μ²+σ²) Φ(α)
        - σ(μ+a) φ(α),


with α=(a-μ)/σ. Setting a=0, μ=-b, σ=√2 gives


    E[Q² 1{Q<0}]
      = (b²+2) Φ(b/√2)
        + √2 b φ(b/√2).


No asymptotic argument, simulation, or unproved theorem is used in this identity.
