2. FIRST-PRINCIPLES DERIVATION


Because the four source jets are independent under the declared planar coalesced covariance, covariance is obtained by summing products of coefficients weighted by the source variances 2,2,2,6.


The exact diagonal terms are


    M11 = s²(3c⁴+3c²s²+s⁴)/24,
    M22 = s²(4c²+s²)/2,
    M33 = 2s².


The only nonzero off-diagonal entry is


    M12 = M21 = -cs²(2c²+s²)/4.


Y3 uses only a_yy, while Y1 and Y2 use only a_xxy, a_xyy, and a_yyy, so


    M13=M23=0.


The determinant therefore reduces to


    det M
      = 2s²(M11 M22 - M12²).


Exact expansion and factorization give


    M11 M22 - M12²
      = s⁶(c²+s²)(3c²+s²)/48,


hence


    det M = s⁸(c²+s²)(3c²+s²)/24.
