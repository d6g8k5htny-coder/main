2. Exact proof


Differentiating gives


    f_λ'(x) = -x^2 + λ,


so the critical points are x=±√λ. Their values are


    f_λ(√λ)  = -(λ^(3/2))/3 + λ^(3/2) = (2/3)λ^(3/2),
    f_λ(-√λ) =  (λ^(3/2))/3 - λ^(3/2) = -(2/3)λ^(3/2).


Therefore


    ℓ = f_λ(√λ)-f_λ(-√λ) = (4/3)λ^(3/2).


Since r=2√λ, we have λ=r^2/4 and hence


    ℓ = (4/3)(r^2/4)^(3/2)
      = (4/3)(r^3/8)
      = r^3/6.
