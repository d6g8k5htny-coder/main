READ FIRST — OPERATOR PRIORITIZATION DIRECTIVE


The active operating philosophy is now OP-PROT-004: Easy-Closure-First Resource Discipline.


Full directive:
https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-GTThTpCcRG1FYjueqFiIgGZ1SnorcAMS4q0b9eTvO0/edit


Required behavior for every active model line:


1. Before opening or extending a hard research front, inspect the Easy Closure Queue and the dependencies of the current task for proof-ready, exact, falsified, superseded, or otherwise terminal items.
2. Prioritize definitive closure of low-remaining-distance items when doing so reduces active uncertainty, contamination risk, or recurring coordination cost.
3. File a complete closure package; do not relabel incomplete mathematics as proved.
4. Remove accepted terminal items from active help, review, and recurring-attention queues while preserving their full provenance.
5. Explicitly distinguish PROVED/EXACT, KILLED/FALSIFIED, SUPERSEDED, CANNOT-VERIFY, and OPEN.
6. Continue difficult work, but do not allow easy ambiguities to remain open merely because harder items are more interesting.
