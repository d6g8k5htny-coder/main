ANSWERS TO THE §8 REQUIRED QUESTIONS
1. Does CL-DATA-051 recompute a_r or import it?  IMPORTS it. The script hard-codes
   PNC = {0.05: 8.449...e-08, 0.025: 8.157...e-09, 0.0125: 1.0826...e-09} and sets
   a_r = 1.0 - PNC[r] with source="CL-AUD-049". No population or trajectory is
   regenerated for a_r. (Confirms F1.)
2. Does its corner set cover all nine dimensions?  NO. relev = [0,1,2,4,6] varies only
   5 of the 9 jet coordinates, giving 2^5 = 32 corners, plus 40 random interior points.
   This is not a nine-dimensional continuum cover. (Confirms F2.)
3. Can finite vertices prove the nonlinear continuum event without an added theorem?  NO.
   A finite corner+interior sample cannot establish B ⊂ A_r for the continuum box. (F2/F4.)
4. Does the source use solver-success, all-finite, and capture-event gates?  NO. captures()
   integrates with an escape event and then reads capture from final distance
   (hypot(P_end+(0.5,0)) < cap) at horizon Tmax; there is no solver-success assertion,
   no all-finite gate, and no dedicated capture event. (Confirms F3.)
5. Is P_MS(B) box membership or certified-capture mass?  MEMBERSHIP. P_MS_box =
   (W * inbox).sum()/W.sum() is determinant-weighted Monte-Carlo membership in the
   coordinate box, not certified-capture mass; a_r >= P_MS(B) would need a proved
   inclusion the source does not supply. (Confirms F4.)
6. Are the retained positive diagnostics stated fairly?  YES. The package correctly
   retains: the box-mass diagnostic near 0.71 at the three rungs (with qualifications),
   the 72/72 tested points, the GP-DATA-104 512-vertex fail-closed captures, the
   1,536-case corpus, and GP-REQ-105 corridor work. The code's all_capture flag and the
   P_MS_box value are exactly these retained quantities; nothing legitimate is discarded.
7. Are exact-field, uniform-r, and theorem claims correctly excluded?  YES. The source
   drops side-24 torus image terms (<1e-100, disclosed), uses local degree-four dynamics,
   and runs only three discrete rungs (F5); the package's exclusion of "exact law",
   "uniform across accessible r", a_r->1, P0.1/P0.2/Theorem B is therefore correct.


ASSESSMENT
The retirement is accurate and appropriately narrow. It removes exactly the five
over-promoted claims (C1–C5) that the source does not support, while preserving every
legitimate numerical diagnostic. It does not over-retract: it does not declare the
historical numbers false, and it keeps the corridor program and P0.1 open.
