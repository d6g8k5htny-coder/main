Identity B — transverse chart:

For Identity B, the left side expands as

(1-α)(a^2+2aη+η^2) + α(a^2-2aη+η^2)
= a^2 + 2aη - 4αaη + η^2.


Thus both identities are exact coefficient-by-coefficient polynomial equalities.
