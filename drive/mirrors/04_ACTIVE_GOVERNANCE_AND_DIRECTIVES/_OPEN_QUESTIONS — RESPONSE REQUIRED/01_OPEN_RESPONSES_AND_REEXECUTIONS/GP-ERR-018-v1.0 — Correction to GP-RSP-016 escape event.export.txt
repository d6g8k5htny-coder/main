# Correction to GP-RSP-016 escape event


## Error


GP-RSP-016 reported a six-rung direct-ODE table as evidence about the adjacency complement. That integration declared failure when


    max(|x|/r, |y|/r^2) > 12.


The |y|/r^2 condition detects exit from the anisotropic O(r^2) trapping corridor constructed for a sufficient positivity event. It is not the definition of gradient nonadjacency.


A branch can leave that narrow corridor and later return to the maximum basin. After reading CW-DER-008, CW-DATA-010, and GP-AUD-012, I recognized that the event used in GP-RSP-016 was measuring a stronger corridor-containment property, not A_r itself.


## Consequence


The six-rung table in GP-RSP-016 must not be cited as an estimate of


    1-a_r.


Its apparent weighted scaling is a corridor-exit diagnostic only. The verdict attached to that table is withdrawn.


The corpus-search conclusion in GP-RSP-016 remains valid:


- no analytic proof of 1-a_r=O(r^3) was found in Q0_MASTER;
- generic and determinant-weighted C013 observations remain measurements only;
- GP-DER-008 proves, at most, a route to inf a_r>0 and does not contain the complement rate.


The OQ-008 section of GP-RSP-016 is unaffected.


## Correct replacement


GP-AUD-017-v1.0 uses the corrected finite-r anisotropic scaled flow


    dX/dtau=P_X,


    dY/dtau=(q/r)Y+(P_Y-qY),


and classifies chart escape by |X|>16, with M capture and finite-time stall reported separately. It reproduces the corrected r=0.025 baseline and extends it over four rungs.


The replacement diagnostic finds slopes approximately


    generic complement: 0.962,


    determinant-weighted complement: 2.939,


under the pure-cubic finite-r model. This supports but does not prove the r^3 mechanism.


## Process lesson


The error was not arithmetic. It was an event-definition substitution: a sufficient trapping corridor was silently reused as if its complement were equivalent to nonadjacency. This is precisely why object definitions and both directions of an event inclusion must be checked separately.
