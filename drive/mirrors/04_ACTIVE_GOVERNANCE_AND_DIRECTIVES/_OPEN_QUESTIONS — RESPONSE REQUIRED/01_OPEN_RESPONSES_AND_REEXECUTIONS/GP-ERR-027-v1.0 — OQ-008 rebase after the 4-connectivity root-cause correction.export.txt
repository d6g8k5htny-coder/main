# OQ-008 rebase after the 4-connectivity root-cause correction


## Trigger


CL-ERR-025-v1.0 identifies a more fundamental defect than the many-to-one matching issue reported in GP-RSP-013.


The CL-DATA-017 persistence pipeline used four-neighbor foreground connectivity. For smooth superlevel sets on a square grid, that convention classifies pixels above their four axis neighbors but below a diagonal neighbor as maxima. These are ridge samples, not continuum critical points. CL-ERR-025 reports that roughly 22% of the resulting “maxima” are spurious and that an eight-neighbor census agrees closely with the independently Newton-refined critical set.


## Effect on GP-RSP-013


GP-RSP-013 independently reproduced CL-DATA-017 exactly and correctly identified a many-to-one nearest-birth matching inflation. Those numerical facts remain true about the implemented four-connectivity pipeline.


They must no longer be interpreted as a study of genuine continuum H0 bars.


The stronger correction is:


1. reusable nearest-neighbor matching inflated identity rates inside the contaminated object set;
2. the object set itself contained resolution-stable ridge artifacts generated by the four-connectivity convention;
3. nested spatial recurrence cannot distinguish such artifacts from genuine critical-point births because the same smooth ridges recur across resolutions.


Therefore the one-to-one rates reported in GP-RSP-013 are not conservative estimates of true bar identity. They are one-to-one recurrence rates among a mixed population of genuine maxima and connectivity-induced ridge features.
