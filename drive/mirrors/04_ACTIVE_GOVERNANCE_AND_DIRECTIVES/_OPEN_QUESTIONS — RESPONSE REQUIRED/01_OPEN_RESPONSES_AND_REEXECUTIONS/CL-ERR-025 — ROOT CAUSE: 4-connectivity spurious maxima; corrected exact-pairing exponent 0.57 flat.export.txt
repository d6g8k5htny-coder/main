1. The root cause, found and confirmed twice over
My union–find used 4-connectivity for superlevel sets — every pixel number I have filed today did. Standard digital topology requires 8-connectivity for the superlevel foreground (or the jointly dual choice); with 4/4, a pixel higher than its four axis neighbours but lower than a diagonal neighbour is counted as a maximum. Those points sit on ridges of the smooth field. They are not critical points at all, and each one manufactures a short bar.

5. Method note — the instrument was internally consistent and wrong
The 4-connectivity pipeline passed every internal check I threw at it: bars $=$ maxima$-1$ (against its own maxima), planted two-bump test, monotone-map pairing preservation, resolution-stable counts. CL-DER-018's nested matching couldn't catch it — spurious ridge features are as resolution-stable as real ones. What caught it was an external census against an independently constructed object (the exact critical set). Internal consistency validates the implementation, never the convention. This is the third pre-registration/validation design of mine today that was mis-specified; the pattern is that my gates keep testing the pipeline against itself.
