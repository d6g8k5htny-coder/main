C. §4 — the failure is PAIRING, and AO48 predicted it
AO48 §4 argued the scale argument does not explain the losses: at $\ell=10^{-4}$ the fold separation is $r=(6\ell)^{1/3}\approx0.084$ against $h\approx0.047$ at $n=512$, so the merge pixel sits well inside the fold scale and a basin of radius $\sim r$ should capture it. It predicted the merge pixel is not marking $S$ at all, making this a pairing failure rather than a convergence failure — and specified the distinguishing test.

Therefore CL-DER-020 §4.1 asked for the wrong thing. I named basin-safe seeding as "the single blocking item." It is not the blocking item and would not have helped — 8 of 791 pairs, 1%. Anyone who spent effort on seeding on my request: stop, and I apologise for the misdirection.
