## 5. A fast screen was not reliable enough


The stable-root screen produced an exceptionally clean apparent scaling:


- unweighted complement approximately proportional to r;
- weighted complement approximately proportional to r^3.


However, direct ODE checks showed that the screen overclassified failures. Among 30 screened failures and 30 screened passes at each of r=0.1,0.05,0.025, the screen agreed with direct integration only 65%–70%. Every sampled screen-pass was an ODE pass, but many screen-fails were actually captured by M.


This is a useful rejection of an easy but invalid confirmation route. All final numbers below use direct ODE integration.

## 7. Frozen adjudication


The data support the proposed qualitative mechanism:


- failure moves into a Q=O(r) layer;
- determinant weighting strongly suppresses that layer;
- the smallest-rung weighted estimates are compatible with an r^3 scale.


But the frozen confirmation criterion is not met.


The slopes are not stable across four consecutive rungs. There is a visible crossover between r=0.025 and r=0.0125; the slow-screen and direct-ODE classifications diverge; and rare weighted failures make the estimated coefficient sensitive to a small number of trajectories. Under GP-PRE-015 reading R5, the result is therefore


INCONCLUSIVE.


It is not a confirmation of 1-a_r=O(r^3), and it is not a falsification. It is substantially stronger evidence for the Q^2 mechanism than the old “1.0000 at sampled rungs” report, because it resolves weighted failures and their small-Q localization.


## 8. Weakest technical issue


The largest uncertainty is no longer whether determinant weighting matters. It clearly changes the observed order by roughly two powers.


The unresolved issue is whether the direct cubic classification represents the full finite-r periodized field and whether the M basin scale used in the mechanism is uniform after higher-order jets are restored.


The cubic vector field is global and unbounded; an escape from its scaled box is not automatically a nonadjacency event in the full analytic field. Conversely, higher-order terms may create or remove endpoint capture channels. This is exactly the endpoint-block gap identified by GP-AUD-009.
