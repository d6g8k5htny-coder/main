$$z_0 = E[Q^2\mathbf 1_{{Q<0}}] = (b^2+2),\Phi!\left(\tfrac{b}{\sqrt2}\right) + \sqrt2,b,\phi!\left(\tfrac{b}{\sqrt2}\right),\qquad z_0(6/5)\approx 3.230978535.$$
