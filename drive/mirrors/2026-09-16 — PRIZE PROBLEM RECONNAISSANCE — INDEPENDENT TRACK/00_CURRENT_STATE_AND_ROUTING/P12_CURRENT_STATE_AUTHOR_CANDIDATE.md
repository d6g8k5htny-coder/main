# Phase12 current mathematical state

Let phi(q)=min(1,-log(1-q)), with phi(1)=1. A^(K) denotes sets not expressible
as unions of K members of the decreasing family A. A generator g covers every
superset of g and costs product of its coordinate prices. Empty family costs 0;
empty generator costs 1. Prices and original probabilities are distinct inputs.

P12-A: heterogeneous sorted-prefix integral covers for positive weighted excess.
Adapted from Talagrand's known singleton rounding proof, explicitly attributed.
For R=24 the full finite symbolic cover costs <1/7 at its own price vector.

P12-B: strict-capacity next-fit packing (non-K-packable implies scalar load>K/2)
and a probability-to-mean estimate (good probability>=3/4 implies mean<2).

P12-C: for d arbitrary nonnegative linear budgets with strict normalized capacity1,
mu_p(A)>=3/4 and c<=phi(p) imply a cover of A^(K) at cost <31/70 for K>=192d.
This is a TERMINAL/ROOT theorem, not a general all-probability gate invariant.

P12-D: disjoint child subtrees whose K-covers cost<=phi(q_i) may feed the weighted
root with the same K palette and no parameter dilution. Graph/threshold child
hierarchies use K=192d; rank3 children use max(368640,192d). A separately certified
exception cover of cost<=1/20 gives total <69/140. No shared-coordinate copying.

P12-E: explicit n193 positive-weight example: a valid prefix cover costs np while
original failure is O(n^2p^2); at p=10^-8 its cost exceeds the available upper
bound on phi(Q) by >520000. The true K192 obstruction is nonempty. This refutes
promotion of THAT constructed cover to a hazard bound, not existence of a better
weighted-gate theorem. Indeed the full-set alternative cover costs p^193.

P12-F: arbitrary unbounded-rank/arity gate, after singleton and irrelevant ports
are separated. If each connected witness component j satisfies
K^(r_j-1) product_(i in W_j)(1-q_i)>=1, where r_j is minimum witness size, its
K-obstruction has cost<=phi(1-mu_q(A)) at prices c<=phi(q). This is a true
compatible-gate extension. Combined with the existing graph/threshold primitives,
it supports arbitrary finite disjoint hierarchy depth using one fixed K>=128.
The component condition is explicit and may fail at high aggregate activation.

The prefix cover gives a terminal extension; the exact-cell lemma gives a
probability-restricted composable extension. These are not the same theorem.
The unrestricted constant-palette Talagrand target remains open in this project.
