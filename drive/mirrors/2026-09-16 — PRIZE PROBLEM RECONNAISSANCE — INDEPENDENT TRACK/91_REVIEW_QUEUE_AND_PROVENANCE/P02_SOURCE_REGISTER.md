# Source and priority register — accessed2026-09-16

## Established mathematical sources

S1. Alexander Walker, *Integer Sets of Large Harmonic Sum Which Avoid Long Arithmetic Progressions*, first posted2022, arXiv:2203.06045; consulted v2 full text.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.06045
https://arxiv.org/html/2203.06045
Primary author paper. Proposition1.1 and Theorems1.2/2.1 supply prior digit-exclusion and digital-approximation results. Table1 supplies the eleven/base22/base55 and seven additional larger-base alphabets used here. The base55 example has approximate harmonic sum4.43975. Our exact numerical reproduction is not a new construction. The paper's heuristically pruned broader searches are not silently reclassified as exhaustive certificates.

S2. Alexander Walker, *Fractal Sets and Arithmetic Progressions* (2020).
https://awwalker.com/2020/02/14/fractal-sets-and-arithmetic-progressions/
Author's earlier exposition, already credited in Phase01. The base11 alphabet is known. S1 is the stronger comparison that Phase01 failed to locate.

S3. James Leng, Ashwin Sah, Mehtaab Sawhney, *Improved Bounds for Szemeredi's Theorem*, arXiv:2402.17995v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.17995v2
Primary research paper. Used here only for the established context that fixed-k AP-free sets have vanishing density and for frontier orientation; we do not claim its quantitative bounds as our work.

S4. Thomas F. Bloom, Erdos Problems #3 and #169.
https://www.erdosproblems.com/3
https://www.erdosproblems.com/169
Problem registry. Search returned the April2026 edited records. #3 is the divergent-reciprocal/full-arbitrary-length question; #169 concerns the harmonic supremum. Treat registry currency as bounded by its displayed update. This phase has not solved either problem.

https://computoergosum.com/en/principia/erdos-169-certs/index.html
The authors describe adaptive/Bellman methods and claim a finite4-AP-free construction beating the Walker benchmark, with Lean sources and reproduction information. We accessed the certificate page and surfaced article text. We have NOT built its Lean environment, validated its numeric table, or independently accepted the theorem. It is nevertheless relevant prior/parallel work: no first-use claim for adaptive Bellman methods is justified here. The narrower priority of our complete2..30 class theorem and single-switch proof remains unresolved.
