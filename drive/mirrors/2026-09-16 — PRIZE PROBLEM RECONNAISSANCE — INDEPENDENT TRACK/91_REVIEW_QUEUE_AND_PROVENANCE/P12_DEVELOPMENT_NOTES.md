  objects, comparison of both distinct snapshots, then a NEW Drive/readback receipt,
  then further research. No unrecorded retrofit of a failed historical write.

  fail-on-conflict prevented replacement; the second ZIP was subsequently downloaded
  to a separate directory. Neither source was damaged or merged.

  produce singleton witnesses but weakly exercise the intended soft-resource case.
  The final probe uses denominators7 so all weights are<1 and genuine resource
  nonpackability is checked. This was a test-strength improvement, not a theorem kill.

  constant terminal cover to the full hazard invariant. The inference was NOT
  promoted. The actual prefix theorem survives; a much cheaper alternative cover
  exists in the counterexample, so no universal impossibility conclusion follows.

  small color search; no polynomial-time claim or all-instance enumeration is made.

  rational lower approximations to Phi, exact odds, and directed exponential upper
  bounds. Those instances are NOT the proof for all real parameter values.
- New verifiers contain no removable assert nodes. Mutation outcomes require both
  exit1 and the expected explicit guard prefix. Arbitrary crashes do not count.
  Two intentional survivors test that the harness does not manufacture rejections.
- Code paths, repeated executions, and this author's mathematical rechecks add zero
  organizational independence. No formal prover or external reviewer was run.
- Exact source hashes and upload receipts prove identity/custody only, not correctness.
