# Phase04 review record and limitations

## Prior proof audit

The Phase03 ZIP's104 manifest entries and CRC were verified before consumption. The relevant files were read completely. The bounded separated-block argument, Behrend layer count, harmonic-profile bridge, and automatic-set sparsity proof were re-examined for scope and inequality direction. No new defect was identified in the portions consumed here; this is same-author re-examination after exposure, NOT independent approval of the complete Phase03 package.

The weighted extremizer proof consumes only bounded separation, the classical sphere lower bound, and compactness for summable weights. It does not assume the open harmonic four-progression claim. The local-marginal obstruction is a separate elementary construction and consumes none of the earlier optimization computations. The Talagrand track has separate notation, field, and probability law; no Gaussian Palm, q0 theorem, or AP extremality claim is used as a premise.

## Actual pre-release defect found here

An initial implementation generalized the easy uniform high-p empty-obstruction argument by using p>=1/k. At equality in the high-measure premise, a k-color union-bound argument need not give positive probability. The exact one-element counterexample D={empty}, p=1/8, k=8 has mu(D)=7/8 and NONEMPTY obstruction, of cover cost1/8.

That implementation was changed to the valid p>=1/2 two-partition argument. The written proof uses only that argument. The positive singleton case and an explicit negative mutation remain in the suite. This error was intercepted before the final proof/package and before any external promotion. Historical test-output files are not treated as authority for a different implementation.

## Failed generalization found here

Replacing laminar scopes by arbitrary overlapping scopes while retaining the doubled-capacity decomposition is false. The three crossing pairs on three vertices, all capacity1, accept the whole set under doubled caps but admit only singletons before union. Two members cannot cover it. The final theorem is restricted, and the intersection extension increases the number of pieces explicitly.
