1. Ruben Ascoli, Xiaoyu He, Jinyoung Park, Michel Talagrand, *A reformulation of the discrete Convexity Conjecture via k-thresholds*, arXiv:2608.11183v1. FULL_TEXT HTML inspected, especially Definition1.1, Conjectures1.2/1.4, Theorem1.8 and Section8. https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11183v1 . The source supplies the exact definitions of k-unions, complement obstructions, p-small covers, and universal quantifiers. Its graph-property decomposition theorem is not automatically a theorem about arbitrary read-once threshold trees. The discrete conjecture is stated as open in this source. We do not claim the source proves our candidate or that our candidate supersedes its graph-containment results.

2. Jinyoung Park and Huy Tuan Pham, *A Proof of the Kahn--Kalai Conjecture*, arXiv:2203.17207v2. FULL_TEXT HTML inspected. https://arxiv.org/html/2203.17207v2 . Established threshold/expectation-threshold comparison contains logarithmic witness-rank dependence. It already gives qualitative fixed-rank results; those cannot be claimed as newly discovered here. This phase examines a different fixed-palette/no-dilution tradeoff under a read-once structural hypothesis. No numerical constant from this paper is used in P10-A--D.
