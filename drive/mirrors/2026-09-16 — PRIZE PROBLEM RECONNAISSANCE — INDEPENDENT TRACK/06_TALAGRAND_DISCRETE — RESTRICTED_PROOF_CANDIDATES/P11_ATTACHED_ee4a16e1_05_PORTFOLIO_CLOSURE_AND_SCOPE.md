## Honest quantifiers

P11 proves for each fixed primitive rank r a fixed palette K_r for all finite ground sets and finite disjoint hierarchy depths. It does NOT prove one palette for all r. The published unrestricted conjecture asks for constants uniform over arbitrary decreasing families, including unbounded ranks and arbitrary shared-variable dependence.

The fixed-rank tradeoff must be compared with established Kahn--Kalai/threshold theory; existence of rank-dependent constants is not claimed as a new discovery. Our candidate contribution is a direct inflated-price hazard inequality and an explicit no-dilution composition mechanism. A bounded source search cannot certify that this tradeoff is new.

## Why the result genuinely extends P10's expression class

P10 allowed arbitrary graphs only when encoded inside small-arity primitives or some permitted read-once threshold representation. P11 allows an arbitrary graph of child ports as ONE primitive, irrespective of its arity. In particular, the path-on65 gate used by P10 as a representation boundary is admitted now. That example is itself2-colorable; its purpose is scope separation, not a nonempty-obstruction benchmark.

Nonvacuous128-piece obstructions exist in the new graph setting: a129-clique, or a129-clique with private leaves, requires129 colors. At sufficiently small probabilities its bad-event probability is less than1/4. The exact latter example in the computational companion exercises both the expensive-core extraction and positive residual generator cost.

Replacing each port by a disjoint AND of m original coordinates makes the original forbidden rank2m without changing the fixed128 palette. More levels may follow. This does not permit copying the same original random variable into different children.
