# PR-TAL-010 — Crossing-graph decomposition certificates

A crossing-graph color class is laminar. A finite certificate consists of scopes/capacities plus any proper coloring. Exact chromatic-number computation is unnecessary.

The checker verifies crossing edges, proper coloring, laminarity, the inclusion D^(k^d) subset union_i D_i^(k) on exhaustive small instances, and the rational budget algebra.
