The target numerical object is the finite band-limited Bargmann–Fock field generated from one frozen coefficient array on the flat torus, together with:


1. its Newton-refined critical points;

## 3. Mandatory controls


### V1 — Torus Euler identity


For every field: #maxima + #minima - #saddles = 0.


A failure blocks the field and the aggregate fit.


### V2 — Finite-bar count


After all saddle endpoints are resolved and elder-rule merging is executed: #finite H0 bars = #maxima - 1.


This is an executable assertion, not a printed diagnostic.


### V3 — Complete branch resolution


Every unstable saddle branch must resolve to a maximum in the registered census. Unresolved branches remain in the receipt and block the field. They may not be silently excluded from the fit.


### V4 — Endpoint refinement stability


Every branch is integrated twice on the same exact field: primary adaptive tolerance, then stricter replay tolerance and smaller maximum step. Both runs must identify the same maximum. A mismatch blocks the field.


### V5 — Critical-census replay


The critical count signature (maxima, minima, saddles) must agree between the primary Newton seed lattice and a denser replay lattice. This does not prove completeness, but disagreement falsifies the current census.


### V6 — Known-object controls


The source must pass an analytic Morse critical-type/Euler control, a combinatorial elder-rule fixture, an analytic exact-field endpoint control, and negative controls showing that Euler, bar-count, unresolved-endpoint, endpoint-instability, census-replay, and failed-field conditions block fitting.


## 4. No-fit rule

## 5. Inference rule


When fitting is allowed:


- fields are the resampling clusters;
- uncertainty is computed by a field-clustered bootstrap;
- every fit window reports its genuine bar count;
- underpowered windows report UNDERPOWERED, not an exponent;
- the bulk-range fit must not be described as the asymptotic law.
