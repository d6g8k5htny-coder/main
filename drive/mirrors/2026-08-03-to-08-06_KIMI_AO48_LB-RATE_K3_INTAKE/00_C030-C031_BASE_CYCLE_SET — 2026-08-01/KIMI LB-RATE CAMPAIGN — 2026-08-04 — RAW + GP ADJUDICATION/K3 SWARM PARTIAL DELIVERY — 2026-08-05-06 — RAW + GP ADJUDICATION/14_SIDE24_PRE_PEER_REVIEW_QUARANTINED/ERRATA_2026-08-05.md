## The defect

The window-saddle intensity upper bound used in the WP work (and reported in the manuscript as the
"certified rigorous-class upper bound") carries the factor

    p_grad(0) · sqrt(E[det(H)^2 | grad=0]) · min(P_type, P_window),

whereas the Cauchy–Schwarz chain gives

    E[|det H|·1_{type∩window}] ≤ sqrt(E[det(H)^2]) · sqrt(P(type ∩ window))
                               ≤ sqrt(E[det(H)^2]) · sqrt(min(P_type, P_window)).

Since p ≤ sqrt(p) on [0,1], the missing square root makes the reported quantity SMALLER — it is not a
valid generic upper bound. Numerically, at the manuscript's witness point (−0.04, −0.58):
missing-sqrt value 1.31310e-4 vs CS-valid 2.30247e-2 (inflation 175.3×).
