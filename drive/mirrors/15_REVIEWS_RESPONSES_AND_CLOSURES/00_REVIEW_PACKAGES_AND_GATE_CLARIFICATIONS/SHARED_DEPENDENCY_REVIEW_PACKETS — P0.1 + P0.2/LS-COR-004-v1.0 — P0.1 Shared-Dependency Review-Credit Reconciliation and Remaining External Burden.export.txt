- one methodological reconstruction;
- one organizational-independence credit;

A result appearing as P02-LM-005, EC-021 evidence, Domain G evidence, or a P0.1 dependency does not create additional independence merely through relabeling or reuse.


RULE 5 — DO NOT IMPORT NON-LOAD-BEARING INTERFACES


A closed P0.2 interface is not automatically a P0.1 obligation. Review burden should follow the actual frozen proof dependency graph, not the set of all adjacent results.
