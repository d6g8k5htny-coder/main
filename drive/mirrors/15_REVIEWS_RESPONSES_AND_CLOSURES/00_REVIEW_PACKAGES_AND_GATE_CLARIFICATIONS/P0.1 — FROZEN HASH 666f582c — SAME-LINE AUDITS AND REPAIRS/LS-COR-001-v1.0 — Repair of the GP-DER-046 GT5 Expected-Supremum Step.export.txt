1. EXECUTIVE RESULT


GP-DER-046-v1.0 contains one false displayed inequality in Section 7:


sup_x |Z(x)| <= sup_x Z(x) + sup_x[-Z(x)].


The inequality fails when either one-sided supremum is negative. A singleton centered-process realization is enough to disprove it pointwise: if Z=-2 at the sole index, the left side is 2 and the right side is -2+2=0.


This defect invalidates the particular two-one-sided-suprema derivation of the bound B_5=2B_+. It does not invalidate the GT5 theorem, because the same section already identifies the correct alternative: apply Dudley directly to the signed Gaussian process


Y(s,x)=s Z(x),   s in {+1,-1}.


The supremum of Y is exactly sup_x|Z(x)|. The signed index set has a uniform entropy bound inherited from two copies of the original index set, and Borell–TIS in the next section already uses precisely this signed process. This document supplies the missing complete argument.


2. FALSE STEP AND COUNTEREXAMPLE


The pointwise identity is


sup_x |Z(x)| = max{sup_x Z(x), sup_x[-Z(x)]}.


It is not generally bounded by the sum unless both one-sided suprema are known nonnegative. A continuous Gaussian process on a nontrivial index set may still have an all-negative sample path with positive probability, so nonnegativity cannot be inserted silently.


Machine-recorded counterexample in LS-DATA-001:


index set: singleton,
Z=-2,
sup|Z|=2,
sup Z + sup(-Z)=0.


The validation result records inequality_holds=false.


3. CORRECT SIGNED PROCESS


For each fifth-derivative multi-index alpha and each r, let K_r=rD and let Z_{r,alpha}(x) be the centered exact six-pin conditioned derivative process.


Define the signed index set


T_r={-1,+1} x K_r


and the centered Gaussian process


Y_{r,alpha}(s,x)=s Z_{r,alpha}(x).


Then


sup_{(s,x) in T_r} Y_{r,alpha}(s,x)
=max{sup_x Z_{r,alpha}(x), sup_x[-Z_{r,alpha}(x)]}
=sup_x |Z_{r,alpha}(x)|.


The process is separable because K_r has a continuous separable version and the sign factor is finite.


4. SIGNED CANONICAL METRIC


Let


d_Y((s,x),(t,y))^2
=E[(sZ(x)-tZ(y))^2].


If s=t, then


d_Y((s,x),(s,y))=d_Z(x,y)<=L_6|x-y|.


If s=-t, then


d_Y((s,x),(-s,y))^2
=E[(Z(x)+Z(y))^2]
<=2E[Z(x)^2]+2E[Z(y)^2]
<=4 sigma_5^2.


Thus the full signed metric diameter is at most 2 sigma_5. Within each sign copy, the original uniform Lipschitz metric bound remains valid.


5. COVERING NUMBERS


For every epsilon>0, cover each sign copy separately by an epsilon-net for K_r under d_Z. The union of the two nets covers T_r. Therefore


N(T_r,d_Y,epsilon)
<=2 N(K_r,d_Z,epsilon)
<=2 C_D(1+A_D/epsilon)^2


for the small-epsilon regime used in the entropy integral.


For epsilon at least the signed diameter, one point covers T_r. More generally, the bound may be replaced by


N(T_r,d_Y,epsilon)
<=max{1,2C_D(1+A_D/epsilon)^2}.


The constants are independent of r and alpha.


6. UNIFORM DUDLEY INTEGRAL


Dudley’s entropy integral for the signed process is bounded by


integral_0^{2 sigma_5}
 sqrt(log N(T_r,d_Y,epsilon)) d epsilon.


Using the covering bound above, the integrand is controlled near zero by


sqrt(C_0+2 log(1+A_D/epsilon)),


whose integral is finite. Consequently there is a finite constant B_abs, independent of r and alpha, such that


E sup_{x in K_r}|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|
=E sup_{(s,x) in T_r}Y_{r,alpha}(s,x)
<=B_abs.


This replaces the invalid B_5=2B_+ derivation. The symbol B_5 may be retained if redefined to mean this signed-index Dudley bound.


7. BORELL–TIS


The maximal pointwise variance of Y is


sup_{(s,x)} Var(Y(s,x))
=sup_x Var(Z(x))
<=sigma_5^2.


Borell–TIS therefore gives, for u>=0,


P(sup_x|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|>B_abs+u)
<=exp[-u^2/(2sigma_5^2)].


There are exactly six total-order-five multi-indices in two dimensions. A union bound yields


P(max_{|alpha|=5} sup_x|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|>B_abs+u)
<=6 exp[-u^2/(2sigma_5^2)].


Combining with the previously established uniform conditional-mean bound M_5 gives


P(H_{5,r}(D)>M_5+B_abs+u | exact six pins)
<=6 exp[-u^2/(2sigma_5^2)].


Taking t_0=2(M_5+B_abs) and u=t-(M_5+B_abs)>=t/2 yields


P(H_{5,r}(D)>t | exact six pins)
<=6 exp[-t^2/(8sigma_5^2)]


for t>=t_0, uniformly in sufficiently small r.


8. DEPENDENCY CHECKS


The repair uses the same exact six-pin conditioned law as GP-DER-046. It does not require independence among derivative coordinates. It does not use a finite-Q4 process. It does not use the determinant weight or normalizer for the conditioned-law GT5 theorem.


The later Palm-tail corollary still additionally requires the named determinant second-moment estimate DM2 and exact normalizer lower bound. Those are separate from GT5 itself and are not needed for the P0.1 subtraction step in frozen GP-DER-118-v1.3.

11. FALSIFIERS FOR THE REPAIR


The repair would fail if:


- the signed process is not centered or separable;
- the conditional pointwise variance lacks a uniform bound;
- the within-copy canonical metric lacks the uniform Lipschitz bound;
- the two-copy covering-number construction fails;
- the signed metric entropy integral diverges;
- Borell–TIS is applied to a nonseparable or unbounded process.


None of these failures is present under the analytic exact-torus six-pin assumptions already used by GP-DER-046.


No theorem promotion or canonical status change is made by this correction.
