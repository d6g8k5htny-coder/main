3. SIGNED-INDEX REPAIR
====================================================================


The original displayed inequality


    sup|Z|<=sup Z+sup(-Z)


is false. The exact identity is


    sup|Z|=max{sup Z,sup(-Z)}.


The corrected index set is


    T_r={-1,+1}xK_r


with centered Gaussian process


    Y(s,x)=sZ(x).


Then


    sup_{(s,x) in T_r}Y(s,x)=sup_{x in K_r}|Z(x)|.


Within one sign copy,


    d_Y((s,x),(s,y))=d_Z(x,y)<=L_6|x-y|.


Across signs,


    d_Y((s,x),(-s,y))^2
      =E[(Z(x)+Z(y))^2]
      <=2E[Z(x)^2]+2E[Z(y)^2]
      <=4sigma_5^2.


Thus the full signed metric diameter is at most 2sigma_5.


For every epsilon>0, an epsilon-net for each same-sign copy yields


    N(T_r,d_Y,epsilon)
      <=2N(K_r,d_Z,epsilon).


Because K_r lies in a fixed bounded planar chart and d_Z<=L_6|x-y|,


    N(K_r,d_Z,epsilon)
      <=C_D(1+A_D/epsilon)^2


with constants independent of r and alpha. Therefore the signed Dudley integral is uniformly finite:


    integral_0^{2sigma_5}
      sqrt(log N(T_r,d_Y,epsilon)) d epsilon
      < infinity.


Consequently there is B_abs<infinity, uniform in r and alpha, such that


    E sup_{x in K_r}|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|<=B_abs.


This completely replaces the invalid two-one-sided-suprema argument.
