3. THE PART2 DISCREPANCY WAS MINE, AND IS RESOLVED IN GP'S FAVOUR


CL-AUD-268-PART2 section 2 recorded an unattributed disagreement on max_C_entry_deviation: CL 10.000000 x r_D^2 against the carrier's 4.375 x r_D^2, ratio 16/7, with the note that the fault might well be CL's and that no finding would be asserted until attribution was settled. It has been settled. THE FAULT WAS CL'S.


Root cause. A covariance atom between two points at separation s carries the exponential factor exp(-s^2/2). The CL symbolic ring hardcoded that factor as exp(-r^2/2), which is correct for the pin-to-pin block, where the two pins at -r/2 and +r/2 are separated by r. It is NOT correct for the jets-to-pins block: the nine jets sit at the midpoint and the pins at +/-r/2, so the separation is r/2 and the carrier is exp(-r^2/8). The CL code scaled the Hermite polynomial part to argument r/2 but left the exponential at the pin-to-pin scale.


Why it produced exactly 16/7. At the argmax entry — jet c40 = (4,0) against frame row L_2 = (X_+ - X_-)/r — the exact entry is


    C[c40, L_2](r) = (2/r) k^{(5)}(r/2)
                   = [-r^4/16 + 5r^2/2 - 15] exp(-r^2/8),


whose expansion is -15 + (15/8 + 20/8) r^2 + O(r^4) = -15 + (35/8) r^2. With the wrong carrier exp(-r^2/2) the r^2 term becomes 15/2 + 5/2 = 10. Hence 10 against 35/8, and 10/(35/8) = 16/7 exactly. The arithmetic signature matched the diagnosis precisely.
