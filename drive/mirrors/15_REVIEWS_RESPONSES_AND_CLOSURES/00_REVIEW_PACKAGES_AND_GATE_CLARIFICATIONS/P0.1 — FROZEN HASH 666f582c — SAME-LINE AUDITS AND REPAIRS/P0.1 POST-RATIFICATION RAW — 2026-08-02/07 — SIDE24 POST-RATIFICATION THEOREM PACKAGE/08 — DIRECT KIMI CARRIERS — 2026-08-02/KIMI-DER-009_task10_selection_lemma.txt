THEOREM (modulo PREMISE P). Let f be an a.s.-analytic centered Gaussian
field on a compact manifold, γ̇ = ∇f(γ), B₅ the slab ball, band =
{ξ ∈ ∂B₅ : f(ξ) ∈ [v*, b]}. Let Ξ be the trajectory's first
band-crossing (∅ if none), and F the downstream failure event. Then
  P(F) ≤ sup_{ξ ∈ band} p̄(ξ),
where p̄(ξ) is the C028-grade conditional barrier at a level-pinned
boundary point (the R3′ object), provided PREMISE P holds.

PROOF (measure-theoretic steps, complete).
1. MEASURABLE SELECTION. The level sets of an a.s.-analytic f are
   analytic; crossings at band levels are a.s. transverse except on a
   null tangency set (handled as a null case). The first-exit map of a
   continuous flow on a compact ball into an open band is then defined
   and measurable on a full-measure set; Ξ : Ω → band ∪ {∅} is
   measurable. (Standard; no Gaussian input.)
2. INTERIOR MEASURABILITY. The flow up to the crossing is driven by
   ∇f on B₅, so Ξ = Ξ(f|_{B₅}): the crossing location is a measurable
   function of the INTERIOR field. (This is the step the naive
   Palm-restart gets wrong; see receipt 4.)
3. REGULAR CONDITIONAL DISTRIBUTION. The field's law on a separable
   Banach completion of the analytic class is Gaussian, hence admits a
   regular conditional distribution Q given the σ-field I = σ(f|_{B₅});
   the exterior conditional law is Gaussian with CM space
   H_I = {h ∈ H : h ≡ 0 on B₅}.
4. TOWER BOUND. P(F) = E[ Q(F | I) ]. Since Ξ is I-measurable,
   Q(F | I)(ω) = Q(F | I, Ξ = Ξ(ω))(ω). If for Q-a.e. interior field
   the conditional failure probability at the field-selected crossing
   is ≤ p̄(Ξ(ω)) — this is PREMISE P — then Q(F | I)(ω) ≤ p̄(Ξ(ω)) ≤
   sup_{band} p̄, and integration gives P(F) ≤ sup p̄. The Rice count
   2.43 (expected number of band components) NEVER ENTERS: the tower
   bound is applied to a probability, not to a count; multiplicity of
   components is irrelevant because the trajectory selects exactly one
   crossing.
