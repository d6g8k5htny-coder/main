

0. EXECUTIVE RESULT


The exact selected-branch event used by P0.1 and P0.2 can be defined as a Borel event on a separable C^3 field space.


Fix r>0, the ordered configured endpoints


    M_r=(-r/2,0),
    S_r=(+r/2,0),


and the exact six-pin affine fiber. For a field whose exact gradient-ascent vector field has:


- a strict local maximum at M_r;
- a nondegenerate index-one saddle at S_r;
- a unique unstable eigenspace at S_r;
- nonzero projection of that eigenspace onto M_r-S_r;


orient the unstable eigendirection toward M_r. The corresponding connected unstable branch is a measurable dynamical mark of the field. Define A_r to hold exactly when that selected exact branch converges to M_r in forward time, and define A_r=false outside the strict typed/nonorthogonal domain.


Then A_r is Borel measurable under the exact six-pin conditional field law Q_{6,r}. Consequently


    P_r^{MS}(A_r)
      =E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact} 1_{A_r}]/Z_r^{exact}


is a well-defined exact Palm probability whenever 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity.


This repairs an unsupported assertion in GP-PROT-065 and LS-DER-005. Those documents state that the selected branch is measurable but did not provide the branch-selection, parameter-dependence, or Borel convergence argument.


The proof below does not prove that A_r occurs. It proves that A_r is a legitimate measurable event and that the deterministic certificate event used in the research program refers to the same selected branch.


1. FIELD SPACE AND CONDITIONAL LAW


Let T_L^2 be the side-L flat torus with L=24. Let


    X=C^3(T_L^2;R)


with its usual C^3 norm. X is a separable Banach space and hence a standard Borel space.


The normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock field has an analytic version. Its unconditioned law may therefore be viewed as a Borel probability measure on X. Conditioning on the six endpoint value/gradient pins gives the exact Gaussian regular conditional law Q_{6,r}, also regarded as a Borel probability on X.


Define the closed affine pin fiber


    X_r^{pin}
      ={f in X:
          (f,fx,fy)(M_r)=(b,0,0),
          (f,fx,fy)(S_r)=(b-r^3/6,0,0)}.


Q_{6,r}(X_r^{pin})=1.


All measurability statements may be proved on X_r^{pin} and extended to X by assigning failure outside that fiber.


For f in X define the physical gradient-ascent vector field


    V_f=grad f.


The map


    f -> V_f


is continuous from C^3 fields to C^2 vector fields. In particular it is continuous into C^1, the topology required for finite-time ODE dependence.


The same proof applies to the normalized scaled vector field used in GP-PROT-065 because the physical/scaled coordinate map is deterministic and continuous for fixed r>0.


2. STRICT TYPED AND NONORTHOGONAL DOMAIN


Let H_M(f) and H_S(f) be the exact physical endpoint Hessians. Let


    d_r=M_r-S_r=(-r,0).


Define O_r subset X_r^{pin} by the conjunction:


1. H_M(f) is negative definite;
2. H_S(f) has one positive and one negative eigenvalue;
3. the projection of d_r onto the unstable eigenspace of H_S(f) is nonzero.


The evaluation maps f->H_M(f), f->H_S(f) are continuous in C^2 and hence in C^3.


Negative definiteness and index-one saddle type are open conditions on symmetric matrices. On the saddle set, the two eigenvalues are distinct. The unstable spectral projector varies continuously. Nonzero projection is also open. Therefore O_r is open in the relative topology of X_r^{pin}, and hence Borel.


Outside O_r the selected branch is declared undefined and A_r is assigned false. This matches the fail-closed convention of GP-PROT-065.


3. CONTINUOUS ORIENTED UNSTABLE EIGENVECTOR


For a symmetric two-by-two saddle Hessian H let


    lambda_u(H)>0>lambda_s(H)


be its ordered eigenvalues. Define the unstable spectral projector


    P_u(H)
      =(H-lambda_s(H)I)/(lambda_u(H)-lambda_s(H)).


This formula is continuous on the strict saddle set.


For f in O_r define


    e_u(f)
      =P_u(H_S(f))d_r / ||P_u(H_S(f))d_r||.


The denominator is nonzero by definition of O_r. Hence f->e_u(f) is continuous on O_r.


Moreover


    e_u(f) dot d_r
      =||P_u(H_S(f))d_r||>0.


Thus this formula gives exactly the GP-PROT-065 M-ward sign convention without an arbitrary eigenvector sign choice.


The explicit projector formula is important: a numerical or symbolic eigensolver may return either sign, but the mathematical mark is single-valued and continuous on O_r.


4. LOCAL UNSTABLE BRANCH WITH PARAMETER DEPENDENCE


Use the standard parameter-dependent local unstable-manifold theorem for C^1 vector fields with a hyperbolic equilibrium.


For every f_0 in O_r there exist:


- a relative open neighborhood U of f_0 in O_r;
- a neighborhood N of S_r in the torus;
- a continuous family of one-dimensional local unstable manifolds W^u_{loc}(f,S_r) for f in U;
- a continuous local parameterization


      psi_f:(-delta,delta)->T_L^2


  satisfying


      psi_f(0)=S_r,
      d psi_f/dt(0)=e_u(f),


  and whose positive parameter side lies on the M-ward branch;


- a fixed eta in (0,delta) such that


      b_U(f):=psi_f(eta)


  is continuous in f and belongs to the selected branch.


The hypotheses are met because:


- f->V_f is continuous into C^1;
- S_r remains an equilibrium on the exact pin fiber;
- strict saddle hyperbolicity is open;
- e_u(f) is continuously oriented on U.


No global uniform spectral gap is required. The theorem is applied locally in field space.


5. COUNTABLE BOREL BRANCH SELECTOR


O_r is an open subset of a separable metric space and is therefore second countable and Lindelof.


Choose a countable collection of unstable-manifold neighborhoods


    U_1,U_2,...


covering O_r, with continuous selected-branch points b_j on U_j as in Section 4.


Define disjoint Borel strata


    B_1=U_1,
    B_j=U_j minus union_{i<j}U_i  for j>=2.


The B_j form a Borel partition of O_r.


Define


    b_r(f)=b_j(f)  when f in B_j.


Then b_r:O_r->T_L^2 is Borel measurable.


Different neighborhoods may choose different positive points on the same selected unstable branch. This causes no ambiguity in the forward omega-limit: every nonstationary point on one connected one-dimensional unstable branch differs only by a flow-time shift along that branch and has the same forward omega-limit set.


Thus b_r is a measurable representative point for the GP-PROT-065 selected branch.


6. MEASURABLE GLOBAL FLOW


Because the torus is compact and V_f is C^1, its flow exists for every real finite time. Write


    Phi(f,t,x)


for the gradient-ascent flow at time t from x.


Standard continuous dependence of ODE solutions gives continuity of


    (f,t,x)->Phi(f,t,x)


on C^1 vector fields, finite compact time intervals, and initial points. Since f->V_f is continuous, Phi is jointly continuous as a function of (f,t,x) for each finite t.


For every fixed t>=0 the selected trajectory evaluation


    f -> Phi(f,t,b_r(f))


is therefore Borel on O_r.


Equivalently, the map from f to the continuous path


    t -> Phi(f,t,b_r(f))


is Borel into C([0,infinity),T_L^2) equipped with the compact-open topology.


7. BOREL SET OF PATHS CONVERGING TO M_r


Let C_M be the set of continuous paths gamma:[0,infinity)->T_L^2 satisfying


    lim_{t->infinity} gamma(t)=M_r.


C_M is Borel in the compact-open path space. One explicit countable representation is


    C_M
      =intersection_{k>=1}
         union_{N>=1}
          intersection_{q in Q, q>=N}
            {gamma:dist(gamma(q),M_r)<=1/k}.


Every set in braces is closed under evaluation at the fixed rational time q, and the expression uses countable unions and intersections.


For a continuous path, the rational-time condition implies the corresponding all-real-time bound by density of the rationals and continuity. Taking k to infinity is equivalent to convergence to M_r.


8. BOREL EXACT ADJACENCY EVENT


Define


    A_r
      ={f in O_r:
          t->Phi(f,t,b_r(f)) belongs to C_M}.


Since O_r is Borel, b_r is Borel, the selected path map is Borel, and C_M is Borel, A_r is Borel.


Extend A_r to all of X by assigning false outside O_r and outside X_r^{pin}. The extension remains Borel.


This event agrees with GP-PROT-065:


- the branch is the exact unstable branch at S_r;
- its sign is oriented by the configured displacement toward M_r;
- the opposite branch is not substituted;
- no adjacency conditioning enters the base law;
- failure of a sufficient certificate is not identified with A_r^c.


9. INDEPENDENCE FROM THE LOCAL SELECTOR


Suppose b_r and b'_r are two Borel selectors constructed from possibly different countable local covers, but both select positive points on the M-ward unstable branch.


For each f in O_r, b_r(f) and b'_r(f) lie on the same connected component of W^u(S_r;f) minus {S_r}. That component is one flow orbit. Hence there exists a finite time shift tau(f) such that


    b'_r(f)=Phi(f,tau(f),b_r(f))


with the sign of tau determined by their order along the branch.


Forward convergence to M_r is invariant under finite time shifts. Therefore both selectors define the same event A_r pointwise.


The event is thus intrinsic to the selected branch, not to the auxiliary local unstable-manifold chart.


10. COMPATIBILITY WITH THE DETERMINISTIC CERTIFICATE


The exact capture certificate used in P0.1 and P0.2 supplies strict margins for:


- saddle type and spectral gap;
- nonzero M-ward projection;
- entry of the selected unstable germ into the declared cone;
- section crossings;
- invariant strip transit;
- entry into the exact invariant M-neighborhood;
- convergence to M_r.


Therefore the certified event E_r lies inside O_r and the certificate follows the same connected branch selected by e_u(f).


Consequently


    E_r subset A_r


is an inclusion between Borel field events.


This measurability result does not change the deterministic proof or its constants. It supplies the missing event-theoretic foundation required to insert 1_{A_r} into exact expectations.


11. EXACT PALM QUOTIENT


Let


    W_r^{exact}
      =|det H_M det H_S|
         1{H_M negative definite}
         1{H_S index one},


and


    Z_r^{exact}=E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact}].


W_r^{exact} is Borel because endpoint derivative evaluations and matrix determinants/eigenvalue inequalities are continuous finite-dimensional functions of f.


If 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity, define


    dP_r^{MS}/dQ_{6,r}
      =W_r^{exact}/Z_r^{exact}.


Since A_r is Borel,


    P_r^{MS}(A_r)
      =E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact}1_{A_r}]/Z_r^{exact}


is well defined.


No adjacency indicator is inserted into W_r^{exact}. A_r remains the event whose probability is evaluated.

13. FAILURE MODES


This derivation fails or requires amendment if:


1. Q_{6,r} cannot be realized as a Borel law on C^3;
2. the exact field does not generate a global C^1 flow on the compact torus;
3. strict saddle/nonorthogonality is not a Borel/open condition;
4. the oriented projector formula does not match GP-PROT-065;
5. parameter-dependent unstable manifolds do not admit local continuous branch parameterizations under the stated topology;
6. the selected local positive branch is not unique;
7. two positive local branch points can have different forward omega-limit sets;
8. convergence to M_r is not Borel in path space;
9. the deterministic certificate follows a different unstable branch;
10. the underlying Palm law already conditions on adjacency.


No such failure was found in this same-line reconstruction.
