5. DETERMINANT AND TYPE LIMIT


Define


D_M(r)=det H_M/r=A_M C_M-r B_M^2,
D_S(r)=det H_S/r=A_S C_S-r B_S^2.


The joint convergence above gives


D_M(r)⇒-Q_L,
D_S(r)⇒+Q_L.


For a real symmetric 2×2 matrix, M is a strict maximum iff its first axial entry is negative and its determinant is positive; S is an index-one saddle iff its determinant is negative.


If Q_L<0, then with probability tending to one along the coupled limiting vector,
A_M<0, D_M>0, and D_S<0, so M is a maximum and S a saddle.
If Q_L>0, D_M<0 in the limit, so the maximum-saddle type combination fails.
The other potential sign boundaries have deterministic nonzero limits A_M=-1 and A_S=+1. The only limiting discontinuity boundary is Q_L=0.


GP-DER-044 proves Q_L is a nondegenerate Gaussian, hence P(Q_L=0)=0. The extended continuous-mapping theorem therefore yields


[W_r^{exact}/r^2] ⇒ Q_L^2 1{Q_L<0}.


This discharges G5 and G6 conditional on GP-DER-044.


6. EXPLICIT UNIFORM-INTEGRABILITY PROOF


Because type indicators are at most one,


0≤W_r^{exact}/r^2≤|D_M(r)D_S(r)|.


For r≤1,


|D_j|≤|A_j C_j|+|B_j|^2,


and therefore


|D_M D_S|^2
≤4(|A_M C_M|^2+|B_M|^4)(|A_S C_S|^2+|B_S|^4).


Every term on the right is a monomial of total degree at most eight in the components of V_r. The uniform fixed-order moment bound from Section 4 and Hölder’s inequality give


sup_{0<r≤r_0} E[(W_r^{exact}/r^2)^2] < ∞.


Thus {W_r^{exact}/r^2:0<r≤r_0} is uniformly integrable. This is stronger than the required L^{1+ε} criterion and makes the previously implicit “uniform finite moments” step explicit. This discharges G7 conditional on GP-DER-044.
