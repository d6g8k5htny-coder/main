NUMERATOR-ONLY POLYNOMIAL BOUNDARY-LAYER PROPOSITION


1. SETTING


Fix an integer m>=1 and r_0 in (0,1]. For every r in (0,r_0], let gamma_r be a probability measure on R x R^m with jointly measurable Lebesgue density phi_r(q,U).


Let V_r:R x R^m->[0,infinity) be measurable, and let B_r be a measurable event. Define the unnormalized numerator mass


    N_r
      = integral_{R x R^m}
          V_r(q,U) 1_{B_r}(q,U)
          phi_r(q,U) dq dU.


Let


    C_r:R^m->[0,infinity),
    U_r:R^m->R


be measurable. For every U define the possibly empty nonoriented interval


    I_r(U)={s in R:-C_r(U)<s<U_r(U)}.


If U_r(U)<=-C_r(U), then I_r(U) is empty.


2. HYPOTHESES


Assume there exist constants


    K_phi>0, c_phi>0,
    K_C>0, K_U>0, K_D>0,


and nonnegative integers d_C,d_U,d_D, all independent of r, such that for every r in (0,r_0]:


H1 — DENSITY ENVELOPE.
For every (q,U),


    phi_r(q,U)<=K_phi exp(-c_phi||U||^2).


H2 — CONTRIBUTING SUPPORT INCLUSION.
For gamma_r-almost every (q,U) with V_r(q,U)>0,


    (q,U) in B_r


implies


    q/r in I_r(U).


H3 — POLYNOMIAL ENDPOINT GROWTH.
For every U,


    0<=C_r(U)<=K_C(1+||U||^{d_C}),


    |U_r(U)|<=K_U(1+||U||^{d_U}).


H4 — r^4 NUMERATOR-WEIGHT MAJORANT.
For every U and every s in I_r(U),


    V_r(rs,U)
      <=r^4 K_D(1+|s|+||U||)^{d_D}.


3. PROPOSITION


Under H1–H4, there exists a finite constant K_N, depending only on


    m,r_0,K_phi,c_phi,
    K_C,K_U,K_D,d_C,d_U,d_D,


and not on r, such that


    N_r<=K_N r^5


for every r in (0,r_0].


No normalizer is defined or assumed. No independence assumption is imposed. Empty intervals contribute zero. No optimal value of K_N is asserted.


4. PROOF


Because V_r>=0, H2 gives, gamma_r-almost everywhere,


    V_r(q,U)1_{B_r}(q,U)
      <=V_r(q,U)1_{I_r(U)}(q/r).


Tonelli's theorem and q=rs, dq=r ds, yield


    N_r
      <=r integral_{R^m}
          integral_{I_r(U)}
            V_r(rs,U)phi_r(rs,U) ds dU.


By H1 and H4,


    N_r
      <=K_phi K_D r^5
        integral_{R^m}
          exp(-c_phi||U||^2)J_r(U)dU,


where


    J_r(U)
      =integral_{I_r(U)}
        (1+|s|+||U||)^{d_D}ds.


The integral is over the measurable set I_r(U), not an oriented integral. Hence J_r(U)=0 when I_r(U) is empty.


Set


    d=max{1,d_C,d_U},
    t=||U||,
    K_A=2 max{1,K_C,K_U}.


Since t^j<=1+t^d for every integer 0<=j<=d, H3 implies


    C_r(U)<=K_A(1+t^d),
    |U_r(U)|<=K_A(1+t^d).


Let A(U)=K_A(1+t^d). Then


    I_r(U) subset [-A(U),A(U)],
    |I_r(U)|<=2A(U).


For s in I_r(U),


    1+|s|+t
      <=(K_A+2)(1+t^d).


Therefore


    J_r(U)
      <=2K_A(K_A+2)^{d_D}
        (1+t^d)^{d_D+1}.


With n=d_D+1 and M=dn,


    (1+t^d)^n<=2^{n-1}(1+t^{dn}),


so there is a finite K_P, independent of r, such that


    J_r(U)<=K_P(1+||U||^M).


The Gaussian polynomial integral


    integral_{R^m}
      exp(-c_phi||U||^2)(1+||U||^M)dU


is finite. Hence


    N_r<=K_N r^5


for a finite r-independent K_N. QED.


5. APPLICATION MAP


P02-LM-003.
Take V_r=W_r and B_r equal to the determinant-weighted bad event. The proposition supplies the unnormalized numerator estimate N_r=O(r^5). The separate normalizer lower bound Z_r>=c_Zr^2 then gives P_r(B_r)=O(r^3).


P02-LM-010 v1.1.
Take V_r(q,U) to be the good-event conditional subweight


    tildeW_r(q,U)
      =E[W_r^{exact}1_{G_r}|J_r=(q,U)]


using either jet-law-almost-everywhere statements or the support-respecting bounded version in LCR-COR-041. The proposition gives the exact good-boundary numerator N_r=O(r^5). Division remains by the full exact normalizer Z_r^{exact}, never by the integral of tildeW_r.
