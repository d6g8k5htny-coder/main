HOSTILE CHECKS


1. Symmetry is genuinely used.
The proof sets e_s=J e_u and then asserts that e_s spans the stable eigenspace. This implication is valid for a real symmetric 2x2 linearization because the stable and unstable eigenspaces are orthogonal. It is not valid for a general nonsymmetric saddle. Therefore the symmetry hypothesis belongs in the theorem statement.


2. The repair is sufficient.
Section 1 already assumes A_p=DX_p(0) is symmetric. Section 10 applies the abstract lemma to X_f=grad f, for which A_f=H_{S_r}(f) is symmetric. The v1.1 theorem now matches both the proof and the application.


3. The repair does not narrow the intended Gaussian-field scope.
Every exact six-pin application uses a gradient vector field. Its linearization at the saddle is the Hessian, hence symmetric. No downstream P0.1 or P0.2 use requires a nonsymmetric vector field.


4. Hyperbolicity remains locally uniform.
At the base parameter, one eigenvalue is positive and one negative. Continuity of the symmetric matrix family permits shrinking the parameter neighborhood to obtain positive lower bounds alpha_* and beta_* and continuous spectral projectors. No global spectral-gap claim is made.


5. Orientation remains continuous and single-valued.
The nonzero reference vector d has nonzero unstable projection at the base point. After shrinking the neighborhood, P_u(A_p)d remains nonzero, so e_u(p)=P_u(A_p)d/||P_u(A_p)d|| is continuous. Positive xi continues to identify the configured M-ward branch.


6. The coordinate frame is legitimate.
For symmetric planar matrices, Q_p=(e_u,e_s) with e_s=J e_u is orthogonal and diagonalizes A_p with eigenvalues alpha_p and -beta_p. The nonlinear remainder has zero value and derivative at the equilibrium as used.


7. The weighted-space estimates survive unchanged.
The unstable kernel contributes (alpha_*-eta)^(-1), the stable kernel contributes (beta_*+eta)^(-1), and the conservative sum defines q<1/2. The self-map estimate with R_0=2xi_0 is valid.


8. ODE recovery and local graph construction survive unchanged.
The fixed point differentiates to the original coordinate ODE, is unique in the weighted local class, yields h_p(xi), and satisfies h_p(xi)=o(xi). The selected point b(p)=Q_p(xi_*,h_p(xi_*)) is continuous and nonzero.


9. Exact six-pin application remains identical.

10. Scope firewall is preserved.

ADVERSARIAL MUTATIONS


- Remove symmetry: e_s=J e_u need not be stable; theorem fails as stated.
- Allow the equilibrium to move without recentering: X_p(0)=0 fails and the fixed integral equation no longer represents the family.
- Let P_u(A_p)d vanish: orientation becomes undefined.
- Permit the spectral gap to close: uniform alpha_*, beta_* fail.
- Use a spatial ball not contained in the physical chart: the nonlinear estimate is not justified.
- Choose q>=1: the contraction and self-map conclusions fail.
- Treat the local selector as globally continuous: unsupported.
- Treat certificate failure as nonadjacency: unsupported.
