2. EXACT DEFECT IN GP-DATA-114


The original source defines helpers equivalent to:


lower(x) -> float(x.a)
upper(x) -> float(x.b)
absmax(x) -> max(abs(lower(x)),abs(upper(x)))


It then computes several decisive quantities using ordinary Python floats after interval extraction, including the eigenslope upper bound, cone-slope margin, handoff margin, chart-containment margins, and Gershgorin lower bounds.


Byte-exact reconstruction and rerun therefore established only that the same implementation reproducibly generated the same receipt. They did not establish that every final lower bound remained an outward-rounded enclosure. Reproducibility of an implementation is not proof of the implementation’s numerical validity.


Severity: HIGH for certificate wording and downstream evidentiary use; LOW for sign stability because the smallest margin is about 7.5×10^-3, enormously larger than binary-float rounding error.

No contradiction or first-line logical failure was found in those dependencies during this session.
