This is not a corner case in the application. The conclusion is very likely true: the transverse dynamics drive Y toward T^(-1) times the a-forcing, where Y^T T Y is of order R^2/tau, not lambda_max(T)m^2. The Euclidean cylinder is simply the wrong region to read the level off.
