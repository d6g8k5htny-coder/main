1. EXACT OBJECT AND FROZEN WINDOW

    K_L(x)=Z_L^(-1) sum_{k in Z^2} exp(-2*pi^2*|k|^2/L^2) exp(2*pi*i*k.x/L),
    L=24,


with every Fourier weight strictly positive.


Freeze


    B_*=[23/20,5/4],
    K_*=[3/4,5/4],
    P_*=S^1 x B_* x K_*.


For theta in S^1 let t=(cos theta,sin theta), let s=(-sin theta,cos theta), and for 0<r<12 set


    M=x-(r/2)t,
    S=x+(r/2)t.


The directed convention is maximum-to-saddle: S-M=r t. The base point x is integrated per unit torus area. Reversing the pair is not another atom because the two critical-point types already order the pair.


For (theta,b,kappa) in P_* impose the exact six pins


    f(M)=b,
    f(S)=b-kappa*r^3/6,
    grad f(M)=grad f(S)=0.


Write the raw pin vector in the t,s frame as


    X_{r,theta}=(f(M),f(S),f_t(M),f_s(M),f_t(S),f_s(S))^T.

    G_t^+    =[f_t(S)+f_t(M)]/2,
    G_t^-    =[f_t(S)-f_t(M)]/r,
    G_s^+    =[f_s(S)+f_s(M)]/2,
    G_s^-    =[f_s(S)-f_s(M)]/r.


In the raw ordering above,

2. CORRECTED PIN COVARIANCE


Let


    Sigma_pin(r,theta)=Cov(Y_{r,theta}).


Every entry is a finite linear combination of directional derivatives and divided differences of K_L at 0 and +/-r t. Equivalently, for derivative multi-indices alpha,beta,


    Cov(partial^alpha f(u),partial^beta f(v))
      =partial_u^alpha partial_v^beta K_L(u-v),


with the signs already determined by differentiating u-v. Therefore Sigma_pin is an exact, displayed finite-dimensional covariance object derived from K_L and T_r.


Taylor expansion in the corrected frame gives the limiting functionals


    (f,-f_ttt/12,f_t,+/-f_tt,f_s,+/-f_ts),


where the harmless signs of the zero-constrained second-derivative coordinates depend on the declared orientation convention. These six functionals are linearly independent in the Gaussian Hilbert space: their Fourier symbols are distinct polynomials after an invertible rotation of coordinates, and every lattice spectral weight is positive.


Consequently Sigma_pin(0,theta) is positive definite for every theta. Its entries depend continuously on (r,theta). Compactness of S^1 gives constants r_pin>0 and


    0<lambda_pin^-<=lambda_pin^+<infinity


such that


    lambda_pin^- I_6 <= Sigma_pin(r,theta) <= lambda_pin^+ I_6


for 0<=r<=r_pin and all theta.


Because y_r ranges over a compact set and converges uniformly to


    y_0(theta,b,kappa)=(b,-kappa/6,0,0,0,0),


the exact Gaussian density formula implies uniform convergence


    p_Y(r,theta,b,kappa) -> p_0(theta,b,kappa)                       (2)

    0<c_pin<=p_0(theta,b,kappa)<=C_pin<infinity.                     (3)


3. PIN-ADJUSTED ENDPOINT HESSIANS


Let h=r/2. Along the pair axis define g(u)=f_t(x+u t) and k(u)=f_s(x+u t). Set


    S_g^r=[g(h)-g(-h)]/(2h),
    S_k^r=[k(h)-k(-h)]/(2h),


and


    A_M^r=[g'(-h)-S_g^r]/r,
    A_S^r=[g'(+h)-S_g^r]/r,
    B_M^r=[k'(-h)-S_k^r]/r,
    B_S^r=[k'(+h)-S_k^r]/r,
    C_M^r=f_ss(M),
    C_S^r=f_ss(S).


On the six-pin affine subspace, g(-h)=g(h)=k(-h)=k(h)=0, hence exactly


    A_M^r=f_tt(M)/r,       A_S^r=f_tt(S)/r,
    B_M^r=f_ts(M)/r,       B_S^r=f_ts(S)/r.


Define


    Delta_M^r=A_M^r C_M^r-r(B_M^r)^2,
    Delta_S^r=A_S^r C_S^r-r(B_S^r)^2.


Then exactly


    det H_M=r Delta_M^r,
    det H_S=r Delta_S^r.                                            (4)


The joint covariance of the corrected pins and the six endpoint variables above consists of analytic divided differences of K_L with removable singularities at r=0. Gaussian regression therefore gives conditional mean and covariance blocks


    m_V=C_VY Sigma_pin^-1 y_r,
    Gamma_V=Sigma_V-C_VY Sigma_pin^-1 C_YV,                          (5)


which extend continuously in (r,theta,b,kappa). The covariance block is independent of the target (b,kappa); the mean is affine in y_r. On the compact parameter set, all finite conditional moments are uniformly bounded.


The corrected value pin forces


    -f_ttt(x)/12=-kappa/6,


so the limiting conditioned longitudinal cubic coefficient is


    f_ttt(x)=2 kappa.                                                (6)


Let a=f_tts(x), and let Q_{theta,b,kappa} be the limiting conditional transverse curvature f_ss(x). Then under the confluent conditional law,


    A_M^r -> -kappa,       A_S^r -> +kappa,
    B_M^r -> -a/2,         B_S^r -> +a/2,
    C_M^r,C_S^r -> Q_{theta,b,kappa}.                               (7)


The limiting joint finite-jet covariance is a Schur complement of a positive-definite Fourier Gram matrix. Hence Q_{theta,b,kappa} is a nondegenerate Gaussian. Its mean and variance are continuous in (theta,b,kappa), and compactness gives


    |E Q_{theta,b,kappa}|<=M_Q,
    0<sigma_Q^-<=sd(Q_{theta,b,kappa})<=sigma_Q^+<infinity.          (8)


4. TYPED DETERMINANT-MOMENT LIMIT


From (4) and (7),


    Delta_M^r -> -kappa Q_{theta,b,kappa},
    Delta_S^r -> +kappa Q_{theta,b,kappa}.                           (9)


For kappa>0, away from the null boundary Q=0:


- if Q<0, then H_M is negative definite and H_S has index one for all sufficiently nearby configurations;
- if Q>0, then the maximum type at M fails for all sufficiently nearby configurations.

    W_r/r^2
      -> kappa^2 Q_{theta,b,kappa}^2 1{Q_{theta,b,kappa}<0}.         (10)

    0<=W_r/r^2<=||V_r||^4

5. CONTACT-INTENSITY THEOREM


Combining (1), det T_r=-r^-5, and z_r=Z_r/r^2 gives the exact cancellation


    J_r=(1/6) p_Y z_r,
    r^3*r^-5*r^2=r^0.                                               (14)


Equations (2), (11), and (14) imply uniform convergence


    J_r(theta,b,kappa)->J_0(theta,b,kappa)                           (15)


on P_*, with


    J_0(theta,b,kappa)
      =(1/6) p_0(theta,b,kappa)
             kappa^2 E[Q_{theta,b,kappa}^2
                        1{Q_{theta,b,kappa}<0}].                    (16)


The limit is continuous. By (3) and (13), there exist r_*>0 and constants


    0<c_*<=C_*<infinity


such that


    c_*<=J_r(theta,b,kappa)<=C_*                                    (17)


for every 0<=r<=r_* and every (theta,b,kappa) in P_*, with r=0 interpreted by J_0.


Consequently,


    0<
    integral_{S^1 x B_* x K_*}
      J_0(theta,b,kappa) kappa^(-2/3)
      dtheta db dkappa
    <infinity.                                                       (18)


This proves the TB-G2 compact-mark finite-positive all-typed contact-intensity requirement on the frozen positive-measure window.

    q_r^adj=P{D_f(M)=S | A,T_r},

    p_r=a_r q_r^adj+eta_r,
    0<=eta_r<=1-a_r.                                                (19)
