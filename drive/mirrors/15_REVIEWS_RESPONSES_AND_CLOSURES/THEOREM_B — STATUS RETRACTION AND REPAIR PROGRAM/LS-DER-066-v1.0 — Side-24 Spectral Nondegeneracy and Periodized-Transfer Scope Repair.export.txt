3. FULL-SPECTRUM DERIVATIVE NONDEGENERACY


LEMMA 3.1 -- FINITE DERIVATIVE EVALUATIONS ARE STRICTLY NONDEGENERATE.


Let x_1,...,x_N be distinct points of T_24^3. At each x_j choose finitely many constant-coefficient differential operators D_(j,k). Remove exact duplicate functionals. Then the covariance matrix of all random variables D_(j,k)f(x_j) is strictly positive definite.


PROOF.
For coefficients c_(j,k), the variance of their linear combination equals


    Z_24^(-1) sum_(m in Z^3) a_m |F(m)|^2,                  (4)


where


    F(m)=sum_(j,k)c_(j,k)p_(j,k)(m)
          exp[2pi i m·x_j/24].                              (5)


Because every a_m is positive, zero variance implies F(m)=0 for every m in Z^3. But F(m) is the Fourier coefficient of the finite distribution


    T=sum_(j,k)c_(j,k) D_(j,k)^* delta_(x_j)                (6)


on the torus. All Fourier coefficients of a distribution vanish only when the distribution is zero. Distributions supported at distinct points have disjoint supports, and the derivatives of delta at one point are linearly independent. Therefore every c_(j,k)=0. QED.
