B. LS-DER-021 (TB-G2) - THE KEYSTONE - CHECKED, HOLDS
============================================================
This artifact derives both the finite-positive contact intensity and the normalizer scaling. The load-bearing chain is:


  J_r = (r^3/6) * p_X * Z_r ,   p_X = |det T_r| * p_Y ,   Z_r = E_Q[W_r].


B1. Density factor det T_r = -r^-5 (eq. "det T_r=-r^-5"). CONFIRMED.
    The six-pin divided-difference frame T_r is block upper-triangular after one column transposition into groups {f}, {f_t}, {f_s}; the diagonal blocks have determinants r^-3, r^-1, r^-1, and the single transposition supplies the sign. Hand result: det T_r = -(r^-3)(r^-1)(r^-1) = -r^-5. I also built T_r numerically and computed the 6x6 determinant at r = 0.3, 0.1, 0.03, 0.007: matches -r^-5 to machine precision (rel. err <= 1.8e-16).


B2. Endpoint Hessian scaling det H_M, det H_S = O(r). CONFIRMED consistent.
    From the exact six-pin relations, A_M^r = f_tt(M)/r with A_M^r -> -kappa, i.e. the LONGITUDINAL curvature f_tt(M) -> -kappa r. Transverse curvature C_M^r -> Q = O(1). Hence det H_M = r*Delta_M^r with Delta_M^r -> -kappa Q, so det H_M ~ (-kappa Q) r = O(r), and likewise det H_S ~ (+kappa Q) r. This is exactly the O(r) longitudinal-curvature scaling I had only argued heuristically in the TB-G3B review; LS-DER-021 makes it exact, and it is correct.


B3. Normalizer Z_r ~ r^2 with strictly positive limit. CONFIRMED (modulo standard nondegeneracy).
    W_r = |det H_M det H_S| * type-indicators = r^2 |Delta_M^r Delta_S^r| * 1{...} -> r^2 * kappa^2 Q^2 * 1{Q<0}. Therefore Z_r = E_Q[W_r] = r^2 z_r with z_r -> z_0 = kappa^2 E[Q^2 1{Q<0}]. Since Q is a nondegenerate Gaussian, P(Q <= -1) >= rho_- > 0, so z_0 >= (3/4)^2 rho_- > 0 on K_* = [3/4,5/4]. The r^2 exponent and the strict positivity are both sound. What I did NOT re-derive: the uniform positive eigenvalue floor of the 6-pin covariance and the uniform moment/integrability bounds (the argument leans on the periodized Bargmann-Fock field's strictly positive Fourier weights - standard and plausible, but I did not reconstruct it).


B4. Power cancellation J_r = (1/6) p_Y z_r via r^3 * r^-5 * r^2 = r^0. CONFIRMED (the three exponents above multiply to r^0, giving an O(1) finite positive contact intensity).


Net for LS-DER-021: the keystone is genuine mathematics and its two exact sub-claims (det T_r = -r^-5 ; W_r ~ r^2) are independently confirmed. ============================================================

    From total probability, 1 - p = P(E^c ∩ A) + P(E^c ∩ A^c), and P(E^c ∩ A^c) = (1 - a) - eta with eta = P(E ∩ A^c) >= 0. Hence 1 - p = (1 - a) + P(E^c ∩ A) - eta <= (1 - a) + P(E^c ∩ A). It is a careful Boole/total-probability step; its one virtue worth noting is that it correctly RETAINS eta >= 0 (the selected-but-nonadjacent remainder) instead of assuming eta = 0, which the artifact repeatedly and correctly warns against. Composition Theorem 3.1 (1 - p <= (C_A + C_B) r^3) then follows trivially from (5) + the two O(r^3) inputs. Valid.
