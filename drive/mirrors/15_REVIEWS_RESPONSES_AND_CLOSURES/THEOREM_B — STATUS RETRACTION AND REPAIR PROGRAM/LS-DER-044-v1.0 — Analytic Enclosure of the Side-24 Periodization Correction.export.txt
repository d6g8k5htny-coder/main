ANALYTIC ENCLOSURE OF THE SIDE-24 PERIODIZATION CORRECTION


1. TARGET


Let c_24 be the exact specific coefficient defined by the side-24 angular integral of LS-DER-043, and let


    c_infinity
      =2^(2/3)3^(5/6)Gamma(1/6)/(54pi^(3/2))                     (1)


be the exact planar coefficient of LS-DER-042.


The objective is a conservative analytic bound on


    |c_24-c_infinity|                                            (2)


that does not rely on the sign or final digits of the 220-digit floating-point computation.


2. PERIODIZED DERIVATIVE-MOMENT TAIL


For


    k_L(t)
      =[sum_{n in Z}exp(-(t+nL)^2/2)]
       /[sum_{n in Z}exp(-(nL)^2/2)],                             (3)


let


    m_j(L)=k_L^(j)(0),  0<=j<=6.                                  (4)


The planar moments are


    m_0(infinity)=1,
    m_2(infinity)=-1,
    m_4(infinity)=3,
    m_6(infinity)=-15.                                           (5)


For even j,


    d^j exp(-u^2/2)/du^j
      =He_j(u)exp(-u^2/2),                                       (6)


where He_j is the probabilists' Hermite polynomial. At L=24, the denominator in (3) is at least one, and direct comparison of the n=+/-1 image terms with the n>=2 Gaussian tail gives


    max_{j in {0,2,4,6}}
      |m_j(24)-m_j(infinity)|
      <10^(-116).                                                (7)


A sufficient elementary estimate is obtained from


    |He_j(x)|<=(1+x)^6,  j<=6, x>=0,                             (8)


followed by the ratio bound on


    (1+24n)^6 exp[-(24n)^2/2].                                   (9)


The n=1 contribution is below 4 x 10^(-117), while the complete n>=2 tail is below 10^(-490). The rounded bound (7) is deliberately conservative.


3. COVARIANCE-BLOCK PERTURBATION


Every covariance entry used in LS-DER-043 has total derivative order at most six. The l1 norm of the Cartesian expansion of a directional derivative of order d is at most


    (|cos theta|+|sin theta|)^d<=2^(d/2).                         (10)


For two operators with total combined order at most six, the product expansion has l1 norm at most eight.


All planar even moments through order six have absolute value at most 15. From (7), each product of one-dimensional moments changes by less than


    33 x 10^(-116).                                              (11)


Therefore every entry of the pin covariance Sigma_theta, the q-to-pin vector v_theta, and the scalar Var(q_theta) changes by less than


    epsilon_entry=3 x 10^(-114)                                  (12)


uniformly in theta.


For the six-by-six pin covariance,


    ||Sigma_24(theta)-Sigma_infinity||_2
      <=6 epsilon_entry
      <2 x 10^(-113).                                            (13)


4. UNIFORM INVERSE CONTROL

    lambda_min(Sigma_infinity)>1/32.                              (15)

Equations (13) and (16) imply

The Neumann resolvent identity therefore gives

    ||Sigma_24^(-1)-Sigma_infinity^(-1)||_2
      <3 x 10^(-110).                                            (19)


All bounds are uniform in theta.

6. COEFFICIENT-INTEGRAND LIPSCHITZ BOUND


Define

        [sqrt(pi/A)/2]
        [sigma^2+mu^2/(2A)]
        [(1/2)B^(-7/6)Gamma(7/6)],                               (29)
