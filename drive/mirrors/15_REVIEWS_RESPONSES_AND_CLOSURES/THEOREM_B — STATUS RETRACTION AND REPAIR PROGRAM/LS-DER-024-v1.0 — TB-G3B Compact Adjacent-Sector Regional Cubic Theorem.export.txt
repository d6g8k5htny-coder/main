Case I — prior merger of M's component.

Therefore failure of E forces


    h_{r,lambda}<f(M_2)<b.


Thus M_2 is a window maximum. It is counted by N_add if it lies in C_r and by N_crit outside the collars.


Case III — S is a self-merge or loop saddle for H0.

Therefore there is an alternative path in a superlevel set above h_{r,lambda} connecting the arms. The mountain-pass value of the alternative connection is attained at another index-one saddle y. It is strictly above h_{r,lambda}. It is strictly below b because at level immediately below b the newly born M component is local and cannot already contain both arms through a distinct path; distinct critical values exclude equality at b. Hence y lies in W_{r,lambda}^{val}. Again it is counted by N_add or the outside-collar window count according to location.
