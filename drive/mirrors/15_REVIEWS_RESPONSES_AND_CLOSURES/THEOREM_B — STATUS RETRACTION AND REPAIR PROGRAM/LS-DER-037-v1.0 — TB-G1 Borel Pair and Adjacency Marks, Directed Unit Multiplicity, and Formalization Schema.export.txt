2. FIELD SPACE AND GENERIC LOCUS


Let X be a compact connected smooth surface equipped with a fixed smooth Riemannian metric. The application is the flat torus T_L^2 with L=24. Let


    F = C^3(X;R)


with its C^3 norm. F is a separable Banach space and therefore a standard Borel space.


Let G_MS be the subset of functions f satisfying:
1. f is Morse;
2. all critical values are pairwise distinct;
3. the gradient flow of f is Morse–Smale.


The Morse and distinct-value conditions are open in C^2. Morse–Smale gradient flows are structurally stable and open in the C^1 vector-field topology. Since f -> grad f is continuous from C^3 functions to C^2 vector fields, G_MS is open in F and hence Borel.


Every mark defined below is assigned the value false, or the zero point measure, outside G_MS. This fail-closed convention is Borel and prevents ties, degenerate critical points, nontransverse separatrix bifurcations, or recurrent non-Morse dynamics from being silently included.


3. LOCAL CRITICAL-POINT LABELS AND THE ELDER MARK


Fix f_0 in G_MS. Because f_0 has finitely many nondegenerate critical points, there is a neighborhood U of f_0 and continuous critical-point continuations


    c_1(f),...,c_N(f),   f in U,


that exhaust Crit(f), preserve Morse index, and preserve the strict ordering of all critical values.


Choose one regular level between every two consecutive critical values of f_0. After shrinking U, all chosen levels remain regular. Structural stability and the isotopy lemma identify the superlevel cobordisms between adjacent regular levels. Expressed in the continued labels, the following finite combinatorial data are constant on U:
- which index-one saddles merge two distinct H0 components;
- which index-one saddles are loop/self-attachment saddles;
- the elder representative of every superlevel component;
- the death label D_f(M)=S for every non-global maximum M.


Thus the elder-rule indicator


    d_ij(f)=1{c_i(f) is a maximum, c_j(f) is an index-one saddle, and D_f(c_i(f))=c_j(f)}


is locally constant on U.


4. DIRECT BOREL PROOF FOR THE CANDIDATE AND PERSISTENCE PROCESSES


Fix 0<rho<inj(X). For a locally labelled maximum M_i(f) and saddle S_j(f), define


    r_ij(f)=dist(M_i(f),S_j(f)),
    b_i(f)=f(M_i(f)),
    ell_ij(f)=f(M_i(f))-f(S_j(f)),
    kappa_ij(f)=6 ell_ij(f)/r_ij(f)^3


on {r_ij>0}. On the flat torus with rho<L/2, the unique short displacement Log_{M_i}(S_j) is continuous. The directed angle


    theta_ij(f)=Arg(Log_{M_i(f)} S_j(f)) in [0,2pi)


is Borel. It is not continuous across the half-open branch cut, and continuity is not asserted.


The cutoff


    1{0<r_ij(f)<rho}


is Borel because r_ij is continuous. This explicitly repairs clarification C1: a pair at distance exactly rho may enter or leave under perturbation, but its inclusion label remains Borel.


Let E be the geometric mark space carrying base point, r, theta, b, kappa, and the labelled critical points. On U define


    Xi_all,rho(f)
      = sum_{i,j} 1{M_i max}1{S_j saddle1}1{0<r_ij<rho}1{ell_ij>0}
          delta_{atom_ij(f)},


    Xi_pers,rho(f)
      = sum_{i,j} 1{0<r_ij<rho} d_ij(f) delta_{atom_ij(f)}.


For every bounded continuous compactly supported test function phi on E, the maps


    f -> integral phi dXi_all,rho(f),
    f -> integral phi dXi_pers,rho(f)


are finite sums of products of continuous functions, Borel cutoff/angle functions, and locally constant elder labels. Hence they are Borel on U. A countable cover of G_MS by such U, together with a countable convergence-determining family of test functions on E, proves that


    f -> Xi_all,rho(f),
    f -> Xi_pers,rho(f)


are Borel maps into the space of finite point measures with the vague topology. Extension by the zero measure outside G_MS is Borel.


No local-continuity claim is needed. The exact statement of S2-DER-010 Theorem 5.1 survives; only its proof route is replaced.


5. TWO-BRANCH BOREL ADJACENCY MARK


Fix a local label neighborhood U and one continued saddle S_j(f). The Hessian H_j(f)=Hess f(S_j(f)) is a continuous family of symmetric index-one matrices. Its one-dimensional unstable eigenspace L_j(f) varies continuously as a line.


After shrinking U if needed, choose a continuous local unit vector e_j(f) spanning L_j(f). Its sign is arbitrary. The parameter-dependent local unstable-manifold theorem gives two continuous nonstationary representative points


    z_j^+(f), z_j^-(f)


lying on the two connected components of W^u(S_j(f);f)\{S_j(f)}, with initial tangents +e_j(f) and -e_j(f).


The choice of sign only swaps z_j^+ and z_j^-. The existential adjacency mark below is therefore independent of that local orientation.


Because G_MS is second countable, choose countably many such neighborhoods U_n covering it. Form the disjoint Borel partition


    B_1=U_1,
    B_n=U_n\union_{m<n}U_m.


On each B_n use its two continuous branch representatives. This yields Borel maps z_j^+,z_j^- on every local critical-label stratum. The construction is independent of the countable cover in the only sense used below: any two nonstationary representatives on the same connected branch differ by a finite flow-time shift and have the same forward omega-limit.


Let Phi(f,t,x) be the gradient-ascent flow. Compactness of X and C^1 regularity give a global flow, and finite-time ODE dependence makes


    (f,t,x) -> Phi(f,t,x)


jointly continuous on the relevant local field spaces.


For a continued maximum M_i(f), define


    C_ij^sigma
      ={f: lim_{t->infinity} Phi(f,t,z_j^sigma(f))=M_i(f)},
      sigma in {+,-}.


This is Borel. An explicit countable representation is


    C_ij^sigma
      = intersection_{k>=1} union_{N>=1} intersection_{q in Q, q>=N}
          {f: dist(Phi(f,q,z_j^sigma(f)),M_i(f))<1/k}.


Each set in braces is Borel; continuity of the path and density of rational times make the representation equivalent to convergence.


Define the existential direct-gradient-adjacency mark


    A_ij(f)=1{f in C_ij^+ union C_ij^-}.


Therefore A_ij is Borel on G_MS. It is invariant under swapping the two branches and under replacing either representative point by another point on the same branch. Extend A_ij=0 outside G_MS and whenever the labelled pair is not maximum/index-one-saddle typed. The extension is Borel on F.


This proves clarification C2: the symbol A_f(M,S) is a legitimate Borel event. No adjacency condition is inserted into the base field law or the Kac–Rice weight.


6. BOREL ADJACENCY-MARKED POINT PROCESSES


Define


    Xi_adj,rho(f)
      =sum_{i,j} A_ij(f) 1{typed candidate and 0<r_ij<rho} delta_{atom_ij(f)},


    Xi_adj-pers,rho(f)
      =sum_{i,j} A_ij(f)d_ij(f)1{0<r_ij<rho} delta_{atom_ij(f)},


    Xi_nonadj-pers,rho(f)
      =sum_{i,j} (1-A_ij(f))d_ij(f)1{0<r_ij<rho} delta_{atom_ij(f)}.


All three are Borel finite point-measure maps. Pointwise,


    Xi_pers,rho=Xi_adj-pers,rho+Xi_nonadj-pers,rho.


This identity includes every branch configuration. A pair with both ascending branches ending at M has A_ij=1 but contributes one atom, not two. A loop/self-merge saddle can carry A_ij=1 in Xi_adj, but it has d_ij=0 and therefore is not an H0 persistence death in Xi_pers or Xi_adj-pers.


7. EXACT PROBABILITY DECOMPOSITION


Let T_r(lambda) be the exact all-typed ordered maximum-saddle conditioning object on a standard Borel field space. Since D_f(M)=S and A_f(M,S) are Borel, their regular conditional probabilities are well defined for almost every mark and admit measurable versions.


Define


    p_r=P(D=S|T_r),
    a_r=P(A=1|T_r),
    eta_r=P(D=S,A=0|T_r).


Where a_r>0 define q_r^adj=P(D=S|A=1,T_r). Where a_r=0, define the product a_r q_r^adj directly as P(D=S,A=1|T_r), without assigning an arbitrary conditional probability on a null conditioning event.


Partitioning {D=S} by A gives the exact identity


    p_r=a_r q_r^adj+eta_r,


and


    0<=eta_r<=1-a_r.


This is an equality of the same exact law and the same ordered-pair convention. It neither assumes nor implies eta_r=0.


8. UNIT MULTIPLICITY, DIRECTION, AND ANGULAR CONVERSION


The externally reviewed deterministic result remains controlling:
- every non-global maximum has one death saddle;
- every H0 merge saddle kills one elder representative;
- every finite ordinary superlevel H0 bar is represented once;
- loop saddles kill no H0 class;
- the global maximum represents the one essential class.


The candidate domain Max(f) x Sad_1(f) is already ordered by critical-point type. Reversal is not another atom of that domain. Hence there is no unordered-pair factor 1/2.


The directed angle theta in [0,2pi) uses the displacement from maximum to saddle. If a different source uses an unoriented line angle alpha in [0,pi), the density conversion is


    J_line(alpha)=J_dir(alpha)+J_dir(alpha+pi).


Conversely,


    integral_0^pi J_line(alpha)dalpha
      =integral_0^{2pi} J_dir(theta)dtheta.


Only under an additional pi-rotation symmetry giving J_dir(alpha)=J_dir(alpha+pi) may one write J_line=2J_dir on [0,pi). This is a coordinate pushforward, not a multiplicity correction to the ordered critical-pair process.


9. BASE-AREA CONVENTION


The first-moment intensity is specific per unit ordinary base-point area. Under stationarity, with x denoting the base point used to place the ordered pair,


    E Xi_all(dx dr dtheta db dkappa)
      =J_r(theta,b,kappa) dx r dr dtheta db dkappa.


Thus J_r is a density per unit Lebesgue area in x. Integrating over the side-24 torus multiplies the specific intensity by


    area(T_24^2)=24^2=576.


This anchors clarification C3 at TB-G1. It changes no frozen numerical claim. Every downstream formula must state whether it is specific per unit area or total over the torus.


10. EDGE-CASE FIREWALL


Ties: distinct critical values are required. Outside that locus all marks fail closed.


Degenerate critical points: excluded by the Morse condition. A monkey saddle may kill more than one class and is not covered.


Non-Morse-Smale flows: adjacency is assigned false by the declared fail-closed convention; elder pairing may still exist but is not inserted through this adjacency interface.


Distance diagonal: r=0 is excluded. The normalized gap kappa is undefined there and no atom is created.


Cut locus: rho<inj(X); on T_L^2 this is rho<L/2. The short logarithm is then unique.


Angle branch cut: Arg in [0,2pi) is Borel, not globally continuous. The proof uses Borel measurability only.


Reversed orientation: (S,M) is not a maximum-saddle atom. theta+pi is not a duplicate of the same labelled atom.


Two branches to one maximum: adjacency is existential Boolean and contributes one pair atom.


Self-merge/loop saddle: may be adjacency-marked in Xi_all or Xi_adj, but never receives D_f(M)=S because it is not an H0 merge.


Selected-but-nonadjacent pair: retained by Xi_nonadj-pers and eta_r. It is never silently discarded.
