1. PURPOSE


The preceding chain proves selected short-lifetime persistence for the normalized side-24 periodized Bargmann–Fock field. The proof actually uses two logically separate mechanisms:


LOCAL COLLISION MECHANISM
- the corrected pair-pin covariance and endpoint fold normalizer;
- the structural collar factorization;

- deterministic Hessian collision zeros.


GLOBAL FIXED-DISTANCE MECHANISM
- strict covariance positivity for the corrected pair frame together with remote value-gradient coordinates;

LS-DER-065 shows that the local mechanism is stable in an explicit six-jet and local C8 neighbourhood of the planar Bargmann–Fock kernel. LS-DER-067 and LS-DER-069 show that the global mechanism follows from complete positive Fourier support once the actual derivative-distribution basis is linearly independent. This theorem combines those facts into a genuine field-class result.


2. PERIODIC FIELD CLASS


Let T_L^3 be a flat three-dimensional torus of side L>0. Let f be a centered, unit-variance, stationary real-analytic Gaussian field with covariance


    K(x)=sum_{m in Z^3} w_m exp(2pi i m·x/L),               (1)


where


    w_m=w_{-m}>0 for every m in Z^3,
    sum_m w_m=1,                                            (2)


and the weights have exponential decay sufficient for real analyticity and all derivative moments used below.


Lift K to the fundamental Euclidean chart around zero. Let


    K_BF(x)=exp(-|x|^2/2).                                  (3)


Assume the explicit local neighbourhood of LS-DER-065:


    ||J_6(K)-J_6(K_BF)||_max <=10^(-110),                   (4)


    ||K-K_BF||_{C^8(B(0,1))} <=10^(-100).                  (5)


The numerical thresholds are deliberately inherited unchanged. Any later enlargement of this neighbourhood requires a new successor.

The important point is structural: the collision zeros are exact Taylor factors and are not destroyed by the perturbation (4)–(5).


4. FULL-SPECTRUM GRAM POSITIVITY


Let Lambda_1,...,Lambda_N be any finite family of constant-coefficient derivative-evaluation functionals on T_L^3. Write


    T=sum_j c_j Lambda_j.                                   (6)


Using (1),


    Var[T(f)]
      =sum_{m in Z^3} w_m |That(m)|^2.                      (7)


Because every w_m is strictly positive,


    Var[T(f)]=0
      iff That(m)=0 for every m in Z^3
      iff T=0 as a distribution.                            (8)


Therefore the covariance matrix of Lambda_1,...,Lambda_N is positive definite exactly when the associated derivative distributions are linearly independent.


For the actual corrected pair frame at contact, the limiting coordinates are an invertible triangular transform of distinct derivatives at the pair midpoint. Their polynomial symbols are linearly independent. Appending remote value-gradient or Hessian coordinates at a point y separated from the midpoint preserves independence because distributions at distinct support points have disjoint supports.


THEOREM 4.1 — FULL-SPECTRUM PERIODIC NONDEGENERACY.
For every fixed positive support separation and every compact configuration family, all corrected pair-plus-remote Kac–Rice covariance matrices used in G7 have a uniform positive eigenfloor.


PROOF.
Pointwise positivity follows from (8). Covariance entries depend continuously on the compact configuration parameters. The smallest eigenvalue is positive and continuous, hence has a positive compact minimum. QED.


5. FIXED-DISTANCE G7 AND CONDITIONAL MOMENTS


Fix delta>0 and restrict the third point y to


    dist(y,{M,S})>=delta.                                   (9)


Theorem 4.1 gives a uniform eigenfloor for the corrected pair-plus-remote value-gradient density block. Only this density block is inverted. Endpoint and remote Hessians enter through raw bounded cross-covariances and conditional polynomial moments, as clarified by LS-DER-069.


Schur complements therefore yield uniformly bounded conditional means and covariances. For some finite N,


    E[|det H_M det H_S det H_y|
       | pair pins,f(y)=u,grad f(y)=0]
      <=C[1+|b|+kappa+|u|]^N.                              (10)


The corresponding Gaussian density has an exponential quadratic envelope. Since the third critical value must lie in the pair value window of width O(r^3), the expected fixed-distance witness count is O(r^3). Thus G7 holds.


6. ALL-MARK DOMINATION


The corrected pair target is an injective affine-linear function of (b,kappa). The pair covariance inverse is uniformly bounded above and below on the compact direction charts. Consequently


    p_pair(target(b,kappa))
      <=C exp[-c(b^2+kappa^2)].                             (11)


Conditional Hessian moments grow at most polynomially in |b|+kappa. After the exact lifetime pushforward, both the all-typed and selected integrands are bounded by


    C[1+(|b|+kappa)^N]
      exp[-c(b^2+kappa^2)] kappa^(-2/3),                    (12)


which is integrable on R x (0,infinity). Compact-mark convergence therefore extends to all birth heights and normalized gaps.
