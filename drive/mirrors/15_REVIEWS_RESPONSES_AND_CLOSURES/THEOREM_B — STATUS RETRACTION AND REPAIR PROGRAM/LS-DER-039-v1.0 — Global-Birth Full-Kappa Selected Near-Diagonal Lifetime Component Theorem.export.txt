1. EXACT OBJECT


Let f be the normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock Gaussian field on the ordinary flat side-24 torus. For theta in S^1, b in R, kappa>0, and sufficiently small r, place

    f(S)=b-kappa r^3/6,
    grad f(M)=grad f(S)=0.


Let J_r(theta,b,kappa) be the exact all-typed ordered maximum–saddle contact intensity per unit ordinary base-point area relative to


    r dr dtheta db dkappa.


Let p_r(theta,b,kappa) be the exact elder-rule selection probability under the identical determinant-weighted Palm law, and define

2. GLOBAL JOINT GAUSSIAN DECAY IN BIRTH HEIGHT AND NORMALIZED GAP


The corrected pin transform gives

where z_r=Z_r/r^2 and the Gaussian target contains the two nonzero coordinates

      <=C[1+(|b|+kappa)^4] exp[-c(b^2+kappa^2)]                     (8)
