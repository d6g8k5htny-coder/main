CENTRAL FINDING — THE EXACT IMPORT POINT OF THE LS-DER-024 DEFECT


LS-DER-035-v1.0, published 2026-07-27 under DQ-049, exhibits an explicit Morse-Smale torus counterexample to LS-DER-024 Lemma 2.1 Case III. The falsified object is the deterministic inclusion E^c intersect A subset B. The corrected reduction retains a third class:


    E^c intersect A subset P union G_young union L_same,


where L_same is the same-maximum self-loop class in which both ascending separatrices of S converge to M. LS-DER-035 Section 7 gives the corrected probability form


    P(E^c intersect A) <= E N_add + E N_crit + P(L_same),


and records that the first two terms are exactly what LS-DER-024 estimated, while the third is not controlled by those estimates. LS-DER-035 Section 14 records SAME-MAXIMUM SELF-LOOP PROBABILITY: OPEN AND LOAD-BEARING.


The import point in LS-DER-025 is equation (5), which reads in full:


    LS-DER-024 gives constants r_B>0 and C_B<infinity such that
    sup_{lambda in P_*} P^{MS}_{r,lambda}(E^c intersect A) <= C_B r^3
    for 0<r<=r_B.


This is exactly the falsified claim. The distinction matters and is worth stating precisely. LS-DER-024's Kac-Rice counting bound, its equation (37) on P^MS(B), is NOT falsified by the counterexample and remains evidence. What is falsified is the deterministic inclusion that converts a bound on B into a bound on E^c intersect A. LS-DER-025 imported the converted conclusion rather than the two pieces, so it inherits the failure in full and cannot be repaired by re-deriving from LS-DER-024's surviving estimates alone.


Propagation. Equation (5) feeds (6), which feeds (7) uniform selection, which with (8) and (9) feeds (11) uniform convergence of K_r, which with the compact dominated convergence of Section 5 feeds Theorem 5.1, equations (16) through (18). Every one of these is therefore conditional on an L_same control that does not currently exist. Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 5.1 are both affected.


Classification. This is an IMPORTED DEPENDENCY DEFECT, not a composition defect. LS-DER-025 cites LS-DER-024 for precisely the statement LS-DER-024 claimed to prove, in the correct logical position, with the correct quantifiers, and combines it with LS-DER-023 correctly through an inequality that remains valid. No step of the assembly is wrong. The document also anticipated this exact contingency: its own Section 8 falsifier list includes "any frozen input identity is incorrect or superseded" and "either compact cubic bound fails." The composition failed in the way its author declared it could fail, which is the behaviour a falsifier list exists to produce.


SECOND FINDING — THE CUBIC RATE IS NOT LOAD-BEARING FOR THEOREM 5.1


LS-DER-035 Section 7 proposes as the required successor an estimate (L3): sup_lambda P(L_same) = O(r^3). For LS-DER-025 Theorem 2.1, which states the cubic rate itself, that is indeed what is needed. For Theorem 5.1 it is more than is needed, and the gap is worth recording because it changes what a successor must prove.


Trace the rate through the argument. Equation (6) is consumed only at (11), through the bound


    |J_r p_r - J_0| <= |J_r - J_0| + J_r|1 - p_r|.


With J_r uniformly bounded and J_r -> J_0 uniformly, the right side tends to zero as soon as sup_{lambda in P_*}|1 - p_r(lambda)| tends to zero. No rate is used. Downstream, (15) evaluates the integrand at radius r_ell(kappa) = (6 ell/kappa)^(1/3), which on the compact window K_* = [3/4, 5/4] satisfies r_ell(kappa) <= (8 ell)^(1/3) and therefore tends to zero uniformly as ell decreases. Uniform convergence of K_r plus boundedness of kappa^(-2/3) on K_* plus finite measure of P_* then give (16) through (18) directly.


Three candidate decay laws for sup(1 - p_r) were evaluated at ell from 1e-3 to 1e-12: the cubic r^3 of equation (6); a linear r; and a rate-free 1/log(1/r). All three tend to zero, and all three therefore suffice for (11) and hence for Theorem 5.1.


Consequently the minimal repair for the component law is strictly weaker than (L3):


    sup_{lambda in P_*} P^{MS}_{r,lambda}(L_same,r,lambda) -> 0 as r decreases to zero,


with no rate required. A successor that proves only this recovers Theorem 5.1 in full, including the exponent, positivity and finiteness, while leaving Theorem 2.1's cubic statement amend-required. This is offered as a strictly easier sufficient target for the open work, not as a claim that it is achievable; L_same may be hard to control at any rate, and this reviewer takes no position on that.
