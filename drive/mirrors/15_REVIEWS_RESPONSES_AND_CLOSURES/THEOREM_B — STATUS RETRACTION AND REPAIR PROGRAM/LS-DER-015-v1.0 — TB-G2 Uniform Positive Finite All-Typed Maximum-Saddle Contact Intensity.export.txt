======================================================================
2. SECOND-ORDER KAC–RICE FORMULA
======================================================================


For each fixed r>0, the field is analytic and the joint Gaussian law of the values and gradients at two distinct points is nondegenerate. The standard second-order Kac–Rice formula therefore gives the first-moment intensity of ordered typed pairs.


Changing the two value coordinates


    (u,v)=(f(M),f(S))


to


    (b,kappa)
      =(u,6(u-v)/r^3)


has inverse


    u=b,
    v=b-kappa r^3/6


and absolute Jacobian


    |partial(u,v)/partial(b,kappa)|=r^3/6.


The spatial displacement measure in polar coordinates is


    dS=r dr dtheta.


Stationarity makes the base-point integral equal to the torus area L^2. Hence the all-typed whole-torus density with respect to


    r dr dtheta db dkappa


is


    J_r(theta,b,kappa)
      =L^2 (r^3/6)
       p_{P_{r,theta}}(z_r(b,kappa))
       E[ |det H_M det H_S| 1_{Tau_r}
          | P_{r,theta}=z_r(b,kappa) ].          (2.1)


The corresponding per-unit-area density is J_r/L^2.


There is no factor 1/2: the process is ordered and the direction is fixed from maximum to saddle. Introducing 1/2 would change the frozen object.

======================================================================
4. STABLE ENDPOINT HESSIAN VARIABLES
======================================================================


Off the pin affine space, raw quantities such as D^2_{ee}f(M)/r are singular Gaussian coordinates. Use the pin-adjusted representatives


    A_M^r
      =D^2_{ee}f(M)/r
       +[D_e f(M)-D_e f(S)]/r^2,


    A_S^r
      =D^2_{ee}f(S)/r
       +[D_e f(M)-D_e f(S)]/r^2,


    B_M^r
      =D^2_{en}f(M)/r
       +[D_n f(M)-D_n f(S)]/r^2,


    B_S^r
      =D^2_{en}f(S)/r
       +[D_n f(M)-D_n f(S)]/r^2,

    C_S^r=D^2_{nn}f(S).


On the six-pin event, the gradient secants vanish, so these equal the physical normalized Hessian entries.


Define


    Delta_M^r=A_M^r C_M^r-r(B_M^r)^2,
    Delta_S^r=A_S^r C_S^r-r(B_S^r)^2.            (4.1)


Conditioned on the pins,


    det H_M=r Delta_M^r,
    det H_S=r Delta_S^r.                          (4.2)


Substituting (3.3) and (4.2) into (2.1) gives the exact finite-r identity


    J_r(theta,b,kappa)

The contact prefactor is exactly


    (r^3/6)(12/r^5)(r^2)=2.                      (4.4)


Thus no unbalanced power of r remains.


======================================================================
5. ANALYTIC CONFLUENCE AND CONDITIONAL GAUSSIAN FAMILY
======================================================================


The exact side-24 covariance kernel is analytic. Every covariance among transformed pins and pin-adjusted endpoint variables is an analytic Hermite divided difference of that kernel. All apparent negative powers of r have removable singularities.


Therefore the joint Gaussian family

The limiting transformed pin symbols in Fourier coordinates

    v=xi dot n_theta

    1, u, u^2, v, uv, u^3.                       (5.1)


The normalized-periodized Gaussian has strictly positive spectral weight at every lattice frequency. If a linear combination of the six functionals had zero variance, its finite-degree polynomial symbol would vanish at every lattice point. A polynomial vanishing on the full integer lattice is identically zero. The six monomials in (5.1) are linearly independent, so the limiting pin Gram G_{0,theta} is positive definite for every theta.


The dependence on theta is continuous. Compactness of S^1 gives uniform constants


    0<lambda_pin^-<=lambda_min(G_{0,theta}),
    lambda_max(G_{0,theta})<=lambda_pin^+<infinity.


After reducing r_0, the finite-r pin Gram remains uniformly invertible.


Conditional means and covariances of the endpoint variables are therefore continuous in

    [0,r_0] x S^1 x B x K.


All fixed polynomial moments are uniformly bounded.
