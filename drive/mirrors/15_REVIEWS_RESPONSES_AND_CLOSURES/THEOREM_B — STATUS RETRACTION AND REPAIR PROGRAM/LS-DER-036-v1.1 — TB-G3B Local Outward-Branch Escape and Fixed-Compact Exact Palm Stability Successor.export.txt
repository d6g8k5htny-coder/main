 <= C_D exp(-c_D' r^{-2})

PROOF.


Fix a rotated fifth derivative L_{alpha,theta} with |alpha|=5. Under Q_{r,lambda},

sup_{lambda,y,alpha}|m_{r,lambda,alpha}(y)|<=M_0.


Conditioning subtracts a positive semidefinite regression covariance. Therefore, for every y,z,
Var[g(y)]<=Var[L_{alpha,theta}f(y)]<=sigma_0^2,
and

 <=E[(L_{alpha,theta}f(y)-L_{alpha,theta}f(z))^2]

The last inequality follows by the mean-value formula and the finite sixth spectral moment of the periodized Bargmann-Fock field, uniformly in theta.

Dudley's entropy integral therefore yields a uniform finite bound on the expected centered supremum; in fact it is O(r), but boundedness is sufficient. Adding the conditional mean and taking the finite maximum over fifth-order multi-indices gives
