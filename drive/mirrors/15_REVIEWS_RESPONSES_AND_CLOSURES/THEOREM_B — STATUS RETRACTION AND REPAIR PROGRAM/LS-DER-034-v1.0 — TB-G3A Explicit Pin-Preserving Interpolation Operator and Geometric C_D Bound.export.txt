2. The fixed Hermite-gradient projection

Write the endpoint residual vectors

Define


    A=(e_+^1+e_-^1)/2,
    B=(e_+^2+e_-^2)/2,
    C=e_+^1-e_-^1,
    D=e_+^2-e_-^2,
