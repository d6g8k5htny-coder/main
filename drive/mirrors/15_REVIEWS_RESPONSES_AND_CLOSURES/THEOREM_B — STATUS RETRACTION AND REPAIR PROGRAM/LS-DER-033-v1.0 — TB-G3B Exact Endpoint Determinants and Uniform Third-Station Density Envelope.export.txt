3. Exact generic endpoint and third-point determinants


Let M=(-eta/2,0) and S=(eta/2,0) in the (X,Y) chart. Direct substitution into det D^2P gives

      = [kappa^2 eta^2/(64c^2s^2)]
        [64(1-alpha)c^2 eta^2-(K+8c eta)^2],                  (3.2)
