CLARIFICATIONS


D1 — THE HAAR CLAUSE IS STATED BACKWARDS. NONMATERIAL, INERT, BUT WORTH REPAIRING.
Section 1 says: "If a source instead uses normalized Haar area with total mass one, then its density is numerically bar_nu and every dx or dx dy integral must use that normalized measure consistently."
Let mu = dx / A_L with mu(T) = 1. A density g with respect to mu satisfies N = integral_T g dmu, and since the intensity is constant in the base point by stationarity, N = g * mu(T) = g. So a per-unit-Haar-area density is numerically the total nu_tot, not bar_nu. The clause names the wrong one of the two quantities.
Materiality: none. The very next sentence establishes that no current source declares Haar and that ordinary area plus equations (5)-(6) is controlling, and no computation in the document uses the Haar branch. But this document is the program's controlling statement of the area convention, and a future reader repairing a source under a Haar reading would be led to the wrong identification by exactly this sentence.
Exact repair: replace "its density is numerically bar_nu" with "its density is numerically nu_tot, because the whole torus has unit mass under that measure".


D2 — SCOPE CONFLATION IN THE SECTION 9 AUDIT ROW FOR LS-DER-025. NONMATERIAL.

LS-DER-025's frozen body equations (15)-(17) integrate over P_* = S^1 x B_* x K_* with K_* = [3/4, 5/4], whereas c_{B_*}^{spec} in equation (25) integrates kappa over (0, infinity). The exposure is that Section 9 is the audit a future reader consults to learn what each source's coefficient means. Exact repair: amend the Section 9 row to read that LS-DER-025's displayed coefficient is the specific compact-kappa component coefficient over K_* = [3/4, 5/4], that it is not equal to c_{B_*}^{spec} of equation (25), and that the full-kappa specific coefficient is supplied by LS-DER-026.
