REPRODUCTION GUIDE — SYMBOLIC, NUMERICAL, AND REVIEW CHECKS


1. REPRODUCTION LEVELS


Level A — exact algebra only.
Verify rational identities, Jacobians, determinant factors, gamma-function simplifications, power counts, and margin inequalities.


Level B — deterministic coefficient reconstruction.
Recompute the finite-side angular Gaussian-cone formula and the side-24 relative correction using independent arbitrary-precision arithmetic.


Level C — proof audit.
Check the Palm rate, deterministic selection geometry, and dominated-convergence interfaces at equation level.


2. EXACT ALGEBRA CHECKLIST


- midpoint/displacement block determinant equals one;
- du=r^(d-1)dr d sigma_(d-1);
- pin transformation determinant r^(-(d+3));
- endpoint soft determinant product contributes r^2;
- gap Jacobian contributes r^3/6;
- d=3 radial exponent: 3-6+2+2=1;
- lifetime pushforward:


    r dr=(6^(2/3)/3)kappa^(-2/3)ell^(-1/3)dell;


- no duplicate factor 1/6;
- no unordered-pair factor 1/2;
- Gamma(7/6)=Gamma(1/6)/6;
- exact closed-form simplification for c_(3,infinity).

3. FINITE-SIDE RELATIVE CHECK


Reconstruct


    delta_24=c_(3,24)/c_(3,infinity)-1.


Two independent routes should agree within their justified precision:


- analytic first-image formula plus rigorously bounded remainder;
- direct finite-L nonlinear angular/cone evaluation.


The analytic enclosure is controlling for the rigorous relative interval. The direct computation is corroborative and should not claim more digits than its working precision supports.


4. T-ENERGY GEOMETRY CHECK


Use E(Y)=Y^T T Y. Confirm:


- the outward unstable eigenvector enters the energy cone;
- T^2>=tau T for T>=tau I;
- the cone flux is strictly inward on its boundary;
- the energy strip is invariant;
- the strip implies a complete bound on (1/2)Y^T T Y;
- the branch reaches X=5/4 above the maximum level;
- the inward tapered tube converges to M;
- all estimates use tau=lambda_min(T), never lambda_max(T);
- the C1 exact-field perturbation consumes less than the displayed margins.


5. PALM RATE CHECK


Confirm the exact endpoint integral identities force one r into the axial row and column of each endpoint Hessian. Cofactor expansion gives an r factor per endpoint determinant. Carry the resulting r^2 through the value window and divide explicitly by Z_r~r^2.


For the shallow transverse eigenvalue layer, check that two determinant powers plus one integration-width power yield unnormalized O(r^5), hence Palm probability O(r^3).


6. PAIR-COUNTING CHECK


Verify that t and -t correspond to endpoint-swapped tuples. Since the pair space is typed Max x Sad_(d-1), the swapped tuple is not another atom. Confirm that two unstable branches are an existential adjacency mark, not two pair counts.


7. ENVIRONMENT RECORD


Every computational reproduction should record:


- software and version;
- arbitrary-precision backend;
- working precision and guard digits;
- quadrature orders and tolerances;
- exact source identity or exported text hash;
- platform and date;
- whether results are ordinary floating approximations, arbitrary-precision approximations, or certified intervals.


8. SUCCESS CRITERIA


A reproduction passes only when it states which claim it supports. Agreement of decimals does not prove the persistence-selection theorem. A proof audit does not certify the long nonlinear quadrature. The analytic enclosure, direct reconstruction, and geometric selection proof are distinct evidence channels and should remain distinct.
