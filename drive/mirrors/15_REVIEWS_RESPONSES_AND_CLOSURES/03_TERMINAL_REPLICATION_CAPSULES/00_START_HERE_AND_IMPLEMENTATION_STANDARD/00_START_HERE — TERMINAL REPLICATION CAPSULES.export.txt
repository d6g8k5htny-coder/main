1. PURPOSE


This area contains post-terminal replication packages for exact closed objects. A capsule preserves the exact statement, reading order, evidence identities, operator record, corrections, exclusions, and reproducibility information needed to reconstruct the narrow closure without rebuilding the whole Drive citation graph.


Capsule identity is distinct from mathematical truth. A root or seal certifies package identity and completeness within its declared scope; it does not broaden or independently prove the mathematical closure.
