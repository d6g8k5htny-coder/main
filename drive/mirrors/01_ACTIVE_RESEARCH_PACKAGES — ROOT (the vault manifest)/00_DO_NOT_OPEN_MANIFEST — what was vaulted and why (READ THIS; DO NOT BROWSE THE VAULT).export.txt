RULES REQUIRED TO ENTER THE VAULT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════


An item may be moved into 99_DO_NOT_OPEN only when ALL of the following are true.


A. Category (must match at least one)
A1. Superseded mirror — a duplicate or older copy whose live authority replacement already exists outside the vault (same SHA / same role / explicitly superseded title).
A2. Dead end — abandoned branch, empty hub stub, or unfinished shell that looks like a live folder but has no usable content.
A3. Trap copy — stale narrative, wrong-SHA twin, quarantine clone, or "looks current" file that would mislead models into treating it as authority.
A4. Operator designation — the human operator (or CoS acting on an explicit ask) names the item for vaulting with a written why.


B. Hard requirements (every move — no exceptions)
B1. Authority replacement named — title AND Drive ID of the live carrier that replaces this item, and that replacement MUST remain outside the vault.
B2. Not the only copy — never vault the sole remaining copy of an authority carrier, certificate, sealed custody object, or submission package piece.
B3. Not live HOLD research — HOLD_NOT_FOR_SUBMISSION is unfinished live work. DO_NOT_OPEN is retired clutter. Do not conflate them. Do not vault HOLD items just because they are incomplete.
B4. Not active PKG contents — do not vault files that are currently part of PKG-01 through PKG-05 submission packages (or their ERRATA fronts) unless those packages have been formally retired and replaced.
B5. Not routing / constitution — do not vault MASTER pointers, this manifest, Fresh Start constitution docs, or lane indexes that bots use to navigate. (STOP stubs inside the vault may be updated; old STOP copies may be vaulted as A1 when replaced.)
B6. Same-turn manifest row — in the same turn as the move, add a complete row to the Manifest table below: Date (CT), Item title, Drive ID, Moved from (path/id), Why moved (cite A1–A4), Authority replacement (title + id), Moved by.
B7. Relocate only — never delete, never permanent-delete, never empty trash as part of vaulting. Move into the vault folder.
B8. Confirm before bulk — seed candidates and any batch of more than one item require operator/CoS confirmation before relocate. No silent mass moves.


C. Forbidden (automatic reject — do not enter)
C1. Sole copy of D1 / LPW / certified-rung / certificate artifacts.
C2. Active peer-review submission package carriers still cited by PKG routing.
C3. Prize sealed custody (e.g. PR-TAL-003–008) with no outside replacement named.
C4. This live manifest document itself.
C5. Items with no named authority replacement.
C6. Items whose only "problem" is that they are hard; difficulty ≠ vault eligibility.




How to use
1. Before searching Drive for "the" copy of a file, check this manifest.
2. If a path or title is listed here, use the AUTHORITY REPLACEMENT column instead.
3. To retire a mirror: confirm B1–B8, move it into the vault, then ADD A ROW HERE in the same turn.
4. Never put the only copy of an authority carrier into the vault.
5. HOLD_NOT_FOR_SUBMISSION is different: HOLD is live research not ready to submit. DO_NOT_OPEN is superseded / duplicate / trap. Do not conflate them.
