## Purpose
Home for whole workstreams / folders that are **no longer being worked on but are still correct** — finished, paused, or set aside, with nothing wrong with them. This is the "folders no longer in use" home Dylan asked for.

## How this differs from the other not-active buckets
- **RETIRED** (this folder) = fine, just finished/inactive. Kept for reference.
- **OUTDATED — SUPERSEDED PROVENANCE** = a newer/better version exists; the item here is obsolete.
- **CORRUPTED — FAILED OR UNREADABLE** = broken / unreadable / failed.
- **DELETE — QUARANTINED CANDIDATES** = intended for removal (needs your authorization).
- **02_LEGACY_Q0_ARCHIVE** = the one specific completed Q0 research line — not a general home.

## Rules (consistent with AMENDMENT 1)
- Moving a folder here is **reversible and non-destructive**. Nothing is deleted or trashed.
- To pull something back out, drop a note in `TAKE OUT OF FOLDER — REQUESTS` (subfolder here). Removal follows the same restore protocol as the other quarantine buckets.
- Optional: if volume grows, group inside by `YYYY / MM_MONTHNAME`.
