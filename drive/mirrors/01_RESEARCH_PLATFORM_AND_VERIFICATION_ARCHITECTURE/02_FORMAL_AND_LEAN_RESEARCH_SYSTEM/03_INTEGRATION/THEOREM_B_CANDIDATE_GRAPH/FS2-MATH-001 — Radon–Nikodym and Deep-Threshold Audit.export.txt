## 1. Correct Radon–Nikodym algebra


The exact typed Palm law is


\[
dP^{MS}_{r,\lambda}=\frac{W_{r,\lambda}}{Z_{r,\lambda}}\,dQ_{r,\lambda}.
\]


A single pointwise scaling “on the loop event” is not available without first decomposing the loop event. Under LS-DER-036,


\[
L_{\mathrm{same}}\subset B_r^*\cup \mathrm{REG}_r^c\cup G_r^c.
\]


On the boundary layer \(B_r^*\):


- the physical endpoint determinant product satisfies \(W_r=O(r^4)\);
- the full normalizer satisfies \(Z_r=\Theta(r^2)\);
- therefore \(dP/dQ=O(r^2)\) up to the stated polynomial jet majorant;
- the change \(q=rs\) gives boundary width \(dq=r\,ds\), hence \(Q(B_r^*)=O(r)\);
- consequently \(P(B_r^*)=O(r^3)\).


The originally proposed \(O(r^{-3})\) verdict is directionally reversed. A derivative of order \(r^{-3}\) would amplify, not suppress, conditioned-law mass.


On generic typed configurations with \(q=O(1)\), each endpoint determinant is typically \(O(r)\), so \(W_r=O(r^2)\), \(Z_r=\Theta(r^2)\), and \(dP/dQ=O(1)\). Therefore a conditioned-law loop plateau would threaten the theorem only if a non-negligible loop set survives outside the certified boundary/REG/H5 decomposition.

## 3. Certified-range obstruction


The deterministic regularity condition is


\[
rR^5\le\epsilon_0,\qquad \epsilon_0=\frac{1}{1024\cdot4096}
=\frac{1}{4194304}\approx2.38418579\times10^{-7}.
\]


Since \(R\ge1\), the best possible certified range is


\[
r\le2.38418579\times10^{-7}.
\]


The deep threshold also obeys


\[
-s/\kappa\ge2KR^5+2R,
\]


whose minimum at \(R=1\), \(K=4096\), is \(8194\). For a typical \(q=O(1)\), this begins only around \(r\lesssim10^{-4}\). The REG condition is even tighter.


Therefore simulations at \(r\in[0.05,0.4]\) do not test the current proved asymptotic regime. They test finite-\(r\) extrapolation and practical relevance.
