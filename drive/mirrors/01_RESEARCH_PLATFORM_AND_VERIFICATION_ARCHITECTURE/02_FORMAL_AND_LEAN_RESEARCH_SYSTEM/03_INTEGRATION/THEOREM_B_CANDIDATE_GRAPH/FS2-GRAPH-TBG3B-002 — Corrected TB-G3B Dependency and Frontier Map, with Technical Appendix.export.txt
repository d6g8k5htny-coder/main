A1. THE HOLDER ROUTE — COMPLETE INEQUALITY, AND ITS ACTUAL SCOPE


Setting: Q = Q_{r,lambda} the exact six-pin conditioned law;
W = |det H_M det H_S| 1{H_M negative definite} 1{H_S index one} with PHYSICAL
Hessians; Z = E_Q[W]; dP^{MS} = (W/Z)dQ; E any measurable event.


For conjugate exponents 1/p + 1/q = 1, Holder gives


    E_Q[W 1_E] <= ||W||_{L^p(Q)} · ||1_E||_{L^q(Q)}
                = ||W||_{L^p(Q)} · Q(E)^{1/q}
                = ||W||_{L^p(Q)} · Q(E)^{1 - 1/p}.


Yes — the form the operator proposed is correct. Dividing by Z and using
Z >= c_Z r^2:


    P^{MS}(E) <= ||W||_{L^p(Q)} · Q(E)^{1-1/p} / (c_Z r^2).


Weight norm scaling. From LS-DER-023 (10), H_M = r · [[-kappa + O(r), -a/2 + O(r)],
[-a/2 + O(r), s - w/2 + O(r)]] with s = q/r. Hence


    det H_M = r^2 · [(-kappa)(s - w/2) - (a/2)^2 + O(r)]
            ≈ -kappa · q · r,          since r^2 · s = r q / r · r = q r,

typical jets, not r^4. The r^4 majorant (18) applies only on the boundary layer
