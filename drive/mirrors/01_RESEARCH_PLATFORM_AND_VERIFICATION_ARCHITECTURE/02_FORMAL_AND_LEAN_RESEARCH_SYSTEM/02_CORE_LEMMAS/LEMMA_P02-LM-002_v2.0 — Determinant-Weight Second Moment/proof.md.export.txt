2. FROZEN EXACT STATEMENT


Let 0<r<=1 and let


(q,a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04) in R^9.


Define


R=1+|q|+|a|+|w|+|z|+|c40|+|c31|+|c22|+|c13|+|c04|.


Define the exact endpoint Hessians


H_M=
[[-r+r^2c40/12, -ar/2+r^2c31/12],
 [-ar/2+r^2c31/12, q-rw/2+r^2c22/8]],


H_S=
[[ r+r^2c40/12,  ar/2+r^2c31/12],
 [ ar/2+r^2c31/12, q+rw/2+r^2c22/8]].


Let


I_M=1{H_M is negative definite},
I_S=1{det H_S<0},


and define the nonnegative typed determinant weight


W_r=|det H_M det H_S| I_M I_S.


THEOREM P02-LM-002.


For every such jet vector and every 0<r<=1,


W_r^2<=16r^4R^8.


Consequently, for any probability law mu_r on the jet vector for which


M_8:=sup_{0<r<=r_0} E_mu_r[R^8]<infinity


for some 0<r_0<=1, one has uniformly for 0<r<=r_0,


E_mu_r[W_r^2]<=16M_8r^4.


No Gaussian assumption is needed for the pointwise inequality. The probability-law corollary uses only the displayed eighth-moment premise.


3. EXACT PROOF


Define


A_M=-1+rc40/12,
B_M=-a/2+rc31/12,
C_M=q-rw/2+r^2c22/8,


A_S=1+rc40/12,
B_S=a/2+rc31/12,
C_S=q+rw/2+r^2c22/8.


Then exactly


H_M=[[rA_M,rB_M],[rB_M,C_M]],
H_S=[[rA_S,rB_S],[rB_S,C_S]].


Therefore


det H_M=rD_M,
det H_S=rD_S,


where


D_M=A_MC_M-rB_M^2,
D_S=A_SC_S-rB_S^2.


Since the indicators are idempotent and bounded by one,


W_r^2
=r^4D_M^2D_S^2I_MI_S
<=r^4D_M^2D_S^2.


For 0<r<=1, every coefficient appearing in A_M,A_S,B_M,B_S,C_M,C_S has absolute value at most one and every jet absolute value is included in R. Hence


|A_M|,|A_S|,|B_M|,|B_S|,|C_M|,|C_S|<=R.


Thus


|D_M|<=|A_MC_M|+r|B_M|^2<=2R^2,
|D_S|<=|A_SC_S|+r|B_S|^2<=2R^2.


Therefore


W_r^2<=r^4(2R^2)^2(2R^2)^2=16r^4R^8.


Taking expectations gives the stated corollary.
