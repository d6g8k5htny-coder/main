P02-LM-007 — EXACT UNIFORM CONDITIONED FIFTH-DERIVATIVE SUPREMUM TAIL


1. EXACT FIELD, PINS, AND CHART


Fix the normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock Gaussian field on the side-24 flat torus and fix b=6/5. For every r>0 let


    M=(-r/2,0),
    S=(+r/2,0),


and let Q_{6,r} be the exact Gaussian field law conditioned on


    (f,f_x,f_y)(M)=(b,0,0),


    (f,f_x,f_y)(S)=(b-r^3/6,0,0).


Let D^+ be a fixed compact convex subset of R^2 containing the complete scaled certificate chart. Choose r_chart>0 so that rD^+ lies in one fixed Euclidean lift of the torus whenever 0<r<=r_chart.


For a multi-index alpha=(alpha_1,alpha_2) with |alpha|=5 define on the physical chart K_r=rD^+


    X_{r,alpha}(x)=partial^alpha f(x)


under Q_{6,r}. Define


    H_{5,r}(D^+)
      =max_{|alpha|=5} sup_{x in K_r}|X_{r,alpha}(x)|.


There are exactly six multi-indices of total order five in two dimensions.


2. CONFLUENT CONDITIONING INPUT


Use the exact invertible six-pin confluent divided-difference frame L_r. Let G_r be its covariance matrix and y_r its prescribed value vector.


The exact six-pin Gaussian regularity interface supplies an r_0>0 such that for 0<=r<=r_0:


- G_r extends continuously to r=0;
- G_r is uniformly positive definite;
- sup_r||G_r^{-1}||<infinity;
- y_r is uniformly bounded;
- covariances between fixed derivative functionals and L_r are analytic divided differences with removable singularities at r=0.


All constants below refer to the exact side-24 law and are independent of r in a sufficiently small interval.


3. CONDITIONAL MEAN


For each |alpha|=5 write


    X_{r,alpha}(x)
      =m_{r,alpha}(x)+Z_{r,alpha}(x),


where


    m_{r,alpha}(x)
      =E_{Q_{6,r}}[partial^alpha f(x)]


and Z_{r,alpha} is centered under Q_{6,r}.


The Gaussian conditioning formula gives


    m_{r,alpha}(x)
      =Cov(partial^alpha f(x),L_r)G_r^{-1}y_r.


For x=rX with X in D^+, the cross-covariance vector is an analytic divided-difference function of (r,X) and extends continuously to the compact set [0,r_0]xD^+. It is therefore uniformly bounded. Together with uniform bounds on G_r^{-1} and y_r, this gives a finite constant M_5 such that


    max_{|alpha|=5}
    sup_{0<r<=r_0}
    sup_{x in K_r}
    |m_{r,alpha}(x)|
      <=M_5.


4. RESIDUAL VARIANCE


For every linear Gaussian functional A, conditioning on L_r subtracts a positive-semidefinite Schur term. Hence


    Var(A | L_r=y_r)<=Var(A).


By stationarity of the unconditioned field, define


    sigma_5^2
      =max_{|alpha|=5} Var(partial^alpha f(0))
      <infinity.


Then


    sup_{0<r<=r_0}
    sup_{|alpha|=5}
    sup_{x in K_r}
    Var(Z_{r,alpha}(x))
      <=sigma_5^2.


The constant sigma_5^2 is strictly positive. No numerical value is required.


5. CANONICAL METRIC


Let


    d_{r,alpha}(x,y)^2
      =E_{Q_{6,r}}[(Z_{r,alpha}(x)-Z_{r,alpha}(y))^2].


Conditioning reduces the variance of the derivative difference, so


    d_{r,alpha}(x,y)^2
      <=Var(partial^alpha f(x)-partial^alpha f(y)).


Because D^+ is convex, the segment from x to y lies in K_r. By the fundamental theorem of calculus,


    partial^alpha f(x)-partial^alpha f(y)
      =integral_0^1
         (x-y) dot grad partial^alpha f(y+t(x-y)) dt.


Cauchy–Schwarz and stationarity give


    Var(partial^alpha f(x)-partial^alpha f(y))
      <=L_6^2||x-y||_2^2,


where


    L_6^2
      =max_{|alpha|=5}
        E[||grad partial^alpha f(0)||_2^2]
      <infinity.


Thus


    d_{r,alpha}(x,y)<=L_6||x-y||_2


uniformly in r and alpha.


6. SIGNED-INDEX PROCESS


The identity needed for the absolute supremum is


    sup_x|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|
      =sup_{(s,x) in {-1,+1}xK_r}
         s Z_{r,alpha}(x).


Define the centered Gaussian process


    Y_{r,alpha}(s,x)=sZ_{r,alpha}(x)


on


    T_r={-1,+1}xK_r.


This signed-index construction is essential. The inequality


    sup|Z|<=sup Z+sup(-Z)


is not valid in general and is not used.


For a fixed sign,


    d_Y((s,x),(s,y))
      =d_{r,alpha}(x,y)
      <=L_6||x-y||_2.


Across signs,


    d_Y((s,x),(-s,y))^2
      =E[(Z_{r,alpha}(x)+Z_{r,alpha}(y))^2]
      <=4sigma_5^2.


Therefore the signed metric diameter is at most 2sigma_5.


7. UNIFORM ENTROPY AND EXPECTED SUPREMUM


Let A_D be the Euclidean diameter of D^+. Since K_r=rD^+ and r<=r_0, it lies in a fixed bounded planar set. Euclidean covering estimates and the metric domination in Section 5 give constants C_D,A<infinity, independent of r and alpha, such that


    N(K_r,d_{r,alpha},epsilon)
      <=C_D(1+A/epsilon)^2


for every epsilon>0.


Cover the two sign copies separately. Then


    N(T_r,d_Y,epsilon)
      <=2C_D(1+A/epsilon)^2.


The entropy integral


    integral_0^{2sigma_5}
      sqrt(log[2C_D(1+A/epsilon)^2]) d epsilon


is finite. Kernel analyticity supplies a continuous, hence separable, version of the process. Dudley's entropy bound therefore gives a finite constant B_5, independent of r and alpha, satisfying


    E_{Q_{6,r}}
      [sup_{x in K_r}|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|]
      <=B_5.


8. BORELL–TIS


The signed process is centered and separable, its expected supremum is finite, and its pointwise variance is at most sigma_5^2. Borell–TIS gives, for every u>=0,


    Q_{6,r}
      (sup_{x in K_r}|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|>B_5+u)
      <=exp[-u^2/(2sigma_5^2)].


Taking a union bound over the six order-five multi-indices,


    Q_{6,r}
      (max_{|alpha|=5}
       sup_{x in K_r}|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|
       >B_5+u)
      <=6exp[-u^2/(2sigma_5^2)].


9. FIFTH-DERIVATIVE TAIL


By the mean bound,


    H_{5,r}(D^+)
      <=M_5+
        max_{|alpha|=5}
        sup_{x in K_r}|Z_{r,alpha}(x)|.


Set


    t_0=2(M_5+B_5).


If t>=t_0, then


    u=t-(M_5+B_5)>=t/2.


Therefore, for every 0<r<=min(r_0,r_chart) and every t>=t_0,


    Q_{6,r}(H_{5,r}(D^+)>t)
      <=6exp[-t^2/(8sigma_5^2)].


In particular there exist constants


    C_5=6,
    c_5=1/(8sigma_5^2)>0,
    r_5=min(r_0,r_chart)>0


such that


    Q_{6,r}(H_{5,r}(D^+)>t)
      <=C_5 exp(-c_5t^2)


for every 0<r<=r_5 and t>=t_0.


10. SMALL-r CUTOFF COROLLARY


Define


    G_r={H_{5,r}(D^+)<=r^{-1}}.


For sufficiently small r, r^{-1}>=t_0. Consequently


    Q_{6,r}(G_r^c)
      <=6exp[-c_5r^{-2}].


The right-hand side is super-polynomially small: for every p>0,


    exp(-c_5r^{-2})=o(r^p)


as r->0.


11. THEOREM


For the exact normalized-periodized side-24 field conditioned on the exact six value/gradient pins, the physical fifth derivatives on the shrinking chart rD^+ satisfy a uniform sub-Gaussian supremum tail:


    Q_{6,r}(H_{5,r}(D^+)>t)
      <=C_5exp(-c_5t^2)


for all sufficiently small r and all sufficiently large t, with constants independent of r.
