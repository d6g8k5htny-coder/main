**Object:** P02-LM-008 — Exact determinant-weighted Palm-tail transfer.


Let \(W_r\ge0\) under \(Q_r\), \(Z_r=E_{Q_r}[W_r]\), and
\[
Z_r\ge c_Zr^2,\qquad E_{Q_r}[W_r^2]\le C_Wr^4.
\]
Then for every event \(E\),
\[
P_r(E)=\frac{E_Q[W_r1_E]}{Z_r}
\le\frac{\sqrt{C_W}}{c_Z}\,Q_r(E)^{1/2}.
\]


**Scope:** abstract same-law Cauchy–Schwarz interface only.
