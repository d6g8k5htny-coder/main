**Object:** EC-021 — Uniform positive typed finite-Q4 Palm mass.


For the exact finite-Q4 typed Palm law and box \(B_q=\mathrm{P01\text{-}UNIFORMQ\text{-}D4\text{-}BOX\text{-}001}\), there exist \(r_1>0\) and \(c_{Q4}>0\) such that
\[
\inf_{0<r\le r_1}P^{MS}_{r,Q4}(B_q)\ge c_{Q4}>0.
\]


**Scope:** qualitative finite-dimensional finite-Q4 mass only. No exact full-field mass, adjacency, P0.1/P0.2, or numerical terminal constant.
