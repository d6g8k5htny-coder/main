**Object:** P02-LM-005 — Exact uniform nine-jet Gaussian density and moment interface.


For the exact side-24 six-pin conditional law
\[
\gamma_r=N(m_r,\Sigma_r)
\]
of
\[
J_r=(q,a,w,z,c_{40},c_{31},c_{22},c_{13},c_{04}),
\]
the mean and covariance extend continuously through \(r=0\), \(\Sigma_0\) is positive definite, and on a common small-\(r\) interval the density has a Gaussian upper envelope, positive compact-set minima, and uniformly bounded fixed moments.


**Scope:** finite-dimensional exact Gaussian regularity only.
