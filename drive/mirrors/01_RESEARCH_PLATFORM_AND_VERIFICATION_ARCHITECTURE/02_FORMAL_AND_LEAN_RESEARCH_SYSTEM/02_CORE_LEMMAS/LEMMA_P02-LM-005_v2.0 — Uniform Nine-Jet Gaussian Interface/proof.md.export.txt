3. PROOF OF CONTINUITY AND POSITIVE DEFINITENESS


GP-DER-044 constructs an invertible divided-difference transformation from the six raw endpoint pins to a frame L_r whose components are analytic divided differences and which extends continuously through r=0.


Every covariance between transformed pins and the nine midpoint targets is obtained by applying finitely many derivatives and divided differences to the exact periodized analytic covariance kernel. The apparent singularities are removable. Therefore the joint covariance matrix of

extends continuously through r=0.


At r=0, the fifteen limiting functionals have distinct polynomial Fourier symbols. The periodized Gaussian has strictly positive spectral weight at every lattice frequency. If a linear combination had zero variance, its finite-degree polynomial symbol would vanish on the entire integer lattice. The polynomial-lattice argument forces every coefficient to vanish. Hence the limiting fifteen-functional Gram matrix is positive definite.


The six-pin limiting Gram is therefore positive definite. The conditional nine-jet covariance Sigma_0 is its Schur complement in the full positive-definite fifteen-functional Gram, and is positive definite.


Continuity of the joint covariance and inversion of the uniformly nonsingular six-pin block give continuity of the conditional mean and covariance.


Positive definiteness of Sigma_0 plus continuity implies the uniform eigenvalue bounds on a sufficiently small compact r-interval.


4. UNIFORM DENSITY ENVELOPE


Let d=9 and let


M=sup_{0<=r<=r_GR}||m_r||.


The density is


phi_r(u)
=(2pi)^(-d/2)(det Sigma_r)^(-1/2)
 exp[-(1/2)(u-m_r)^T Sigma_r^(-1)(u-m_r)].


The eigenvalue bounds give


(det Sigma_r)^(-1/2)<=lambda_-^(-d/2)


and


(u-m_r)^T Sigma_r^(-1)(u-m_r)
>=||u-m_r||^2/lambda_+.


Using


||u-m_r||^2>=||u||^2/2-M^2,


one obtains


phi_r(u)
<= (2pi)^(-d/2)lambda_-^(-d/2)
   exp[M^2/(2lambda_+)]
   exp[-||u||^2/(4lambda_+)].


Thus one may take

and the displayed finite K.


This proves the exact density-envelope interface required by P02-LM-003.
