2. EXACT FIELD OBJECT


Field: normalized-periodized Bargmann-Fock Gaussian field on the side-24 torus.
Pins: ordered six value/gradient pins at M=(-r/2,0), S=(r/2,0), with b=6/5 and the declared fold gap.
Conditioned law: Q_r.
Typed stratum: M strict maximum, S index-one saddle.
Weight: W_r=|det H_M det H_S|.
Weighted estimator: sum W_i 1_{L_i} 1_{typed,i} divided by sum W_i 1_{typed,i}.
Loop event: both ascending separatrices of S terminate at the same maximum M, with unresolved trajectories kept separate.
