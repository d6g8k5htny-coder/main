# FS2-PREREG-001 — Exact Conditioned and Weighted Same-Maximum Loop Experiment


**Freeze status:** Drafted before new experiment data. Any change after first execution requires a numbered amendment.  
**Primary field:** normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock Gaussian field on the flat side-24 torus.  
**Primary marks:** \(b=6/5\), \(\kappa=1\), \(\theta=0\).  
**Secondary orientation analysis:** preregistered uniform \(\theta\), reported separately.


## 1. Scientific questions


### Q1 — deterministic decomposition


Can a typed exact six-pin sample satisfy REG, H5, and the deep threshold while both ascending separatrices of \(S\) terminate at \(M\)?


A replicated “yes” is a direct counterexample to the LS-DER-036 deterministic good-event exclusion.


### Q2 — conditioned-only finite-r behavior


Among exact six-pin conditioned samples with typed endpoints, how does the unweighted loop probability under \(Q_{r}\) vary over the practical range?


### Q3 — exact weighted finite-r behavior


How does


\[
P^{MS}_r(L_r)=
\frac{E_{Q_r}[W_r1_{L_r}]}{E_{Q_r}[W_r]}
\]


vary over the practical range?


### Q4 — theorem-regime bridge


Do the REG, H5, and deep-threshold conditions become non-vacuous at numerically accessible \(r\), or must constants/arguments be improved before the asymptotic theorem can be empirically probed?


## 2. Range firewall


### Track A — practical finite-r experiment


\[
r\in\{0.40,0.30,0.20,0.15,0.10,0.075,0.05\}.
\]


This track **cannot falsify the current asymptotic theorem**, because its REG certificate requires \(r\le2.38418579\times10^{-7}\) even at \(R=1\). It tests finite-range models and practical extrapolation.


### Track B — certificate/asymptotic experiment


Use normalized coordinates, confluent conditioning, controlled precision, and


\[
r\in\{2\times10^{-7},10^{-7},5\times10^{-8},2\times10^{-8}\}.
\]


Track B is theorem-sensitive only after numerical conditioning, Taylor, H5, and ODE errors are enclosed rigorously.


## 3. Models for Track A


These are empirical finite-window models, not equivalent to the theorem.


- \(M_3:\ \pi(r)=c_3r^3\).
- \(M_{0+3}:\ \pi(r)=c_0+c_3r^3,\ c_0\ge0\).
- \(M_\alpha:\ \pi(r)=cr^\alpha\).
- \(M_{\mathrm{free}}:\) monotone or nonmonotone spline used only as a diagnostic.


The theorem itself states an eventual upper bound \(O(r^3)\), not exact proportionality.


## 4. Sampling protocol


For every \(r\):


1. generate an independent exact six-pin conditioned field under \(Q_r\);
2. verify pin residuals against a frozen tolerance;
3. compute exact endpoint Hessians;
4. retain the typed stratum: \(H_M\) negative definite and \(H_S\) index one;
5. compute \(W_r=|\det H_M\det H_S|\);
6. compute midpoint jets, REG, deep-threshold, and H5 indicators;
7. trace both ascending separatrices with an adaptive ODE;
8. classify whether each terminates at \(M\), escapes above level \(b\), reaches another maximum, or is unresolved;
9. rerun every loop and every unresolved trajectory at stricter tolerance;
10. preserve all failures and unresolved cases.


## 5. Estimators


Conditioned-only:


\[
\widehat Q_r(L)=\frac{\sum_i1_{L_i}1_{\mathrm{typed},i}}
{\sum_i1_{\mathrm{typed},i}}.
\]


Weighted:


\[
\widehat P^{MS}_r(L)=
\frac{\sum_i W_i1_{L_i}1_{\mathrm{typed},i}}
{\sum_i W_i1_{\mathrm{typed},i}}.
\]


Report weight effective sample size:


\[
ESS_W=\frac{(\sum_iW_i)^2}{\sum_iW_i^2}.
\]


Uncertainty is bootstrapped over independent conditioned-field realizations. Pair-level resampling is prohibited when multiple pairs arise from one field.


## 6. Primary outcomes


1. Count of replicated loops on the complete good event.
2. Weighted loop probability at each \(r\).
3. Conditioned-only loop probability at each \(r\).
4. Fraction of loops in boundary, REG-failure, and H5-failure strata.
5. Exponent/profile likelihood for \(M_\alpha\).
6. Comparison of \(M_3\) and \(M_{0+3}\) by preregistered likelihood and held-out predictive score.
7. ESS and unresolved-trajectory rate.


## 7. Stopping rule


Run in fixed batches. No result-dependent model changes.


- Smoke test: 2,000 samples per \(r\).
- Main stage: continue to 50,000 per \(r\).
- Extension: up to 200,000 per \(r\) only if weighted ESS is below 5,000 or fewer than 30 loop events are observed across the grid.
- Stop early only for a replicated deterministic good-event counterexample.
- The requested exponent standard error \(<0.15\) is a secondary precision target, not the sole stop rule.


## 8. Numerical controls


- two conditioning implementations: direct covariance and confluent/SVD frame;
- two ODE tolerances;
- endpoint Hessian mutation controls;
- branch-label reversal control;
- known comparison-field fixtures;
- independent recomputation of \(W_r\);
- frozen seeds and environment lock;
- exact code and result hashes;
- fail closed on pin, type, ODE, or identity ambiguity.


## 9. Decision language


- A practical-range plateau does not falsify the current theorem.
- A replicated loop on the complete certified good event falsifies the deterministic exclusion.
- Failure to reach the certified regime is reported as `INACCESSIBLE WITH CURRENT CONSTANTS`, not `SURVIVES`.
- Agreement with \(r^3\) is evidence, not proof.
