# SCOPE_FIREWALL.md


The following substitutions are prohibited unless an explicit reviewed bridge exists:


- planar ↔ exact side-24 torus;
- finite-Q4 ↔ exact full field;
- conditioned Gaussian law \(Q\) ↔ determinant-weighted typed Palm law \(P^{MS}\);
- fixed-r ↔ uniform small-r;
- existence/nonemptiness ↔ positive probability;
- positive probability ↔ quantitative lower bound;
- degree-four comparison field ↔ exact field;
- local branch capture ↔ elder-rule persistence pairing;
- empirical scaling ↔ theorem;
- same-family corroboration ↔ organizational independence;
- packet transport checksum ↔ marker-delimited frozen-body hash.


A parent theorem may use only the exact conclusion supplied by a Core object.




## Variable and monotonicity firewall
- fixed physical q ↔ fixed scaled s=q/r;
- hardest endpoint ↔ arbitrary endpoint;
- monotonic improvement as s becomes more negative ↔ compactness of the raw family.


For the current fixed-q corridor, q remains in a fixed negative interval and s=q/r becomes more negative as r decreases. The least-negative s occurs at the largest allowed r. Any future uniform statement must prove its own monotonicity direction and hardest endpoint.


## Normalizer-direction firewall
For a probability quotient N_r/Z_r:
- an upper bound on the probability requires a lower bound on the full same-law normalizer;
- a lower bound on the probability requires an upper bound on the full same-law normalizer.
A restricted-event denominator, finite-Q4 denominator, planar denominator, or predecessor normalizer is prohibited.


## Event and branch firewall
The selected branch convention, local selector, Borel globalization, convergence event, adjacency event, and same-maximum loop event are distinct objects. Approval of one does not approve the others. A local certified trajectory implies the global event only when the exact selected branch and invariant exact capture region are preserved.


## Review and source firewall
- mathematical reconstruction ↔ exact current-hash approval;
- provider diversity ↔ method independence;
- review count ↔ eligible independent paths;
- frozen body ↔ mutable header or title;
- current snapshot hash ↔ historical verdict on an unhashed predecessor.


## Experiment firewall
- practical finite-r behavior ↔ certified asymptotic regime;
- absence of observed failures ↔ proof;
- agreement with r^3 ↔ theorem;
- failure to reach the certificate regime ↔ survival.
Use `INACCESSIBLE WITH CURRENT CONSTANTS` when the theorem regime cannot be numerically reached with controlled error.


## Canonicalization firewall
LMC-1 literal-marker canonicalization and BRH-1 conditional blank-run halving are different rule families. Apply only the rule named by the carrier. A hash mismatch produced by the wrong extraction rule is a transport diagnosis, not evidence of mathematical corruption.
