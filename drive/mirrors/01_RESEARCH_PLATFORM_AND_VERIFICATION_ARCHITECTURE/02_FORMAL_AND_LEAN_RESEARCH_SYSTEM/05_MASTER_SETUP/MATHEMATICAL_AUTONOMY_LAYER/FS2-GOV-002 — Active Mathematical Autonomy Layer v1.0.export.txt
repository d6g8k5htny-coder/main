4. BINDING MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS


I1 — Exact object identity.
Every claim is keyed by object ID, version, exact statement, source hash or frozen identity, law, coordinate convention, and dependency set.


I2 — Domain typing.
Every lemma declares fixed and varying parameters, quantifier order, parameter intervals, coordinate system, law, and hardest endpoint. Missing domain metadata is HOLD.


I3 — Monotonicity endpoint.
A uniform claim based on monotonicity must name the monotone quantity, direction, proof object, and hardest endpoint. Numerical trend is not a monotonicity proof.


I4 — Power ledger.
Every scaling or mass claim involving r, volume, determinants, Jacobians, density, or normalization must provide a symbolic ledger. The final exponent must be reproducible by exact addition/subtraction of declared contributions.


I5 — Same-law normalization.
A numerator, weight, event, and denominator must belong to the same declared base law unless a reviewed bridge exists. Good-event or adjacency-conditioned denominators cannot silently replace the full normalizer.


I6 — Interface closure.
A theorem inherits every unresolved transitive dependency. An unresolved interface produces CONDITIONAL or HOLD; folder placement cannot upgrade it.


I7 — Vulnerability scan.
Known failure signatures are scanned before review and again before promotion. A warning does not prove a defect, but an unresolved load-bearing warning blocks automatic PASS.


I8 — Reviewer-method independence.
Provider labels alone do not establish independence. The review record must include authorship, source exposure, method, implementation reuse, vulnerability conflicts, and post-freeze comparison.


I9 — Contradiction reopening.
A new exact counterexample, identity mismatch, domain mismatch, or failed control automatically reopens the narrow affected scope.


I10 — No status transfer.
A closed interface does not promote its parent theorem. A successful finite-Q4 object does not become an exact full-field result. A practical-r experiment does not prove an asymptotic theorem.


5. VULNERABILITY SIGNATURES


VUL-001 — Quotient without denominator gate: division by an object not previously proved nonzero or bounded away from zero.


VUL-002 — Supremum/sum interchange: replacing sup(f+g) by sup(f)+sup(g), or the reverse direction, without a valid inequality and scope.


VUL-003 — Finite-sample continuum extrapolation: a finite grid or coordinate subset is presented as certification of a full continuum or higher-dimensional box.


VUL-004 — Parameter-domain substitution: fixed-r, uniform-r, fixed-q, fixed-s, compact-mark, or asymptotic domains are interchanged without a bridge.


VUL-005 — Law substitution: planar, finite-Q4, conditioned Gaussian, exact torus, typed Palm, or adjacency-conditioned laws are interchanged.


VUL-006 — Normalizer-direction or denominator substitution: a lower bound is used in the wrong direction or a restricted denominator replaces the full exact normalizer.


VUL-007 — Hash/version drift: a verdict, source, or dependency points to a predecessor, mutable addendum, or mismatched frozen body.


VUL-008 — Hidden monotonicity endpoint: a uniform statement omits which endpoint is worst or assumes the direction from sampled values.


VUL-009 — Coordinate-volume mismatch: physical and normalized coordinates, Jacobians, or box volumes are combined without an explicit change-of-variables ledger.


VUL-010 — Consensus laundering: repeated same-family, shared-source, or copied-implementation agreement is counted as independent evidence.


6. REVIEWER ELIGIBILITY LATTICE


Eligibility is task-specific, not a permanent judgment about a person, model, or provider.


ELIGIBLE-SOLE: reviewer is not the author, has no pre-freeze access to the target derivation beyond the permitted specification, uses an independently described method, reproduces exact identity, executes required controls, and has no unresolved same-signature conflict.


ELIGIBLE-WITH-COMPENSATION: reviewer has relevant prior exposure, authored or approved a historical object carrying the same vulnerability signature, or shares implementation ancestry. The review may contribute evidence but cannot be the sole independent path unless paired with a distinct method, formal verifier, or second eligible reviewer.


CORROBORATION-ONLY: same author line, copied implementation, post-result reconstruction, or shared hidden assumptions. Zero independent credit.


INELIGIBLE-FOR-TARGET: target author reviewing own result, unreconciled source access, missing identity reproduction, active contradiction, or refusal/inability to execute the target gate.


No provider family is declared intrinsically incapable. The restriction follows the exact target, exposure, method, and vulnerability signature.
