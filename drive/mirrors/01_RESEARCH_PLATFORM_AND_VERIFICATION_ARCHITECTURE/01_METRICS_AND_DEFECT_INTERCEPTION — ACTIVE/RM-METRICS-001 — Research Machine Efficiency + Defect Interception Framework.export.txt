RM-METRICS-001 — RESEARCH MACHINE EFFICIENCY + DEFECT INTERCEPTION FRAMEWORK


Status: ACTIVE — prospective measurement framework
Effective: 2026-09-14
Authority: Dylan Roy direct instruction; implementation by ChatGPT under OP-PROT-012
Mathematical impact: NONE by adoption alone
Purpose: quantify whether the long-horizon AI research architecture becomes more efficient and intercepts defects earlier over successive campaigns.


1. CORE CAMPAIGN METRICS


For each research campaign i, record:


T_i = wall-clock elapsed time from frozen campaign start to the terminal campaign disposition.
A_i = agent-hours or best available normalized agent-compute-hours, with provider/model/session lineage recorded.
C_i = compute consumed, separated by CPU/GPU/high-precision/interval/formal-verifier workloads where measurable.
N_i = novel mathematical objects created and retained after review. Count theorem/lemma/definition/method objects separately; do not count restatements or document copies.
K_i = claims, routes, constants, algorithms, or interpretations killed before promotion. Record defect class and whether the kill prevented downstream use.
R_i = reusable components produced: proof lemmas, covariance libraries, pin transforms, rigorous-numerics modules, test batteries, provenance machinery, review procedures, and research-language components.
E_i = post-review defects discovered after an object had already received its current review grade. Severity and blast radius must be recorded.


Every metric must carry a measurement grade: EXACT, RECEIPTED, ESTIMATED, or UNKNOWN. Missing historical data remains UNKNOWN; it is never backfilled by guesswork.


2. REVIEW-SURVIVING MATHEMATICAL PROGRESS


Define P_i as a vector, not a raw document count. P_i should measure newly created mathematical state that survives the campaign's terminal adversarial review. Recommended components:


P_theorem = theorem-sized claims newly closed at their declared scope.
P_lemma = reusable load-bearing lemmas newly closed.
P_method = novel reusable methods or reductions that survive review.
P_cert = rigorous computational/formal certificates newly established.
P_kill = verified kills that eliminate a false load-bearing route.
P_reuse = components demonstrably reused in a later campaign.


Do not collapse P_i into one scalar until weights are frozen prospectively. The raw vector is authoritative; scalar productivity scores are analytical views only.


3. RESEARCH EFFICIENCY


After a prospective cost normalization is frozen, define an exploratory efficiency index


eta_i = P_i / (A_i + lambda_C C_i + lambda_H H_i),


where H_i denotes direct human research-hours if separately measured and lambda_C, lambda_H are frozen conversion weights. Until those weights are validated, report the numerator vector and resource vector side by side rather than claiming one universal efficiency scalar.


Primary architectural question:


Does efficiency improve over successive comparable campaigns?


Operationally test trends in eta_i, time-to-closure, agent effort per surviving result, and reusable-component reuse rate. Do not interpret one campaign-to-campaign improvement as a causal architecture effect without controls.


4. DEFECT INTERCEPTION DEPTH


For each defect j define:


s0_j = stage/node where the defect first entered a load-bearing artifact.
sdet_j = stage/node where it was first detected.
D_defect,j = shortest load-bearing dependency distance from s0_j to sdet_j.


Also record:


B_defect,j = number of downstream load-bearing objects touched before detection (blast radius).
L_defect,j = wall-clock latency from introduction to detection when timestamps are reconstructable.
G_defect,j = severity class: LOCAL, ROUTE-INVALIDATING, CLAIM-INVALIDATING, CAMPAIGN-SYSTEMIC.
I_defect,j = interception layer that caught it: deterministic test, mutation test, independent derivation, adversarial reviewer, formal verifier, source/provenance audit, or human review.


Architectural targets:


median(D_defect) decreases;
95th-percentile D_defect decreases;
B_defect decreases;
L_defect decreases;
fraction caught before promotion increases;
fraction caught by deterministic/fail-closed checks increases.


Ideal state is D_defect = 0 or 1 for known defect classes: introduction is rejected locally or at the next validation boundary.


5. REGRESSION FROM DISCOVERED DEFECTS


Every material defect must generate one of:


(a) a deterministic invariant,
(b) a mutation test,
(c) a conformance test,
(d) a new review obligation,
(e) an explicit reason why no general detector is currently possible.


Named benchmark defects from q0/LPW include:


- missing square root in a Cauchy–Schwarz upper bound;
- four-neighbor false-maxima interpretation;
- 3D theorem combined with a 2D theorem;
- upward-rounded lower eigenfloor;
- planar-reference identities described as exact finite-torus identities;
- sine terms omitted at nonzero separation;
- center/rung Gaussian density confused with a uniform floor;
- reproducibility confused with certification;
- multiple same-ancestry agents confused with independent evidence;
- false fourth moment E[(|X|+|Y|)^4] = 12 + 16/pi instead of 12 + 32/pi;
- published decimal lower bound exceeding the exact consumed fraction.


These defects form the initial regression corpus. Future campaigns must test whether equivalent errors are intercepted earlier than in their original occurrence.


6. ARCHITECTURAL LEARNING METRICS


Track additionally:


ReuseRate_i = fraction of load-bearing campaign components imported from previously reviewed reusable components.
ReworkRate_i = fraction of campaign effort spent repairing previously promoted or reviewed objects.
IndependentRouteYield_i = qualifying provenance-distinct verification routes produced per major claim.
FalseClosureRate_i = fraction of promoted/frozen claims later reopened for a load-bearing defect.
KillUtility_i = downstream work avoided by pre-promotion kills, where reconstructable.
StateCompression_i = ratio between total raw campaign artifacts and the minimal current-state package required for a new qualified agent to continue correctly.


7. DATA-CAPTURE RULE GOING FORWARD


At campaign start, create a METRICS_START receipt with start timestamp, exact target, initial dependency state, available agents, and instrumentation capabilities.


During execution, each substantive agent/workstream records start/end time, role, source/result exposure, artifacts created, compute class, and terminal disposition.


At every defect, record DEFECT_EVENT immediately with s0 if known, detection location, affected descendants, severity, detector, repair, and new regression test.


At campaign freeze, create METRICS_CLOSE with terminal theorem state, surviving objects, kills, reusable outputs, unresolved obligations, resource totals, and review defects.


8. BASELINE POLICY


Historical q0 campaigns may be reconstructed only from receipts that actually exist. Do not fabricate agent-hours or compute costs retrospectively.


Use q0/LPW as Baseline Campaign Family 0. Its historical metrics will initially be incomplete, but its defect chronology and reusable-component inventory are rich enough to establish qualitative baselines.


The first fully instrumented campaign after adoption becomes Baseline Campaign 1. The matching 2D upper campaign may be used only if instrumentation begins before its current running work is treated as the campaign start; otherwise classify it as PARTIALLY INSTRUMENTED and begin full instrumentation at the next frozen campaign boundary.


9. INTERPRETATION FIREWALL


A lower T_i or A_i does not prove the architecture caused the improvement. Model capability, task difficulty, prior mathematical knowledge, compute budget, and problem similarity are confounders.


Report architectural progress when multiple signals align: shorter closure time on comparable tasks, higher reuse, lower D_defect/B_defect, fewer post-review defects, and equal-or-higher independent review quality.


The objective is not maximum theorem count. The objective is increasing verified research progress per unit resource while reducing the distance and damage of errors.
