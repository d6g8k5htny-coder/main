3. EXECUTABLE STATUS MODEL


The prototype requires an explicit ordered status lattice. The fixture uses:


REFUTED < AMEND_REQUIRED < HOLD < BLOCKED < UNATTACKED < SELF_CONSISTENT < ADVERSARIALLY_SURVIVED < CORE_FORMALLY_CHECKED < EXTERNALLY_VERIFIED.


This order is a prototype policy input, not a live scientific reclassification. Negative statuses propagate fail closed. A dependent’s effective status is the minimum of its local status and every dependency’s effective status. A requested status above the effective status is rejected.


4. CLAIM AND OBLIGATION CONTRACT


Every claim has:
- unique claim_id;
- exact natural-language statement;
- explicit dependency IDs;
- local status;
- requested status;
- refutation-required Boolean;
- atomic obligations.


Every obligation has:
- unique obligation_id;
- exact statement;
- maximum feasible tier;
- status;
- discharging artifacts.


Supported prototype tiers:
- TIER0_FINITE — exhaustive finite decision procedure;
- TIER0_ALGEBRA — sound algebraic checker;
- TIER1_FORMAL — formal proof artifact;
- TIER2_ADVERSARIAL — both a distinct adversarial-review PASS and a dedicated refutation PASS are required for a positive status.


A counterexample or negative result does not need a positive-tier artifact to remain negative. It remains explicit and propagates downstream.


5. FINITE EXHAUSTIVENESS CERTIFICATE


A case certificate declares:
- finite variables and complete domains;
- pattern rules with explicit wildcards;
- the classification assigned by each rule;
- the only residual classes allowed by the theorem statement.


The verifier enumerates the Cartesian product and rejects:
- uncovered assignments;
- assignments matching more than one rule;
- forbidden residual classes;
- rules matching no assignment.


For the original TB-G3B split the verifier finds two concrete uncovered assignments. They differ only in the irrelevant fN_lt_b flag and represent the same missing semantic leaf:


prior_merge=false,
self_merge=true,
other_to_M=true.


This is L_same. The original certificate is rejected.


The corrected certificate adds the explicit rule


self_merge=true and other_to_M=true -> RESIDUAL:L_SAME,


and declares RESIDUAL:L_SAME as allowed. It covers all 16 Boolean assignments exactly once and is accepted.
