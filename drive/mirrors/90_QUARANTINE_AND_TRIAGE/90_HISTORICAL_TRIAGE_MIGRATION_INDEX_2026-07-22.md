## Purpose
The **one** place for any item that can't yet be confidently classified. Replaces the 10 scattered "UNKNOWN ACTION — PEER REVIEW" trees.

## Rule
No per-area `UNKNOWN` / `90_UNKNOWN` folders. Anything unresolved comes **here** and is logged in the index below with a pointer to where it came from, so it never loses its context.

## Migration (for GP / ChatGPT)
Move the *contents* of the existing 10 UNKNOWN trees into this inbox, record each in the index, then quarantine the emptied scaffolds. The 10 origin trees are listed in the standard, §2 (P1).
