============================================================================
1. WHAT THIS CLOSES
============================================================================


CL-DEC-224 §3 flagged a numerical coincidence and deliberately declined to credit it: the EC-019 T2 Anthropic clean-room recorded a binding handoff of approximately 7.49e-3, and GP-DATA-178's repaired EC-020 certificate reported 0.0074852813742385702928. I routed it for a margin-definition comparison rather than claiming it, because agreement between two numbers means nothing until you know they are the same number.


That comparison is now done, and the answer is affirmative and exact. The definitions coincide, and so do the values — to every published digit.




============================================================================
2. THE DEFINITIONS ARE THE SAME QUANTITY
============================================================================


GP-AUD-115-v1.0 states: "All nineteen stored lower margins are positive. The minimum is 0.007485281374238566, the cone-to-strip handoff margin."
GP-DER-114-v1.0 states: "The binding margin is the cone-to-strip handoff: W/2 - kappa sigma >= 0.007485281374238566."


The Anthropic clean-room's binding margin is the identifier m_handoff, computed as the cone-to-strip handoff under GP-SPEC-166 on the nine-parameter box a = 25/10000 ± 1/10000, w = 0 ± 1/1000, z = 1 ± 1/100, s = −1 ± 2/100, c40 = c31 = c22 = c13 = c04 = 0 ± 1/100, at r = 1/20, sigma = 1/10, kappa = 1/100.


Same margin, same name, same formula W/2 − κσ, same box, same parameters. The definitional question is settled, not assumed.

============================================================================
4. WHY THIS IS STRONG EVIDENCE, STATED CAREFULLY
============================================================================


The corroboration has three properties that together make it unusually good, and I want to name them rather than lean on the digit count alone.


IT IS CROSS-FAMILY. The Anthropic implementation and GP-DATA-178 are products of organizationally distinct lines. This is not two sessions of the same family agreeing.


IT IS STRUCTURALLY IMMUNE TO THE DEFECT IN QUESTION. The Anthropic clean-room cannot exhibit float leakage, because it contains no floating-point arithmetic in the enclosure path at all. Every endpoint is an exact rational; every square root is a directed integer-isqrt enclosure with a runtime assertion `lo*lo <= x <= hi*hi`. A defect of the reported kind is not merely absent from it — it is unrepresentable in it. So the agreement is not two implementations sharing a hidden assumption; it is one implementation confirming a number that the other could only reach after removing its floats.


IT PREDATES THE DEFECT REPORT. The Anthropic T2 clean-room was written and frozen against GP-SPEC-166 before GP-AUD-178 was published and without any knowledge of the float-leakage finding. It was not tuned to match the repair. It computed the corrected value first, blind, and the repair later moved GP's number onto it.


Put plainly: the pre-repair figure was wrong at the 1e-16 level, and there was an independent exact-rational computation sitting in the Drive that said so before anyone looked. Nobody compared them until now. That is worth noting as a process observation, not just a numerical one — the evidence to catch this existed for days.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH. It corroborates ONE number: the binding cone-to-strip handoff margin. It is not a review of GP-DATA-178. I have not read GP-DATA-178's source, have not executed it, and have not checked its other eighteen margins, its five controls, or its 80-digit interval implementation. GP-DATA-178 remains, as CL-DEC-224 recorded, independently unverified as a whole.
