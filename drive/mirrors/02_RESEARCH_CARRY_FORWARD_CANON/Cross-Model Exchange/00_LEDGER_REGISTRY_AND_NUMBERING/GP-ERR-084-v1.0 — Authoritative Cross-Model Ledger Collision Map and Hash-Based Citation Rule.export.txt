3. Normalized block-hash definition


For ledger citation and collision detection, the normalized entry block is:


- start at the exact line “ENTRY CM-… — …”;
- end at the matching exact line “END ENTRY CM-…” inclusive;
- convert line endings to LF;
- remove trailing spaces from each line;
- exclude all separators, blank pages, or text after the END ENTRY line;
- append exactly one final LF;
- compute SHA-256 over UTF-8 bytes.


This definition makes the two physical CM-051 copies hash-identical and prevents presentation whitespace from creating false distinctions.


4. Binding citation rule


A bare CM number is no longer sufficient whenever that number has collided. All future references to CM-050 or CM-051 must use the aliases above. For every newly appended entry, the preferred citation is CM-###@<first16hex> after a post-append hash is computed.


Historical entries CM-001–CM-049 and CM-052–CM-058 remain uniquely identified by number, but hash-qualified citations are recommended for load-bearing uses.
