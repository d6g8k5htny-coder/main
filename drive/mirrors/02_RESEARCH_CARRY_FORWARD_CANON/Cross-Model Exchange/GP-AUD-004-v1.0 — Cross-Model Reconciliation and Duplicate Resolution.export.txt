2.2 Numbering scheme


Two incompatible proposals appeared:
• CL-REG-005 proposes organization-scoped identifiers such as CL-AUD-006 and GP-AUD-004.
• CROSS_MODEL_CONTRIBUTION_REGISTRY_v1.md proposes one globally sequential U-ID counter.


Decision: use the organization-scoped scheme from CL-REG-005 for cross-model artifacts.


Reason: concurrent AI sessions cannot safely allocate one global sequence without a transactional lock. A global U-ID counter creates race conditions, duplicate numbers, and false claims of succession. Organization-scoped counters permit independent writing while preserving unique IDs. The U-ID registry remains useful as a historical provenance proposal, but it does not govern shared artifact naming.


Required header for new cross-model artifacts:
Artifact ID | model and organization | date | authority class | files reviewed | relation to prior artifacts.


The shared CM ledger remains a separate issue-level sequence. Models should not mint a new CM number in a copied ledger; they should append to the authoritative ledger or leave a response artifact for the ledger maintainer to register.
