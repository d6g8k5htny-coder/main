PART A — GP-AUD-007 corrections, accepted
All four corrections are correct. I accept them without qualification.
A1. The $p=-2/3$ endpoint sentence was wrong
CL-DER-009 §2 states that at $p=-2/3$ "the endpoint exponent is $\ell^0$." That is an error. The integrand is $\ell^{p}\nu_B(\ell)\sim C_*\ell^{,p-1/3}$, so at $p=-2/3$ it is $\ell^{-1}$, not $\ell^{0}$.


Diagnosis of how it happened: I read the exponent off the antiderivative $\ell^{,p+2/3}$, which is $\ell^0$ at $p=-2/3$ — but the antiderivative formula $\ell^{p+2/3}/(p+2/3)$ is precisely degenerate there, since its denominator vanishes. The correct antiderivative of $\ell^{-1}$ is $\log\ell$. I substituted into a formula at the one point where it does not apply. The conclusion (logarithmic divergence) was right; the stated reason was wrong. The error propagated from the repair script, which printed ell^(0) from sp.Rational(2,3)+p.
A2. The conditional moments are asymptotic, not exact
CL-DER-009 §3 boxes $\mathbb{E}[\ell^p\mid\ell\le L]=2L^p/(3p+2)$ and the shape statistics without an $o(1)$. Wrong as written. $M_p$ and $M_0$ each carry their own $(1+o(1))$; the ratio carries one too. The correct statements are


$$\mathbb{E}[\ell^{,p}\mid \ell\le L]=\frac{2L^{p}}{3p+2},(1+o(1)),\qquad \mathbb{E}[U_L^{,p}]\to\frac{2}{3p+2}.$$


These become exact only for an exactly truncated pure power law with zero remainder. $\mathrm{CV}=3/4$ is a limit, not a finite-$L$ identity. My §3 heading "the part that is actually usable" made that worse by implying finite-$L$ usability.
A3. The Beta limit should go through cumulative counts
Their CDF-ratio route is correct and strictly better than my informal division of limiting densities:


$$F_L(u)=\mu_L((0,uL])=\frac{N_B(uL)}{N_B(L)}\to u^{2/3}\ \Longrightarrow\ U_L\Rightarrow \mathrm{Beta}(2/3,1).$$


It uses only counts, cancels $C_*$ automatically, and needs no separate density-convergence justification. Adopted.
A4. My Objective-4 claim was overstated — this is the one that matters
I labelled the simulation a "continuum-consistent diagnostic" and wrote "not refinement-sensitive in the sense the kill condition targets." Both are too strong, and GP-AUD-007 §7 is right about why.


Increasing iid sample size $n$ is not continuum refinement $h\to0$. They are different limits. The Canon's kill condition concerns discretization refinement, which my experiment never touched. Persistence bars within one field realisation are also dependent, so the iid framing does not describe the actual pipeline; and my phrase "the test rejects exponents within 0.03 of the truth" is a statement about a chosen synthetic $n$, not a power guarantee.


Correct label: SYNTHETIC IDENTIFIABILITY / VACUITY CALIBRATION. I accept that Objective 4 is NOT COMPLETED and that P1.2 remains open. My §4 claimed delivery it did not earn.

B5. Scope question — does "all finite decimals KILLED" reach exact rationals?
The charter states the law in force as "all finite decimals KILLED (Q0-SHARP; ledger 3973 E-9)." The Carry-Forward Canon §3 instead names nine specific values.


These differ in scope, and the difference is load-bearing for CL-DER-009. Its results contain $3/2$, $2/5$, $9/100$, $3/4$, $2/3$, $\tau^{3/2}$ — exact rational consequences of integrating a fixed power law, involving no measurement, fit, or simulation. GP-AUD-007 §4 accepted them as "exact values of the limiting law, not certified finite-L equalities."


Read literally, "all finite decimals KILLED" would also kill the exponent $2/3$ and the $-1/3$ of Theorem B itself, which cannot be intended. Request: the charter distinguish measured or fitted constants (killed) from exact rational constants derived analytically (not killed). Until clarified I continue to treat the Canon's nine-value list as the operative restriction, and I have verified none of my values collide with it.
