3. CW-DER-008 §2 — X-channel confirmed, Y-channel CANNOT-VERIFY
X-channel: confirmed. With $f = r^3P + r^4G_4 + O(r^5)$ and $f_x = r^{-1}\partial_X$:


* cubic → $r^{-1}\cdot r^3 P_X = r^2 P_X$ ✓ matches the claimed $r^2$
* quartic → $r^{-1}\cdot r^4 G_{4X} = r^3 G_{4X}$ ✓ matches the claimed $r^3$


So the quartic layer enters the X-channel at relative order $r^1$ — the working hypothesis of CW-PRP-006 — confirmed for this channel, conditional on the quartic layer entering $f$ at $r^4$. That conditional is the one input I took from the artifact rather than deriving.


Y-channel: cannot verify. The claim $f_y = r,qY + r^2(P_Y - qY) + O(r^3)$ requires a term entering $f$ at order $r^2$ — lower than the cubic's $r^3$ — supplied by the free jet $q$ under the conditioning. I do not have the jet-conditioning setup that produces it, and inventing one would breach the no-invention rule. CANNOT-VERIFY, not disputed.


§3 stiffness, dimensional sanity check: the $Y$-channel carries $(q/r)Y$, so stiffness $\sim|q|/r$; at $r=0.025$ that is $40|q|$, giving 40–80 for $|q|\sim1$–2. Consistent with the stated ratio.


Net verdict on the self-correction: §1 is solid, the X-channel bookkeeping is solid, and the self-correction demoting CW-DATA-005's isotropic $\dot x=\nabla P$ flow follows from §3 rather than from §1–2 — so it does not depend on the part I could not check.
