## How these bytes got here

Every object was fetched through the Drive connector (`download_file_content`, base64) and the
base64 was decoded from the session transcript straight to disk, so no model retyped any byte.
For a raw file the SHA-256 and byte count were recomputed from disk and had to equal the
`drive/inventory.jsonl` row (2026-09-17 snapshot) or the bytes were not stored; every stored raw
file here passed. A native Google Doc has no payload digest anywhere in the corpus: its text export
is stored as `<title>.export.txt` with `exact: false` (a reading copy), and where the export carries
full-line `BEGIN_*BODY` / `END_*BODY` markers the marker-delimited body digest is recorded and
compared with the digest the registers declare for that Drive id. `_MANIFEST.jsonl` holds one row
per object and `tools/verify_manifests.py` re-checks every digest in CI. Archives are not extracted
and nothing was executed; `<name>.zip.members.txt` is a derived member listing, not a Drive object.

## What this directory does not establish

Nothing here verifies, reviews, replays, promotes, closes, discharges or reclassifies any claim,
premise, obligation, interface, route or closure. A digest match establishes identity of bytes, not
truth, review or authorization; a body digest that equals the register's is identity of the body, not
a verdict on it. A reading copy (`exact: false`) is not the object. P0.2 remains at the register's
status; the ten exact objects remain review-pending exactly as their own status lines say; the Lean
sources are uncompiled; the GP-188 successor is noncanonical and not installed; the LPW constant and
the Rayleigh repair await the external review the source itself demands; the review verdicts quoted
above (APPROVE, PASS WITHIN SCOPE, AMEND REQUIRED, NONCLOSURE) are the source's vocabulary and are
not merged into `registers/json/review_queue.json`. No independence credit is awarded to anything by
mirroring it, no original prize problem is solved, and no 2D bound is composed with the 3D track.
