1. CORRECTIONS PROPERLY MADE


The erratum does three important things correctly:


1. It reports that the fitted separation exponent varies strongly with resolution and fitting range.
2. It withdraws the apparent agreement obtained by inserting s=-0.263 into the exponent dictionary.
3. It refuses to reinterpret the preregistered R3 outcome as confirmation.


This is a valid correction of CL-DER-012's quantitative diagnosis.


2. REPRODUCIBILITY BLOCK


The erratum names floor_test.py and master seed 20260720. Drive search did not locate floor_test.py, raw pair tables, field realizations, the preregistered R1/R2/R3 artifact, environment files, command lines, or hashes.


Consequently, the displayed values at n=128,256,512,1024 are reported results, not independently reproduced results. The erratum improves epistemic status by withdrawing claims, but it does not create verified replacement evidence.


3. GRID-FLOOR FORMULA NEEDS THE KAPPA OBJECT CARD


The erratum uses


    ell approximately r^3/3


when converting a lifetime floor h^2 to a separation threshold. The exact canonical A2 normal form gives ell=r^3/6 only in its canonical coordinate. The governing physical-field object is


    ell=kappa r^3/6.


Therefore any separation floor derived from a lifetime error must be kappa-aware:


    r_min approximately (6 ell_floor/kappa)^(1/3).


A fixed coefficient 1/3 cannot be used without explaining the coordinate convention or measured kappa. Since kappa varies with local jets, a single global r_min may be a coarse diagnostic but is not an exact trust boundary.


4. THE BETA SEQUENCE DOES NOT VERIFY CONVERGENCE TO 3


The erratum reports beta values


    2.425, 2.599, 2.727, 2.740.


The sequence is monotone, but the last change is only 0.013 and the final value remains 0.260 below 3. Without uncertainty intervals, identical matched continuum saddles, local interpolation-error control, and a demonstrated asymptotic window, this is not evidence sufficient to claim convergence to 3. It is a useful refinement diagnostic only.


The exact cubic mechanism remains supported symbolically by the A2 normal form. Numerical support for the physical-field fold relation requires sub-grid critical-point and saddle reconstruction and estimation of kappa, not pixel-to-merge-pixel distances alone.


5. NEGATIVE FITTED s VALUES DO NOT ESTABLISH A CONTINUUM LAW


The erratum notes that every fitted s is negative. However, the same estimator is explicitly unstable over a range from about -1.09 to -0.09 and is affected by resolution, fit range, and the global floor cut. A common sign across unstable fits may motivate further investigation, but it does not identify the sign of a continuum radial candidate-pair intensity.


In addition, the measured histogram uses discrete birth-to-merge-pixel distances. It has not been shown to equal the radial intensity entering the typed maximum-saddle Palm representation after Kac-Rice weighting, adjacency, kappa marks, and elder-rule selection.


Therefore the statement that the unconditioned continuum separation density is qualitatively non-planar remains a hypothesis, not a verified conclusion.


6. CONSTANT BELOW-FLOOR FRACTION IS NOT IDENTIFYING


A roughly constant fraction of bars below h^2 while h decreases is compatible with a genuine accumulation of short bars, but it is not uniquely diagnostic of that mechanism. The same behavior can be affected by:


- growth in the total number of resolved pairs under refinement;
- persistence-pair creation caused by discretization and triangulation;
- use of the merge pixel instead of the continuum saddle;
- changing local curvature and interpolation error;
- a global threshold that mixes regions with different error scales;
- unmatched bars between resolutions.


To support a genuine divergent continuum population, one needs nested matching of the same bars, local error certificates, stability under triangulation choices, and a continuum extrapolation of counts or quantiles. The erratum correctly labels this as an observation, but its explanatory sentence is stronger than the evidence.


7. CL-DER-011 IS NOT 'UNAFFECTED' IN VERIFICATION STATUS


The withdrawal of s does not algebraically alter CL-DER-011's direct lifetime fit. Nevertheless, a≈0.205 remains:


- refinement-sensitive;
- measured in broad windows containing a substantial fraction of all bars;
- based on an unconditioned full-field object rather than the typed-Palm theorem object;
- unreproducible from Drive because the scripts and raw outputs are missing;
- materially inconsistent with the earlier C104 full-field pipeline.


Thus CL-DER-011 remains a provisional protocol diagnostic. It does not stand as a verified negative result.
