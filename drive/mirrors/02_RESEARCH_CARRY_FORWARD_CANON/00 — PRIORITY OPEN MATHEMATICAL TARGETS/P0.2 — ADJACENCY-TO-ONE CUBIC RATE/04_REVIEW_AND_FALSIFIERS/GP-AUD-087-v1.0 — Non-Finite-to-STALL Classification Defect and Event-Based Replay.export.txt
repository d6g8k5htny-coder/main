1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT


A material classifier defect is present in the published trajectory source. The program integrates to a fixed horizon, reads Pe=sol.y[:,-1], computes dM=hypot(Pe[0]+0.5,Pe[1]), then tests CAPTURE and ESCAPE before falling through to STALL. It does not require sol.success, inspect solver status, stop on chart-boundary events, or reject non-finite states.


For Pe containing NaN, dM is NaN and every comparison used by the classifier is false. The trajectory is therefore labeled STALL. Thus the statement “failure mode is stalling, not escape” is not supported by the current source. At least part of the STALL population is a numerical-invalid fallthrough class.


Verdict: MATERIAL DEFECT CONFIRMED. The reported three-rung mass remains an exploratory upper estimate only. Its exponent and constant must not be promoted until the exact fixture set is replayed with failure-complete event handling.


2. STATIC FAILURE MECHANISM


Published logic, in equivalent form:


Pe = sol.y[:,-1]
dM = hypot(Pe[0]+0.5, Pe[1])
if dM < 0.05: CAPTURE
if abs(Pe[0]) > 0.75 or abs(Pe[1]) > 1.0: ESCAPE
else: STALL


Missing gates:


- no all-finite check on sol.y or Pe;
- no requirement sol.success is true with an acceptable status;
- no event-driven capture or escape termination;
- no separate SOLVER_FAIL or NUMERICAL_INVALID state;
- no fixture preservation sufficient to replay every reported STALL;
- no independent integrator agreement before labeling a finite unresolved orbit STALL.


NaN comparisons are false under NumPy/Python semantics, so a non-finite solution deterministically falls through to STALL.


3. ADVERSARIAL REPLAY BATTERY A


A reduced deterministic replay used the published builder, field, seed, r=0.025, 30 non-q jet draws, and an 18-point q grid. It produced 335 typed trajectories:


- 330 CAPTURE;
- 2 ESCAPE;
- 3 STALL under the original classifier.


All 3 original STALL outcomes had Pe=(NaN,NaN), dM=NaN, while solve_ivp reported that it reached the end of the interval after 24,654 to 33,717 evaluations.


The same three fixtures were rerun with terminal capture and chart-boundary events, finite-state validation, max_step=0.2, and three solver families: LSODA, DOP853, and Radau. All three solvers agreed fixture-by-fixture:


- fixture A1: CAPTURE at t≈16.86737, crossing d(M)=0.05;
- fixture A2: ESCAPE at t≈20.17124 through Y=+1;
- fixture A3: ESCAPE at t≈17.07761 through Y=+1.


The original STALL labels were therefore wrong for all three fixtures; one was a censored capture and two were chart escapes.


4. ADVERSARIAL REPLAY BATTERY B


A second independently parameterized reduced replay used 40 non-q jet draws and a 20-point q grid at each r in {0.05,0.025,0.0125}. It evaluated 1,396 typed trajectories. The original classifier produced 11 STALL outcomes.


All 11 had non-finite final states and dM=NaN. Event-driven reruns with LSODA, DOP853, and Radau agreed on every fixture: all 11 crossed a declared chart boundary and were ESCAPE, with event times between approximately 9.262 and 46.712.


These batteries do not estimate the full original 300-by-36 mass. They establish the defect and demonstrate that the named STALL class is contaminated enough that its scientific interpretation must be withdrawn pending a full fixture replay.
