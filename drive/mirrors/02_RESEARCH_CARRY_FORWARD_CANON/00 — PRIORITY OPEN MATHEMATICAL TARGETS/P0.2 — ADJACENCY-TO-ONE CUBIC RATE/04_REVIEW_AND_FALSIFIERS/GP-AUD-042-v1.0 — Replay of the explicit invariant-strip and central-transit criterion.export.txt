## 1. Criterion replayed

    F=|a|/8+r|c31|/48,
    A=(|z|+r|c13|/2)F,
    B=r|c04|F^2/2.


For mu>0,


    C_mu=|w|/2+mu+r|c22|/8+A/mu+B/mu^2,
    W_mu=F/mu.


GP-DER-037 proves that


    s <= -C_mu


makes |Y|<=W_mu forward invariant for the explicit cubic–quartic truncation while |X|<=1/2.


The minimum of C_mu occurs at the unique positive solution of


    mu^3-A mu-2B=0.


The replay first evaluated the optimized strip condition


    s <= -min_mu C_mu.


For central transit, the longitudinal error bound was


    E_X(W)=|a|W/2+|w|W^2/2

Whenever the strip condition was feasible, the largest feasible mu was used, because E_X(F/mu) decreases with mu. For sigma=0.1 the central condition is


    E_X(W_mu)<sigma(1-sigma)=0.09,


which gives a central half-width 1/2-sigma=0.4.
