This protocol is designed to find a counterexample, not confirm a preferred exponent. 4. FAIL-CLOSED OUTCOME SCHEMA


Every integration must terminate in exactly one of these classes:
- CAPTURE_CERTIFIED: successful integration and certified entry into the invariant M-ball or another declared convergence certificate;
- ESCAPE_CERTIFIED: successful integration and a declared chart-escape event with finite state;
- STALL_FINITE: successful integration reaches the horizon with all states finite and neither capture nor escape event;
- SOLVER_FAIL: solver success is false, status indicates failure, or no valid terminal solution exists;
- NONFINITE: any state, derivative, event value, or terminal value is NaN or infinite;
- NOT_FLOW_SADDLE: the required saddle/eigen-branch conditions fail;
- DEGENERATE: declared nondegeneracy threshold fails;
- AMBIGUOUS: multiple incompatible events, event bracketing failure, or classification cannot be certified.


SOLVER_FAIL, NONFINITE, and AMBIGUOUS must never be merged into STALL, ESCAPE, or CAPTURE. They are not geometric evidence. All class counts and Palm-weighted mass contributions must be reported separately.

A final-distance threshold without an event or invariant-region certificate is only a diagnostic and must not be labeled eventual capture.
