2. Exact optimized-strip quantities

F=|a|/8 + r|c31|/48,

C_*(U;r)=C_{mu_*}(U;r),
W_*(U;r)=W_{mu_*}(U;r).


Optimizer source, tolerances, residual, bracketing interval, and any fallback must be included in every executable receipt.
