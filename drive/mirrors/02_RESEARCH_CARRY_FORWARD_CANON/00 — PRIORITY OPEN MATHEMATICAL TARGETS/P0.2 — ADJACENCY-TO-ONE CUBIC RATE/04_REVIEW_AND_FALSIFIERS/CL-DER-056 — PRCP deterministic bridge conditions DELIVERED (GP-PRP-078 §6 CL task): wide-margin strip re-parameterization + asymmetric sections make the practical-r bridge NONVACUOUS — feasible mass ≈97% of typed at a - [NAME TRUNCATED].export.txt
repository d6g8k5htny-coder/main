1. Two design results (the "weakest assumptions" answer)
D1 — Wide-margin strip re-parameterization. S_r fixes only s ≤ −C_(U;r). But the GP-DER-037 strip lemma holds at EVERY μ with C_μ(U;r) ≤ |s|, at level W_μ = F/μ with damping rate μ (proof: sup over the strip of ∂_YẎ ≤ s + |w|/2 + r|c22|/8 + A/μ + B/μ² = s + C_μ − μ ≤ −μ; boundary flux Ẏ(X,±W) then telescopes to ≤ 0 via |Ẏ(X,0)| ≤ F = μW — same chains as CL-AUD-049 §§3.1–3.2, now parametric in μ). Since typed draws have enormous s-margin (|s| = |q|/r ~ b/r ≫ C_ ~ O(1)), define μ_big := the largest positive root of μ³ + (β−|s|)μ² + Aμ + B = 0, β = |w|/2 + r|c22|/8 (equivalent to C_μ = |s|), giving the narrowest admissible strip W_bridge = F/μ_big ≈ F·r/|q|. The event S_r is UNCHANGED; only the bridge geometry uses the margin. Median W_bridge at the rungs: 0.0048, 0.0024, 0.0012, 0.0006 — versus optimized-μ widths that block ball entry. This single change moves feasible mass from ≈22% to ≈83% of typed weight.


D2 — Asymmetric transit sections (named variant T_r^asym per GP-REG-079 §2). Split the single σ into σ_S (cone-side entry section, frozen 0.1 or 0.05) and σ_M (M-side section co-designed with the capture ball: σ_M = min(0.1, 0.7ρ)). Transit needs E_X(W_bridge) < min(σ_S(1−σ_S), σ_M(1−σ_M)); ball entry needs √(σ_M²+W_bridge²) < ρ. Decoupling the two sides removes the artificial requirement that the entry section fit inside the capture ball.
2. The bridge conditions P1–P9 (all constants explicit, none inherits rR⁵ ≤ ε₀)
With J_S/J_M the normal-form endpoint Hessians (gradient field ⟹ symmetric), G := A_S−D_S, and Λ(U,r,y) the explicit second-derivative budget (Λ_X = 2+r(|c40|/2+|c31|y) + 2(|a|+r(|c31|/2+|c22|y)) + (|w|+r(|c22|/2+|c13|y)); Λ_Y analogous; both r-linear, s-free — the huge |s| never enters the remainder):


* P1 (= S_r): s ≤ −C_*.
* P2 transit: E_X(W_bridge) < min(σ_S(1−σ_S), σ_M(1−σ_M)).
* P3 cone expansion: c1 = A_S − κ|B_S| − ε√(1+κ²) > 0, ε = ½Λ(·, κσ_S)(1+κ²)σ_S.
* P4 cone invariance: c2 = κG − |B_S|(1+κ²) − ε(1+κ)√(1+κ²) > 0. (Only c1, c2 > 0 — the 047 thresholds 7/8 and 511R are rate constants, NOT needed for reachability; this is the constant reduction GP asked about.)
* P5 germ in cone: |B_S|/G < κ (exact, from CL-AUD-049 §3.7's identity slope ≤ |B|/G).
* P6 handoff: κσ_S ≤ W_bridge. ⟹ feasible κ-interval = (|B_S|/G, W_bridge/σ_S]; necessary condition |B_S|σ_S < W_bridge·G.
* P7 sink: D′>A′ and λ := A′ − B′²/(D′−A′) > 0 (exact lower bound for λ_min(−J_M)).
* P8 ball: ρ = min(0.3, λ/(2C_M)), C_M = ½Λ(·,ρ) (self-consistent iteration) — gives forward invariance, exponential contraction at rate ≥ λ/2, and no other equilibrium in B(M,ρ) (|J_M d| ≥ λ|d| > C_M|d|² — discharges H3's local uniqueness).
* P9 entry: √(σ_M² + W_bridge²) < ρ.
