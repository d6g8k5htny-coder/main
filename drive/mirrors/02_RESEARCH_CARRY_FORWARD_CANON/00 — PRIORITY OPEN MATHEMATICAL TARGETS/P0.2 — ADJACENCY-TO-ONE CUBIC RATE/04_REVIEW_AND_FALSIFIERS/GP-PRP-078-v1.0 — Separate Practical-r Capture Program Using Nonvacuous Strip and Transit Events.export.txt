1. Separation rule


GP-DER-047 remains an ultra-small-r asymptotic certificate on REG_r={rR(U)^5<=epsilon_0} with s<=-C_cap. It will not be weakened or retuned to create apparent coverage at the four numerical rungs.


This proposal creates a distinct object:


PRACTICAL-r CAPTURE PROGRAM (PRCP)


Its purpose is to study nonvacuous sufficient events for branch capture at declared finite r values, initially r in {0.05,0.025,0.0125,0.00625}. Any theorem, certificate, probability, or diagnostic produced here must carry the PRCP label and may not be cited as the GP-DER-047 event or proof.


2. Candidate event hierarchy


Let C_mu(U;r) be the explicit invariant-strip threshold at parameter mu and C_*(U;r)=inf_{mu>0} C_mu(U;r), with the optimizer and admissible mu-domain declared.


Define candidate events under the exact typed pair-Palm law P_r^MS:


S_r := {s <= -C_*(U;r)}


T_r := S_r ∩ {the selected exact branch reaches the certified central-transit section while remaining in the exact-field strip}


H_r := T_r ∩ {the selected exact branch enters a verified forward-invariant neighborhood of M}


A_r remains the exact global adjacency event. Therefore H_r⊆A_r is the desired deterministic implication. S_r and T_r are not adjacency definitions and certificate failure is not A_r complement.


Alternative event variants may be proposed, but they must be named separately and related by proved set inclusions.


3. Initial research questions


Q1. What is the weakest deterministic condition, beyond optimized strip invariance, that guarantees the selected branch reaches the central-transit section at the registered rungs?


Q2. Can a local cone/handoff condition be formulated with constants that are nonvacuous on a positive-mass exact-law set without inheriting rR^5<=epsilon_0?


Q3. What exact probability scales do P_r^MS(S_r^c), P_r^MS(T_r^c), and P_r^MS(H_r^c) exhibit over the finite rung schedule? These are finite-r diagnostics, not asymptotic theorem constants unless separately proved.


Q4. Which failures are caused by strip nonentry, strip escape, insufficient longitudinal transit, branch-offset ambiguity, exact-field remainder, or genuinely nonlocal geometry?


Q5. Is there an analytically tractable intermediate event between S_r and H_r whose complement has the expected r^3 behavior and whose deterministic implication is uniform over an exact-law high-probability set?


4. Prohibited shortcuts


The following are forbidden:
- reusing REG_r/C_cap while describing the event as accessible-rung;

8. Success and kill criteria


Component success requires a nonvacuous exact-law event at one or more registered rungs, a verified deterministic implication to exact-field capture, full failure accounting, and independent execution.


The program must be killed or redesigned if:
- the candidate event has negligible or zero exact-law mass at every rung;
- event identity changes under implementation details;

- the deterministic implication requires assumptions equivalent to the original vacuous REG_r gate;
