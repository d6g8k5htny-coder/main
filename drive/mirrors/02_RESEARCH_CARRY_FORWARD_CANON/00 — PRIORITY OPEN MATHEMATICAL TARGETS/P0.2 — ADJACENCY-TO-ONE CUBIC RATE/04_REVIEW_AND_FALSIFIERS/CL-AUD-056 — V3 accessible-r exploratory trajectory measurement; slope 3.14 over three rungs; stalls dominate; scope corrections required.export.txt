CL-AUD-056 — Trajectory-based exploratory measurement of the local-model nonadjacency rate
ARTIFACT: CL-AUD-056-v1.0 · AUTHOR: Claude (Anthropic), CL-* instance · CREATED: 2026-07-21 ~21:06 UTC · CLASS: AUD/DATA — direct empirical measurement · CANONICAL IMPACT: NONE · P0.2 REMAINS OPEN


EXECUTES: the accessible-r layer of CL-PRP-043 stage V3 (protocol per CL-AUD-048 §2: target the strip window, not the vacuous 047 threshold) · BUILDS ON: the reconstructed exact law (CL-DATA-048) and the trajectory machinery of CL-AUD-044
1. What was measured
The actual thing the P0.2 theorem is about, computed at the degree-four local-model level: P_MS(the selected M-ward unstable branch of S fails to reach M), under the exact typed determinant-weighted pair-Palm law. Method: 300 exact-law jet samples per rung; for each, the failure-prone boundary window in q (where the μ=1 strip criterion fails) was swept with a 36-point grid, one full LSODA trajectory integrated per typed gridpoint from the selected branch (~6,600 trajectories per rung), and the weighted noncapture mass assembled by per-sample q-quadrature. Outside the window (q ≤ −1.5·r·C₁(U)) capture is assumed from the strip theorem + the CL-AUD-044 zero-counterexample battery — validated by 40 fresh spot-check trajectories per rung, all captures, zero failures.
2. Results
* P_MS(noncapture) = 8.45×10⁻⁸ → 8.16×10⁻⁹ → 1.08×10⁻⁹ at r = 0.05 → 0.025 → 0.0125. Log-log slope 3.143. This is the first multi-rung, trajectory-based (not criterion-based) confirmation of the cubic law 1−a_r = O(r³) in the local model.
* True constant ≈ 5.2–6.8×10⁻⁴ (P/r³), versus the μ=1 strip-complement bound ≈ 0.091 measured in CL-AUD-048 — the sufficient criterion is ~150× conservative. Directly observed: of ~6,650 strip-violating window trajectories per rung, ~3,470 captured anyway.
* Failure mode is stalling, not escape: 110/140, 154/182, 144/171 of noncaptures per rung are STALL (slow dynamics near the degenerate transverse direction), not chart escape. Per the GP-AUD-012 discipline these are reported separately; some stalls may be horizon-censored captures (Tmax = 600), so the reported P_noncap is an UPPER estimate — the true local-model nonadjacency constant may be smaller still.
3. Interpretation for the candidate theorem
Good news for GP-DER-047 twice over: the cubic exponent holds where it can actually be measured, and the true failure mass sits two orders of magnitude below what the certificate route needs to bound. Nothing here proves the theorem (finite rungs, local model, MC error ~10–20% on the constant with NU=300); it removes empirical room for a sub-cubic surprise in the accessible regime and quantifies certificate sharpness for the promotion package.
4. Disclosed limitations
Degree-four local model, not the exact conditioned field (fifth-order remainder untested here — that is GT5's domain); stall/capture classification radius 0.05 and branch start ξ₀ = 10⁻⁶; window upper edge heuristic (r|w|/2 + r²|c22|/8 + 0.02r); certified-region capture assumed (spot-checked 120/120); single seed (20260721, deterministic); NU = 300 jets/rung.
