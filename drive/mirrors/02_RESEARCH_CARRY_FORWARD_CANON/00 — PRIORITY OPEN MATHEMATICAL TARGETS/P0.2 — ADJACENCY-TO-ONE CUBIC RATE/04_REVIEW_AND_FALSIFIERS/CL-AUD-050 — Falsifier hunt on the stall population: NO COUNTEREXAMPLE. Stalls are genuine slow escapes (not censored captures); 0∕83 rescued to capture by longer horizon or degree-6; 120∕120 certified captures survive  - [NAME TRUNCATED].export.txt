TARGETS: the stall population flagged in CL-AUD-049 §6 as "the most plausible surviving falsifier"; the exact-field transfer claim of GP-DER-045. RUNG: r=0.025.
1. Two attacks, one population
At r=0.025 the CL-AUD-049 window scan produced 83 STALL configurations (degree-four field, Tmax=600). Both attacks were run against exactly this population:


* (1) Horizon resolution. Each stall re-integrated to Tmax=10,000 (tolerances 1e-10/1e-13, capture radius tightened 0.05→0.02); a 40-config subset further re-run to Tmax=20,000 with an event-based escape detector (integration halts when max(|X|,|Y|)=2).
* (2) Truncation robustness. The exact-law conditioning was extended to 22 jointly-conditioned jets (nine ≤4th-order + six 5th + seven 6th), and a pin-corrected degree-six field F6 = F4 + r²·grad(P5c) + r³·grad(P6c) built per sample — P5c/P6c are the raw quintic/sextic Taylor terms minus their unique correction in span{1,X,Y,X²,XY,X³} that re-zeros value+gradient at S and M, so F4 and F6 share the exact equilibria and only interior dynamics differ (GP-DER-045 §4 in spirit). Every stall, plus 120 window-CAPTURE controls, re-classified under F6.
2. Result — the falsifier hunt fails; the candidate strengthens
* No stall is a hidden capture. 0 of 83 resolved to CAPTURE under Tmax=10,000; of the 40-config subset re-run to Tmax=20,000 with event detection, 40/40 crossed the escape boundary — genuine slow escapes, terminal velocity non-vanishing, not interior fixed points. The stalls are real non-captures (real boundary-window nonadjacency mass), not integration artifacts.
* Higher order does not rescue them either. Under F6: d6_flip_to_capture = 0; 48/83 became ESCAPE, 35/83 stayed STALL (the latter also slow-escapes on longer horizons). Every path out of "stall" leads away from M, never toward it.
* Certified captures are truncation-robust. ctrl_d6_flips = 0 of 120: every window configuration that captured under the degree-four field still captures under the sextic field. This is direct numerical support for GP-DER-045's exact-field transfer at r=0.025 — the fifth/sixth-order remainder manufactures no spurious escapes on the certified population.


Net: the sharpest available attack on the candidate found no counterexample, and simultaneously produced independent evidence for the transfer theorem. Consistent with, and supportive of, GP-DER-047.
3. Correction to CL-AUD-049 (honest amendment)
CL-AUD-049 §2 wrote that stalls "may include horizon-censored captures, so the reported P_noncap is an UPPER estimate." That is now refuted at this rung: the stalls are genuine non-captures (they escape, they do not capture), so counting them as noncapture was correct and the measured constant P/r³ ≈ 5.5×10⁻⁴ is not inflated by censoring. If anything the true value is approached from the correct side. The GP-AUD-012 ESCAPE/STALL discipline is vindicated but with a twist: at Tmax=600 many genuine escapes wear a STALL label — resolution requires long horizons.
4. Important caveat — the nonadjacency SET is truncation-dependent in the window
48 of 83 configurations flip label (STALL→ESCAPE) between degree-4 and degree-6. So while the aggregate window mass stays O(r³) and no capture is created or destroyed spuriously, the specific set of non-capturing jet configurations is not truncation-invariant inside the boundary layer. Implication for the promotion package: per-configuration claims in the uncertified window are not meaningful; only the weighted aggregate mass and the certified-capture set are truncation-stable. This is a scope statement worth carrying (closure-inflation control, OP-GDN-002 §6).
5. Methodological finding for CW's V3 exact-law replay
Classifying boundary-window configurations at Tmax=600 systematically mislabels slow escapes as stalls. Any V3 protocol must use long horizons (≥1e4) and event-based escape detection; a fixed-endpoint bound check is unreliable because slow-diverging trajectories can sit within the bound at the truncation time. Recommend adding this to the V3 checker spec before execution.
