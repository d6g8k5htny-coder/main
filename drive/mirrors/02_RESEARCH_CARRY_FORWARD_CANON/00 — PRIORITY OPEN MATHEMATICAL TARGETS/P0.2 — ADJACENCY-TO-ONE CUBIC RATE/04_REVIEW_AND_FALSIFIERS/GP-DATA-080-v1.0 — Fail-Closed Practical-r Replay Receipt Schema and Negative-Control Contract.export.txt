This contract defines what an exact-law PRCP replay must record before any result concerning S_r, T_r, H_r, or A_r may be interpreted. It contains no expected probability, slope, or preferred conclusion.

5. Denominator rules


The denominator for P_r^MS probabilities is the total determinant-weighted typed mass under the exact governing law.


The following must remain in the denominator when typed:
- optimizer failures;
- unresolved branches;
- solver failures;
- event nonentry;
- strip exits;
- transit failures;
- capture-block failures;
- endpoint ambiguity;
- perturbation instability.


A failure may block a claim or classify an event as false/unadjudicated according to the frozen event definition, but it may not be silently removed.


Any importance-sampling, rejection-sampling, truncation, or conditioning step must provide the exact Radon-Nikodym weight or an explicit statement that the output is diagnostic only.

7. Mandatory replay sensitivities


A claim-eligible implementation must rerun or analytically check:


- independent mu optimizer or root solver;
- tighter optimizer tolerance;
- branch offset factors at preregistered values;
- tighter ODE tolerances and smaller max step;
- saddle and maximum localization replay;
- exact field versus any truncated or approximate field;
- exact-torus versus planar approximation, if both are used;
- at least two sigma values unless sigma is analytically fixed;
- source reconstruction from Drive or another independently verifiable source.


Any event membership change must be recorded and classified; it may not be resolved by majority vote among numerical settings.


8. Mandatory negative controls


NC1_OPTIMIZER_SIGN:
Replace s<=-C_* by the wrong sign or negate C_*. The mutation must change event membership on a planted fixture or be detected structurally.


NC2_FIXED_MU_SUBSTITUTION:
Replace mu_* by mu=1 while retaining the optimized event label. The verifier must reject the mislabeled receipt.


NC3_LAW_CONDITIONING:
Insert hidden conditioning on S_r, T_r, H_r, A_r, or successful solver resolution. The verifier must reject the changed denominator/law.


NC4_BRANCH_SWAP:
Launch the opposite unstable branch. The receipt must show the projection sign failure or distinct branch mark.


NC5_AUXILIARY_FIELD:
Integrate a truncated or different field while labeling it exact. The source/object hash gate must fail.


NC6_DROP_FAILURES:
Remove unresolved or failed typed draws before aggregation. Denominator reconciliation must fail.


NC7_FALSE_EVENT_ARRIVAL:
Terminate at max arclength or solver failure, Newton-snap to M, and mark capture. Event-certification gates must reject it.


NC8_EVENT_LAUNDERING:
Treat S_r or scalar transit as T_r/H_r without branch entry, exact transit, or capture-block evidence. Relation gates must reject it.


NC9_FIXED_VS_OPTIMIZED_CONSTANT:
Combine or average fixed-mu and optimized-mu probabilities under one event ID. Event-card hash validation must fail.


NC10_VACUOUS_REGIME_SUBSTITUTION:
Use GP-DER-047 REG_r/C_cap at accessible r while labeling the result PRCP. Object-card and regime gates must reject it.


NC11_ENDPOINT_AMBIGUITY:
Plant a trajectory with perturbation-dependent endpoint. Claim eligibility must fail.


NC12_SOURCE_HASH_MUTATION:
Change one executable byte. Reconstruction or hash verification must fail before execution evidence is accepted.


9. Claim grades


DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY:
Any source, law, event, denominator, failure, or sensitivity gate is missing.


INSTRUMENT_PASS:
Source and controls pass, but no outside execution or exact-field production result exists.


OUTSIDE_REPLAY_PASS:
A nonauthor line reconstructs and executes the exact source and agrees on event memberships and aggregation.


COMPONENT_EVIDENCE:
Exact-law replay, failure accounting, sensitivities, and outside execution pass for the declared finite r schedule. This is still not a theorem.


THEOREM_SUPPORT:
Requires a separate analytic result proving the relevant event inclusion and probability bound. No numerical receipt grants this grade automatically.
