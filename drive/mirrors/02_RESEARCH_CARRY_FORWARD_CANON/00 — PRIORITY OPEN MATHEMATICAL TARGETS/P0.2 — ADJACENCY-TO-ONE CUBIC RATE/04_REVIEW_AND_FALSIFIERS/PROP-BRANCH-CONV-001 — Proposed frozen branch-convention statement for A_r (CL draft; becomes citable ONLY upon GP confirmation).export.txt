PROPOSED FROZEN TEXT (for GP confirmation)
BRANCH-CONVENTION-001 (normalized coordinates; S=(1/2,0), M=(−1/2,0); scaled field F_r as in GP-DER-045 §3):


1. At the exact saddle S, the Jacobian DF_r(S) is symmetric with one positive eigenvalue λ_u and one negative eigenvalue λ_s. The unstable eigenspace is one-dimensional; fix the unit unstable eigenvector e_u by the orientation (e_u)_X < 0 (the "M-ward" or "leftward" orientation). This orientation is well-defined whenever (e_u)_X ≠ 0, which holds under the certificate hypotheses (GP-DER-045 §6: ν = −(e_u)_X ≥ ν₀ − θ_S > 0).


2. The SELECTED UNSTABLE BRANCH is the unique maximal integral curve Γ of F_r that is forward-asymptotic from S along +e_u: Γ(t) → S as t → −∞ with (Γ(t) − S)/|Γ(t) − S| → +e_u. Equivalently, Γ is the M-ward ascending separatrix of S for the potential P_r (ascending: P_r increases along Γ leaving the saddle toward the maximum).


3. THE EVENT A_r is: Γ is defined for all forward time and Γ(t) → M as t → +∞. (Termination at the exact conditioned maximum M; convergence to any other limit set, escape from the torus chart structure, or nonconvergence all place the configuration in A_r^c.)


4. MEASURABILITY: A_r is determined by the conditioned field f through the continuous dependence of (S, M, DF_r(S), e_u, Γ) on f in C² on a neighborhood of the chart; A_r is a measurable event under P_r^MS. (The P0.1 card's measurability requirement; a one-line proof from continuity should accompany GP's confirmation.)


5. SYMMETRY NOTE: under the y → −y reflection symmetry of the conditioning (all transverse pin values are 0), the convention selects the same branch, since e_u at S lies in the y=0 axis exactly when the off-diagonal Jacobian entry B=0; for B≠0 the two reflected configurations are distinct fields and each carries its own selected branch. No arbitrary tie-break is introduced.
