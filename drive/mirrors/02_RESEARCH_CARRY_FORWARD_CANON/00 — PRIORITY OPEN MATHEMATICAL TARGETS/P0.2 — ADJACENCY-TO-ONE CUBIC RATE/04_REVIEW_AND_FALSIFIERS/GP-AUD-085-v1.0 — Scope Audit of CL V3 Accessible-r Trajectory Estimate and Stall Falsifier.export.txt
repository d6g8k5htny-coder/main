3. What the program actually computes


For each r in {0.05,0.025,0.0125}, the program:
- draws NU=300 samples of the eight non-q jets U=(a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04) from a float64 confluent-frame planar/local conditional Gaussian builder;
- evaluates q only in a heuristic failure window using nq=36 grid points;
- defines the lower window edge as q_low=-1.5 r C_1(U);
- defines the upper edge as q_high=r|w|/2+r^2|c22|/8+0.02r;
- integrates the degree-four polynomial flow with LSODA to Tmax=600 from branch offset xi0=10^-6;
- classifies CAPTURE when final distance to M is below 0.05, ESCAPE when the chart bounds are crossed, and every remaining trajectory as STALL;
- counts ESCAPE, STALL, NOT_FLOW_SADDLE, and DEGENERATE as noncapture;
- assumes capture outside the lower window and checks only 40 such trajectories per rung;
- estimates the Palm-weighted numerator by q quadrature and divides by a separate 400-point q-grid denominator estimate.


This is neither a direct all-q trajectory integral nor an exact conditioned-field integration. It is a window-assisted local-model estimate with an analytically motivated outside-window assumption.

5. Material scope corrections


A. Window dependence.
The numerator is integrated only over a heuristic q window. Capture outside q_low is assumed from a sufficient criterion and spot-checked 120 times total. Therefore the estimate is not criterion-free. Any unmeasured failure mass outside the window must be bounded or directly integrated before the estimate is failure-complete.


B. Stall censoring.
The majority of counted failures are STALL at Tmax=600. A stall may be a genuine noncapture, a near-degenerate orbit requiring longer time, a solver/classification artifact, or a horizon-censored capture. Counting all stalls as failures makes the reported mass an upper estimate under the current classification.


C. Capture-radius dependence.
CAPTURE is defined only by final distance <0.05. No verified Lyapunov block or sensitivity sweep establishes that this radius corresponds uniformly to eventual convergence, nor that trajectories just outside it are failures.


D. Initial-offset dependence.
The selected branch is initiated at xi0=10^-6. No xi0 convergence study is supplied.


E. Quadrature dependence.
The numerator uses 36 q points and the denominator 400. No 72/144 or 800/1600 convergence check is provided. The q_high formula is heuristic and not accompanied by an omitted-tail bound.


F. Statistical uncertainty.
The run uses one seed and NU=300 jets per rung. No bootstrap, between-seed variation, or confidence interval is included. The reported approximate 10–20% error is qualitative rather than receipt-derived.


G. Model scope.
The flow is the degree-four local model. Fifth-order remainder, exact conditioned field, exact global branch, local-to-global adjacency, and Theorem-B configured-pair transfer remain outside the computation.


H. Exact-torus wording.
The source’s Gaussian builder uses the planar/confluent float64 route. Side-24 torus corrections are negligible at ordinary precision but are not literally evaluated by this V3 source. The report must state planar/confluent local-law sampling rather than exact-torus execution.


6. Strong result retained


The computation materially advances V3 in three ways:
- it supplies the first multi-rung trajectory-assisted estimate of local-model noncapture mass near the failure boundary;
- the measured mass is compatible with an r^3 law across the three accessible rungs;
- it localizes the sharpest surviving adversarial target: slow/stalled trajectories near transverse degeneracy, not obvious chart escapes.


The roughly 150-fold difference between the mu=1 strip-complement mass and this estimated noncapture upper mass is evidence that the sufficient strip criterion is conservative. It is not yet a certified sharpness ratio because numerator completeness and stall classification remain open.
