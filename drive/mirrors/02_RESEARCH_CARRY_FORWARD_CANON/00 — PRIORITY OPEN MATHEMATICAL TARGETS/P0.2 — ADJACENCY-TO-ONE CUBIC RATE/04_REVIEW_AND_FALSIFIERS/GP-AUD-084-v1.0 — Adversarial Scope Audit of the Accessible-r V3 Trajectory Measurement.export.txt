1. Verdict


AGREES WITH AMENDMENT.


The observed three-rung trajectory data are meaningful exploratory evidence consistent with an r^3 local-model noncapture law. They are not sufficient to establish the exact P0.2 theorem, an exact-conditioned-field replay, or a literal exact-torus result. The strongest new scientific signal is not the fitted exponent itself but the concentration of unresolved outcomes in the STALL class, which identifies a concrete adversarial target for the next V3 pass.


2. What the source actually implements


The raw source was read line by line. It implements:
- a planar Bargmann–Fock kernel k(t)=exp(-t^2/2) through Hermite derivatives, without periodized side-24 image terms;
- a confluent-frame float64 conditional Gaussian builder;
- 300 sampled non-q jet vectors per rung at r=0.05, 0.025, and 0.0125;
- a 400-point q grid for the determinant-weighted denominator;
- a 36-point q grid on a heuristic failure window for the numerator;
- trapezoidal quadrature;
- LSODA trajectories with Tmax=600, rtol=1e-9, atol=1e-12, max_step=2;
- capture declared at terminal distance below 0.05 from M;
- escape declared outside a fixed chart box;
- every other unresolved path classified STALL;
- outside-window capture assumed below q=-1.5 r C1(U), with 40 spot checks per rung.


These implementation details are valid for an exploratory diagnostic but must accompany every citation of the numerical slope or constant.


3. Positive evidence retained


The reported data are internally coherent:
- noncapture estimates decrease across all three halving rungs;
- the fitted log-log slope is approximately 3.143;
- P/r^3 lies near 5.2e-4 to 6.8e-4;
- the sufficient strip-complement bound is far more conservative than the trajectory estimate;
- 120 deep-certified-region spot checks report zero failures;
- many strip-criterion violations still capture, confirming the sufficient criterion is not necessary;
- STALL outcomes dominate the unresolved population rather than chart escapes.


This is a useful component-level attack on practical-r behavior and supports continued investigation of cubic scaling.
