1. μ* optimizer — unique, bracketed, residual-checkable (P0) [PROVEN]
C_μ = |w|/2 + μ + r|c22|/8 + A/μ + B/μ², W_μ = F/μ, with F = |a|/8 + r|c31|/48, A = (|z|+r|c13|/2)F, B = r|c04|F²/2 (GP-REG-079 §2). dC/dμ=0 gives the frozen cubic μ³ − Aμ − 2B = 0.


Claim. For A,B ≥ 0 not both zero the positive root μ* is unique and lies in [ max(√A,(2B)^{1/3}), √A+(2B)^{1/3} ].


Proof. g(μ)=μ³−Aμ−2B: g(0)=−2B≤0, g(∞)=∞, g′=3μ²−A has a single positive zero ⇒ exactly one positive root. With u=√A, v=(2B)^{1/3}: g(u)=−2B≤0 and g(v)=−Av≤0 ⇒ μ≥max(u,v); g(u+v)=2u²v+3uv²=uv(2u+3v)≥0 ⇒ μ≤u+v. ∎ The upper end u+v is EC-012's explicit μ̂, so the design inherits EC-012's terminal-closed majorant. Degenerate A=B=0: use the EC-012 A=B=0 bypass C_=|w|/2+r|c22|/8; declare this branch; substituting μ=1 is negative control NC2. Bisection to |g(μ)|≤10⁻¹² is reproducible; residual is a mandatory receipt field (GP-DATA-080 §3).


Reducible-constant answer (GP-PRP-078 §6 CL sub-task). 3. Weakest S_r → T_r chain (P1–P4, T5)
T_r is NOT S_r + a scalar inequality (GP-REG-079 §4): it needs exact-branch ENTRY through an S-side section and transit inside the strip. Weakest sufficient package:


P1 — cone contains the unstable germ. Unstable-eigenvector slope bound σ_u=|B_S|/(A_S−D_S) (CL-AUD-049 §3.7; A_S−D_S>0 under S_r since D_S≤−C_*+w/2+rc22/8<0<A_S). Require design cone half-aperture κ_p ≥ 2σ_u (nonzero-measure cone, not a knife edge).


P2 — cone forward-invariance under the degree-four remainder. With ξ=1/2−X, on the cone {0≤ξ≤σ, |Y|≤κ_pξ} require κ_p·(C_+1−σ−G_X) ≥ G_Y, where G_X,G_Y are explicit remainder envelopes (polynomials in κ_p,σ,|jets|,r; listed in CL-DATA-059 §A). κ_p = least fixed point of κ↦max(2σ_u, G_Y(κ)/(C_+1−σ−G_X(κ))), existing while the denominator stays positive (S_r + σ<1/2 guarantees this on the §6 admissible set).


P3 — handoff into half-strip (binding constraint). At cone exit ξ=σ, |Y|≤κ_p·σ; require κ_p·σ ≤ W_/2. Practical-r analogue of CL-AUD-049 §3.11's exact κσ=W/4, with optimized W_ and design κ_p. This is the binding edge (CL-RSP-050): the cone must be narrow enough that its exit lands inside half the invariant strip.


P4 — no premature longitudinal stall. G_X < 1−σ, so the M-ward drift stays negative across the cone and the branch reaches ξ=σ rather than turning.


T5 — central transit (GP-REG-079 §4). E_X(W_) < σ(1−σ), E_X(W)=|a|W/2+|w|W²/2+r[|c40|/(72√3)+|c31|W/12+|c22|W²/4+|c13|W³/6] — the exact-field transit allowance CL-AUD-049 §3.3–3.4 bounded, at W_.


If P1–P4, T5 hold on the same exact branch: the germ enters the S-side section, stays in |Y|≤W_* (S_r invariance), and reaches the central-transit section X=−1/2+σ in the M-ward direction — i.e. T_r holds. Entry + exact-field transit are inside the event, per GP-REG-079 §4; no scalar proxy is substituted.
4. Weakest T_r → H_r capture (P5–P6) [structure PROVEN, CL-AUD-049 §3.9–3.11]

With ρ_p = 2√(σ²+W_²) require λ_min(−J_M) > ρ_p·N2, where N2 = 2+2|a|+2|w|+|z|+r(2|c40|+3|c31|+3|c22|+3|c13|+|c04|) bounds the Hessian variation over B(M,ρ_p).
