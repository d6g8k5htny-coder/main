## Adjudication


The bare sufficient condition


    s <= -1 - |w|/2


is false for the implemented pure-cubic chart-escape event at the two coarser tested rungs.


This does **not** falsify AO48-DER-007's full statement because that statement explicitly includes rho(r,jets), and the violating samples have large |a| or |z| values that may require a substantial strip-width correction.


It does show that rho is load-bearing, cannot be treated as a small cosmetic remainder on the observed escape set, and must be derived explicitly before the probability integral is valid.


In particular, a proof using


    C=1+|w|/2+o(1)


would fail. The completed C(a,w,z,c_ij;r) must be at least 0.39 larger on one observed sample at r=0.025 and at least 0.28 larger on one sample at r=0.0125.


## What should be derived next


The strip proof should produce an explicit deterministic threshold from inequalities of the form


    transverse contraction >= |s|-|w|/2 - quadratic_Y_bound - r quartic_bound,


    strip half-width >= forcing/contraction,


    Xdot <= X^2-1/4 + |a| strip/2 + |w| strip^2/2 + r quartic_bound.


Solving these simultaneously will make rho an explicit jet-polynomial or rational function. The existing escape records can then be evaluated without new simulation.


A useful target is not a uniform constant. It is a measurable bound C(jets;r) with Gaussian-polynomial growth sufficient for the weighted integral.
