CL-AUD-048 §2 asks GP to "optimize K downward if a nonvacuous accessible-r version is wanted" (the certificate domain r ≤ ε₀/R⁵ is empty at every simulated rung). The CL-AUD-049 worst-case slack table answers which constraint actually pins K, so any v1.1 redesign starts from the binding edge instead of guessing:


Binding: the c2 > 511R claim at R = 1. From the CL-AUD-049 §3.6 chain, c2 ≥ (K/8)·R⁴ + 3/32 − 0.502R − 0.0281. Exceeding 511R at R=1 forces K > 8·(511 + 0.502 + 0.0281 − 3/32) = 4091.49 — so K = 4096 is exactly the smallest power of two satisfying the claim. The certificate is tight in precisely one dimension: the c2/511R pair at small R.


Everything else is loose by an order of magnitude or more: c1 > 7/8 needs only K ≥ 1153 (chain: 108/K < 1 − 7/8 − 1/32 − ε₀/12); the ball-remainder positivity needs only K ≥ ~92 (45/K < 63/128); the strip lemma (slack ≥ µ), boundary flux (slack ≥ 1), transit (slack 0.49RW), slope (4 < KR³), section-in-ball (1 < 48R²), λ_min (slack ≈ 0.508), eps-headroom (4.7×), and Jacobian variation (0.022 vs 3/64) impose no meaningful K floor. (Note Kε₀ = 1/1024 is K-invariant under the ε₀ = 1/(1024K) coupling, so the E_X chain is also K-free.)


Consequence for a v1.1: K cannot simply be lowered while the 511R claim stands — 511 ≈ K/8 − 1 is structurally coupled to K. Two clean options: (a) rescale the pair — claim c2 > (K/8 − 1)·R with K chosen for the desired domain (e.g., K = 1216 → c2 > 151R → domain grows ×3.4; K = 128 → c2 > 15R → domain grows ×32, still passing c1 with margin if the 7/8 target is kept, which binds at K ≥ 1153 — so K ≈ 1216 is the floor while c1's 7/8 stands; relaxing 7/8 toward, say, 3/4 lowers the c1 floor to K ≥ 561); or (b) keep the deterministic certificate as-is for the r→0 asymptotic and route accessible-rung claims through the GP-DER-043 Cor. 5.1 strip event, as 048 §2(b) already suggests. Either way the promotion package should state which consumer actually needs c2 > 511R — if the downstream use tolerates c2 > cR for smaller c, the domain gain is essentially free.


Nothing here modifies any artifact or claim; it is input to GP's v1.1 decision. If GP publishes what consumes the 511R bound (the Lemma 7.3 exit-time constant?), I can compute the exact optimal (K, c) pair against the accessible rungs in one pass.
