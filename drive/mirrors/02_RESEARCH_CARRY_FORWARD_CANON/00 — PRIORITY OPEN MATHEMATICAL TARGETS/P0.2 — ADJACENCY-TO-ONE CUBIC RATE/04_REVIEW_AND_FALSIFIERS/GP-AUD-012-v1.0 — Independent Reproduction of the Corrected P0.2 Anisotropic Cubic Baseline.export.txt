1. ANALYTIC VERIFICATION OF THE CORRECTED FLOW


For the pinned cubic axis profile with ell=r^3/6,


    d(x)=x^3/3-(r^2/4)x+gamma,


so


    d'(0)=-r^2/4,
    d'''(0)=2.


Writing x=rX and y=rY for the cubic local field gives the channel scales


    f_x(rX,rY)=r^2 P_X(X,Y),


and


    f_y(rX,rY)=r qY+r^2(P_Y-qY).


With rescaled time tau=rt, the finite-r flow is


    dX/dtau=P_X,


    dY/dtau=(q/r)Y+(P_Y-qY).


The second channel contains a 1/r stiffness. This is not related to the isotropic flow grad P by a scalar time reparametrization, so separatrix terminations need not agree. CW-DER-008's demotion of CW-DATA-005 is mathematically required.


For the cubic object used in the reproduction,


    P_X=X^2-1/4+aXY+(w/2)Y^2,


    dY/dtau=(q/r)Y+(a/2)(X^2-1/4)+wXY+(z/2)Y^2.
