1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT


The optimized-strip idea and the separation S_r→T_r→H_r are useful. The μ-cubic for A+B>0 is correct, and the intended cone/strip/capture architecture is plausible. The current bridge is nevertheless NOT executable or proved as written. Four load-bearing issues require amendment:


F1. The A=B=0 scalar bypass does not define W_* and can make the optimized strip event ill-defined. The infimum of C_μ may be unattained while W_μ diverges.


F2. The cone-slope formula uses |B_S|/(A_S-D_S) without an explicit A_S-D_S>0 gate or exact-eigenvector fallback. S_r plus endpoint typing does not imply D_S<0<A_S. An exact typed S_r counterexample makes the displayed denominator zero.


F3. The P1–P6 derivation is for the degree-four field, while T_r and H_r in GP-REG-079 are exact-field events. The document sometimes concludes statements about the exact selected branch without including the required C^1 perturbation/remainder gate.


F4. The T_r and P6 section geometry is inconsistent. The object card names X=1/2−σ, while the bridge uses the M-side exit X=−1/2+σ. During central transit the cone bound κ_pσ is not preserved; only |Y|≤W_* is. The capture conclusion can be repaired using W_*, but the current proof text uses the wrong bound at the wrong section.


Additional blocker: CL-DATA-059, cited as the executable definition of G_X and G_Y, is not locatable in Drive by exact title/ID search. P2 and its mutations are therefore CANNOT-VERIFY as an executable object.

2. WHAT PASSES


2.1 Optimizer for A+B>0


For


C_μ=β+μ+A/μ+B/μ²,
β=|w|/2+r|c22|/8,
A=(|z|+r|c13|/2)F,
B=r|c04|F²/2,
F=|a|/8+r|c31|/48,


the stationary equation μ³−Aμ−2B=0 has one positive solution when A,B≥0 and not both zero. The bracket


max(√A,(2B)^(1/3)) ≤ μ_* ≤ √A+(2B)^(1/3)


is valid. This part agrees with CL-DER-058 and EC-012.


2.2 Structural architecture


It is correct to distinguish:


- strip invariance S_r;
- selected-branch entry and central passage T_r;
- verified local capture H_r;
- exact global adjacency A_r.


Failure of S_r, T_r, or H_r is not A_r^c. Denominator failures must remain visible. The fixed-μ and optimized-μ channels must have separate event IDs.


2.3 Capture-ball strategy


For an exact or degree-four field with a verified negative-definite Jacobian at M and a valid Jacobian-variation bound on the full ball, a strict inequality


λ_min(−J_M) > ρ N_2


supports a Lyapunov contraction argument. The architecture is sound if N_2 is proved on the declared ball and the field layer is labeled correctly.


3. F1 — DEGENERATE OPTIMIZER DOES NOT DEFINE W_*


GP-REG-079 defines C_* and W_* through μ_*. It says degenerate cases require a declared limiting rule. CL-DER-058 imports EC-012’s scalar A=B=0 bypass for C_* and then uses W_* in every geometric condition.


This is not enough.


When A=B=0,


C_μ=β+μ,


so inf_{μ>0}C_μ=β, but the infimum is not attained. If F>0,


W_μ=F/μ→∞ as μ↓0.


Therefore the scalar bypass C_*=β does not produce a finite optimized width. S_r={s≤−C_*} may be true even though no μ_* and no W_* exist.


Exact example:


r=0.05,
a=4,
w=9,
z=0,
c40=−480,
c31=c22=c13=c04=0,
s=q/r=−5.5.


Then F=1/2, A=B=0, β=4.5, so the scalar event s≤−C_* holds under the bypass. But μ_* does not exist and W_μ=(1/2)/μ is unbounded as the infimum is approached. The event card cannot evaluate T5, P3, P5, or P6.


The claimed “finite bypass limit” is also path-dependent even with A>0 and B=0. For fixed r and zero quartics/w:


Path I: a=ε, z=ε gives W_*→1/√8.


Path II: a=ε, z=ε³ gives W_*∼1/(√8 ε)→∞.


Path III: a=ε², z=1 gives W_*∼ε/√8→0.


There is no unique limiting W_* as the jets approach the degenerate point.


Required repair:


- Define S_r using an explicit WITNESS μ>0 satisfying s≤−C_μ, rather than the scalar infimum alone; or
- restrict the optimized event to A+B>0 and treat A=B=0 as a separately named event with a chosen μ determined from available threshold slack; and
- retain a fail-closed OPTIMIZER_UNATTAINED class at s=−β or whenever no finite witness is supplied.


The scalar EC-012 closure remains valid. The defect appears only when its nonattained infimum is promoted into a geometric strip width.


4. F2 — MISSING AXIAL ENTRY GATE / INVALID SLOPE BOUND


CL-DER-058 states that under S_r, D_S<0<A_S and uses


σ_u=|B_S|/(A_S−D_S)


as an unstable-eigenvector slope bound.


S_r controls the transverse entry D_S through s. It does not control


A_S=1+rc40/12.


Endpoint typing det J_S<0 also does not imply A_S>0. A symmetric 2×2 saddle can have both diagonal entries negative when the off-diagonal term is large.


Exact counterexample with a nondegenerate optimizer:


r=0.05,
a=4,
w=9,
z=0.01,
c40=−480,
c31=c22=c13=c04=0,
s=−5.5.


Optimized-strip quantities:


F=0.5,
A=0.005,
B=0,
μ_*=√0.005≈0.0707106781,
C_*=4.5+2√0.005≈4.6414213562,
W_*=F/μ_*≈7.0710678119.


Hence S_r holds because −5.5≤−4.6414213562.


Endpoint Jacobians are


J_S=[[-1, 2],[2, -1]],
J_M=[[-3, -2],[-2, -10]].


J_S has eigenvalues −3 and +1, so S is a valid saddle. J_M has eigenvalues approximately −10.5311 and −2.46887, so M is a valid maximum. Both typed gates pass strictly.


But


A_S−D_S=−1−(−1)=0,


so the proposed σ_u formula is undefined. The exact unstable eigenvector has slope magnitude 1 in the (ξ,Y) coordinates.


This disproves the statement that S_r plus typing supplies D_S<0<A_S or the displayed denominator.


Required repair:


- Compute the exact positive-eigenvalue eigenvector and define σ_u from it; or
- require explicit axial gates A_S−D_S>0 and A_S>0 before using the bound;
- if the denominator is nonpositive or too small, classify P1_FAIL rather than divide or silently accept;
- add the counterexample above as a mandatory negative control.


This does not show the admissible set is empty. It shows the proposed P1 formula is not defined on all typed S_r configurations.


5. F3 — DEGREE-FOUR AND EXACT-FIELD EVENTS ARE CONFLATED


CL-DER-058 derives P1–P6 from the displayed degree-four polynomial field. GP-REG-079 defines T_r and H_r using the SAME EXACT SELECTED BRANCH in the SAME EXACT FINITE FIELD, including Taylor/remainder allowance and exact-field capture.


The following statements in CL-DER-058 therefore overreach their proved layer:


- P1–P4 imply that “the exact branch” enters and transits;
- P1–P6 imply H_r⊆A_r;
- the degree-four capture ball is treated as the exact-field capture block.


A correct relation ledger must first define


T_r^(4), H_r^(4)


for the degree-four flow. Then an exact-field perturbation gate must verify, on each fixture or analytically on the event, that the C^1 difference is smaller than every relevant margin:


- saddle type and branch-direction margin;
- cone-boundary inward margin;
- longitudinal drift margin;
- strip-boundary margin;
- M-ball contraction margin;
- event-section separation.


Only after that gate may one infer T_r or H_r for the exact field. A future numerical replay of the degree-four source is not by itself an exact-field transfer proof.


Required repair:


- relabel the current implications as degree-four design implications;
- add an explicit exact-field margin vector and remainder receipt;
- require exact-torus field or rigorous approximation enclosure under GP-DATA-080 / GP-REQ-086-v1.1;
- retain exact-field mismatch as a denominator-visible failure class.


6. F4 — SECTION AND TRANSVERSE-BOUND MISMATCH


GP-REG-079 T4 names the section X=1/2−σ. CL-DER-058’s transit proof and capture handoff use the M-side section X=−1/2+σ. These are different sections. The event card must state whether T_r means:


- arrival at the S-side central boundary;
- passage across the entire central rectangle; or
- arrival at the M-side capture section.


For the intended S-to-M transit, both entry and exit sections should be explicit.


There is also a bound mismatch. P3 yields


|Y|≤κ_pσ


at the S-side cone exit. During central transit, strip invariance preserves only


|Y|≤W_*.


It does not preserve the cone bound κ_pσ. At the M-side exit, the correct distance estimate is


sqrt(σ²+Y²)≤sqrt(σ²+W_*²)=ρ_p/2


when ρ_p=2sqrt(σ²+W_*²).


Thus the P6 conclusion can be repaired and is actually simpler: the full M-side strip section lies in B(M,ρ_p/2) by definition. P3 is needed for entry into the strip, not to bound Y at the M-side exit.


Required repair:


- freeze two section names X_S=1/2−σ and X_M=−1/2+σ;
- define T_r as arrival at X_M after verified passage from X_S;
- use |Y|≤W_* at X_M;
- amend P6 and all receipts accordingly.

8. NONVACUITY CLAIM


The statement “ADM_r is open and nonempty, hence positive mass” is not established by the displayed limit argument.


First, the conditions involve absolute values, eigenvalues, an algebraic optimizer, a least fixed point, and potentially fallback branches. They may define a continuous semialgebraic/open set on a regular stratum, but they are not simply “strict polynomial inequalities” as stated.


Second, the proposed interior path sends all relevant jets toward zero and invokes a finite W_* bypass. Section 3 shows that W_* has no unique limit and may diverge.


A valid nonvacuity proof requires one explicit finite numerical or exact jet point satisfying every defined gate with strict positive margins, followed by continuity on a declared regular stratum. No such complete point is currently displayed because G_X/G_Y and the exact-field gates are unavailable.


Current status: NONVACUITY PLAUSIBLE BUT UNPROVED FOR THE FULL ADM_r OBJECT.
