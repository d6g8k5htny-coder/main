## Executive result


CW-DER-008 is correct that the physical finite-r local flow is anisotropic. In scaled coordinates X=x/r, Y=y/r and time tau=rt,


    dX/dtau = X^2-1/4+aXY+(w/2)Y^2,


    dY/dtau = (q/r)Y+(a/2)(X^2-1/4)+wXY+(z/2)Y^2.


The isotropic flow grad P used in CW-DATA-005 is a different dynamical object and its 1.09e-3 weighted complement is invalid for the target adjacency event.


I independently implemented the corrected anisotropic flow with a Numba-compiled RK4 integrator, used independent seeds, increased the sample size to one million draws per rung, and extended the corrected baseline from one rung to


    r = 0.05, 0.025, 0.0125, 0.00625.


The generic complement scales approximately as r^0.962. The determinant-weighted complement scales approximately as r^2.939. This directly reproduces the predicted two-power suppression over four consecutive rungs in the pure-cubic finite-r diagnostic.


This is strong diagnostic evidence for the Q^2 determinant-weight mechanism. It is not a proof of the exact torus theorem.


## Object card


For each rung I sampled one million independent vectors


    q ~ N(-1.2,2),
    a ~ N(0,2),
    w ~ N(0,2),
    z ~ N(0,6).


The exact finite-r endpoint Hessians were


    H(M)=[[-r,-ar/2],[-ar/2,q-wr/2]],


    H(S)=[[r,ar/2],[ar/2,q+wr/2]].


I retained det H(M)>0 and det H(S)<0 and used


    W=|det H(M) det H(S)|.


The M-ward positive-eigenvalue branch of the corrected flow Jacobian at S was initialized at offset 10^-6.


Classification:


- M-CAPTURE: scaled distance to M below 10^-3;
- ESCAPE: |X|>16;
- STALL: neither outcome by tau=1000.


No transverse O(r^2) corridor-exit condition was used in this corrected run.


To reduce cost without silently assuming the result, I integrated every typed sample with q>-2r and independently checked 3000 typed samples outside that screen at every rung. No outside-screen failure was observed. This screening remains a diagnostic limitation, not an analytic exclusion theorem.


The time step was min(0.005,r/20). A dt/2 run on the first 1000 screened samples at each rung produced zero non-stall classification disagreements.
