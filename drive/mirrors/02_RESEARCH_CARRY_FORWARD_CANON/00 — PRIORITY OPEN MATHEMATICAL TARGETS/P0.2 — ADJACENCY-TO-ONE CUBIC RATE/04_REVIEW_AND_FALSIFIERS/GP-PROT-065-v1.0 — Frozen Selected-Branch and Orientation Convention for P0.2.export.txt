2. Coordinate-free branch orientation


Let H_S be the exact Hessian of the scaled potential at S. On the event that S is a nondegenerate index-one saddle, let e_u be a unit eigenvector for the unique positive eigenvalue of H_S.


Its sign is fixed by the configured pair displacement:
