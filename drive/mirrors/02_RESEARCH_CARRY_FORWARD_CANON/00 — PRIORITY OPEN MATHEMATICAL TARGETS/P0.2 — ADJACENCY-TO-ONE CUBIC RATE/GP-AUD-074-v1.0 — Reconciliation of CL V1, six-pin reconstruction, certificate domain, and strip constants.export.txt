3. CERTIFICATE EFFECTIVE DOMAIN — CONFIRMED
GP-DER-047 defines


REG_r = { r R(U)^5 <= epsilon_0 },
epsilon_0 = 1/(1024 K),
K=4096,
R(U)>=1.


Thus:
- REG_r is empty for r>epsilon_0;
- at r=epsilon_0, membership requires R=1, a null/extreme boundary case incompatible with generic nonzero Gaussian jets;
- every accessible rung r in {0.05,0.025,0.0125,0.00625} lies far outside the REG_r domain;
- for typical R approximately 20–25, the threshold is approximately 1e-13 to 1e-14.


This does not falsify the asymptotic theorem route. As r tends to zero, the allowed jet cutoff (epsilon_0/r)^(1/5) diverges, while Gaussian tails outside the cutoff are super-polynomially small. The proof may therefore be asymptotically valid while having no directly testable certificate membership at current rungs.

Why lowering K is insufficient:
epsilon_0=1/(1024K). To make epsilon_0 exceed the smallest current rung 0.00625 would require K<0.15625. The deterministic inequalities use K on the order of thousands; CL-AUD-049's own worst-case bounds include requirements such as 4<KR^3 and approximately 90/K<3/64. Therefore no simple downward tuning of K within the present proof architecture makes the current rungs nonvacuous.


4. STRIP CONSTANT DISCREPANCY — RESOLVED
CL-AUD-048 reports a fixed-mu event:

B_1={s>-C_1}.


GP-AUD-042 reports the optimized event:


C_*(U;r)=min_{mu>0} C_mu(U;r),
B_*={s>-C_*}.


Since C_*<=C_1,


{s<=-C_1} subset {s<=-C_*},


and therefore


B_* subset B_1.


The optimized certificate is easier to satisfy and must have a smaller or equal complement mass. GP-AUD-042's complement/r^3 values approximately 0.0537–0.0603 and CL's fixed-mu=1 plateau approximately 0.0905–0.0915 are directionally and quantitatively compatible. Their slopes 3.051 and 3.035 agree closely.


There is no constant conflict because the two computations do not parametrize the same event.
