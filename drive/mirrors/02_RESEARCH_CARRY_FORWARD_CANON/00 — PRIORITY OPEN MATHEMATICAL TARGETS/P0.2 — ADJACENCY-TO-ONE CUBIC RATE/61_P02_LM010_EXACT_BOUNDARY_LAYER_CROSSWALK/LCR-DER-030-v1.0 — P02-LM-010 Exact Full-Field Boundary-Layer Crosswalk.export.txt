P02-LM-010 — EXACT FULL-FIELD BOUNDARY-LAYER CROSSWALK ON THE GOOD-REMAINDER EVENT


1. EXACT LAW AND JET COORDINATES


Fix the normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock field on the side-24 torus and b=6/5. For r>0 set


    M=(-r/2,0),
    S=(+r/2,0).


Let Q_{6,r} be the exact field law conditioned on


    (f,f_x,f_y)(M)=(b,0,0),
    (f,f_x,f_y)(S)=(b-r^3/6,0,0).


Let


    J_r=(q,U),


    q=f_yy(0),


    U=(a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04)


be the exact physical midpoint nine-jet under Q_{6,r}, with


    a=f_xxy(0),
    w=f_xyy(0),
    z=f_yyy(0),


    c40=f_xxxx(0),
    c31=f_xxxy(0),
    c22=f_xxyy(0),
    c13=f_xyyy(0),
    c04=f_yyyy(0).


Write


    s=q/r


and


    R(U)=1+|a|+|w|+|z|
          +|c40|+|c31|+|c22|+|c13|+|c04|.


Fix


    K=4096,


    C_cap(U)=2KR(U)^5+2R(U).


2. EXACT TYPED WEIGHT


Let H_M^{exact} and H_S^{exact} be the exact physical endpoint Hessians. Define


    W_r^{exact}
      =|det H_M^{exact} det H_S^{exact}|
       1{H_M^{exact} is negative definite}
       1{H_S^{exact} has index one},


    Z_r^{exact}
      =E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact}],


and


    dP_r^{MS}
      =(W_r^{exact}/Z_r^{exact})dQ_{6,r}.


3. DEGREE-FOUR COMPARISON HESSIANS


The pin-preserving degree-four comparison field with the identical exact midpoint jet has physical endpoint Hessians


    H_M^(4)
      =r [[-1+r c40/12, -a/2+r c31/12],
           [-a/2+r c31/12, s-w/2+r c22/8]],


    H_S^(4)
      =r [[ 1+r c40/12,  a/2+r c31/12],
           [ a/2+r c31/12, s+w/2+r c22/8]].


These matrices are deterministic functions of (r,s,U). They are comparison objects only. The exact typed weight and exact type indicators remain those of Section 2.


4. HYPOTHESES


Assume there exist constants


    r_0 in (0,1],
    K_phi>0,
    c_phi>0,
    c_Z>0,
    C_T<infinity,


independent of r, such that for every 0<r<=r_0:


H1 — EXACT NINE-JET DENSITY ENVELOPE.
The exact joint density phi_r(q,U) of J_r under Q_{6,r} satisfies


    phi_r(q,U)
      <=K_phi exp(-c_phi||U||_2^2)


for every (q,U).


H2 — EXACT NORMALIZER LOWER BOUND.


    Z_r^{exact}>=c_Zr^2.


H3 — ENDPOINT HESSIAN REMAINDER ON THE GOOD EVENT.
Let G_r be a measurable exact-field event. On G_r,


    ||H_M^{exact}-H_M^(4)||_op<=C_Tr^2,


    ||H_S^{exact}-H_S^(4)||_op<=C_Tr^2.


For the intended Taylor application,


    G_r={H_{5,r}(D^+)<=r^{-1}}


and H3 follows from the exact pin-preserving C1 Taylor estimate after converting scaled-Jacobian error to physical-Hessian error.


5. EXACT BOUNDARY EVENT


Define


    B_r
      =G_r
       intersect {s>-C_cap(U)}
       intersect {H_M^{exact} is negative definite}
       intersect {H_S^{exact} has index one}.


This is the good-remainder portion of the exact typed threshold boundary. It is measurable under Q_{6,r}.


6. EXACT MAXIMUM TYPE GIVES A FINITE UPPER ENDPOINT


On B_r, negative definiteness of H_M^{exact} implies


    (H_M^{exact})_22<0.


By H3 and the explicit comparison entry,


    |(H_M^{exact})_22
      -r(s-w/2+r c22/8)|
      <=C_Tr^2.


Therefore


    r(s-w/2+r c22/8)
      <C_Tr^2.


Since r>0,


    s<U_r(U),


where


    U_r(U)=w/2-r c22/8+C_T r.


The correction term is C_T r because the physical yy-entry error is bounded by C_T r^2 and division by the positive factor r produces C_T r.


Consequently every point of B_r satisfies the finite interval condition


    -C_cap(U)<s<U_r(U).


The upper endpoint has uniform polynomial growth:


    |U_r(U)|
      <=C_U(1+||U||_2)


for a constant C_U independent of r.


7. EXACT r^4 WEIGHT FACTORIZATION AND POLYNOMIAL MAJORANT


Define normalized exact Hessians


    Hhat_M^{exact}=H_M^{exact}/r,
    Hhat_S^{exact}=H_S^{exact}/r.


By H3,


    ||Hhat_M^{exact}-H_M^(4)/r||_op<=C_Tr,


    ||Hhat_S^{exact}-H_S^(4)/r||_op<=C_Tr.


For r<=1, every entry of either normalized exact Hessian is bounded by


    C_H(1+|s|+||U||_2)


for a finite constant C_H depending only on C_T and the displayed comparison formulas.


For a real two-by-two matrix A,


    |det A|<=||A||_op^2.


Hence on G_r,


    |det Hhat_M^{exact} det Hhat_S^{exact}|
      <=K_D(1+|s|+||U||_2)^4


for a finite constant K_D independent of r.


Because


    det H_X^{exact}=r^2 det Hhat_X^{exact},


one has exactly


    W_r^{exact}
      <=r^4K_D(1+|s|+||U||_2)^4


on the relevant interval and good event. The exact type indicators only decrease the nonnegative weight.


8. POLYNOMIAL ENDPOINT GROWTH


The lower endpoint satisfies


    C_cap(U)=2KR(U)^5+2R(U)
      <=K_C(1+||U||_2^5)


for some finite K_C.


The upper endpoint satisfies the linear bound in Section 6. Therefore the interval endpoints meet the polynomial-growth hypotheses of P02-LM-003.


9. THEOREM


Assume H1–H3. Then there exists a finite constant K_B, independent of r, such that


    P_r^{MS}(B_r)<=K_Br^3


for every sufficiently small r.


10. PROOF


Apply P02-LM-003 with:


- gamma_r equal to the exact nine-jet marginal under Q_{6,r};
- W_r equal to the exact typed weight W_r^{exact}, viewed by conditional expectation over the remaining exact-field randomness if necessary;
- B_r equal to the event in Section 5;
- lower endpoint C_r(U)=C_cap(U);
- upper endpoint U_r(U) from Section 6;
- density envelope H1;
- normalizer lower bound H2;
- determinant-weight majorant from Section 7.


Equivalently, one may integrate the full exact field by disintegration over J_r. On B_r, the interval and weight inequalities hold pointwise for every full-field realization in the conditional fiber. Taking conditional expectation preserves the inequalities.


P02-LM-003 yields


    P_r^{MS}(B_r)<=K_Br^3.


QED.


11. DISINTEGRATION CLARIFICATION


P02-LM-003 is written for a density in variables (q,U) and a weight measurable in those variables. The exact weight also depends on higher-order field information. The application is justified by regular conditional expectation.


Define


    wbar_r(q,U)
      =E_{Q_{6,r}}
         [W_r^{exact}1_{G_r}
          |J_r=(q,U)].


Then wbar_r is nonnegative and measurable, and


    E[W_r^{exact}1_{B_r}]
      =integral wbar_r(q,U)
         1{s>-C_cap(U)}
         1{typed interval condition}
         phi_r(q,U)dq dU.


The pointwise full-field bound in Section 7 implies the same bound for wbar_r. Thus the exact full-field numerator is legitimately reduced to the finite-dimensional integral without replacing the exact weight by a quartic weight.


12. EDGE CASES AND FIREWALLS


GOOD-EVENT RESTRICTION.
The theorem bounds only the threshold boundary intersected with G_r. The complement G_r^c must be handled separately, for example by P02-LM-007 and P02-LM-008.


EXACT VERSUS QUARTIC TYPE.
The upper endpoint comes from exact maximum type and an exact-to-quartic Hessian error bound. Quartic type is not substituted.


ERROR SCALING.
A scaled-Jacobian error of order C_T r becomes physical-Hessian error C_T r^2. Dividing the physical yy-entry error by r produces the upper-endpoint correction C_T r, not a constant C_T. The exact endpoint is


    w/2-r c22/8+C_T r.


CONDITIONAL WEIGHT.
The proof uses conditional expectation of the exact weight given the nine-jet. It does not assume that the exact weight is nine-jet measurable.


EMPTY INTERVAL.
If U_r(U)<=-C_cap(U), the contributing interval is empty.


NO REG PREMISE.
REG_r is not needed for the boundary-layer integral itself because C_cap has polynomial growth globally. REG_r is needed for the deterministic capture implication and is handled in the final union decomposition.
