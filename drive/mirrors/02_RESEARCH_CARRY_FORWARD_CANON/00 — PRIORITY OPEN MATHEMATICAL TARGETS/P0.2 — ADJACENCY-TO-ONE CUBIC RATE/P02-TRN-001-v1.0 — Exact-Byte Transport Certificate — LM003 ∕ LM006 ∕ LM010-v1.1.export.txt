2. Verification protocol
1. Fetch the stored native text/plain carrier from its exact Drive file ID.
2. Compute the SHA-256 digest of the complete UTF-8 byte sequence without rewriting the file.
3. For the frozen body, normalize CRLF and CR line endings to LF.
4. Locate a line exactly equal to BEGIN_FROZEN_LEMMA_BODY; substring matches in the freeze-policy prose are invalid.
5. Take the text after that marker line and before the first subsequent line exactly equal to END_FROZEN_LEMMA_BODY.
6. Remove trailing LF characters, append exactly one LF, encode as UTF-8 without BOM, and compute SHA-256.
7. Require simultaneous agreement of byte count and digest for both the whole carrier and frozen body.
