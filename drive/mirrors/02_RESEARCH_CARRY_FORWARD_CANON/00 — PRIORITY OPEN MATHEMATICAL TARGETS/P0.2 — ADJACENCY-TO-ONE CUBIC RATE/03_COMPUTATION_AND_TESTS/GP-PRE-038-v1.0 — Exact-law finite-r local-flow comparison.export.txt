# Exact-law finite-r local-flow comparison


## 1. Question


How much of the previously observed r^3-compatible determinant-weighted local failure scale changes when the limiting cubic Gaussian law is replaced, in controlled stages, by the exact normalized-periodized finite-r six-pin Gaussian law and its normalized quartic correction?


This is a local dynamical diagnostic. It is not a theorem, and chart escape is not automatically global nonadjacency.


## 2. Frozen Gaussian objects


Torus side L=24, b=6/5, ell=r^3/6, rungs


    r ∈ {0.05, 0.025, 0.0125, 0.00625}.


At each rung draw N=1,000,000 common nine-dimensional standard-normal vectors with seed


    seed(rung_index)=202607203800+rung_index,


rung_index=0,1,2,3 in decreasing-r order.


The exact finite-r law is GP-DER-034 / GP-DATA-035, source SHA-256


    ec2e83e357cb95aa3ff92bba91aaf3870cc8d91e3f4db2f75bbd3a74f6fe968e.


Three model stages use the same underlying standard-normal vectors:


A. LIMIT-CUBIC
   - coalesced limiting mean/covariance;
   - use q,a,w,z only;
   - quartic layer off.


B. FINITE-CUBIC
   - exact finite-r marginal law of q_r,a_r,w_r,z_r;
   - quartic layer off.


C. FINITE-Q4
   - exact finite-r joint law of q_r,a_r,w_r,z_r,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04;
   - normalized pin-preserving quartic representative on.


The comparison isolates the Gaussian-law correction A→B and quartic-flow correction B→C.


## 3. Frozen potentials and flow


The cubic terms are


    P3_X = X^2-1/4+aXY+(w/2)Y^2,


    F3_Y = (a/2)(X^2-1/4)+wXY+(z/2)Y^2.


For models A and B,


    dX/dtau = P3_X,
    dY/dtau = (q/r)Y+F3_Y.


For model C, define


    P4=(c40/24)(X^2-1/4)^2
       +(c31/6)X(X^2-1/4)Y
       +(c22/4)X^2Y^2
       +(c13/6)XY^3
       +(c04/24)Y^4,


and use


    dX/dtau = P3_X+r P4_X,
    dY/dtau = (q/r)Y+F3_Y+r P4_Y.


No arbitrary quartic lambda modes are included. This freezes the exact-midpoint-jet normalized coordinate convention of GP-DER-031.


## 4. Typing and determinant weights


For A and B,


    H(M)=[[-r, -ar/2],[-ar/2, q-wr/2]],
    H(S)=[[ r,  ar/2],[ ar/2, q+wr/2]].


For C,


    H(M)=[[-r+r^2 c40/12, -ar/2+r^2 c31/12],
          [-ar/2+r^2 c31/12, q-wr/2+r^2 c22/8]],


    H(S)=[[ r+r^2 c40/12,  ar/2+r^2 c31/12],
          [ ar/2+r^2 c31/12, q+wr/2+r^2 c22/8]].


Retain exactly


    H(M) negative definite,
    det H(S)<0.


Set


    W=|det H(M) det H(S)|


on the typed event and W=0 otherwise.


## 5. Branch convention


At S=(1/2,0), compute the positive-eigenvalue eigenvector of the frozen scaled-flow Jacobian for the relevant model. Choose its sign so the X component is negative. Initialize at


    S+10^-6 v.


This is the M-ward ascending branch.


## 6. Integrator and event classification


Use second-order Strang splitting for the stiff linear term (q/r)Y and fixed-step RK4 for the remaining nonlinear field.


    dt=0.005,
    tau_max=500.


Classification is mutually exclusive and ordered:


1. M-CAPTURE:
       distance to M=(-1/2,0) <= 10^-3.


2. CHART-EXIT:
       max(|X|,|Y|) >= 16.


3. OTHER-STATIONARY:
       tau>=5,
       gradient norm <=10^-7,
       distance from M and S >10^-3.
   The terminal point must be Newton-refined and its Hessian signature reported.


4. STALL:
       none of the above by tau=500.


A stall is never silently counted as failure or capture. Report its raw count and determinant weight separately.


## 7. Boundary-layer screen and independent kill test


For computational economy, integrate every typed sample satisfying


    m22_S > -2r,


where m22_S is the model-specific physical transverse Hessian entry at S. Treat all observed CHART-EXIT and OTHER-STATIONARY outcomes within this region as screened failures.


For each model/rung, independently sample 10,000 typed indices outside the screen using seed


    202607204800 + 10*rung_index + model_index.


Integrate those indices with the same solver.


Interpretation:
- any outside-screen failure falsifies the screen for that model/rung;
- zero observed outside-screen failures means only “screen not falsified in the tested sample”;
- it is not affirmative proof that no outside-screen failures exist.


The reported screened weighted complement is therefore a lower-bound/localization diagnostic unless an analytic screen theorem is later supplied.


## 8. Numerical stability audit


For the first 2,000 integrated typed trajectories in every model/rung, repeat with dt/2.


The run is invalid for that model/rung if more than 0.5% of trajectories with non-STALL outcomes change class. Every changed index must be logged.

## 10. Precommitted readings


LAW-STABLE:
- at least three of four adjudicable B/A ratios lie in [1/2,2];
- no outside-screen failure;
- no material new OTHER-STATIONARY population.


Q4-STABLE:
- at least three of four adjudicable C/B ratios lie in [1/2,2];
- no outside-screen failure;
- C screened weighted failure mass remains below 10^-4 at r=0.025.


MATERIAL-FINITE-LAW EFFECT:
- B/A interval lies wholly outside [1/4,4] at two consecutive rungs.


NEW-Q4-MECHANISM:
- C/B interval lies wholly above 10 at two consecutive rungs; or
- C screened weighted failure mass exceeds 10^-4 at r=0.025 with its interval excluding 10^-4; or
- a new OTHER-STATIONARY mechanism carries at least 10% of C's failure weight at two consecutive rungs.


SCREEN-FALSIFIED:
- any outside-screen audit trajectory reaches CHART-EXIT or OTHER-STATIONARY.


INCONCLUSIVE:
- insufficient raw failures/failure ESS;
- numerical-stability failure;
- more than 1% stalls by count or determinant weight;
- or mixed results not meeting the readings above.


All outcomes register. No slope is fitted unless all four rungs are valid and at least three have failure-weight ESS >=10.
