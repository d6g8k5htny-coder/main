       =(f_yy,f_xxy,f_xyy,f_yyy,
         f_xxxx,f_xxxy,f_xxyy,f_xyyy,f_yyyy)(O).


The evaluator computes the exact normalized-periodized covariance entries with arbitrary precision and applies the finite-r Schur complement.  It does not replace the six-pin law by the planar limit.  The raw pin Gram matrix becomes severely ill-conditioned as r decreases, so ordinary double-precision inversion is not reliable; the source defaults to 100 decimal digits.


At r=0.00625 the raw six-pin Gram condition estimate is approximately 2.79×10^15, while the returned nine-jet covariance remains positive definite with minimum eigenvalue approximately 0.4332852749.


## 1. Exact model


The one-dimensional normalized periodized kernel is


    k_L(t)= [Σ_{n∈Z} exp(-(t+nL)^2/2)]
            /[Σ_{n∈Z} exp(-(nL)^2/2)],


and the two-dimensional covariance factorizes:


    K_L((x,y),(x',y'))=k_L(x-x')k_L(y-y').


For multi-indices α,β,


    Cov(∂^α f(p),∂^β f(q))
      =(-1)^{|β|}
       k_L^{(α_x+β_x)}(p_x-q_x)
       k_L^{(α_y+β_y)}(p_y-q_y).


Every derivative is evaluated by the probabilists' Hermite recurrence


    d^m/dt^m exp(-t^2/2)
       =(-1)^m He_m(t) exp(-t^2/2),


inside the periodized sum.


Let P be the six pin functionals and T the nine midpoint jets.  If


    G=Cov(P,P),
    C_TP=Cov(T,P),
    C_TT=Cov(T,T),
    y=(b,0,0,b-r^3/6,0,0)^T,


then the exact finite-r law is


    E[T|P=y] = C_TP G^{-1} y,


    Cov(T|P=y)
      = C_TT-C_TP G^{-1} C_TP^T.


The computation is performed before conversion to ordinary decimal output.
