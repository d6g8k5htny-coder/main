1. Exact scope

    span{1,X,X^2,X^3,Y,XY}


that restores value, X-gradient, and Y-gradient at the two normalized endpoints X=±1/2,Y=0.


The norm used here is


    ||F||_{C^1(D)}

For Y-gradient residuals e_-,e_+, use
