## 1. Limiting constraints at the midpoint


Let the pair be M=(-r/2,0), S=(r/2,0), with


    f(M)=b,
    f(S)=b-r^3/6,
    grad f(M)=grad f(S)=0.


The axial Hermite interpolant is


    d_r(x)=b+x^3/3-r^2 x/4-r^3/12.


As r tends to zero, the corrected midpoint constraints are


    f=b,
    f_x=0,
    f_xx=0,
    f_y=0,
    f_xy=0,
    f_xxx=2.


The f_xx=0 and f_xxx=2 constraints are the coalesced gradient-difference and value-gap constraints.

where m_n is the nth derivative of exp(-t^2/2) at zero:


    m_n=0 for odd n,

## 3. Exact pin-preserving quartic basis


Use scaled coordinates


    x=rX,
    y=rY.


Write the local field, through quartic order, as


    f(rX,rY)
      = b + r^2 (q/2)Y^2 + r^3 P_3(X,Y) + r^4 P_4(X,Y) + O(r^5),


where


    P_3(X,Y)
      = X^3/3-X/4-1/12
        +(a/2)(X^2-1/4)Y
        +(w/2)XY^2
        +(z/6)Y^3.


The unique quartic correction with the prescribed fourth derivatives and with value and gradient zero at both (X,Y)=(+-1/2,0) is


    P_4(X,Y)
      = (c40/24)(X^2-1/4)^2
        +(c31/6)X(X^2-1/4)Y
        +(c22/4)X^2Y^2
        +(c13/6)XY^3
        +(c04/24)Y^4.


Verification is immediate:


- (X^2-1/4)^2 has a double zero at X=+-1/2;
- X(X^2-1/4) vanishes at both endpoints;
- every remaining term contains Y^2 or higher;
- the fourth derivatives at the origin are exactly c40,c31,c22,c13,c04.


Therefore


    P_4(+-1/2,0)=0,
    grad P_4(+-1/2,0)=0.


The quartic truncation preserves M and S as exact critical points. No Newton re-solve is needed for this compensated polynomial.


## 4. Exact r-power bookkeeping

    f_x(rX,rY)=r^2 P_{3,X}+r^3 P_{4,X}+O(r^4),

For gradient flow and rescaled time tau=rt,


    dX/dtau = P_{3,X}+r P_{4,X}+O(r^2),
