# Exact-torus Gaussian regularity via confluent divided differences


## Executive result


The sole Gaussian-regularity premise used by GP-DER-043 can be discharged for the exact normalized-periodized six-pin midpoint nine-jet family, after one precision correction.


For every sufficiently small r>0, replace the ill-conditioned raw six-pin coordinates by an invertible confluent Hermite/divided-difference frame. In that frame:


1. the pin covariance, target-pin covariance, conditional mean, and conditional nine-jet covariance extend continuously to r=0;
2. the limiting pin Gram matrix is positive definite;
3. the limiting joint Gram matrix of the six constraints and nine target jets is positive definite;
4. therefore the limiting conditional nine-jet covariance is positive definite and the finite-r family has uniform upper and lower eigenvalue bounds for all sufficiently small r;
5. the midpoint q-coordinate converges to a nondegenerate exact-torus Gaussian Q_L.


This proves the regularity and uniform density bounds required by the determinant-weighted boundary-layer reduction.


Precision correction: for fixed torus side L=24, Q_L is exponentially close to, but not literally equal to, the planar N(-b,2) law. The periodized kernel contains nonzero image terms. Thus the exact normalizer limit is


    z_0,L = E[Q_L^2 1{Q_L<0}] > 0,


not exactly the planar decimal 3.230978535287005.... The O(r^3) theorem and all two-sided r^2 normalizer bounds are unchanged.

## 2. Raw six-pin object


Let h=r/2 and write the axial and transverse endpoint data as


    F_- = f(-h,0),      X_- = f_x(-h,0),      Y_- = f_y(-h,0),
    F_+ = f(+h,0),      X_+ = f_x(+h,0),      Y_+ = f_y(+h,0).


The conditioned values are


    F_- = b,
    F_+ = b-r^3/6,
    X_- = X_+ = Y_- = Y_+ = 0.


The nine midpoint targets are


    T=(q,a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04)


with derivative multi-indices


    (0,2), (2,1), (1,2), (0,3),
    (4,0), (3,1), (2,2), (1,3), (0,4).


The raw six-pin Gram matrix becomes badly conditioned as r approaches zero because the two evaluation points coalesce. This is a coordinate singularity, not degeneration of the underlying confluent jet object.


## 3. Exact invertible divided-difference frame


Define six transformed pin functionals:


    L_0 = (F_+ + F_-)/2,


    L_1 = (F_+ - F_-)/(2h),


    L_2 = (X_+ - X_-)/(2h),


    L_3 = (Y_+ + Y_-)/2,


    L_4 = (Y_+ - Y_-)/(2h),


    L_5 = (3/h^2) [ (X_+ + X_-)/2 - L_1 ].


For every h>0 this change of coordinates is invertible. The inverse relations are


    F_+ = L_0 + h L_1,
    F_- = L_0 - h L_1,


    X_+ = L_1 + (h^2/3)L_5 + h L_2,
    X_- = L_1 + (h^2/3)L_5 - h L_2,


    Y_+ = L_3 + h L_4,
    Y_- = L_3 - h L_4.


Conditioning on the raw pins is therefore exactly equivalent, for each r>0, to conditioning on L=(L_0,...,L_5).


Under the prescribed endpoint values,


    L_0 = b-r^3/12,
    L_1 = -r^2/6,
    L_2 = 0,
    L_3 = 0,
    L_4 = 0,
    L_5 = 2.


Hence the transformed conditioning vector converges to


    y_0=(b,0,0,0,0,2).


## 4. Confluent limit of the functionals


Taylor expansion at the midpoint gives


    L_0 -> f,
    L_1 -> f_x,
    L_2 -> f_xx,
    L_3 -> f_y,
    L_4 -> f_xy,
    L_5 -> f_xxx.


The last relation follows from


    (X_+ + X_-)/2
      = f_x + (h^2/2)f_xxx + O(h^4),


    (F_+ - F_-)/(2h)
      = f_x + (h^2/6)f_xxx + O(h^4),


so their difference equals (h^2/3)f_xxx+O(h^4).


Because the periodized Gaussian kernel is real analytic, every covariance among the transformed pin functionals and fixed midpoint derivative functionals has a removable singularity at h=0. Thus the covariance blocks


    G_r = Cov(L,L),
    C_r = Cov(T,L),
    S = Cov(T,T)


extend continuously to r=0. The target block S is independent of r because all targets are at the fixed midpoint.


The conditional law is invariant under the invertible pin-coordinate change:


    m_r = C_r G_r^{-1} y_r,


    Sigma_r = S-C_r G_r^{-1} C_r^T.


It remains to prove that G_0 and Sigma_0 are positive definite.


## 5. Strictly positive torus Fourier weights


The normalized one-dimensional periodized kernel is


    k_L(t)= [sum_{n in Z} exp(-(t+nL)^2/2)]
            /[sum_{n in Z} exp(-(nL)^2/2)].


Poisson summation gives a Fourier series


    k_L(t)=sum_{m in Z} rho_m exp(i 2 pi m t/L),


where every rho_m is strictly positive. The two-dimensional kernel factorizes, so every lattice frequency k=(m,n) has strictly positive weight rho_m rho_n.


For a constant-coefficient differential polynomial P(partial_x,partial_y) applied at one point,


    Var(Pf(0))
      = sum_{(m,n) in Z^2} rho_m rho_n
          |P(i 2 pi m/L, i 2 pi n/L)|^2.


Therefore the variance is zero only if the associated polynomial vanishes on every point of Z^2.


Polynomial lattice lemma: a finite-degree polynomial p(x,y) that vanishes on all of Z^2 is identically zero. For each fixed integer y=n, p(x,n) has infinitely many integer roots, so it vanishes identically in x. Each coefficient polynomial in n then vanishes for infinitely many n and is therefore zero.


## 6. Positive definiteness of the limiting pin Gram


The six limiting constraints correspond, up to nonzero factors and signs, to the distinct monomials


    1, x, x^2, y, xy, x^3.


If a linear combination of these derivative functionals had zero variance, the corresponding polynomial would vanish on Z^2. By the polynomial lattice lemma it would vanish identically, and because the six monomials are distinct, every coefficient would be zero.


Hence G_0 is positive definite.


By continuity there exist r_0>0 and constants 0<g_-<=g_+<infinity such that


    g_- I <= G_r <= g_+ I


for 0<=r<=r_0.


## 7. Positive definiteness of the limiting conditional covariance


The nine target jets correspond, again up to nonzero factors and signs, to the distinct monomials


    y^2,
    x^2 y,
    x y^2,
    y^3,
    x^4,
    x^3 y,
    x^2 y^2,
    x y^3,
    y^4.


None duplicates a constraint monomial. Thus the complete set of fifteen constraint-plus-target monomials is linearly independent.


The same Fourier argument proves that the full fifteen-by-fifteen limiting joint Gram matrix is positive definite. Since G_0 is positive definite, its Schur complement


    Sigma_0=S-C_0 G_0^{-1} C_0^T


is positive definite.


Consequently, by continuity, there are constants 0<lambda_-<=lambda_+<infinity such that


    lambda_- I <= Sigma_r <= lambda_+ I


for all 0<=r<=r_0.


The conditional means m_r are continuous and therefore uniformly bounded on this interval.


This proves the uniform Gaussian density and moment controls used in GP-DER-043.


## 8. Exact-torus q limit and correction to the planar statement


The q-coordinate under the limiting conditional law is a one-dimensional nondegenerate Gaussian


    Q_L ~ N(mu_{q,L}, sigma_{q,L}^2),


with sigma_{q,L}^2>0 because Sigma_0 is positive definite.


For the planar Bargmann–Fock kernel, the corresponding law is exactly N(-b,2). For the fixed normalized periodized torus kernel, image terms at nonzero lattice translates contribute nonzero, exponentially small corrections to same-point derivative moments. At L=24 these corrections are far below the displayed numerical precision, but they are not mathematically zero.


Therefore the exact fixed-torus normalizer limit is


    z_0,L=E[Q_L^2 1{Q_L<0}]>0.


The planar expression


    (b^2+2) Phi(b/sqrt(2)) + sqrt(2)b phi(b/sqrt(2))


and its decimal 3.230978535287005... should be labeled the planar-limit value, not the literal exact side-24 torus value.


This correction does not change the power count. Positivity of z_0,L and continuity give constants c_Z,C_Z>0 such that


    c_Z r^2 <= Z_r <= C_Z r^2


for all sufficiently small r.
