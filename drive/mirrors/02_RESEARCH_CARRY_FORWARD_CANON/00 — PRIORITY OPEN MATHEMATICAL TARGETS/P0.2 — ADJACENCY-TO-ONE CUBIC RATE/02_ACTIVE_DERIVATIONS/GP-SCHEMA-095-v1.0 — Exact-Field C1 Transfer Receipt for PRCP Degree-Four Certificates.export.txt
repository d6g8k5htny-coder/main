PURPOSE


This schema defines the minimum receipt required to transfer a certified degree-four PRCP event H_r^(4) to an exact-field event H_r. It does not itself prove transfer. Every field is mandatory unless explicitly marked optional. Missing, nonfinite, unbound, or source-ambiguous values fail closed.

B. DOMAIN LEDGER


Declare every domain on which transfer is claimed:


D_endpoint_S — neighborhood used for saddle typing and unstable eigendirection.
D_cone — full S-side cone from the saddle germ to X_S.
D_strip — full central strip from X_S to X_M.
D_section_M — M-side handoff section.
D_capture — complete capture ball B(M,rho).
D_union — exact union of all above domains.


Each domain must store coordinates, closed/open boundary convention, chart containment margin, and a reproducible membership test.


C. DEGREE-FOUR MARGIN VECTOR


Store strict positive degree-four margins:


m_S_type — saddle eigenvalue/sign margin.
m_M_type — maximum negative-definiteness margin.
m_branch_xi — positive oriented xi-component margin of unstable eigenvector.
m_cone_axis — longitudinal cone-axis margin.
m_cone_upper — upper cone-boundary inward margin.
m_cone_lower — lower cone-boundary inward margin.
m_handoff — cone-to-strip inclusion margin.
m_strip_top — top strip-boundary inward margin.
m_strip_bottom — bottom strip-boundary inward margin.
m_drift — strictly M-directed longitudinal drift margin over D_strip.
m_section — M-side section containment margin or exact identity with a separate downstream strict margin.
m_capture — contraction/Lyapunov margin over D_capture.
m_chart — minimum chart-containment margin over D_union.


Every margin must include method, interval enclosure, precision, and location of the worst-case box or point.


D. EXACT-MINUS-DEGREE-FOUR REMAINDER BOUNDS


Let R_0 = F_exact − F^(4) and R_1 = DF_exact − DF^(4).


Store rigorous bounds:


eps0_endpoint_S = sup_{D_endpoint_S} ||R_0||
eps1_endpoint_S = sup_{D_endpoint_S} ||R_1||
eps0_cone, eps1_cone
eps0_strip, eps1_strip
eps0_section_M, eps1_section_M
eps0_capture, eps1_capture


Each bound must identify the norm, derivative convention, exact field representation, truncation order, tail/enclosure method, and all omitted-domain contributions. Sampling-only estimates are diagnostic and cannot populate these fields.


E. TRANSFER INEQUALITIES


The receipt must prove strict inequalities sufficient for each preserved property. At minimum:


T1 Endpoint typing: eps1_endpoint_S < m_S_type and the analogous M inequality.
T2 Branch orientation: perturbation bound preserves the positive oriented xi-component and eigenvalue separation.
T3 Cone axis: the exact-field loss induced by eps0/eps1 is strictly below m_cone_axis.
T4 Cone boundaries: exact-field losses are strictly below m_cone_upper and m_cone_lower.
T5 Handoff: exact-field branch/position uncertainty is strictly below m_handoff.
T6 Strip invariance: exact-field losses are strictly below m_strip_top and m_strip_bottom.
T7 Longitudinal transit: exact-field loss is strictly below m_drift.
T8 Section containment: propagated position uncertainty plus field error preserves entry into the declared M-side capture region.
T9 Capture: eps1_capture plus any center/position uncertainty is strictly below m_capture.
T10 Chart validity: all propagated uncertainty remains strictly below m_chart.


For every T1–T10 store left side, right side, strict slack, units, proof method, and PASS/FAIL/UNRESOLVED.


F. PROPAGATION AND TRAJECTORY CONTROL


If transfer uses trajectory closeness, store:


1. time horizon or section-to-section stopping rule;
2. Lipschitz/Jacobian bound used;
3. Gronwall or validated-integrator enclosure;
4. event-detection uncertainty;
5. section-crossing transversality margin;
6. maximum trajectory tube radius;
7. proof that the tube remains inside D_union;
8. fail-closed handling of nonfinite, solver, event-order, or enclosure-growth failures.


A numerical trajectory without a validated tube cannot establish exact-field transfer.

H. PLANTED NEGATIVE CONTROLS


NC-T1: increase eps1_endpoint_S above the saddle typing margin; must fail endpoint transfer.
NC-T2: flip or erase the oriented xi-component margin; must fail branch orientation.
NC-T3: enlarge cone remainder above one boundary margin; must fail only the targeted cone gate.
NC-T4: swap X_S and X_M or reuse kappa*sigma at the M-side section; must fail section-object validation.
NC-T5: remove exact-field source/hash; must return EXACT_FIELD_SOURCE_MISSING.
NC-T6: insert a NaN/nonfinite interval; must return NONFINITE_BOUND, never PASS or STALL.
NC-T7: make capture eps1 exceed m_capture; must fail capture transfer.
NC-T8: force the trajectory tube outside D_union; must return DOMAIN_ESCAPE.


I. ACCEPTANCE RULE


Exact-field transfer is accepted only if:


1. all object/source/hash checks pass;
2. all domains are declared and enclosed;
3. every degree-four margin is strictly positive;
4. every remainder bound is rigorous and finite;
5. T1–T10 all pass with strict positive slack;
6. all planted controls fail in the intended class;
7. an outside line independently reconstructs at least one endpoint, one cone/strip, and the capture inequality;
8. the exact field, fixture identity, and conditioning law match the target P0.2 object.


Otherwise status remains EXACT_FIELD_TRANSFER_OPEN.

K. SCOPE


A PASS under this schema proves only the declared local exact-field deterministic transfer for the declared fixture/event. It does not by itself prove positive probability, occupancy scaling, r^3 behavior, adjacency probability, elder-rule pairing, P0.2, Theorem B, a machine promotion, a ballot, or a release.
