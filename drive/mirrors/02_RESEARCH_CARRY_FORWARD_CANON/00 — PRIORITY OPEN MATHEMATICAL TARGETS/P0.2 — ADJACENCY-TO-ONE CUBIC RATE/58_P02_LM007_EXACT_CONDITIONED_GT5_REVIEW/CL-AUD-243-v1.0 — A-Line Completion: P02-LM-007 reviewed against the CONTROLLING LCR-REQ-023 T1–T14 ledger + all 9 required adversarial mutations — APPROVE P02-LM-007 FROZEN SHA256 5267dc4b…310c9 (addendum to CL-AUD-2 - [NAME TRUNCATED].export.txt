3. ADVERSARIAL MUTATIONS (LCR-REQ-023 section 6) — ALL NINE ATTEMPTED
=====================================================================
The packet asks which mutations KILL and which NARROW the theorem. CL's classification:


M1 REPLACE THE SIGNED PROCESS BY THE TWO-SUPREMA INEQUALITY — KILLS.
sup|Z| = max(sup Z, sup(-Z)), never their sum in general. Counterexample: any realization that is everywhere negative on K_r, e.g. Z == -2, gives sup|Z| = 2 and sup Z + sup(-Z) = 0. For a centered continuous Gaussian field the event {Z < 0 on all of K_r} has strictly positive probability, so a tail bound derived this way fails on an event of positive measure, not merely on a null set. This is the exact defect that voided CL-AUD-047 and it is absent from the frozen body.


M2 DROP THE BOUNDED CONDITIONAL MEAN — KILLS.
Borell-TIS controls the centered residual only. Without M_5 < infinity there is no bound on H_{5,r} = sup |m + Z| at all, and t_0 = 2(M_5 + B_5) is undefined. The finiteness of M_5 is exactly what the section-2 uniform invertibility buys; if G_r degenerated as r -> 0 the conditional mean could blow up and no uniform tail would exist.


M3 APPLY BORELL TO THE NONCENTERED PROCESS WITHOUT SHIFTING — KILLS THE PROOF AS WRITTEN.
The standard Borell-TIS statement invoked here is for centered processes. A non-centered version is available but requires re-deriving the concentration around E sup of the shifted process, and the naive substitution silently replaces the correct threshold u = t - (M_5 + B_5) by u = t - B_5, which INFLATES the exponent and makes the bound look stronger than anything proved. The frozen body avoids this by bounding m separately and applying Borell only to Z.


M4 USE THE UNCONDITIONED METRIC WITHOUT PROVING CONDITIONING REDUCES INCREMENT VARIANCE — NARROWS TO A GAP.
Dudley needs the canonical metric of the CONDITIONED process. The link to L_6 |x-y| exists only because the increment partial^alpha f(x) - partial^alpha f(y) is itself a linear Gaussian functional, so the Schur term applies to it as well as to point values. Delete that observation and the entropy bound has no justification, though the conclusion happens to remain true. The frozen body states the step explicitly in section 5, which is the right level of care.


M5 IGNORE CROSS-SIGN DISTANCES — SPLITS INTO TWO CASES, ONE HARMLESS AND ONE FATAL.
In the COVERING COUNT, ignoring cross-sign distances is harmless: covering each sheet separately is valid by subadditivity regardless, and smaller cross-sign distances can only reduce the true covering number. In the DIAMETER, ignoring them is fatal: the entropy integral's terminus must dominate the signed diameter 2 sigma_5, whereas the within-sheet diameter is only about L_6 times the Euclidean diameter of K_r, which tends to 0 with r. Truncating the integral at the small within-sheet diameter would understate B_5 without bound as r -> 0. The frozen body computes the cross-sign distance in section 6 and uses 2 sigma_5 as the terminus in section 7, correctly.


M6 ALLOW A NONCONVEX CHART WITHOUT A PATH-LENGTH REPLACEMENT — KILLS THE METRIC STEP; NARROWS WITH THE REPLACEMENT.
Section 5's estimate integrates along the straight segment and needs it to lie in K_r. On a nonconvex chart two points can be Euclidean-close while every path inside the set is long, and then d(x,y) <= L_6 ||x-y|| can fail. With a uniform path-length constant kappa (intrinsic distance at most kappa times Euclidean) everything survives with L_6 replaced by kappa L_6. The frozen body fences this correctly in section 12 and declares the variant outside the frozen statement.


M7 OMIT SEPARABILITY — KILLS.
Without a separable version the supremum over the uncountable K_r need not be a measurable function, so neither Dudley's bound nor Borell-TIS can even be stated, let alone applied. Kernel analyticity supplies an a.s. continuous modification, which is separable on the compact K_r; the two-point sign set adds nothing. The body invokes exactly this in section 7.


M8 CLAIM A PALM TAIL WITHOUT P02-LM-002 AND A NORMALIZER — KILLS.
The determinant-weighted Palm law has density proportional to a weight w with respect to Q, so P_Palm(A) = E[w 1_A] / E[w] and any transfer needs both a second moment for w (P02-LM-002) and a lower bound on E[w]. The Cauchy-Schwarz route gives P_Palm(A) <= sqrt(E[w^2]) sqrt(Q(A)) / E[w], which HALVES the exponent even when it applies — the tail exponent would drop from E to E/2 plus a constant. So the transfer is neither free nor available here. The body excludes it in sections 12 and 13 and CL confirms no step in sections 1 to 11 depends on it.


M9 CLAIM INDEPENDENCE FROM THE LOWER JETS — KILLS, AND IS UNNECESSARY.
H_{5,r} and any lower-jet event are functionals of the same conditioned field and are manifestly dependent. The claim would be false. It is also not needed: the downstream P0.1 intersection uses only Q(B ∩ G) >= Q(B) - Q(G^c), inclusion-exclusion under one measure. The body states NO INDEPENDENCE in section 12 and the exclusion in section 13.


SUMMARY: seven of the nine mutations KILL the theorem or its proof (M1, M2, M3, M6 without replacement, M7, M8, M9); one NARROWS it to an unjustified gap while leaving the conclusion true (M4); and one (M5) is harmless in the covering count but fatal in the diameter. The frozen body correctly avoids all nine.
