P0.1 — UNIFORM ADJACENCY POSITIVITY
Read First / Exact Proof Obligation


Priority: P0 — load-bearing theorem-status target
Canonical impact: NONE until formally promoted.


TARGET
For the exact determinant-weighted typed six-pin maximum–saddle pair-Palm law P_r^MS, let A_r be the specified gradient-adjacency event and


a_r = P_r^MS(A_r).


Prove that there exist r0>0 and c_A>0 such that


inf_{0<r≤r0} a_r ≥ c_A.


The theorem does not require a certified numerical value of c_A. It requires a rigorous positive lower bound uniform on the full stated r-domain and exact mark box.


WHY THIS IS P0
The exact conditioning identity is


P_r^adj(B) = P_r^MS(B∩A_r)/a_r.


If the typed regional obstruction probability is at most C_B r^3 and a_r≥c_A>0, then


1-q_adj(r) ≤ (C_B/c_A)r^3.


Thus this target closes the typed-to-adjacent transfer needed by the corrected selection theorem. A positive limiting adjacency factor also supplies positivity for the contact coefficient in Theorem B.


EXACT OBJECT CARD TO FREEZE
• Field: exact normalized periodized Bargmann–Fock field on T² with side 24.
• Scaling: maximum–saddle separation r, with the exact six pins used by the governing cycle.
• Base law: conditioned Gaussian six-pin law.
• Weight: |det H_M det H_S| with maximum and saddle type indicators.
• Law: normalized determinant-weighted typed pair-Palm law P_r^MS.
• Event A_r: the precisely identified M-ward ascending separatrix of S terminates at M under the configured branch convention.
• Domain: the exact r interval and any κ/mark restrictions used by the theorem.
• Measurability: A_r must be defined as a measurable event under P_r^MS.


CURRENT STATUS
A program-grade positive-limit argument exists through scaled-field convergence, structural stability, and an open trapping event. This is sufficient as a research route but remains an external proof sketch. Finite-r measurements may support plausibility; they do not prove uniform positivity.


REQUIRED PROOF DELIVERABLES
1. Exact branch convention and event definition.
2. Blow-up/scaled-field convergence in a topology strong enough to control the relevant separatrix.
3. An explicit finite trapping-corridor event in the limiting field.
4. Proof that the corridor event is open and has strictly positive Gaussian probability.
5. Structural stability showing the event persists for all sufficiently small r.
6. Compatibility with maximum/saddle type conditioning and determinant weighting.
7. A normalization argument converting positive weighted mass into a uniform lower bound for a_r.
8. Uniformity across the full theorem domain and permitted mark range.
9. A named lemma with dependencies, falsifiers, and relation to ADJ-CONDITIONING-TRANSFER.


PREFERRED PROOF ROUTE
Construct a deterministic reference jet or local field whose gradient flow contains a robust corridor from S to M, with all competing exits excluded by strict inequalities. Surround it by an open C² or C¹ neighborhood. Use finite-jet nondegeneracy/RKHS support to prove that this neighborhood has positive limiting Gaussian probability. Prove convergence of the conditioned, determinant-weighted scaled laws to transfer a positive amount of normalized mass uniformly to small r.


ADVERSARIAL CHECKS
• Does the corridor enforce the exact oriented branch, rather than some adjacency?
• Can another critical point enter the corridor under perturbation?
• Is the event open in the topology supplied by the Gaussian convergence?
• Do determinant weights vanish or become non-uniform on the proposed event?
• Does Palm normalization remain bounded above and below?
• Are torus-periodization effects controlled uniformly?
• Is the argument valid under every mark restriction used downstream?


DECISIVE FALSIFIERS
• a_r→0 along a sequence r_n→0.
• The limiting trapping event has zero probability.
• The event is not open or is destroyed by arbitrarily small allowed perturbations.
• A_r is not measurable under the chosen branch labeling.
• Determinant weighting or type conditioning removes all positive mass from the corridor.
• Uniformity fails on the claimed r-domain or mark box.
