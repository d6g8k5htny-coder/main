0. EXECUTIVE RESULT


Let Q_{6,r} be the exact normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock field on the side-24 torus conditioned on the six exact endpoint value/gradient pins at


M=(-r/2,0), S=(+r/2,0),


with values b and b-r^3/6 and zero gradients.


Let H_M(f),H_S(f) be the exact physical Hessians of the full field at M,S and define


W_r^{exact}(f)=|det H_M(f)det H_S(f)|
1{H_M(f) negative definite}
1{H_S(f) has index one},


Z_r^{exact}=E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact}].


Then there exists r_0>0 such that:


1. the normalized exact endpoint-Hessian vector defined below has a Gaussian law whose mean and covariance extend continuously through r=0 and whose polynomial moments are uniformly bounded;
2. E[(W_r^{exact})^2]<=C_W r^4 for 0<r<=r_0;
3. Z_r^{exact}=r^2 z_r^{exact}, where z_r^{exact}->z_{0,L}>0;
4. consequently there are 0<c_Z<=C_Z<infinity with


c_Z r^2<=Z_r^{exact}<=C_Z r^2


for every sufficiently small r.


This is the exact full-field normalizer, not the finite-Q4 normalizer.


1. PIN AND ENDPOINT NOTATION


Write h=r/2 and along the pair axis define


g(x)=f_x(x,0),
k(x)=f_y(x,0).


The gradient pins are


g(-h)=g(h)=0,
k(-h)=k(h)=0.


Let the secant functionals be


S_g=[g(h)-g(-h)]/(2h),
S_k=[k(h)-k(-h)]/(2h).


They vanish on the conditioned affine subspace, but retaining them inside the definitions below removes the apparent 1/r covariance singularities before conditioning.


2. EXACT PIN-ADJUSTED NORMALIZED ENDPOINT HESSIANS


Define six continuous linear functionals for r>0:


A_M^r(f)=[g'(-h)-S_g]/r,
A_S^r(f)=[g'(+h)-S_g]/r,


B_M^r(f)=[k'(-h)-S_k]/r,
B_S^r(f)=[k'(+h)-S_k]/r,


D_M^r(f)=f_yy(-h,0),
D_S^r(f)=f_yy(+h,0).


On the exact six-pin affine subspace S_g=S_k=0, so


A_M^r=f_xx(M)/r,
A_S^r=f_xx(S)/r,
B_M^r=f_xy(M)/r,
B_S^r=f_xy(S)/r,
D_M^r=f_yy(M),
D_S^r=f_yy(S).


Thus the exact physical endpoint Hessians are exactly


H_M=
[[r A_M^r, r B_M^r],
 [r B_M^r, D_M^r]],


H_S=
[[r A_S^r, r B_S^r],
 [r B_S^r, D_S^r]].


No quartic approximation appears.


3. CONFLUENT LIMITS


Taylor expansion at the midpoint gives


S_g=g'(0)+h^2 g'''(0)/6+O(h^4),


g'(-h)=g'(0)-h g''(0)+h^2 g'''(0)/2+O(h^3),


g'(+h)=g'(0)+h g''(0)+h^2 g'''(0)/2+O(h^3).


Since r=2h,


A_M^r -> -(1/2)g''(0)=-(1/2)f_xxx(0),
A_S^r -> +(1/2)f_xxx(0).


Likewise


B_M^r -> -(1/2)f_xxy(0),
B_S^r -> +(1/2)f_xxy(0),


and


D_M^r,D_S^r -> Q_L:=f_yy(0)


under the coalesced conditional law.


The exact confluent six-pin conditioning in GP-DER-044 fixes the limiting f_xxx coordinate to 2. Hence on the limiting conditioned affine law,


A_M^0=-1,
A_S^0=+1,


while B_M^0,B_S^0 remain finite Gaussian coordinates and D_M^0=D_S^0=Q_L.


4. ANALYTIC COVARIANCE EXTENSION


The pin-adjusted functionals in Section 2 are Hermite divided differences of endpoint derivative evaluations. For an analytic covariance kernel, every covariance among:


- the six confluent pin functionals L_r of GP-DER-044;
- A_M^r,A_S^r,B_M^r,B_S^r,D_M^r,D_S^r


has a removable singularity at r=0 and extends continuously there.


Let V_r denote the six-component endpoint vector. The joint Gaussian covariance blocks


G_r=Cov(L_r,L_r),
C_r=Cov(V_r,L_r),
S_r=Cov(V_r,V_r)


extend continuously to r=0. GP-DER-044 proves G_0 positive definite and G_r uniformly invertible for small r.


Therefore the exact conditional law


V_r | {L_r=y_r}


has mean


m_V(r)=C_r G_r^{-1}y_r


and covariance


Sigma_V(r)=S_r-C_rG_r^{-1}C_r^T


continuous on a compact interval [0,r_0].


Consequently all fixed polynomial moments of V_r are uniformly bounded.


This argument concerns the exact endpoint derivatives of the full field. It does not condition on or truncate to the midpoint nine-jet vector.


5. EXACT NORMALIZED DETERMINANTS


Define


Delta_M^r=A_M^rD_M^r-r(B_M^r)^2,
Delta_S^r=A_S^rD_S^r-r(B_S^r)^2.


Then exactly


det H_M=r Delta_M^r,
det H_S=r Delta_S^r.


Hence


W_r^{exact}
=r^2 |Delta_M^r Delta_S^r|
1{H_M negative definite}
1{H_S index one}.


At r=0,


Delta_M^0=-Q_L,
Delta_S^0=+Q_L.


Off the null event Q_L=0:


- if Q_L<0, the exact limiting M type is maximum and the S determinant is negative;
- if Q_L>0, the typed maximum-saddle condition fails.


Since Q_L is a nondegenerate Gaussian under GP-DER-044,


P(Q_L=0)=0.


Therefore


W_r^{exact}/r^2 -> Q_L^2 1{Q_L<0}


in probability under a continuous Gaussian coupling and almost surely under a standard continuous square-root coupling of the finite-dimensional family.


6. UNIFORM SECOND-MOMENT BOUND


Let


R_V=1+|A_M|+|A_S|+|B_M|+|B_S|+|D_M|+|D_S|.


For 0<r<=1,


|Delta_M|+|Delta_S|<=C R_V^2.


The type indicators are at most one, so


(W_r^{exact})^2<=C r^4 R_V^8.


Uniform Gaussian eighth moments of V_r give


sup_{0<r<=r_0}E[R_V^8]<infinity.


Thus


E[(W_r^{exact})^2]<=C_W r^4.


In particular, by Cauchy-Schwarz,


Z_r^{exact}<=sqrt(C_W) r^2.


7. NORMALIZER LIMIT


The domination in Section 6 gives uniform integrability of W_r^{exact}/r^2. The pointwise type/weight convergence in Section 5 therefore yields


Z_r^{exact}/r^2
->
z_{0,L}:=E[Q_L^2 1{Q_L<0}]>0.


Define


z_r^{exact}=Z_r^{exact}/r^2.


Then z_r^{exact}->z_{0,L}. Hence, after reducing r_0,


0<c_Z<=z_r^{exact}<=C_Z<infinity.


Equivalently,


c_Z r^2<=Z_r^{exact}<=C_Z r^2.


The planar truncated-normal decimal is not asserted as the literal exact side-24 torus value.
