inf_{0<r<=r_1} P_{r,Q4}^{MS}(B_q) >= c_{Q4}.

This is a uniform finite-dimensional degree-four theorem candidate. It is not the exact full-field P0.1 theorem.

6. UNIFORM POSITIVE MASS


The finite-Q4 Palm mass of B_q is

=

7. WHY FIXED q IS LOAD-BEARING


On a fixed negative q-box,


W_{r,Q4}/r^2 -> q^2


uniformly on compact jet sets, while the Gaussian q-width remains fixed. Division by the r^2 Palm normalizer therefore leaves an order-one mass contribution.


For a fixed-s box, q=rs and the q-width contributes an additional factor r. On the typed boundary layer the determinant product is order r^4 and division by the r^2 normalizer leaves an O(r^3) normalized contribution.


Therefore the fixed-s Route-B box is suitable for fixed-r existence and boundary-layer analysis, but the fixed-q box is the correct geometry for uniform adjacency positivity.
