=====================================================================
2. THE INVARIANT
=====================================================================
DEFINITION.  q(r) := y_r' G_r^{-1} y_r.


LEMMA 1 (BASIS INVARIANCE).  q(r) does not depend on the choice of frame. It depends only on the conditioning SUBSPACE Lambda and on the prescribed VALUES.
PROOF.  A change of frame is L' = T L with T invertible; then y' = T y and G' = T G T'. Hence
  y'' G'^{-1} y' = y' T' (T G T')^{-1} T y = y' T' T'^{-1} G^{-1} T^{-1} T y = y' G^{-1} y.  QED


Equivalently, and more usefully,
  q(r) = sup { (prescribed value of W)^2 / Var(W) : W in Lambda, Var(W) > 0 },
the supremum being attained at W corresponding to v = G^{-1} y. So q is the largest squared standardized value carried by any linear combination of the pins — manifestly a property of the conditioning problem, not of its coordinates.


VERIFICATION.  q(r_D) was computed in four genuinely different coordinatizations: the anchored/triangular confluent frame A, the parity-adapted symmetric frame B, an arbitrary invertible mixing C of frame A (chosen ugly on purpose; ||G_A - G_C||_F = 826.90), and the raw pin basis itself with no transform. All four agree to

with a maximum spread of 1.08e-193 across the four, i.e. agreement to the working precision. For contrast, ||G_A - G_B||_F = 2.4255139e-4, so the frames really are distinct objects.

3. THE BOUND
=====================================================================
THEOREM 2 (FRAME-FREE CONDITIONAL MEAN).  For every multi-index alpha with |alpha| = 5, every x, and every r > 0,
    |E_{Q_{6,r}}[partial^alpha f(x)]|  <=  sigma_alpha sqrt(q(r))  <=  sigma_5 sqrt(q(r)),
where sigma_alpha^2 = Var(partial^alpha f(x)) and sigma_5^2 = max_{|alpha|=5} sigma_alpha^2 <= 946.
PROOF.  With c = Cov(partial^alpha f(x), L_r), the Gaussian conditioning formula gives m = c' G_r^{-1} y_r. Since G_r^{-1} is positive definite it defines an inner product, and Cauchy-Schwarz in that inner product gives
    |c' G_r^{-1} y_r| <= sqrt(c' G_r^{-1} c) sqrt(y_r' G_r^{-1} y_r).
The first factor is exactly the Schur reduction term, which by conditioning monotonicity satisfies c' G_r^{-1} c <= Var(partial^alpha f(x)) = sigma_alpha^2. The second is sqrt(q(r)).  QED


REMARK.  Both sides are frame-free: the left because the conditional mean is a property of the law, the right by Lemma 1. This is the structural reason the bound is immune to the frame ambiguity recorded as finding F1 in CL-AUD-234.


VERIFICATION.  At 36 probe pairs (six multi-indices x six chart points, including corners of rD_plus and the pin sites), the exact conditional mean computed in the raw pin basis never exceeds the bound: max |m| = 20.0019770284793 against the bound 51.7109917397130.
