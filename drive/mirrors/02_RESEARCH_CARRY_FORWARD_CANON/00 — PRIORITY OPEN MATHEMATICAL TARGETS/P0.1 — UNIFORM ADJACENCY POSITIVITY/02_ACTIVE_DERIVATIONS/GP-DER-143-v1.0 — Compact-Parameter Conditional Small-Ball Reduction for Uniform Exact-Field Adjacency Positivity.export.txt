1. PURPOSE AND CURRENT INPUTS


The terminal and reviewed inputs now establish three different objects:


- EC-020: one fixed-q, full-dimensional degree-four corridor B_q satisfies the deterministic capture inequalities uniformly for 0<r<=1/20.
- EC-021: B_q has uniformly positive mass under the typed finite-Q4 Palm law for sufficiently small r.
- EC-019: the exact full-field typed-Palm selected-branch adjacency event has positive probability at the single fixed rung r=1/20, subject only to separate terminal approval.


None of these statements proves a uniform exact full-field lower bound on an r interval. This document gives a precise sufficient-condition theorem reducing that missing bridge to three named analytic inputs.

Let

where 0<r_1<=1/20 and B' is a compact positive-volume subbox strictly inside the terminal EC-020 corridor B_q.


For r>0, let Q_{r,6} be the exact normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock field conditioned on the six value/gradient pins for the ordered pair (M_r,S_r). For u in B', let Q_{r,u} be a regular conditional law of the exact field given the six pins and J_r=u.


Let S_r be the exact affine-scaled local-potential map into the fixed normalized chart D^+ and let P_{r,u}^{(4)} be the pin-preserving degree-four reference potential. Under the fixed-q convention, the polynomial coefficients vary continuously with (r,u); EC-020 supplies one compact deterministic family.


Write


nu_{r,u}=(S_r)_# Q_{r,u},


a Gaussian probability measure on the common separable Banach space

The exact capture neighborhood is


N_{r,u}(epsilon)={G in E: ||G-P_{r,u}^{(4)}||_{C^2}<epsilon}.


3. ABSTRACT UNIFORM SMALL-BALL LEMMA


LEMMA 3.1 — Compact positive lower bound for moving open balls.


Let K be compact, E a separable Banach space, k->nu_k a weakly continuous family of Borel probability measures on E, and k->p_k a continuous map from K to E. Fix epsilon>0. Assume


nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon))>0


for every k in K. Then


inf_{k in K} nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon))>0.


PROOF.


Define F(k)=nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon)). Fix k_0 in K. Because


B_E(p_{k_0},epsilon)=union_{n sufficiently large} B_E(p_{k_0},epsilon-1/n),


positivity of F(k_0) implies that for some eta>0,


nu_{k_0}(B_E(p_{k_0},epsilon-2eta))>0.


For k near k_0, continuity of p_k gives ||p_k-p_{k_0}||<eta, hence


B_E(p_{k_0},epsilon-2eta) subset B_E(p_k,epsilon-eta) subset B_E(p_k,epsilon).


For any sequence k_n->k_0, weak continuity and the Portmanteau theorem applied to the fixed open ball give


liminf_n nu_{k_n}(B_E(p_{k_0},epsilon-2eta))
>=nu_{k_0}(B_E(p_{k_0},epsilon-2eta))>0.


Therefore liminf_n F(k_n)>0. Thus F is positive and lower semicontinuous on compact K. A lower semicontinuous real function on a compact set attains its minimum; because F(k)>0 at every k, that minimum is strictly positive. QED.


IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE.


Pointwise support positivity alone on the noncompact interval (0,r_1] does not imply a uniform lower bound. The compactified r=0 endpoint and weak continuity there are essential. This is the exact analytic gap that fixed-r EC-019 does not settle.

7. CONDITIONAL UNIFORM EXACT-FIELD POSITIVITY THEOREM


THEOREM 7.1 — Route-B compact-parameter reduction.


Assume:


GR. The nine-jet densities phi_r(u) satisfy


inf_{(r,u) in K} phi_r(u)>=c_phi>0.


This follows from GP-DER-044 once B' and r_1 lie inside its regularity interval.


U1. The scaled conditional family is weakly continuous on K and every common capture ball N_{r,u}(epsilon_*) has positive conditional probability.


U2. Every field in N_{r,u}(epsilon_*) has exact selected-branch capture and W_exact>=c_W r^2.


U3. Z_exact(r)<=C_Z^exact r^2.


Then there exists c_A>0 such that


inf_{0<r<=r_1} P_r^{MS}(A_r)>=c_A>0.


PROOF.


By Lemma 3.1 and U1, there is p_*>0 such that


Q_{r,u}(N_{r,u}(epsilon_*))>=p_*


for all (r,u) in K.


Disintegrate Q_{r,6} over J_r=u. The exact weighted numerator obeys


E_{Q_{r,6}}[W_exact 1_{A_r}]
>= integral_{B'} E_{Q_{r,u}}[W_exact 1_{N_{r,u}(epsilon_*)}] phi_r(u) du.


By U2 and GR,


E_{Q_{r,6}}[W_exact 1_{A_r}]
>= c_W r^2 p_* c_phi Vol(B').


Divide by U3:


P_r^{MS}(A_r)
=E_{Q_{r,6}}[W_exact 1_{A_r}]/Z_exact(r)
>= c_W p_* c_phi Vol(B')/C_Z^exact
=:c_A>0.


Every factor in c_A is independent of r. QED.
