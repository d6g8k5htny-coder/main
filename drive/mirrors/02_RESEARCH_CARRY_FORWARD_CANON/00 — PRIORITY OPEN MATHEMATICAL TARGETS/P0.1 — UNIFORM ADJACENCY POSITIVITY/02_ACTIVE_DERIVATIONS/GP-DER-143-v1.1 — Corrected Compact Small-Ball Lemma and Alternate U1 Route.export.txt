2. CORRECTED COMPACT MOVING-BALL LEMMA


LEMMA 2.1.


Let K be a compact metric space, E a separable Banach space, k↦nu_k a weakly continuous family of Borel probability measures on E, and k↦p_k a continuous map from K to E. Fix epsilon>0. Assume


nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon))>0


for every k∈K. Then


inf_{k∈K}nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon))>0.


PROOF.


Define


F(k)=nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon)).


Let k_n→k. For any delta with 0<delta<epsilon, continuity of p gives, for all sufficiently large n,


||p_{k_n}-p_k||<delta.


Therefore


B_E(p_k,epsilon-delta) subset B_E(p_{k_n},epsilon).


Since B_E(p_k,epsilon-delta) is a fixed open set, Portmanteau gives


liminf_n F(k_n)
>=liminf_n nu_{k_n}(B_E(p_k,epsilon-delta))
>=nu_k(B_E(p_k,epsilon-delta)).


Let delta↓0. The inner balls increase to B_E(p_k,epsilon), so continuity from below yields


liminf_n F(k_n)>=F(k).


Thus F is lower semicontinuous. A lower-semicontinuous real function on compact K attains its minimum. Because F(k)>0 at every k, the attained minimum is strictly positive. QED.


The v1.0 conclusion was correct; this revision supplies the missing full lower-semicontinuity step.
