UNIFORM ADJACENCY POSITIVITY FROM AN ANISOTROPIC TRAPPING CORRIDOR


TARGET


For the exact normalized periodized Bargmann–Fock field on T^2 with side 24, let


M=(-r/2,0),  S=(r/2,0),


and impose the six pins


f(M)=b, ∇f(M)=0,


f(S)=b-r^3/6, ∇f(S)=0,  b=6/5.

W_MS = |det H_M det H_S| 1_{H_M negative definite} 1_{det H_S<0}.


The typed pair-Palm law is


dP_r^MS = W_MS dγ_r / Z_r,

No numerical c_A is claimed.

1. EXACT BRANCH CONVENTION AND MEASURABILITY


On the type event, S has one positive and one negative Hessian eigenvalue. For sufficiently small r on the event constructed below, the positive eigendirection has nonzero x-component and is close to the x-axis.


The M-ward branch is the component of the local unstable manifold of S whose tangent vector has negative x-component.


Let U_M,U_S be disjoint rational rectangles around M,S, and let Σ_S be a vertical rational section between them. On the open set where M,S are hyperbolic, the selected unstable branch crosses Σ_S transversely exactly once, and the crossing lies in a specified corridor, the branch and its first crossing depend continuously on the field in the C^1 topology. The event that its forward orbit enters a fixed attracting block for M before leaving the corridor is therefore Borel measurable.


Equivalently, A_r may be defined by a countable union over rational sections, rational corridor widths, and rational crossing-time bounds.


2. EXACT ONE-DIMENSIONAL HERMITE FACTORIZATIONS


Write g_r(x)=f(x,0). The unique cubic satisfying


p_r(-r/2)=b,  p_r(r/2)=b-r^3/6,


p_r'(-r/2)=p_r'(r/2)=0


is


p_r(x)=b+x^3/3-r^2x/4-r^3/12.


Therefore


g_r(x)-p_r(x)=(x^2-r^2/4)^2 h_r(x)


for a smooth confluent divided difference h_r. Differentiating,


f_x(x,0)=x^2-r^2/4 + 4x(x^2-r^2/4)h_r(x) + (x^2-r^2/4)^2 h_r'(x).


On |x|≤r/2,


|f_x(x,0)-(x^2-r^2/4)| ≤ (r^3/2)||h_r||_∞ + (r^4/16)||h_r'||_∞.      (2.1)


Now let v_r(x)=f_y(x,0). The transverse gradient pins give v_r(-r/2)=v_r(r/2)=0, hence


v_r(x)=(x^2-r^2/4)k_r(x).                                      (2.2)


At the coalesced limit, k_r(0)→(1/2)f_xxy(0). The corrected six-pin Gaussian law makes this limiting coefficient centered Gaussian and nondegenerate.


3. DETERMINISTIC TRAPPING LEMMA


Fix 0<η<1/4, β>0, and M_0<∞. Choose δ>0 and ε_k>0 so that


M_0 δ < (1/4)[1/4-(1/2-η)^2],                                  (3.1)


ε_k/4 < βδ/2.                                                   (3.2)


Define the anisotropic corridor


R_r={-r/2≤x≤r/2, |y|≤δr^2}


and the central strip


R_r^cen={-r(1/2-η)≤x≤r(1/2-η), |y|≤δr^2}.


LEMMA 3.1. There exists r_*=r_*(η,β,δ,M_0)>0 such that the following conditions imply A_r for every 0<r≤r_*:


(i) f_yy(x,y)≤-β on R_r.
(ii) ||k_r||_{L∞([-r/2,r/2])}≤ε_k.
(iii) ||f_xy||_{L∞(R_r)}≤M_0.
(iv) ||h_r||_∞+||h_r'||_∞≤M_0.
(v) f_xx(M)<0<f_xx(S), and |f_xy(M)|+|f_xy(S)|≤ε_k r, with the inequalities strong enough that M is a maximum and S is a saddle.


PROOF.


Top and bottom boundaries. From (2.2), |f_y(x,0)|≤ε_k r^2/4. For y=δr^2, the mean-value theorem and f_yy≤-β give


f_y(x,δr^2)≤ε_k r^2/4-βδr^2≤-(βδ/2)r^2.


For y=-δr^2,


f_y(x,-δr^2)≥(βδ/2)r^2.


Thus the gradient points strictly inward on both horizontal boundaries.


Leftward motion. Put


m_η=1/4-(1/2-η)^2>0.


On the central strip, x^2-r^2/4≤-m_ηr^2. By (2.1), for sufficiently small r,


f_x(x,0)≤-(3/4)m_ηr^2.


Using ||f_xy||≤M_0,


|f_x(x,y)-f_x(x,0)|≤M_0|y|≤M_0δr^2.


By (3.1),


f_x(x,y)≤-(1/2)m_ηr^2<0                                      (3.3)


throughout R_r^cen. Hence no critical point lies in the central strip and every orbit in it moves strictly from right to left while remaining between the horizontal boundaries.


Departure from S. Condition (v), together with f_yy(S)≤-β, gives a hyperbolic saddle whose positive eigendirection is O(ε_k)-close to the x-axis in the anisotropic coordinates X=x/r, Y=y/r^2. For the reference field


f_r^0(x,y)=p_r(x)-(β/2)y^2,


the negative-x unstable branch of S is the segment y=0 moving toward M. The strict boundary inequalities and hyperbolic continuation imply that the selected branch crosses the right boundary of the central strip inside |y|<δr^2.


Capture by M. At M, condition (v) and transverse concavity give a negative-definite Hessian. The reference field has a local attracting block in the left endpoint portion of R_r. Hyperbolic continuation preserves such a block. The selected branch enters the central strip, cannot leave through the top or bottom, moves strictly left by (3.3), and enters the attracting block of M. It therefore terminates at M. ∎


A counterexample satisfying the stated inequalities whose selected branch does not reach M would falsify this lemma. The most likely weak point is the endpoint-block transition rather than the central-strip inequalities.


4. POSITIVE PROBABILITY UNDER THE SIX-PIN GAUSSIAN LAW


Let E_r be the event that:


1. on R_r, -B≤f_yy≤-β for fixed B>β>0;
2. ||k_r||_∞≤ε_k;
3. ||f_xy||_∞≤M_0;
4. ||h_r||_∞+||h_r'||_∞≤M_0;
5. -2r≤f_xx(M)≤-r/2 and r/2≤f_xx(S)≤2r;
6. the endpoint mixed terms satisfy condition (v).


The event is open in corrected local C^3 coordinates.


Under γ_r, the corrected six-pin law and local divided differences extend continuously to r=0. The limiting free jets include


Q=f_yy(0),  A=f_xxy(0),


with


Q~N(-b,2),  A~N(0,2),


and the higher finite jets form a nondegenerate Gaussian vector.


Choose a finite open box in the limiting jet space satisfying


-B<Q<-β,  |A|<2ε_k,


and finite bounds on the remaining jets. This box has strictly positive Gaussian probability.


The corridor shrinks to the midpoint. Taylor's theorem implies that the supremum conditions defining E_r follow from the limiting finite-jet box together with a finite bound on one additional derivative over a fixed neighborhood. For the conditioned analytic Gaussian field, that derivative supremum is finite almost surely. As the allowed bound tends to infinity, its probability tends to one. The finite-jet box can therefore be intersected with a sufficiently large derivative-bound event while retaining positive probability.


Corrected-law convergence gives


liminf_{r→0} γ_r(E_r)>0.                                      (4.1)


A fully formal presentation may replace this paragraph with a support theorem for the corrected conditional Gaussian fields in C^3, or with an explicit finite-jet-plus-Taylor estimate. The shrinking-domain argument avoids needing density of arbitrary C^3 functions in the Bargmann–Fock RKHS.


5. COMPATIBILITY WITH DETERMINANT WEIGHTING


On E_r, for sufficiently small r,


H_M is negative definite and det H_S<0.


Moreover,


|det H_M|≥c_1βr,  |det H_S|≥c_2βr.


Hence


W_MS≥c_W r^2 on E_r.                                         (5.1)


The corrected-frame normalizer asymptotic gives


Z_r≤C_Zr^2                                                     (5.2)


for small r.


Lemma 3.1 gives E_r⊂A_r. Therefore


a_r=P_r^MS(A_r)


≥E_{γ_r}[W_MS 1_{E_r}]/Z_r


≥c_Wr^2 γ_r(E_r)/(C_Zr^2).


Using (4.1),


inf_{0<r≤r_0} a_r ≥ c_A>0


for sufficiently small r_0.


This proves the target conditional on the standard hyperbolic-continuation step in Lemma 3.1 and the corrected-law convergence already used in the local Kac–Rice analysis.


6. RELATION TO THE EXACT CONDITIONING BRIDGE


For any obstruction event B_r satisfying, on A_r,


{D(M)≠S}⊂B_r,


the exact identity is


1-q_r^adj = P_r^MS(D(M)≠S,A_r)/a_r ≤ P_r^MS(B_r)/a_r.


If the typed regional count gives


P_r^MS(B_r)≤C_Br^3,


then


1-q_r^adj≤(C_B/c_A)r^3.


This result does not imply 1-a_r=O(r^3). It closes the adjacent configured-pair theorem, not the stronger unconditioned typed-law theorem.


7. DOMAIN AND NONCLAIMS


The candidate proof concerns the fixed marks b=6/5 and f(M)-f(S)=r^3/6, for sufficiently small r.


The same proof appears to extend to compact b-windows bounded away from zero and compact positive κ-windows, but that extension is not claimed here.


Not proved:


1. No numerical c_A.
2. No estimate 1-a_r=O(r^3).
3. No typed-law conclusion q_r^MS→1.
4. No uniformity as b↓0 or κ↓0.
5. No promotion to the governing theorem state.
