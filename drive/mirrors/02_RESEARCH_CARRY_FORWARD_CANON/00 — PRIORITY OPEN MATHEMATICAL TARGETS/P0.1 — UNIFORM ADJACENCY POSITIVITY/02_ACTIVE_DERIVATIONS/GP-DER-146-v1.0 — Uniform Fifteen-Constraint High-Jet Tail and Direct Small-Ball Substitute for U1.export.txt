1. PURPOSE AND RESULT


GP-DER-143-v1.1 formulates U1 as weak continuity and pointwise support positivity for the fifteen-constraint pushed-forward conditional laws on a compact parameter set. That route is mathematically natural, but it is stronger than the uniform small-ball conclusion actually consumed by its conditional theorem.


This document proves, on the GP line, a direct substitute:


U1* — Uniform conditional capture-neighborhood mass.


There exist r_sb>0 and p_sb>0 such that, for every 0<r<=r_sb and every u in a fixed compact subbox B' of the terminal EC-020 corridor,


Q_{r,u}( ||S_r f-P_{r,u}^{(4)}||_{C^2(D^+)} < epsilon_* ) >= p_sb.


Here Q_{r,u} is the exact field law conditioned on the six endpoint pins and the nine midpoint-jet coordinates J_r=u; S_r is the exact affine-scaled local-potential map; P_{r,u}^{(4)} is the pin-preserving degree-four reference; and epsilon_*>0 is one common deterministic capture threshold supplied by the terminal EC-020 margins and GP-DER-045 robustness.


The proof does not require a limiting r=0 conditional measure, weak convergence on C^2, or the compact moving-ball lemma. It uses a fifteen-constraint version of the GP-DER-046 uniform high-derivative tail plus the deterministic Taylor/Hermite remainder bound.


This is an alternate GP-side candidate. It does not change the frozen GP-DER-118-v1.3 theorem or provide independent-review credit.


2. EXACT FIFTEEN-CONSTRAINT OBJECT


Let L_r=(L_{r,0},...,L_{r,5}) be the confluent divided-difference representation of the exact six endpoint value/gradient pins from GP-DER-044. Let


J_r=(q,a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04)


be the nine midpoint-jet vector in the same physical convention. Define the fifteen-dimensional constraint map


Lambda_r=(L_r,J_r).


For u in B', let y(r,u) be the corresponding fifteen-component conditioning vector: the six transformed pin values together with J_r=u.


Let Q_{r,u} denote a regular conditional Gaussian law of the exact side-24 normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock field given Lambda_r=y(r,u).


GP-DER-044 proves that the full limiting fifteen-constraint joint Gram matrix is positive definite and that its finite-r family extends continuously through r=0. Therefore, after reducing r_0>0 if necessary, there are constants 0<g_-<=g_+<infinity with


g_- I <= G_15(r):=Cov(Lambda_r,Lambda_r) <= g_+ I


for 0<=r<=r_0.


Because B' is compact and the transformed pin vector is continuous in r, the set


{y(r,u): 0<=r<=r_0, u in B'}


is bounded.


3. UNIFORM FIFTH-DERIVATIVE CONDITIONAL MEAN


Fix a compact normalized chart D^+ containing the complete EC-020 certificate domain. For every multi-index alpha with |alpha|=5, define on rD^+


X_{r,u,alpha}(x)=partial^alpha f(x) under Q_{r,u}.


Write


X_{r,u,alpha}(x)=m_{r,u,alpha}(x)+Z_{r,u,alpha}(x),


where Z is centered.


Let


c_{r,alpha}(x)=Cov(partial^alpha f(x),Lambda_r).


The conditional mean is


m_{r,u,alpha}(x)=c_{r,alpha}(x) G_15(r)^{-1} y(r,u).


Claim 3.1. There exists M_15<infinity such that


max_{|alpha|=5} sup_{0<r<=r_0} sup_{u in B'} sup_{x in rD^+} |m_{r,u,alpha}(x)| <= M_15.


Proof. Write x=rX with X in D^+. Each component of c_{r,alpha}(rX) is either an ordinary derivative covariance or an analytic divided difference of the exact torus covariance kernel. The apparent powers of r in the confluent functionals have removable singularities. Taylor expansion of the analytic kernel therefore extends every component continuously to r=0, uniformly on compact X.


Thus c_{r,alpha}(rX) is uniformly bounded. The inverse Gram matrices are uniformly bounded by g_-^{-1}, and y(r,u) is uniformly bounded on the compact parameter set. Multiplication yields M_15. QED.


This is the exact fifteen-constraint analogue of GP-DER-046 Lemma 4.1. The additional nine constraints do not create a singular mean because their joint confluent Gram is uniformly invertible and their conditioned values remain in the fixed compact box B'.


4. UNIFORM CENTERED-PROCESS BOUNDS


Conditioning a Gaussian field on finitely many linear observations subtracts a positive-semidefinite Schur term. Hence, for every linear field functional A,


Var(A | Lambda_r) <= Var(A).


For |alpha|=5,


sup_{r,u,x} Var(Z_{r,u,alpha}(x)) <= sigma_5^2,


where


sigma_5^2=max_{|alpha|=5} Var(partial^alpha f(0))<infinity.


For x,x' in rD^+, define the conditional canonical metric


d_{r,u,alpha}(x,x')^2=E[(Z_{r,u,alpha}(x)-Z_{r,u,alpha}(x'))^2].


Applying the same variance-reduction statement to the difference functional and using the line-integral estimate from GP-DER-046 gives


d_{r,u,alpha}(x,x') <= L_6 |x-x'|,


with L_6 independent of r,u,alpha.


Therefore the covering-number and Dudley estimates in GP-DER-046 apply uniformly after adding u to the parameter list. There exists B_15<infinity such that


sup_{r,u,|alpha|=5} E_{Q_{r,u}} sup_{x in rD^+}|Z_{r,u,alpha}(x)| <= B_15.


Borell–TIS and a union bound over the six order-five multi-indices yield, for every t>=0,


sup_{0<r<=r_0,u in B'} Q_{r,u}
(
H_{5,r}(D^+)>M_15+B_15+t
)
<=6 exp(-t^2/(2 sigma_5^2)),


where


H_{5,r}(D^+)=max_{|alpha|=5} sup_{x in rD^+}|partial^alpha f(x)|.


Theorem 4.1 — Uniform fifteen-constraint high-jet tail.


There exist C_15,c_15,T_15,r_0>0 such that


sup_{u in B'} Q_{r,u}(H_{5,r}(D^+)>T)
<=C_15 exp(-c_15 T^2)


for every 0<r<=r_0 and T>=T_15.


This theorem is a GP-side candidate using the same standard Dudley and Borell–TIS inputs as GP-DER-046. Its project-specific additions are the uniformly invertible fifteen-constraint Gram and the compact u-domain.


5. DETERMINISTIC SCALED REMAINDER


Because Q_{r,u} fixes the exact six pins and the nine midpoint jets, P_{r,u}^{(4)} has the same fifteen constraints as the exact field. GP-DER-045’s Taylor/Hermite argument gives a deterministic constant C_D such that


||S_r f-P_{r,u}^{(4)}||_{C^2(D^+)}
<=C_D r^2 H_{5,r}(D^+).


The constant C_D depends only on the fixed normalized chart and fixed pin-correcting interpolation operator, not on r or u.


Terminal EC-020 supplies a compact family of strict degree-four capture inequalities. Continuity of the finite family of dynamical gates gives one epsilon_*>0 such that every exact scaled potential within epsilon_* in C^2 of P_{r,u}^{(4)}, for u in B' and sufficiently small r, preserves endpoint type, selected branch, cone exit, strip transit, M-neighborhood entry, and convergence to M.


6. DIRECT UNIFORM SMALL-BALL THEOREM


Theorem 6.1 — Direct U1* discharge on the GP line.


There exist r_sb>0 and p_sb>0 such that


inf_{0<r<=r_sb,u in B'}
Q_{r,u}
(
||S_r f-P_{r,u}^{(4)}||_{C^2(D^+)}<epsilon_*
)
>=p_sb>0.


Proof. By the deterministic remainder inequality,


Q_{r,u}(||S_r f-P_{r,u}^{(4)}||_{C^2}>=epsilon_*)
<=Q_{r,u}(H_{5,r}>=epsilon_*/(C_D r^2)).


For sufficiently small r, the threshold exceeds T_15. Theorem 4.1 gives


sup_{u in B'} Q_{r,u}(N_{r,u}(epsilon_*)^c)
<=C_15 exp[-c_15 epsilon_*^2/(C_D^2 r^4)].


The right-hand side tends to zero as r decreases. Choose r_sb<=r_0 so that it is at most 1/2 for every 0<r<=r_sb. Then one may take


p_sb=1/2.


QED.


The value 1/2 is a proof convenience, not a claimed optimized or numerical project constant.

8. RELATION TO GT5


This route does not genuinely eliminate Gaussian high-derivative control. It proves a fifteen-constraint version of the same high-jet estimate used by GP-DER-046 under the six-pin law.


The distinction is architectural:


- the frozen theorem uses the six-pin tail and a subtraction bound before conditioning on J_r;
- U1* conditions on J_r=u first and proves a uniform conditional small-ball estimate over a compact u-box.


Thus U1* relocates and strengthens the conditioning-uniformity work; it does not remove it. Failure of U1* does not by itself falsify the frozen theorem’s six-pin subtraction route.
