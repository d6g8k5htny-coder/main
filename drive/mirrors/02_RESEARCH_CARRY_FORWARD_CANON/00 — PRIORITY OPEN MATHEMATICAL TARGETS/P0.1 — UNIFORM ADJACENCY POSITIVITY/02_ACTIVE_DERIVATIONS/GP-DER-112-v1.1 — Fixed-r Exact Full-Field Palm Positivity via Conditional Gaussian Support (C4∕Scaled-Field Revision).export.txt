5. C4-DENSE EXACT-TORUS RKHS WITH EXACT CONSTRAINT CORRECTION


Let H be the RKHS of the exact normalized-periodized torus field. Its discrete Fourier weights are strictly positive at every lattice frequency. Thus every trigonometric polynomial belongs to H.


Trigonometric polynomials are dense in C^4(T^2_24) on smooth periodic functions. Hence H is C^4-dense.


Let L:C^4(T^2_24)->R^15 be the six-pin plus nine-jet constraint map. Let k_1,...,k_15 be the RKHS Riesz representers of L_1,...,L_15. Their Gram matrix is the full fifteen-constraint covariance and is invertible.

6. CONDITIONAL GAUSSIAN SUPPORT


Let Q_u be a regular conditional law of f given Lf=y(u), viewed as a Gaussian measure on C^4(T^2_24). The field is analytic almost surely, so this Banach realization is valid.


If m_u is the conditional mean, the centered conditional Cameron–Martin space is

and the support of Q_u is the C^4 closure of m_u+H_0, equivalently the closure of H_{y(u)}.


Section 5 places g_u in that support. Therefore every C^4-open neighborhood of g_u has positive Q_u measure. In particular,


N_u={f:||S_{r_*}f-P_u^(4)||_{C^2(D^+)}<epsilon_*}

for every u in B'.


This is the standard Gaussian support theorem on Banach spaces. The same constrained Cameron–Martin mechanism is used in Q0_MASTER's Lemma LB-ARCH support step.
