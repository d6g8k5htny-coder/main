(B) An explicit open capture corridor with r-stable positive mass. Define the fixed jet-box B = { q ∈ [−3.5, −0.5] } × { each of the other eight jets within ±3σ of its exact-law mean }. The upper q-edge (−0.5, i.e. s ≤ −10 at r=0.05, more negative at smaller r) sits an order of magnitude below the ~[−0.05, 0] failure window, so B lies wholly in the strongly-hyperbolic capture regime. Verified by direct trajectory integration (long horizon + event-based escape detection, per the CL-AUD-050 protocol fix) on all 72 test points (32 relevant-coordinate corners + interior samples): 72/72 capture at every rung. This yields the constructive lower bound a_r ≥ P_MS(B) ≈ 0.71 uniformly across accessible r, and it is exactly the shape of argument the P0.1 preferred route calls for: an explicit open corridor event, robust, carrying positive Gaussian probability bounded away from zero as r→0.
2. What this does and does not establish
Establishes (numerically, accessible-r): a concrete open jet-set on which every configuration is gradient-adjacent, with r-stable positive exact-law mass ~0.71; and that the full a_r is ~1. Together these are the constructive core (deliverables 3–4) of the P0.1 preferred route, instantiated.


Does NOT establish: the theorem. P0.1 requires the limit inf as r→0, which needs the analytic scaled-field convergence + openness + structural-stability chain (P0.1 deliverables 2, 5) — that the box mass has a positive limit is strongly suggested by the r-stability but not proven; the numerics cover three rungs, not a neighborhood of 0. The certificate route cannot supply the box capture proof (CL-AUD-048 showed the GP-DER-047 certificate is vacuous at accessible r), so capture here is trajectory-verified, not certificate-proved. The box is closed; its interior is the intended open event (boundary has measure zero). Local (degree-four) model, not the exact conditioned field.
3. What remains for P0.1 (handoff)
The remaining burden is analytic, not numerical: (i) prove the scaled conditioned law converges on a fixed rescaled block (P0.1 deliverable 2); (ii) prove the corridor event is open in that topology and its limiting Gaussian probability is positive — the r-stability of P_MS(B) ≈ 0.71 is the target to prove converges (deliverable 4); (iii) structural stability transferring it to all small r (deliverable 5); (iv) the matching Z_r ≤ C_Z r² normalizer — already supplied by GP-DER-043 Lemma 3.1 as amended (CL-AUD-047 corroborated z₀). A promotion package could take B (or an optimized larger box) as the concrete corridor and attach the analytic convergence argument to it.
