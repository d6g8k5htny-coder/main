6. CL UNIFORM PERIODIZATION LEMMA (the repair for D1)


The reason GP's r^{-6} audit looks alarming is that it treats the frame rows as if they merely multiplied kernel values by large coefficients. They do not. Every row of T, and every endpoint functional, is a DIVIDED-DIFFERENCE functional over the two pin abscissae, and each one annihilates low-degree polynomials. That is precisely what makes the 1/r, 1/r^2 and 12/r^3 entries harmless as r -> 0, and it is what the r^{-6} heuristic fails to see.


Write each frame row and each endpoint functional as L(g) = sum_k a_k(r) g^{(delta_k)}(x_k) with x_k in {-r/2, +r/2}. Let D_L be the least total x-degree that L does not annihilate. The review computed D_L and the Peano constant kappa_L = sum_k |a_k| |x_k|^{D_L - delta_k} / (D_L - delta_k)! symbolically in the Laurent ring, and verified at runtime that every kappa is r-FREE (the assertion that the accumulated power of r is exactly zero is part of the executed code; if any row failed to annihilate as claimed, kappa would carry a negative power of r and the run would abort).


    frame rows:       D = 0, 1, 2, 0, 1, 3   with kappa = 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2
    endpoint A_M/A_S: D = 3, kappa = 3/4     B_M/B_S: D = 2, kappa = 3/4     C_M/C_S: D = 0, kappa = 1


Row 5 of T — the row that carries 12/r^3 and 6/r^2, and the row responsible for the r^{-6} scare — annihilates polynomials up to degree 2 and has kappa = 2. The apparent r^{-3} blow-up is exactly cancelled by third-order differencing. This is the whole content of the repair.


The Peano bound then gives, for each transformed entry, a defect controlled by kappa_i kappa_j times the supremum of the appropriate derivative of the image kernel over the pin square, summed over the side-24 image lattice. Bounding Hermite polynomials by involution numbers (|He_n(x)| <= I_n (1 + |x|)^n, with I_10 = 9496 and I_4 = 10 for the worst orders that arise, n_x <= 10 and n_y <= 4), and summing the lattice exactly over the first three shells with a geometric domination for shell >= 4 (the ratio bound 2^n exp(-(576p + 264)) at p = 4 is verified < 1/2 at runtime), yields


    max transformed-entry torus defect <= 1.56048...e-94,   UNIFORMLY in r on (0, r_D].


That is 24 orders of magnitude inside the 10^-70 reserve the certificate needs, with no r-dependence whatsoever. D1 is closed. Note that this lemma is not merely a tightening of GP's estimate — it is a different argument, and it is the one that holds as r -> 0.
