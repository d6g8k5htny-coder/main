FINDING 1 — NC1 IS ASSERTED, NOT TESTED
Severity: HIGH for test-suite integrity; MEDIUM for the underlying mathematical claim.
Repair status: REPAIR PROPOSED.


Evidence: GP-DATA-168 sets the NC1 result dictionary to `"pass": True` with a prose reason. No altered zero-width box is passed to `compute`; no radius field is preserved by `primary_box`; no validator checks strict positivity of all nine radii.

Minimal repair: represent each parameter by center and radius or preserve a separate radii map; implement `validate_full_dimension(radii)`; execute NC1 by setting c04 radius to zero and require the validator to reject before any dynamical margin calculation.


Acceptance test: mutate each of the nine radii to zero one at a time; each case must fail with a machine-generated FULL_DIMENSIONAL_BOX_FAIL result, and the primary positive-radius box must pass the dimensionality gate.


Falsification condition: any zero-radius case reaches primary PASS or bypasses the dimensionality validator.


FINDING 2 — SQRT OUTWARD ROUNDING IS NOT ESTABLISHED
Severity: HIGH for certificate wording; LOW for current sign stability.
Repair status: REPAIR PROPOSED.


Evidence: `I.sqrt()` calls Decimal `.sqrt()` under ROUND_FLOOR and ROUND_CEILING contexts. Decimal square root is nearest-rounded rather than controlled into distinct floor/ceiling outputs by those context changes. The same nearest result can therefore be returned as both endpoints.


Consequence: MU and rho are not proven outward enclosures by the current implementation. Downstream W, handoff, chart, and Gershgorin bounds are not fully certified as rigorous intervals.


Minimal repair: compute a high-precision nearest square root, then widen unconditionally with context `next_minus` for the lower endpoint and `next_plus` for the upper endpoint, or use a verified interval library whose square root operation guarantees directed enclosure.


Acceptance test: at deliberately low precision, sqrt([2,2]) must return endpoints bracketing the exact square root with lower²≤2≤upper²; the endpoints must not both lie below or both lie above the exact value.


FINDING 3 — ENDPOINT SCALAR ARITHMETIC ESCAPES DIRECTED ROUNDING
Severity: MEDIUM.
Repair status: REPAIR PROPOSED.


Evidence: several margins are computed with ordinary Decimal operations after `.lo` or `.hi` extraction. These operations use the global nearest-rounding context, not explicit outward rounding.


Consequence: the last stored digits are not rigorous one-sided bounds even where the enclosing interval operations were directed.


Minimal repair: keep derived quantities as intervals until the final bound extraction, or wrap each scalar operation in explicit ROUND_FLOOR/ROUND_CEILING calculations appropriate to the required inequality direction.


Acceptance test: run the corrected implementation at 40, 80, and 120 digits. Every lower-bound margin must be monotone/nondecreasing under tighter enclosures or overlap consistently, and every sign must remain positive.
