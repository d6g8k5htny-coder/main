1. EXACT COMPONENTS THAT SURVIVE REVIEW


1.1 Cubic Hermite interpolant


For endpoint values b and b-r^3/6 and zero endpoint derivatives at x=±r/2, the unique cubic is


    p_r(x)=b+x^3/3-r^2x/4-r^3/12.


Direct substitution verifies all four interpolation constraints.


1.2 Confluent factorization


Because g_r(x)-p_r(x) and its first derivative vanish at both endpoints,


    g_r(x)-p_r(x)=(x^2-r^2/4)^2 h_r(x)


for a smooth confluent divided difference h_r. Differentiation gives the displayed formula for f_x(x,0). Likewise, the transverse-gradient pins imply


    f_y(x,0)=(x^2-r^2/4)k_r(x).


These identities are exact.


1.3 Horizontal trapping


If f_yy<=-beta throughout |y|<=delta r^2 and |k_r|<=epsilon_k, then the candidate's mean-value estimates show that the gradient points downward on the top face and upward on the bottom face. This part is valid.


1.4 Central-strip leftward drift


On |x|<=r(1/2-eta), the reference longitudinal derivative is at most -m_eta r^2. Bounds on h_r, h'_r, and f_xy imply f_x<0 throughout the central strip for sufficiently small r. Therefore no critical point is present there and an orbit in that strip cannot reverse longitudinal direction. This part is valid.


1.5 Determinant scaling on a strengthened event


If the longitudinal eigenvalues at M and S are bounded below in magnitude by c r, the transverse eigenvalues are at most -beta, and the mixed Hessian terms are at most epsilon r with epsilon sufficiently small, then


    |det H_M|>=c_M r,
    |det H_S|>=c_S r,


and hence W_MS>=c_W r^2. This algebra is correct once those uniform inequalities are part of the event.


2. LOAD-BEARING ENDPOINT GAP


2.1 What the current hypotheses establish


The candidate establishes:


- a hyperbolic saddle at S;
- a hyperbolic maximum at M;
- inward horizontal corridor faces;
- leftward motion in the central strip.


These facts do not determine what happens inside the two endpoint layers


    r(1/2-eta)<|x|<=r/2.


2.2 Why local hyperbolicity is insufficient


The sentence that hyperbolic continuation preserves the reference exit and capture behavior requires a neighborhood in a scaled vector-field topology in which the entire isolating-block geometry is controlled. The listed assumptions give pointwise endpoint Hessian signs and several L-infinity bounds, but they do not explicitly control:


- the variation of the Hessian through each endpoint block;
- third derivatives such as f_xxx, f_xxy, f_xyy, and f_yyy on the scaled block;
- the signs of the vector field on every vertical and horizontal block face;
- the absence of an additional critical point in an endpoint layer;
- the unique designated exit of the selected branch from the S block;
- the unique designated entry into the M attracting block.


A locally hyperbolic equilibrium can coexist with another critical point or a different separatrix routing farther inside the same endpoint layer. Consequently, the current assumptions do not yet imply E_r is a subset of A_r.


2.3 No counterexample asserted


This audit does not claim to have constructed a field satisfying every listed inequality whose branch fails. It establishes the narrower and sufficient point that the stated implication has not been proved from those inequalities. The burden remains to add the missing quantitative endpoint conditions or produce a separate theorem that derives them.


3. REQUIRED DETERMINISTIC REPAIR


A valid repair can use either of two equivalent formats.


3.1 Explicit isolating-block format


Define scaled endpoint blocks B_S and B_M. Require and prove:


1. The S block contains exactly one zero of the gradient.
2. S is a saddle with a one-dimensional unstable direction close to the negative X-axis.
3. The selected unstable branch exits B_S through one designated left face and cannot exit any other face.
4. The M block contains exactly one zero of the gradient.
5. The vector field points inward on every boundary face of B_M.
6. The central strip connects the designated S exit face to the designated M entry face.


Every boundary sign should have an explicit margin independent of r after anisotropic rescaling.


3.2 Scaled C^1-closeness format


Set X=x/r and Y=y/r^2 and normalize the vector field by its natural component scales. Prove that on fixed X,Y endpoint blocks the corrected conditioned field is uniformly C^1-close to the reference vector field


    (X^2-1/4, -beta Y)


or to the exact torus-specific reference. A structural-stability or isolating-block theorem may then transfer the selected branch and capture behavior. The neighborhood and norm must be explicit enough to imply all six items above.


3.3 Additional deterministic bounds


At minimum, the event should include uniform scaled bounds controlling the derivatives of f_x and f_y in endpoint layers. One possible sufficient package is:


- bounded f_xxx and r-scaled f_xxy;
- bounded f_xyy and r-scaled transverse variations;
- quantitative longitudinal monotonicity of f_x near each pinned point;
- quantitative transverse contraction;
- a no-extra-zero condition derived by degree, contraction, or monotonicity.


The exact package may differ, but it must imply the complete block phase portrait rather than only endpoint Hessian signs.


4. EXACT-TORUS CONDITIONAL LAW


The candidate states


    Q=f_yy(0) ~ N(-b,2),
    A=f_xxy(0) ~ N(0,2).


Those formulas are exact for the planar covariance exp(-|x|^2/2) under the corresponding point conditioning. The research object, however, is the exact normalized periodized covariance on T^2 with side 24. Periodization contributes exponentially small but nonzero image terms to local spectral moments. Therefore the planar numbers must not be labeled exact-torus values unless an exact periodized covariance calculation proves equality.


The theorem does not need these decimals. A sufficient replacement is:


- compute the exact-torus limiting conditional mean and covariance of the corrected free jets;
- prove the covariance is nonsingular;
- identify a nonempty open jet box satisfying the strengthened corridor and endpoint inequalities;
- conclude that the limiting Gaussian gives that box positive probability.


5. POSITIVE-MASS STEP


5.1 Current issue


The event E_r depends on r through shrinking domains, divided differences, endpoint scaling, and suprema. A statement that corrected local coordinates extend continuously to r=0 is not by itself a complete proof of


    liminf_{r->0} gamma_r(E_r)>0.


5.2 Required formal route


Construct corrected random elements X_r in a fixed Banach space, preferably the anisotropically rescaled C^3 or C^4 field on fixed blocks. Then:


1. prove X_r converges in law to X_0;
2. define a fixed open event E_0 in that Banach space;
3. prove E_0 is nonempty, for example by an explicit reference element in the support;
4. prove every element of a smaller open subset of E_0 satisfies the strengthened deterministic trapping conditions;
5. use Portmanteau to obtain


       liminf P(X_r in E_0)>=P(X_0 in E_0)>0.


A finite-jet-plus-remainder proof is also acceptable, but it must give a uniform positive probability for the remainder bound rather than merely note that an analytic derivative supremum is finite almost surely.


6. NORMALIZER AND PALM WEIGHTING


The determinant-weighted ratio argument is valid only with a uniform upper bound


    Z_r=E_gamma_r W_MS <= C_Z r^2.


The candidate refers to a corrected-frame normalizer asymptotic but does not name a theorem, file, equation, or exact dependency. Before promotion, either:


- cite the exact source and verify that it uses the identical six pins, exact torus normalization, mark values, typing event, and W_MS convention; or
- include a short proof using corrected-jet convergence and a uniform integrability or Gaussian-moment bound.


The lower numerator estimate also depends on the strengthened event having probability bounded away from zero. Thus the determinant argument does not repair the endpoint or probability gaps; it becomes valid after those are closed.


7. MEASURABILITY


The branch convention and countable rational-chart description are plausible. A fully formal proof should specify the flow direction—gradient ascent—and define the selected local unstable branch consistently with the positive Hessian eigendirection. Once hyperbolicity and a transverse section are fixed, continuous dependence gives Borel measurability. This is not presently the main obstruction.


8. PERIODIZATION


The deterministic inequalities themselves are local and can accommodate periodization. The issue is not that periodization necessarily destroys the corridor, but that exact-torus covariance and support/convergence statements must replace planar equalities. Any claimed uniform r_0 should be obtained after these exact-torus constants are fixed.
