3. LEDGER B1–B12


B1 — SEPARABLE OR STANDARD BOREL FIELD AND FUNCTION SPACES. AFFIRMATIVE.
Section 1 assumes E separable metric with its Borel sigma-algebra and Y compact metric. Section 10 instantiates E as C^3(T_24^2; R) or the closed exact six-pin affine fiber. C^3 of a compact manifold is a separable Banach space, hence Polish, hence standard Borel. The six-pin fiber is the preimage of a point under six continuous linear functionals — point evaluations of f, f_x, f_y at M and at S, all continuous on C^3 — so it is a closed affine subspace, hence Polish, hence standard Borel. Y = T_24^2 is compact metric. All verified independently.


B2 — COUNTABLE PRIORITY PARTITION. AFFIRMATIVE, with one wording imprecision recorded below.
B_1 = U_1, B_j = U_j minus the union of U_i for i < j. Borel: each B_j is a Borel set minus a finite union of Borel sets. Disjoint: for i < j, B_j is disjoint from U_i and B_i is contained in U_i. Covering: the standard disjointification identity gives union of B_j = union of U_j. Selector Borel: b^{-1}(V) = union over j of [B_j intersect b_j^{-1}(V)], each term Borel because b_j is continuous on U_j and B_j is Borel, and the union is countable. All four claims verified.
Section 2's closing sentence — "No compatibility of b_i and b_j on overlaps is required for measurability because the B_j form a disjoint priority partition" — is correct AND correctly scoped. Compatibility is genuinely not needed for measurability; it IS needed for the selector-independence of section 4, and it is supplied there as hypothesis A4 rather than smuggled in. That scoping is exactly right and I record it as a strength.
IMPRECISION B2-i (non-blocking). A1 says O has a countable Borel cover U_1, U_2, ... but does not require U_j to be a subset of O, whereas section 2 concludes "their union is O". If some U_j sticks out of O the union is a superset. The repair is one word: take the cover to consist of relatively Borel subsets of O, or intersect each U_j with O, which is Borel and changes nothing else. Every conclusion of section 2 survives unchanged under either reading, so this is a wording defect, not a mathematical one.


B3 — ONE-DIMENSIONAL BRANCH IS ONE ORBIT; LOCAL CHOICES DIFFER BY A FINITE TIME SHIFT. AFFIRMATIVE.
The argument: orbits partition the invariant set C_p; the flow-box theorem at a point where the field is nonzero gives a one-dimensional coordinate in which the flow is translation, so each orbit is open in the one-dimensional C_p; a connected space admits no partition into two or more nonempty disjoint open sets; hence C_p is a single orbit; hence any two points of C_p differ by a finite flow time. Verified — the flow-box step requires exactly A5's nonvanishing hypothesis, and the one-dimensionality of C_p is what makes the local orbit segment a NEIGHBOURHOOD in C_p rather than merely a subset. Both are present.


B4 — FORWARD CONVERGENCE INVARIANT UNDER FINITE TIME SHIFT; NO MEASURABILITY OF THE SHIFT REQUIRED. AFFIRMATIVE, and this is the load-bearing subtlety of the whole companion. It is handled correctly.
Section 4 obtains a finite tau(p) with b'(p) = Phi(p, tau(p), b(p)), states plainly "The function tau need not be measurable", and then uses only Phi(p, t, b'(p)) = Phi(p, t + tau(p), b(p)) together with the fact that a finite time translation does not change whether a trajectory converges as t -> infinity.
I checked specifically for the failure mode this item exists to catch — a step that quantifies over tau in a way that would need it measurable. There is none. The conclusion is a POINTWISE set equality: for each individual p, the two convergence statements are equivalent, so the two events coincide as sets. No pushforward of tau, no integration against tau, no measurable selection of tau. The claim that measurability of tau is not required is correct.


B5 — PATH SPACE. AFFIRMATIVE. C([0,infinity), Y) with the compact-open topology is separable metrizable for Y compact metric; its Borel sigma-algebra is generated by evaluations at nonnegative rational times; Gamma(p)(t) = Phi(p, t, b(p)) is Borel because for each fixed rational q the map p -> Phi(p, q, b(p)) is the composition of the Borel map p -> (p, b(p)) with the continuous map (p,x) -> Phi(p,q,x), and the rational evaluations generate. That Gamma(p) actually lies in the path space follows from A3's jointly continuous complete flow. All verified.


B6 — RATIONAL-TIME REPRESENTATION EQUIVALENT TO ALL-TIME CONVERGENCE. AFFIRMATIVE.
The displayed representation intersects over k, unions over N, and intersects over rationals q >= N of the sets {gamma: d(gamma(q), m) <= 1/k}. Each such set is CLOSED — the non-strict inequality is deliberate and is what makes it closed — and the whole expression is countable, so C_m is Borel.
Both directions of the equality check out. Forward is immediate. The converse fixes k, takes N from the representation, and for real t >= N approximates t by rationals q_n >= N, using continuity of gamma and closedness of the distance bound to pass to the limit; this gives sup over t >= N of d(gamma(t), m) <= 1/k, and since k is arbitrary, convergence. The approximation is available even at t = N exactly, by taking rationals in [N, N + 1/n].

4. FINDING B-N1 — THE SOLE BLOCKING RESIDUE


Statement. Section 10 discharges the finite positive normalizer with the sentence "P02-LM-006 gives 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity on its valid domain", and then concludes "Consequently the exact selected-branch convergence event A_r is Borel and P_r^{MS}(A_r) = E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact} 1_{A_r}]/Z_r^{exact} is a legitimate exact Palm probability."


Three defects, in ascending order of severity.


(a) The citation is version-free and hash-free. P02-LM-006 is named with no version, no BODY_BYTES and no BODY_SHA256, for a load-bearing quantitative theorem. This is the identical pattern I refused to accept in CL-AUD-271 V2, where GP-DER-118-v1.6's citation of CL-AUD-205 for GP-DER-197 was not hash-specific. Consistency requires the same treatment here.


(b) The domain intersection is not stated, let alone checked. The qualifier "on its valid domain" concedes that P02-LM-006's conclusion holds only on some domain, but that domain is never named, and no argument is given that it contains O_r, or the r-range Carrier B works in, or the six-pin fiber at that r. This is the same class of gap as finding A9 in CL-AUD-260, and it is substantive independently of any hashing question: an unstated domain intersection is a mathematical gap, not a bookkeeping one.


(c) The parent's CONDITIONAL is silently converted into an UNCONDITIONAL. This is the sharpest of the three and I did not expect to find it. GP-DER-197-v1.0 — the parent this companion extracts sections 5 through 11 from, and the object that already carries a distinct-family PASS — states the same result CONDITIONALLY: "is a well-defined exact Palm probability whenever 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity", and "If 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity, define...". GP-DER-197 does not cite P02-LM-006 at all; it leaves finiteness as a hypothesis. Carrier B discharges that hypothesis by importing an uncited-by-hash lemma and then states the conclusion unconditionally with "Consequently". The companion therefore claims strictly MORE than the parent it is extracted from, on the strength of an import the parent declined to make. That is precisely the quiet strengthening that hash-specific review exists to catch.


Severity and containment. This residue is contained. It touches section 10's final Palm sentence and nothing else. In particular it does NOT touch the Borel-measurability chain of sections 1 through 8, which needs no normalizer at all, and it does NOT touch section 11's theorem, whose three abstract sentences are fully proved under A1–A5 and whose application sentence claims only the measurable selected branch and the Borel adjacency event. Section 11 is clean. The defect is that section 10 reaches past section 11.


MINIMAL REPAIR — either of two one-sentence edits in a v1.1 successor:
(R1) Restore the parent's conditional form. Replace "P02-LM-006 gives 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity on its valid domain" and the following "Consequently ... is a legitimate exact Palm probability" with the GP-DER-197 formulation: "...is a well-defined exact Palm probability whenever 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity." This requires no new mathematics and makes Carrier B claim exactly what its parent claims.
(R2) Keep the discharge but make it exact. Cite P02-LM-006 with its version, BODY_BYTES and BODY_SHA256; state its valid domain explicitly; and state the inclusion of the six-pin fiber at the relevant r-range in that domain, or the intersection of the two r-domains.
