6. PROOF ROADMAP


The proof uses one adaptive width W and two endpoint scales sigma and rho.


First, the threshold s<=-2mu-2R makes the Y-direction strongly contracting throughout the strip. Because mu W=F_*, the horizontal strip boundary flux has a unit buffer rather than merely a nonpositive sign.


Second, the perturbation of X^2-1/4 is at most 0.501RW. The central loss sigma(1-sigma) exceeds 3.99RW, leaving Xdot<=-3RW.


Third, the endpoint Jacobian changes by at most 3/64 in each endpoint ball. At S, the large separation A-D generated by the threshold dominates the mixed term and nonlinear variation, yielding an invariant fixed cone with positive xi growth. At M, the same threshold yields a strongly positive matrix -J_M, and the Jacobian variation is less than half its contraction margin.


Finally, the cone handoff identity kappa sigma=W/4 places the selected branch inside the strip, and the geometric identity sqrt(sigma^2+W^2)<rho places the left central section inside the M ball. The components therefore glue without an uncovered transition region.
