P02-LM-006 — EXACT FULL-FIELD TYPED MAXIMUM–SADDLE NORMALIZER r^2 ASYMPTOTIC


1. EXACT FIELD AND CONDITIONING LAW


Fix the normalized-periodized Bargmann–Fock Gaussian field on the side-24 flat torus and fix b=6/5. For every r>0 set


    M=(-r/2,0),
    S=(+r/2,0).


Let Q_{6,r} be the exact Gaussian field law conditioned on the six endpoint value and gradient constraints


    (f,f_x,f_y)(M)=(b,0,0),


    (f,f_x,f_y)(S)=(b-r^3/6,0,0).


Let H_M(f) and H_S(f) be the exact physical two-by-two Hessians at M and S. Define the exact typed weight


    W_r^{exact}(f)
      =|det H_M(f) det H_S(f)|
       1{H_M(f) is negative definite}
       1{H_S(f) has index one}.


Define


    Z_r^{exact}=E_{Q_{6,r}}[W_r^{exact}].


2. PIN-ADJUSTED ENDPOINT FUNCTIONALS


Set h=r/2 and define the one-dimensional restrictions


    g(x)=f_x(x,0),
    k(x)=f_y(x,0).


Let


    S_g^r=[g(h)-g(-h)]/(2h),
    S_k^r=[k(h)-k(-h)]/(2h).


Define the pin-adjusted endpoint functionals


    A_M^r=[g'(-h)-S_g^r]/r,
    A_S^r=[g'(+h)-S_g^r]/r,


    B_M^r=[k'(-h)-S_k^r]/r,
    B_S^r=[k'(+h)-S_k^r]/r,


    C_M^r=f_yy(M),
    C_S^r=f_yy(S).


On the exact six-pin affine conditioning subspace,


    g(-h)=g(h)=k(-h)=k(h)=0.


Therefore under Q_{6,r}, these pin-adjusted functionals are exactly


    A_M^r=f_xx(M)/r,
    A_S^r=f_xx(S)/r,


    B_M^r=f_xy(M)/r,
    B_S^r=f_xy(S)/r,


    C_M^r=f_yy(M),
    C_S^r=f_yy(S).


The subtraction must occur before division by r. Raw unconditioned derivatives divided by r are not the functionals used in this proof.


3. CONFLUENT CONDITIONAL GAUSSIAN LIMIT


Use the exact invertible six-pin confluent divided-difference frame. The closed exact Gaussian interface P02-LM-005 supplies:


- continuous extension of the transformed pin covariance through r=0;
- uniform invertibility of the transformed pin Gram matrix;
- continuous bounded conditional means;

- nondegeneracy of the limiting exact-torus transverse curvature coordinate Q_L.


Because the periodized covariance kernel is real analytic, every covariance between the transformed pins and the six pin-adjusted endpoint functionals is an analytic divided difference with a removable singularity at r=0. The same is true for their mutual covariance matrix.


Hence the conditional Gaussian vector


    V_r=(A_M^r,A_S^r,B_M^r,B_S^r,C_M^r,C_S^r)


has means and covariance matrices extending continuously through r=0. The limiting covariance of V_r is allowed to be singular because the limiting variables satisfy exact affine relations.


Taylor expansion at the midpoint gives


    A_M^r -> -f_xxx(0)/2,
    A_S^r -> +f_xxx(0)/2,


    B_M^r -> -f_xxy(0)/2,
    B_S^r -> +f_xxy(0)/2,

in the confluent conditional law.


The transformed endpoint constraints force the limiting conditioned value

The variable Q_L is a nondegenerate one-dimensional Gaussian. It is the exact side-24 torus variable, not the planar N(-b,2) approximation.


4. EXACT DETERMINANT FACTORIZATION


Define


    Delta_M^r
      =A_M^r C_M^r-r(B_M^r)^2,


    Delta_S^r
      =A_S^r C_S^r-r(B_S^r)^2.


Then exactly


    det H_M=r Delta_M^r,
    det H_S=r Delta_S^r.


By the joint convergence in Section 3,


    Delta_M^r => -Q_L,
    Delta_S^r => +Q_L


jointly.


5. TYPE-INDICATOR LIMIT


For a real symmetric two-by-two matrix, negative definiteness is equivalent to a negative first diagonal entry and positive determinant. Index one is equivalent to negative determinant.


Fix a limiting configuration with Q_L<0. Then for all sufficiently nearby endpoint vectors and sufficiently small r:


- at M, A_M^r<0 and C_M^r<0;
- Delta_M^r>0, hence det H_M>0;
- therefore H_M is negative definite;
- at S, Delta_S^r<0, hence det H_S<0;
- therefore H_S has index one.


Fix a limiting configuration with Q_L>0. Then the M transverse entry is positive in a sufficiently small neighborhood, so H_M cannot be negative definite. Thus the required maximum–saddle type combination fails.


The only boundary of this limiting type decision is Q_L=0. Since Q_L is nondegenerate Gaussian,


    P(Q_L=0)=0.


Therefore the type indicator converges in the extended continuous-mapping sense to


    1{Q_L<0}.

    W_r^{exact}/r^2

    W_r^{exact}/r^2

This is convergence in distribution under the changing exact conditioned laws Q_{6,r}. No unconstructed almost-sure coupling is asserted.


7. UNIFORM INTEGRABILITY

    |Delta_M^r|
      <=|A_M^r C_M^r|+(B_M^r)^2

    0<=W_r^{exact}/r^2

      <=R_r^4,

    (W_r^{exact}/r^2)^2<=R_r^8.

    sup_{0<r<=r_1} E_{Q_{6,r}}[R_r^8]<infinity

Therefore W_r^{exact}/r^2 is uniformly bounded in L^2 and is uniformly integrable.

    0<z_{0,L}^{exact}<infinity.
