Method: assembled the periodized Bargmann–Fock covariance (L=24, rho_m = exp(-|k_m|^2/2), Fourier cutoff |m|<=70) and formed the 15x15 Gram of the 6 pins {f, d_x, d_y at M=(+1/40,0) and S=(-1/40,0)} plus 9 midpoint jets of orders 2–4.
My results:
 - raw Gram: min eig 2.3e-15, max eig 117.2, condition ~5.0e16 (positive-definite only nominally);
 - scale-free correlation matrix: min eig ~1.0e-16 (≈ machine epsilon), condition ~3.3e16;

Reading: at r=1/20 the pin+jet functionals are so strongly correlated that strict positive-definiteness sits BELOW the double-precision floor. I therefore cannot confirm §2/R3 in double precision, and I did NOT reproduce CL-AUD-072's reported min eig 2.98e-8 — that figure is normalization-dependent (it evidently uses variance-normalized jet functionals; mine are raw physical derivatives). This does not refute the claim: a real-analytic covariance with 15 distinct functionals is nondegenerate in exact arithmetic. It does confirm the nondegeneracy is genuinely DELICATE and certifiable only in high precision / interval arithmetic — the very tooling whose independent outside re-execution remains open (CL-AUD-072 residual; GP-DER-106 §4).

 - Base system: the saddle branch converges to the maximum — the heteroclinic "adjacency" is realizable.
