3. B3 — LIMITING SPECTRAL WITNESSES — DEFECT FOUND; THE CLAIMS ARE NONETHELESS TRUE


3.1 What the source actually certifies.


exact_spectral_witnesses() extracts a 3x3 PRINCIPAL SUBMATRIX of the 9x9 limiting conditional covariance:


    block = sigma0.extract([0, 6, 8], [0, 6, 8])
          = [[2, -2, -12], [-2, 6, 12], [-12, 12, 96]]


and then tests Sylvester minors of block - 0.43*I and of 100*I - block. It raises if any minor is non-positive. Those two tests are the entire spectral evidence in the artifact, and their outputs are what the result carrier records as lambda_min_strictly_above = "0.43" and lambda_max_strictly_below = "100".


3.2 Why that does not prove the stated claims.


For a symmetric matrix A and a principal submatrix B, Cauchy interlacing gives


    lambda_min(A) <= lambda_min(B)   and   lambda_max(B) <= lambda_max(A).


Both inequalities run the WRONG WAY for the claims being made:


  - proving lambda_min(B) > 0.43 gives no lower bound at all on lambda_min(A);
  - proving lambda_max(B) < 100 gives no upper bound at all on lambda_max(A).


So the artifact's spectral evidence is logically inert with respect to Sigma_0 itself. Everything downstream — B9's Weyl step, the 0.42 and 151 finite-r thresholds, and the density lower bound in B11 — inherits from these two numbers, so the gap propagates to the box-mass conclusion.


3.3 CL independent computation on the FULL 9x9.


The full limiting conditional covariance Sigma_0 = S_0 - C_0 G_0^{-1} C_0^T was rebuilt from GP's own definitions (confluent Hermite-at-zero covariance atoms, the six confluent constraints, the nine jet derivatives) in exact rational arithmetic, and its spectrum was bracketed by exact rational LDL definiteness tests on Sigma_0 - tI and tI - Sigma_0:


    lambda_min(Sigma_0) in [0.433285274926709708907812679257,
                            0.433285274926709752275032216939]
    lambda_max(Sigma_0) =  99.094984180889...


Therefore:


    lambda_min(Sigma_0) > 0.43      TRUE   (margin 0.003285..., i.e. 0.76% relative)
    lambda_max(Sigma_0) < 100       TRUE   (margin 0.905...)
    lambda_min(Sigma_0) > 0.42      TRUE   (margin 0.013285...)
    lambda_max(Sigma_0) < 151       TRUE   (margin 51.9)


Sigma_0 was also confirmed symmetric and positive definite.

4. B6 — SIDE-24 PERIODIZATION — PASS, AND UNIFORM IN r


GP-REQ-205 calls B6 "the most important unreviewed step" and requires that an approval "either independently justify that reserve or replace it with a stronger explicit bound." CL justifies it independently, and the result is favourable to GP.


4.1 A pre-registered prediction, and its refutation.


CL-REG-254 section 2.6 recorded, before any of this material was opened, the prediction that "the 10^18 reserve will be found valid at r = r_D and non-uniform below some critical radius, and the Peano route will close the gap uniformly", with the instruction that it be reported "whether it holds or fails".


IT FAILS. THE PREDICTION IS REFUTED. The 10^18 reserve is valid at r_D AND uniform on the whole interval. It is reported as such.


The reason is visible in the source's own comment: GP-DATA-204 justifies its reserve as covering "the finite Hermite DIVIDED-DIFFERENCE and covariance-product coefficients". That is the correct structural reason, and it is exactly the mechanism CL had to supply from scratch for GP-DER-208, whose corresponding comment instead invoked a pointwise r^{-6} audit evaluated only at r_D. On this specific point GP-DATA-204 is the better-argued of the two artifacts.


4.2 CL uniform lemma for the 204 configuration.


Each row of the transformed pin frame T is a divided-difference functional over the two pin abscissae, and each nine-jet coordinate is a pure derivative evaluation at the midpoint. For each, CL computed the least non-annihilated x-degree D and the Peano constant kappa symbolically in the Laurent ring, with a runtime assertion that the accumulated power of r is exactly zero, so every kappa is r-FREE:


    T rows:  D = 0, 1, 2, 0, 1, 3   kappa = 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2
    jets:    D = alpha_x            kappa = 1  (nine coordinates)


Row 5 of T, which carries 12/r^3 and 6/r^2 and is the source of the apparent amplification, annihilates polynomials through degree 2 and has kappa = 2: the r^{-3} blow-up is cancelled by third-order differencing.


Worst-case image-kernel derivative orders and Peano products over the two blocks:


    G block: n_x <= 8,  n_y <= 2,  kappa^2 <= 4
    C block: n_x <= 12, n_y <= 5,  kappa^2 <= 2


so N_x = 12, N_y = 5, with involution numbers I_12 = 140152 and I_5 = 26 bounding the Hermite factors, and the side-24 image lattice summed exactly over the first three shells with a geometric domination for shell >= 4 (the ratio bound is verified below 1/2 at runtime). This yields

Against the declared reserve of 1e-87 that is a factor of 133.6 in hand, with no r-dependence whatsoever.


4.3 The 1e18 factor itself.


CL's own outward exp enclosure gives, for the raw order-twelve nearest-image quantity,


    4 * 36^12 * exp(-(24 - r_D)^2 / 2) <= 1.590199894669e-106,


so GP's stated arithmetic 1.59e-106 * 1e18 = 1.59e-88 < 1e-87 holds, with 6.29x (0.80 orders) of headroom. More importantly, the amplification factor that the CL lemma actually establishes is

so the reserve GP chose is adequate by about 1.4 orders, uniformly in r. B6 PASSES.
