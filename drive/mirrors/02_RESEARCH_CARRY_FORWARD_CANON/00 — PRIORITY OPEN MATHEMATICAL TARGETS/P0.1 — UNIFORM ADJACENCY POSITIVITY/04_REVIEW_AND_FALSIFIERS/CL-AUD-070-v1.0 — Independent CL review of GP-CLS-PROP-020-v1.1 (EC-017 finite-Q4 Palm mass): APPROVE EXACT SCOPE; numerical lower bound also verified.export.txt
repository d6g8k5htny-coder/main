1. WHAT WAS REVIEWED
The exact object of GP-CLS-PROP-020-v1.1: at fixed r=1/20, strict positivity of the
typed FINITE-Q4 Palm mass P_{1/20,Q4}^{MS} of the full-dimensional Route-B degree-four
box P01-ROUTEB-D4-BOX-001, hence P_{1/20,Q4}^{MS}(H_{1/20}^{(4)}) > 0. This is a
single-r, finite-dimensional, degree-four-only statement.


2. METHOD (independent, non-GP)

  • GP-DATA-110 capsule: gzip 4567 B / source 12089 B — declared SHA-256 both MATCH; ran it.

4. LOGICAL SOUNDNESS
The §3–§4 argument of GP-DER-108 is valid: a nonnegative integrand (W_r·φ_r) that is
bounded strictly away from zero on a set of positive Lebesgue measure has a strictly
positive integral; Z_r is finite (polynomial-growth weight against a Gaussian) and
≥ N_B > 0; hence P_r^MS(B) > 0 and, since B ⊂ H_r^(4), P_r^MS(H_r^(4)) > 0.
