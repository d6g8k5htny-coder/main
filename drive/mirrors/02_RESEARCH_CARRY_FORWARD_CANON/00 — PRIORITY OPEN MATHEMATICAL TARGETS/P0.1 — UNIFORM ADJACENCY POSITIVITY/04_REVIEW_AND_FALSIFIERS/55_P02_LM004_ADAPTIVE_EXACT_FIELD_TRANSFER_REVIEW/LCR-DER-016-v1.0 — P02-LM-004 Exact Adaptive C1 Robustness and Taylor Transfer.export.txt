P02-LM-004 — EXACT ADAPTIVE C1 ROBUSTNESS AND TAYLOR-REMAINDER TRANSFER


1. CLOSED CERTIFICATE DEPENDENCY

Thus


    U=(a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04) in R^8,

and F_r^(4) is the exact pin-preserving degree-four gradient vector field of P02-LM-001 on the scaled coordinates, with


    S=(1/2,0),
    M=(-1/2,0).


Define


    F_aux=|a|/8+r|c31|/48,
    F_*=1+F_aux,
    W=F_*/(KR^5),
    sigma=4RW,
    rho=8RW,
    kappa=1/(16R).


Let D be a fixed compact certificate chart containing the closed cone, strip, transit rectangle, endpoint balls, and their boundaries used in P02-LM-001. Let O be an open neighborhood of D.


The authoritative proof of P02-LM-001 establishes the following quantitative facts on its exact hypothesis domain:


Q1 — UNIVERSAL SIZE.


    0<RW<=2/(KR^3)<=1/2048.


Q2 — STRIP BOUNDARY FLUX.
For |X|<=1/2,


    (F_r^(4))_Y(X,W)<=-1,
    (F_r^(4))_Y(X,-W)>=1.

    (F_r^(4))_X<=-3RW.


Q4 — SADDLE AND CONE CERTIFICATE.

    C_S={xi>=0, |Y|<=kappa xi},

These are dependency facts, not new assumptions introduced by this lemma.


2. C1 NORM AND EXACT FIELD


Let F:O->R^2 be a C^1 gradient vector field. Hence DF(x) is symmetric for every x in O.


Assume exact pin preservation:


    F(S)=F(M)=0.


Set


    E=F-F_r^(4).


Define the certificate C^1 norm by


    ||E||_{C^1(D)}
      =max{
          sup_{x in D} ||E(x)||_2,
          sup_{x in D} ||DE(x)||_op
        }.


Assume the adaptive closeness bound


    ||F-F_r^(4)||_{C^1(D)}<=eta,


where


    eta=RW/4.


3. THEOREM


Under the exact P02-LM-001 hypotheses and the conditions in Section 2, the following hold for F:


1. S is a nondegenerate index-one saddle and M is a strict local maximum.
2. The unique positive eigendirection at S is oriented by the exact rule e_u dot (M-S)>0 and lies strictly inside C_S.
3. The selected M-ward unstable branch remains in the exact forward-invariant cone until it reaches xi=sigma.
4. At xi=sigma the branch enters the exact forward-invariant strip with |Y|<W.
5. The branch crosses the central rectangle strictly from right to left in finite forward time.
6. The branch enters the exact forward-invariant contracting ball B(M,rho).
7. The branch converges exponentially to M.


Consequently the exact selected-branch capture event holds for F.


4. PROOF


4.1 Exact equilibria.
The premise F(S)=F(M)=0 preserves the configured critical points exactly. A C^1 perturbation without this pin condition would not prove the theorem as stated.


4.2 Universal absolute perturbation ceiling.
By Q1,


    eta=RW/4<=1/8192.


Thus every constant-order certificate margin has a uniform absolute buffer, while the only deliberately shrinking margin, the central drift, is controlled relatively by RW.


4.3 Strip boundary flux.
For each strip-boundary point, the Euclidean component error is bounded by ||E||_2<=eta. By Q2,


    F_Y(X,W)<=-1+eta<0,


    F_Y(X,-W)>=1-eta>0.


Therefore the exact strip is strictly forward invariant in its horizontal boundaries.


4.4 Central transit.
By Q3,


    F_X
      <=-3RW+eta
      =-(11/4)RW<0


throughout the central rectangle. Since the rectangle has finite horizontal width, every trajectory entering its right section crosses to the left section in finite forward time while remaining in the strip.


4.5 Saddle type.
The quantitative endpoint proof underlying Q4 gives a positive quartic saddle eigenvalue separated from zero by a constant-order margin and a negative eigenvalue whose magnitude is at least constant order on the declared threshold domain. Since DF(S) and DF_r^(4)(S) are symmetric and differ in operator norm by at most eta<=1/8192, Weyl's eigenvalue inequality preserves one positive and one negative eigenvalue. Hence S remains a nondegenerate index-one saddle.


4.6 Exact cone invariance.
Because E(S)=0 and sup_D||DE||_op<=eta, the mean-value theorem gives


    ||E(S+v)||_2<=eta||v||_2


for every segment from S to S+v contained in the saddle region.


On a cone ray v=(-xi,t xi) with |t|<=kappa, the normalized perturbation loss in the xi-axis inequality is at most


    eta sqrt(1+kappa^2).


Using eta<=1/8192 and kappa<=1/16, this is smaller than 1/8000, far below the Q4 axis margin 7/8.


The normalized perturbation loss in either cone-face expression is at most


    eta(1+kappa)sqrt(1+kappa^2),


which is smaller than 1/7000 and hence far below the Q4 face margin 511R>=511.


Therefore the exact cone axis advances and both exact cone faces point strictly inward.


4.7 Selected eigendirection and finite-time exit.
At the symmetric exact saddle, the positive eigenspace is one-dimensional. Strict linear cone invariance forces its positive eigenline into the interior of the cone. Choose the sign by


    e_u dot (M-S)>0.


This is exactly the M-ward branch convention. Along the selected local unstable branch inside the cone, the axis estimate has the form


    d xi/dt>=c xi


for a constant c>0 retained after the perturbation loss. The branch therefore cannot stall and reaches xi=sigma in finite forward time before leaving the saddle ball.


At the section, unchanged geometry gives


    |Y|<=kappa sigma=W/4<W.


Thus the exact branch enters the interior of the exact invariant strip.


4.8 Sink contraction.
By Q5 and the operator perturbation bound,


    lambda_min(-DF(x))
      >=63/128-3/64-eta
      =57/128-eta
      >0.44


for every x in B(M,rho).


Because F is a gradient field and F(M)=0, for x in the ball,


    (x-M) dot F(x)
      = integral_0^1
          (x-M)^T DF(M+t(x-M))(x-M) dt
      <=-0.44||x-M||^2.


Hence B(M,rho) is strictly forward invariant, M is its unique equilibrium, and every trajectory entering it converges exponentially to M.


4.9 Composition.
The selected branch exits the saddle cone through the prescribed section, enters the invariant strip, crosses the central rectangle, reaches the left section, and by Q5 enters B(M,rho). It then converges exponentially to M. Every part of the trajectory remains in D, so no unproved far-field continuation is used. This proves the theorem. QED.


5. EXACT TAYLOR-REMAINDER COROLLARY


Let F_r be an exact pin-preserving C^1 gradient field on O with the same configured equilibria and let F_r^(4) be its degree-four pin-preserving comparison field. Assume a constant C_D>0, independent of r and U, satisfies


    ||F_r-F_r^(4)||_{C^1(D)}
      <=C_D r^2 H_{5,r}(D).


On the cutoff event


    H_{5,r}(D)<=r^{-1},


one has


    ||F_r-F_r^(4)||_{C^1(D)}<=C_Dr.


On REG_r(U),


    R^5<=epsilon_0/r,


so


    R^4<=(epsilon_0/r)^{4/5}.


Since F_*>=1,


    RW=F_*/(KR^4)>=1/(KR^4)
       >=r^{4/5}/(K epsilon_0^{4/5}).


Therefore


    C_Dr/(RW)
      <=C_DK epsilon_0^{4/5} r^{1/5}.


Define


    r_rob
      =min{
          1,
          [4C_DK epsilon_0^{4/5}]^{-5}
        }.


For every 0<r<=r_rob satisfying REG_r(U) and the fifth-derivative cutoff,


    C_Dr<=RW/4.


The theorem therefore applies uniformly in U on the adaptive REG domain.


This corollary is conditional on the deterministic Taylor estimate and cutoff event. It does not assert the probability of that event.


6. EDGE CASES AND SCOPE


PIN FAILURE.
If F(S) or F(M) is nonzero, the critical points may move and the theorem does not apply.


NON-GRADIENT FIELD.
Without gradient symmetry, the symmetric-eigenvalue and contraction arguments require replacement. The theorem does not cover arbitrary nonsymmetric vector fields.


C0-ONLY CONTROL.
C0 closeness cannot preserve endpoint type, cone invariance, or sink contraction. C1 control is essential.


FIXED ABSOLUTE TOLERANCE.
The adaptive domain permits R to grow as r^{-1/5}; RW may shrink as r^{4/5}. A fixed U-independent absolute tolerance is not substituted for RW/4.


THRESHOLD OR REG FAILURE.
The conclusion is sufficient and conditional. Failure of REG_r or the threshold does not imply failure of capture.


TAYLOR CUTOFF FAILURE.
Failure of H_{5,r}(D)<=r^{-1} does not imply nonadjacency; it only prevents use of the corollary.
