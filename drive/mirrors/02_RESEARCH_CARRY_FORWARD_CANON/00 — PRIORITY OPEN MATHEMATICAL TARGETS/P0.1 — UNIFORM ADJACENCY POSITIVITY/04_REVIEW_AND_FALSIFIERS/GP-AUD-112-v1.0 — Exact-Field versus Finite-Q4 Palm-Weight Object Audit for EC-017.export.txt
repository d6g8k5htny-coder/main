4. WHY THE WEIGHTS ARE NOT IDENTICAL


Let F_r be the exact scaled field and F_r^(4) its pin-preserving degree-four approximation. GP-DER-045 defines the remainder R_r=F_r-F_r^(4) and proves only a bound


||R_r||_{C1(D)} <= C_D r^2 H_{5,r}(D).


At the endpoint pins R_r(S)=R_r(M)=0 because gradients are pinned, but DR_r(S) and DR_r(M) need not vanish. Consequently


DF_r(S)=DF_r^(4)(S)+DR_r(S),
DF_r(M)=DF_r^(4)(M)+DR_r(M).


The exact endpoint Hessian determinants and type indicators may therefore differ from the finite-Q4 determinants and types unless a remainder event controls the endpoint Jacobians.


The nine midpoint jets do not determine DR_r(S) and DR_r(M). Hence the exact full-field Palm weight is not measurable with respect to J_r alone.
