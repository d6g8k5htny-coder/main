1. ISSUE


P02-LM-010 v1.1 defines


    barW_r(q,U)=E[W_r^{exact} | J_r=(q,U)],


    tildeW_r(q,U)=E[W_r^{exact} 1_{G_r} | J_r=(q,U)].


As regular conditional expectations, these functions are canonically determined only up to null sets for the jet law mu_r=Law_{Q_{6,r}}(J_r). Therefore statements such as


    tildeW_r(rs,U)=0 outside the declared interval,


and


    tildeW_r(rs,U)<=r^4 K_D(1+|s|+||U||)^4


are initially conclusions for mu_r-almost every (q,U), not literally for every point for an arbitrary chosen version.


The proof uses these statements only inside integrals against the exact jet density phi_r(q,U)dq dU. Thus an almost-everywhere formulation already suffices. Nevertheless, the frozen text is best read with an explicit support-respecting bounded-version convention.


2. STANDARD-BOREL INPUT


The full conditioned-field sample space may be realized as a standard Borel probability space, and J_r takes values in R^9, also standard Borel. Hence regular conditional distributions and regular conditional expectations given J_r exist.


Let mu_r be the exact law of J_r. Choose any measurable versions barW_r^0 and tildeW_r^0 of the two conditional expectations.


3. POINTWISE DOMINATION BEFORE CONDITIONING


On the good event G_r, the exact Hessian remainder hypothesis gives the deterministic jet-dependent bound


    W_r^{exact} 1_{G_r}
      <= B_r(J_r),


where, writing J_r=(q,U) and s=q/r,


    B_r(q,U)
      =r^4 K_D(1+|q|/r+||U||)^4.


This is a pointwise full-field inequality. Conditional monotonicity gives


    0<=tildeW_r^0(q,U)<=B_r(q,U)


for mu_r-almost every (q,U).


Similarly, exact type plus G_r and s>-C_cap(U) imply the upper-endpoint condition


    s<U_r(U)


for every contributing full-field realization. Therefore


    tildeW_r^0(q,U) 1{s>-C_cap(U)}=0


for mu_r-almost every jet outside the nonoriented interval

4. SUPPORT-RESPECTING VERSION SELECTION


Define the measurable interval set


    I_r={(q,U): -C_cap(U)<q/r<U_r(U)}.


A pointwise bounded, support-respecting version may be chosen by


    tildeW_r(q,U)
      =1_{I_r}(q,U)
         min{ B_r(q,U), max{0,tildeW_r^0(q,U)} }.


The modified function differs from tildeW_r^0 only on a mu_r-null set because the nonnegativity, domination, and support properties already hold mu_r-almost everywhere. Therefore it remains a valid regular-conditional-expectation version.


Likewise choose

With these versions:
