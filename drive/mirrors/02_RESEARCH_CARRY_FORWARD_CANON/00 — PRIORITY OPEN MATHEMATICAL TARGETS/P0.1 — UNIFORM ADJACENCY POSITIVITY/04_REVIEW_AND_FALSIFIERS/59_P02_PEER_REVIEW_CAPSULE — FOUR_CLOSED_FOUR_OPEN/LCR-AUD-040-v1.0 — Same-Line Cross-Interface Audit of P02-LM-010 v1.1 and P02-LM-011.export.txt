3. ADVERSARIAL MUTATIONS
====================================================================


The following mutations kill or invalidate the chain:


1. Set barW_r=tildeW_r.
Consequence: the denominator becomes good-event restricted and defines a different probability law.


2. Normalize by integral tildeW_r phi_r.
Consequence: the claimed P_r^{MS}(B_r) quotient is no longer the exact typed Palm probability.


3. Remove G_r from tildeW_r while retaining the Hessian remainder.
Consequence: the pointwise Hessian and r^4 weight bounds are used outside their hypothesis.


4. Replace exact maximum type by quartic maximum type.
Consequence: the upper endpoint is no longer an exact-law support statement.


5. Replace C_T r by C_T in the upper endpoint.
Consequence: the physical/scaled Hessian conversion is wrong and the boundary interval is artificially widened.


6. Claim W_r^{exact} is nine-jet measurable.
Consequence: hidden residual dependence is discarded; disintegration is required instead.


7. Use a normalizer from a finite-Q4 or planar law.
Consequence: object identity and quotient are broken.


8. Omit the finite upper endpoint.
Consequence: the q=rs integral need not have finite polynomial width and the O(r^5) numerator estimate fails.


9. Apply P02-LM-010 to the full threshold event without G_r^c.
Consequence: the exact Hessian approximation is used outside G_r.


10. Omit the square root in P02-LM-008.
Consequence: the REG and H5 Palm exponents are overstated.


11. Use P02-LM-009 with conditioned exponent 6.
Consequence: the square-root transfer gives only O(r^3), which is still sufficient at equality; the frozen synthesis conservatively uses exponent 8. The statement “strictly larger than 6” is sufficient but not necessary. Exponent 6 also suffices for an O(r^3) upper bound if the constant is uniform.


12. Infer independence among B_r, REG_r^c, and G_r^c.
Consequence: unnecessary and unsupported. The proof needs only a union bound.
