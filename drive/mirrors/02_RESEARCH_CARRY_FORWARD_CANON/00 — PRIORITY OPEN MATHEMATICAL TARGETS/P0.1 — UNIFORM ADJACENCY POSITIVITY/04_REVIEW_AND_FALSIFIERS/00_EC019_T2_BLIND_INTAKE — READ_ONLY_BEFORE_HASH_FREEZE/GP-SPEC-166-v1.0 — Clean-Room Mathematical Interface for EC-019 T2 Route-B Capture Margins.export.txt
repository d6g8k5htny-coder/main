3. FIXED CONSTANTS AND PARAMETER BOX


Use scaled coordinates (X,Y) and fix:


r = 1/20
sigma = 1/10
kappa = 1/100
mu = 1/(40 sqrt(2))


Parameter centers and radii:


a: center 0.0025, radius 0.0001
w: center 0, radius 0.001
z: center 1, radius 0.01
s: center -1, radius 0.02
c40,c31,c22,c13,c04: center 0, radius 0.01 each


Thus the primary interval box is:


a in [0.0024,0.0026]
w in [-0.001,0.001]
z in [0.99,1.01]
s in [-1.02,-0.98]
c40,c31,c22,c13,c04 in [-0.01,0.01]


All nine radii must be strictly positive. A zero radius is a full-dimensionality failure before any dynamical gate is evaluated.


4. DEGREE-FOUR FLOW


For every parameter vector p in the full box, define:


F_x(X,Y) = X^2 - 1/4 + a X Y + (w/2)Y^2
 + r[ c40(X^3-X/4)/6 + c31(3X^2-1/4)Y/6 + c22 X Y^2/2 + c13 Y^3/6 ].


F_y(X,Y) = a(X^2-1/4)/2 + sY + wXY + (z/2)Y^2
 + r[ c31(X^3-X/4)/6 + c22 X^2Y/2 + c13 XY^2/2 + c04 Y^3/6 ].


The symmetric Jacobian/Hessian entries are:


J11 = 2X + aY + r[ c40(3X^2-1/4)/6 + c31XY + c22Y^2/2 ].


J12 = aX + wY + r[ c31(3X^2-1/4)/6 + c22XY + c13Y^2/2 ].


J22 = s + wX + zY + r[ c22X^2/2 + c13XY + c04Y^2/2 ].


Every expression must be enclosed over the complete declared parameter and state intervals. Point sampling is not sufficient.


5. WITNESS AND STRIP QUANTITIES


Let interval absolute value mean a rigorous enclosure and let supabs(I)=max(|lower(I)|,|upper(I)|).


Define:


F = |a|/8 + r|c31|/48.
A = (|z| + r|c13|/2) F.
B = r|c04| F^2/2.
C = |w|/2 + mu + r|c22|/8 + A/mu + B/mu^2.
W = F/mu.


Required threshold margin:


m_threshold = lower(-s-C).


6. ENDPOINT TYPING AND UNSTABLE-SLOPE GATES


At the saddle S=(+1/2,0), define:


A_S = 1 + r c40/12.
B_S = a/2 + r c31/12.
D_S = s + w/2 + r c22/8.
det_S = A_S D_S - B_S^2.


At the maximum M=(-1/2,0), define:


A_M = -1 + r c40/12.
B_M = -a/2 + r c31/12.
D_M = s - w/2 + r c22/8.
det_M = A_M D_M - B_M^2.


Define the axial gap:


g = A_S-D_S.


If lower(g)<=0, the cone-slope gate fails. Otherwise define:


tau_upper = supabs(B_S)/lower(g).


Required margins:


m_saddle_det = -upper(det_S).
m_max_A = -upper(A_M).
m_max_D = -upper(D_M).
m_max_det = lower(det_M).
m_axial = lower(g).
m_cone_slope = kappa - 2 tau_upper.


7. S-SIDE CONE AND HANDOFF GATES


Use xi in [0,sigma] and t in [-kappa,kappa], with X=1/2-xi and Y=t xi.


After algebraically cancelling the continuous xi quotient, define:


Q_x(xi,t) = [
 12 a t xi - 6 a t
 - 2 c13 r t^3 xi^2
 + 6 c22 r t^2 xi^2 - 3 c22 r t^2 xi
 - 6 c31 r t xi^2 + 6 c31 r t xi - c31 r t
 + 2 c40 r xi^2 - 3 c40 r xi + c40 r
 - 6 t^2 w xi - 12 xi + 12
]/12.


Q_y(xi,t) = [
 12 a xi - 12 a
 + 4 c04 r t^3 xi^2
 - 12 c13 r t^2 xi^2 + 6 c13 r t^2 xi
 + 12 c22 r t xi^2 - 12 c22 r t xi + 3 c22 r t
 - 4 c31 r xi^2 + 6 c31 r xi - 2 c31 r
 + 24 s t + 12 t^2 xi z - 24 t w xi + 12 t w
]/24.


Required margins:


m_cone_axis = lower(Q_x([0,sigma],[-kappa,kappa])).


m_cone_upper = lower( kappa Q_x([0,sigma],kappa) - Q_y([0,sigma],kappa) ).


m_cone_lower = lower( kappa Q_x([0,sigma],-kappa) + Q_y([0,sigma],-kappa) ).


m_handoff = lower(W)/2 - kappa sigma.


The quotient must be evaluated through these polynomial expressions, including xi=0. Dividing numerically by xi and dropping xi=0 is not accepted.


8. CENTRAL STRIP GATES


Set:


X_c = [-1/2+sigma, 1/2-sigma].
Y_top = W as its full positive interval.
Y_bottom = -W as its full negative interval.
Y_strip = [-upper(W), upper(W)].


Required margins:


m_strip_top = lower( -F_y(X_c,Y_top) ).
m_strip_bottom = lower( F_y(X_c,Y_bottom) ).
m_central_drift = lower( -F_x(X_c,Y_strip) ).
m_transverse_contraction = -upper( J22(X_c,Y_strip) ).


9. M-SIDE CAPTURE-BALL AND SINK GATES


Define the interval:


rho = 2 sqrt(sigma^2 + W^2),


and use rho_u=upper(rho). Set:


X_cap = [-1/2-rho_u, -1/2+rho_u].
Y_cap = [-rho_u,rho_u].


Evaluate J11,J12,J22 over X_cap x Y_cap and the full parameter box. Let:


offdiag = supabs(J12).


Required margins:


m_chart_X = 3/4 - (1/2+rho_u).
m_chart_Y = 1-rho_u.
m_gersh_1 = lower(-J11) - offdiag.
m_gersh_2 = lower(-J22) - offdiag.


Positive Gershgorin margins certify uniform positive definiteness of -DF over the rectangular enclosure of the capture ball.
