1. PURPOSE


The load-bearing imported step in GP-DER-197 is not the ordinary existence of an unstable manifold for one vector field. It is the stronger local statement needed to construct a measurable branch selector across a neighborhood of field parameters:


- the equilibrium S_r is fixed by the exact gradient pins;
- the Jacobian at S_r remains hyperbolic with a one-dimensional unstable subspace;
- the local unstable manifold can be parameterized on one fixed small interval;
- after a continuous orientation convention, the local parameterization or a selected positive-side point depends continuously on the field parameter in the C1 vector-field topology.


This note maps that requirement to primary invariant-manifold literature and gives the exact reduction a reviewer must verify. It does not replace a theorem proof or a line-by-line source check.

Springer, 1977.
DOI: 10.1007/BFb0092042
Publisher page: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/BFb0092042


Relevant architecture:

Application reduction:
A hyperbolic fixed point is a zero-dimensional compact normally hyperbolic invariant manifold. The exact pin condition fixes S_r for every field in the local parameter set. The local unstable manifold is the unstable plaque of that zero-dimensional normally hyperbolic invariant set. The graph-transform/section-theorem construction is the natural source for persistence and continuous dependence under C1-small perturbations.

6. EXACT REDUCTION FROM LM013 TO A STANDARD THEOREM


A reviewer should verify the following chain rather than accepting the phrase “standard parameter-dependent unstable-manifold theorem” without expansion.


R1 — LOCAL COORDINATES.
Choose one smooth coordinate chart around the fixed physical saddle S_r. Exact gradient pins imply S_r is an equilibrium for every field in the pin fiber.


R2 — PARAMETER TO VECTOR FIELD.
The parameter space is a neighborhood in the C3 pin fiber. The map


    f -> X_f=grad f


is continuous into C2 vector fields and therefore into C1 vector fields on the fixed local chart.


R3 — TIME-TAU MAP OPTION.
For sufficiently small fixed tau>0, the local time-tau maps Phi_f^tau depend continuously on f in the C1 topology on a fixed local neighborhood. S_r is a fixed point of every Phi_f^tau.


This converts the vector-field problem to the diffeomorphism version of the local unstable-manifold theorem if the selected source is stated for maps.


R4 — UNIFORM HYPERBOLIC SPLITTING.
At the base field f_0, the saddle has one positive and one negative eigenvalue. By continuity and restriction to a sufficiently small field neighborhood, the spectral gap persists, and the stable/unstable splitting varies continuously.


R5 — LOCAL UNSTABLE GRAPH.
Apply a graph-transform, section-theorem, or parameterized stable-manifold theorem to obtain a local unstable disc W^u_loc(f) represented as a graph over the one-dimensional unstable subspace on one fixed radius after shrinking the field neighborhood.


R6 — CONTINUOUS DEPENDENCE.
Verify that the graph function, embedded disc, or chosen parameterization depends continuously on f in a topology strong enough that evaluation at one fixed small positive parameter is continuous.


R7 — ORIENTATION.
Use the continuous unstable projector


    P_u(H_S(f))


and the displacement d_r=M_r-S_r to orient the unstable line by


    e_u(f)=P_u(H_S(f))d_r / ||P_u(H_S(f))d_r||.


Nonorthogonality guarantees the denominator is nonzero. This removes eigenvector sign ambiguity continuously.


R8 — FIXED POSITIVE-SIDE POINT.
Reparameterize the local unstable graph so its tangent at zero is e_u(f), then select the point at one fixed sufficiently small positive parameter eta. Continuous dependence of the parameterization gives a continuous local selected point b_U(f).


R9 — COUNTABLE GLOBALIZATION.
The standard theorem is needed only locally in field space. Second countability and a countable cover globalize the local continuous selectors into the Borel selector used in GP-DER-197.


7. MINIMAL THEOREM STATEMENT NEEDED


The external reviewer should either identify or prove a theorem equivalent to:


Let P be a metric parameter space, let U be a neighborhood of 0 in R^2, and let X_p be a family of C1 vector fields on U depending continuously on p in the C1 norm. Assume X_p(0)=0 for every p near p_0 and DX_{p_0}(0) is hyperbolic with a one-dimensional unstable subspace. Then, after shrinking the parameter neighborhood and U, there exist eta>0 and local unstable parameterizations


    w_p:(-eta,eta)->U


such that:


- w_p(0)=0;
- w_p((-eta,eta)) is the local unstable manifold of 0 for X_p;
- w_p is C1 in the curve variable;
- p -> w_p is continuous in a topology implying pointwise continuity;
- the tangent w_p'(0) spans the unstable subspace;
- after imposing a continuous orientation of that subspace, the sign of w_p may be chosen continuously.


A theorem for time-tau maps is acceptable if the reviewer verifies that its local unstable manifold is the same local unstable manifold of the flow and that the time-tau maps depend continuously on the field parameter in the required C1 topology.


8. POSSIBLE WEAKENING


GP-DER-197 does not require a globally continuous branch selector. It needs only:


- local continuous selected points on a countable cover;
- a Borel global selector obtained by disjoint stratification.


Therefore an external theorem proving local continuous dependence is sufficient. A proof of global continuous dependence over the entire strict typed domain would be unnecessarily strong.


9. FALSIFIERS


The source application fails or requires amendment if:

- dependence is only upper semicontinuous and does not support local point selection;
- the allowed parameter topology is weaker than the C1 vector-field topology actually available;

- orientation cannot be chosen continuously on the declared nonorthogonal neighborhood;
- the time-tau map theorem does not identify the flow’s positive unstable branch;
- or evaluation at a fixed local parameter is not continuous under the theorem’s manifold topology.
