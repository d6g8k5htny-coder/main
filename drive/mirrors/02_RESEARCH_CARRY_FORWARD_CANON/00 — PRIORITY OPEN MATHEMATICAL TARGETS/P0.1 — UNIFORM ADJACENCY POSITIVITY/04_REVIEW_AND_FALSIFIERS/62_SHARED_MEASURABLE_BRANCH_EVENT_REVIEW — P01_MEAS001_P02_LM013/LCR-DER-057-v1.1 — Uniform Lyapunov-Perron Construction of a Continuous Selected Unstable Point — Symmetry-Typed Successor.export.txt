UNIFORM LYAPUNOV–PERRON SELECTED-POINT LEMMA


1. ABSTRACT SETTING


Let P be a metric space, let p_0 in P, and let B_bar_rho^2 be the closed Euclidean ball of radius bar_rho about 0 in R^2. For p in a neighborhood of p_0, let


    X_p:B_bar_rho^2 -> R^2


be a C^1 vector field satisfying


    X_p(0)=0.


Assume the map


    p -> X_p


is continuous into C^1(B_bar_rho^2;R^2).


Assume A_p=DX_p(0) is symmetric and, at p_0, has one positive and one negative eigenvalue. Assume also that a nonzero reference vector d in R^2 has nonzero projection onto the unstable eigenspace of A_{p_0}.


Then, after shrinking the parameter neighborhood and choosing rho>0 with sqrt(2)rho<=bar_rho, the following data may be chosen uniformly:


- positive numbers alpha_*, beta_*, eta, delta, xi_0;
- a continuous oriented orthonormal frame Q_p=(e_u(p),e_s(p));
- continuous eigenvalues alpha_p>=alpha_*>0 and -beta_p<=-beta_*<0;
- nonlinear terms F_p,G_p on the coordinate max-norm ball B_rho^infinity;


such that in the coordinates y=(u,v)=Q_p^T x,


    u' = alpha_p u + F_p(u,v),
    v' = -beta_p v + G_p(u,v),


with


    F_p(0,0)=G_p(0,0)=0,
    D(F_p,G_p)(0,0)=0,


and


    max{|F_p(y)-F_p(y_tilde)|,
        |G_p(y)-G_p(y_tilde)|}
      <= delta ||y-y_tilde||_infinity


for all y,y_tilde in B_rho^infinity and all p in the chosen parameter neighborhood. Since Q_p is orthogonal, Q_p B_rho^infinity is contained in B_bar_rho^2 by sqrt(2)rho<=bar_rho.


The unstable unit vector is oriented by


    e_u(p)
      = P_u(A_p)d / ||P_u(A_p)d||,


where P_u(A_p) is the orthogonal unstable spectral projector. Let J denote rotation through +pi/2 and set


    e_s(p)=J e_u(p).


Because A_p is symmetric in dimension two, e_s(p) spans the stable eigenspace. The frame Q_p is continuous.


Choose eta with


    0<eta<alpha_*.


Shrink rho and the parameter neighborhood until delta satisfies


    q
      := delta[(alpha_*-eta)^(-1)
               +(beta_*+eta)^(-1)]
      < 1/2.


Finally choose xi_0>0 so that


    2 xi_0 <= rho.


2. BACKWARD-PATH BANACH SPACE


Let Z_eta be the Banach space of continuous functions


    z:(-infinity,0] -> R^2


with finite norm


    ||z||_eta
      = sup_{t<=0} e^{-eta t} ||z(t)||_infinity.


If ||z||_eta<=R, then


    ||z(t)||_infinity <= R e^{eta t} <= R


for every t<=0, and z(t)->0 as t->-infinity.


Let


    R_0=2 xi_0


and let K be the closed ball


    K={z in Z_eta: ||z||_eta<=R_0}.


Every path in K remains in the coordinate ball B_rho^infinity.


3. LYAPUNOV–PERRON OPERATOR


For p in the chosen parameter neighborhood and xi in [-xi_0,xi_0], define T_{p,xi}:K->Z_eta by


    (T_{p,xi}z)_u(t)
      = e^{alpha_p t} xi
        - integral_t^0
            e^{alpha_p(t-s)} F_p(z(s)) ds,


    (T_{p,xi}z)_v(t)
      = integral_{-infinity}^t
            e^{-beta_p(t-s)} G_p(z(s)) ds,


for t<=0.


The improper stable integral converges because


    |G_p(z(s))|
      <= delta ||z(s)||_infinity
      <= delta R_0 e^{eta s}


and beta_p+eta>=beta_*+eta>0.


4. UNIFORM CONTRACTION ESTIMATE


For z,z_tilde in K and t<=0,


    e^{-eta t}
    |(T_{p,xi}z-T_{p,xi}z_tilde)_u(t)|


is at most


    delta ||z-z_tilde||_eta
      integral_t^0
        e^{-(alpha_p-eta)(s-t)} ds


and hence at most


    delta (alpha_*-eta)^(-1)
      ||z-z_tilde||_eta.


Likewise,


    e^{-eta t}
    |(T_{p,xi}z-T_{p,xi}z_tilde)_v(t)|


is at most


    delta ||z-z_tilde||_eta
      integral_{-infinity}^t
        e^{-(beta_p+eta)(t-s)} ds


and hence at most


    delta (beta_*+eta)^(-1)
      ||z-z_tilde||_eta.


Therefore


    ||T_{p,xi}z-T_{p,xi}z_tilde||_eta
      <= q ||z-z_tilde||_eta,


where q<1/2 is independent of p and xi.


5. SELF-MAP ESTIMATE


Since F_p(0)=G_p(0)=0, the same estimates with z_tilde=0 give


    ||T_{p,xi}z||_eta
      <= |xi| + q ||z||_eta.


For z in K and |xi|<=xi_0,


    ||T_{p,xi}z||_eta
      <= xi_0 + q(2xi_0)
      <= 2xi_0
      =R_0.


Thus T_{p,xi} maps K into K and is a contraction with one common constant q<1.


6. EXISTENCE AND UNIQUENESS OF THE BACKWARD ORBIT


By the Banach fixed-point theorem, for every p in the parameter neighborhood and every |xi|<=xi_0 there is a unique


    z_{p,xi} in K


satisfying


    T_{p,xi}z_{p,xi}=z_{p,xi}.


The fixed point obeys


    (z_{p,xi})_u(0)=xi


and


    z_{p,xi}(t)->0


as t->-infinity.


Because the integrands are continuous and exponentially integrable, differentiating the two integral equations gives


    u'(t)=alpha_p u(t)+F_p(u(t),v(t)),
    v'(t)=-beta_p v(t)+G_p(u(t),v(t)).


Hence z_{p,xi} is a classical backward solution of the coordinate vector field. It remains in B_rho^infinity for all t<=0 and converges to the equilibrium.


Conversely, any backward solution in K with unstable coordinate u(0)=xi satisfies the same variation-of-constants equations and therefore equals z_{p,xi}. Thus the construction is unique in the weighted local class.


7. CONTINUOUS DEPENDENCE ON PARAMETER AND UNSTABLE COORDINATE


The map


    (p,xi,z) -> T_{p,xi}z


is continuous from the parameter neighborhood times [-xi_0,xi_0] times K into Z_eta.


Indeed:


- alpha_p and beta_p are continuous;
- Q_p and the coordinate nonlinearities F_p,G_p vary continuously in C^1 on B_rho^infinity;
- the linear term e^{alpha_p t}xi is continuous in the weighted norm;
- the exponential kernels are dominated by the integrable functions associated with alpha_*-eta and beta_*+eta;
- and the nonlinear integrands converge uniformly on B_rho^infinity.


For (p,xi) and (p_tilde,xi_tilde), the fixed-point identity and the common contraction estimate give


    ||z_{p,xi}-z_{p_tilde,xi_tilde}||_eta


at most


    (1-q)^(-1)
    ||T_{p,xi}z_{p_tilde,xi_tilde}
      -T_{p_tilde,xi_tilde}z_{p_tilde,xi_tilde}||_eta.


The right-hand side tends to zero as


    (p,xi)->(p_tilde,xi_tilde).


Therefore


    (p,xi) -> z_{p,xi}


is continuous in Z_eta.


8. CONTINUOUS LOCAL UNSTABLE GRAPH


Define


    h_p(xi)=(z_{p,xi})_v(0).


Evaluation at t=0 is continuous on Z_eta, so


    (p,xi) -> h_p(xi)


is continuous. Also h_p(0)=0 because the zero path is the unique fixed point for xi=0.


The local unstable set represented by backward trajectories in K is the graph


    W^u_loc(p)
      ={Q_p(xi,h_p(xi)):
        |xi|<=xi_0}.


Every point on this graph has a backward orbit remaining in the local ball and tending to the equilibrium. The first coordinate is xi, so the graph is injectively parameterized and the positive half


    W^{u,+}_loc(p)
      ={Q_p(xi,h_p(xi)):
        0<xi<=xi_0}


is connected. Moreover this graph is tangent to the oriented unstable direction e_u(p). To see this, let


    C_q=(1-q)^(-1).


The fixed-point estimate gives


    ||z_{p,xi}||_eta<=C_q|xi|.


For fixed p define


    omega_p(a)
      = sup{||D(F_p,G_p)(y)||:
             ||y||_infinity<=a}.


Since D(F_p,G_p)(0)=0, omega_p(a)->0 as a->0. The stable integral at t=0 gives


    |h_p(xi)|
      <= (beta_*+eta)^(-1)
         omega_p(C_q|xi|)
         C_q|xi|.


Hence h_p(xi)/xi->0 as xi->0. Therefore the positive half is tangent to e_u(p) and is the positive local unstable branch selected by the orientation.


No differentiability of p -> h_p is claimed or needed. Continuous dependence is sufficient for measurable branch selection.


9. CONTINUOUS SELECTED POSITIVE POINT


Fix once and for all


    xi_*=xi_0/2>0.


Define


    b(p)=Q_p(xi_*,h_p(xi_*)).


Then b(p) is continuous in p, nonzero, belongs to W^{u,+}_loc(p), and its backward orbit converges to the equilibrium. Because positive xi is measured in the continuously oriented e_u(p) direction, b(p) is on the M-ward local unstable branch whenever d is the displacement toward the designated maximum.


This is exactly the local continuous selected point required in GP-DER-197.


10. APPLICATION TO THE EXACT SIX-PIN FIELD


Fix r>0 and a base field f_0 in the strict typed/nonorthogonal domain O_r of GP-DER-197. Work in one physical coordinate chart centered at the pinned saddle S_r.


Let the parameter p be the field f in a sufficiently small C^3 neighborhood of f_0 inside the exact six-pin fiber. Set


    X_f=grad f.


Then:


- X_f(0)=0 for every parameter because the saddle-gradient pin is exact;
- f -> X_f is continuous from C^3 to C^1 on the fixed chart;
- A_f=H_{S_r}(f) is symmetric;
- strict index-one type persists after shrinking the field neighborhood;
- the unstable projector and M_r-ward orientation vary continuously;
- and the nonlinear remainder has uniformly small derivative after shrinking the spatial ball and the field neighborhood.


The abstract construction therefore supplies one fixed local radius, one fixed xi_*>0, and a continuous local selected point in physical coordinates


    b_U(f)
      = S_r + Q_f(xi_*,h_f(xi_*))


on the exact M_r-ward unstable branch for every f in the neighborhood U.


Repeating this construction on a countable field-space cover gives the Borel piecewise selector used in GP-DER-197. The remainder of GP-DER-197—global flow, selector independence, Borel path convergence, certificate compatibility, and the exact Palm quotient—is unchanged.


11. THEOREM


For a C^1-continuous family of planar C^1 vector fields with a fixed equilibrium whose linearizations are symmetric hyperbolic saddles and whose one-dimensional unstable eigenspaces are continuously oriented, there exists, locally in the parameter, a continuous choice of one nonzero point on the positive local unstable branch.


In the exact six-pin Gaussian-field application, every field in the strict typed/nonorthogonal domain has a neighborhood on which the exact M_r-ward local unstable branch admits such a continuous selected point.


Thus the local-selector step in GP-DER-197 follows from the explicit contraction above and need not be left as an unspecified parameter-dependent unstable-manifold theorem.
