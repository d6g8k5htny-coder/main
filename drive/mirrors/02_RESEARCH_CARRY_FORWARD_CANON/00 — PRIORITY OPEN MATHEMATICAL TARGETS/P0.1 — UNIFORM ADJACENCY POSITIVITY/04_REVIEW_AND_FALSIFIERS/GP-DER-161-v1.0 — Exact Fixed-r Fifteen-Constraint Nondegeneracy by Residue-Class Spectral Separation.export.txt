3. STRICTLY POSITIVE TORUS SPECTRUM


The normalized periodized Bargmann–Fock covariance has Fourier representation


K(x-y) = sum_{(m,n) in Z^2} rho_{m,n} exp(i xi_{m,n}·(x-y)),


where


xi_{m,n} = (2 pi m/L, 2 pi n/L)


and every spectral weight rho_{m,n} is strictly positive.


For any finite linear combination Lambda of the fifteen derivative/evaluation functionals,


Var(Lambda f) = sum_{(m,n) in Z^2} rho_{m,n} |S_Lambda(m,n)|^2,


where S_Lambda is the corresponding Fourier symbol.


Because every rho_{m,n}>0,


Var(Lambda f)=0


if and only if


S_Lambda(m,n)=0 for every (m,n) in Z^2.


Thus strict positive definiteness reduces to proving that no nonzero linear combination of the fifteen symbols vanishes on the entire frequency lattice.


4. EXPONENTIAL-POLYNOMIAL FORM OF THE SYMBOL


Write


X = i(2 pi m/L),   Y = i(2 pi n/L).


The contribution of the three functionals at x_- is a polynomial A_-(X,Y) of total degree at most one. Likewise the contribution at x_+ is A_+(X,Y) of total degree at most one.


The contribution of the nine midpoint jets is a polynomial A_0(X,Y) in the span of the nine distinct monomials


Y^2,
X^2Y, XY^2, Y^3,
X^4, X^3Y, X^2Y^2, XY^3, Y^4.


Let


z = exp(i 2 pi h/L).


Since h=1/40 and L=24,


z = exp(i pi/480),


so z has exact order 960 and in particular z is neither 1 nor -1.


The complete symbol is


S_Lambda(m,n) = z^{-m} A_-(X,Y) + A_0(X,Y) + z^m A_+(X,Y).


Assume this vanishes for every integer pair (m,n).


5. RESIDUE-CLASS SEPARATION


For each residue c in {0,1,2}, restrict m to


m = c + 960 k,   k in Z.


Because z^960=1,


z^m = z^c.


Therefore, for each c in {0,1,2},


z^{-c} A_-(X,Y) + A_0(X,Y) + z^c A_+(X,Y) = 0


on every point of the affine lattice


m=c+960k, n in Z.


After substituting m=c+960k, the left side is a finite polynomial in (k,n). A polynomial in two variables that vanishes on all of Z^2 is identically zero: fix n, use infinitely many k-roots, then apply the same argument to the coefficient polynomials in n.


Hence, for each c=0,1,2, the following is a polynomial identity in (X,Y):


z^{-c} A_- + A_0 + z^c A_+ = 0.


These three identities form the linear system


[ 1       1       1   ] [A_-]   [0]
[ z^-1    1       z   ] [A_0] = [0]
[ z^-2    1       z^2 ] [A_+]   [0].


Its determinant is


(z-1)^3 (z+1) / z^2,


which is nonzero because z is neither 0, 1, nor -1.


Therefore


A_-=A_0=A_+=0


as polynomials.


6. RECOVERY OF ALL FIFTEEN COEFFICIENTS


A_- and A_+ are linear combinations of the distinct monomials 1, X, Y. Their vanishing forces all six endpoint coefficients to be zero.


A_0 is a linear combination of nine distinct monomials. Its vanishing forces all nine midpoint-jet coefficients to be zero.


Thus the only linear combination of the fifteen functionals having zero variance is the trivial combination.


Therefore the full fifteen-by-fifteen covariance Gram is strictly positive definite at r=1/20.


7. SCHUR-COMPLEMENT CONSEQUENCE

G_joint = [ G_pin   C^T ]

Since G_joint is strictly positive definite, its principal pin block G_pin is strictly positive definite. Therefore the conditional nine-jet covariance

No numerical eigenvalue margin is needed for the fixed-r qualitative conclusion. Positive definiteness alone implies that the conditional nine-jet Gaussian has a continuous strictly positive density on all of R^9.
