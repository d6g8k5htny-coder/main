1. REVIEW QUESTION


Does GP-DER-161 prove, without a floating-point eigenvalue calculation, that the joint covariance Gram of the six endpoint value/gradient functionals and nine midpoint derivatives is strictly positive definite at L=24 and r=1/20?


2. OBJECT AND CONVENTION CHECK


Endpoints are at x_±=(±h,0), h=r/2=1/40. For the Fourier mode indexed by (m,n), define
X=i(2πm/L), Y=i(2πn/L), z=exp(i2πh/L)=exp(iπ/480).


The endpoint evaluation/first-derivative symbols are correctly of the form
z^{-m}A_-(X,Y)+z^mA_+(X,Y),
where A_- and A_+ lie in span{1,X,Y}.


The nine midpoint coordinates crosswalk to the derivative multi-indices
(0,2), (2,1), (1,2), (0,3), (4,0), (3,1), (2,2), (1,3), (0,4),
so their symbol polynomial lies in the span of the nine distinct monomials
Y², X²Y, XY², Y³, X⁴, X³Y, X²Y², XY³, Y⁴.
Nonzero fixed normalization factors and signs do not alter linear independence.


Verdict: PASS.


3. SPECTRAL-POSITIVITY REDUCTION


For the normalized periodized Bargmann–Fock covariance, every lattice spectral weight is strictly positive. Therefore a linear combination has zero variance if and only if its Fourier symbol vanishes at every (m,n)∈Z².


This reduction is exact and does not require a numerical truncation.


Verdict: PASS.


4. PHASE ORDER


Since h/L=(1/40)/24=1/960,
z=exp(i2π/960)=exp(iπ/480).
Its exact multiplicative order is 960. In particular z≠0,1,-1.


Verdict: PASS.


5. RESIDUE-CLASS ARGUMENT


Assume
z^{-m}A_-(X,Y)+A_0(X,Y)+z^mA_+(X,Y)=0
for all integer m,n.


For c=0,1,2 restrict to m=c+960k. Then z^m=z^c and the expression becomes a polynomial in (k,n). Vanishing for all integer k,n forces that polynomial to vanish identically. Because the affine maps k↦X and n↦Y have nonzero slopes, this is equivalently the polynomial identity
z^{-c}A_-+A_0+z^cA_+=0
in X,Y for c=0,1,2.


No density or limiting argument is used; the integer-grid polynomial lemma is sufficient.


Verdict: PASS.


6. THREE-BY-THREE SEPARATION MATRIX


The coefficient matrix is
[1,      1, 1]
[z^{-1}, 1, z]
[z^{-2}, 1, z²].


A fresh exact algebraic recomputation gives


det = (z-1)³(z+1)/z².


This is nonzero because z≠0,1,-1. Hence A_-=A_0=A_+=0.


Verdict: PASS.


7. RECOVERY OF ALL COEFFICIENTS


A_- and A_+ vanish as polynomials in the independent monomials 1,X,Y, forcing all six endpoint coefficients to vanish. A_0 vanishes in nine distinct monomials, forcing all nine midpoint coefficients to vanish.


Thus no nontrivial real or complex linear combination of the fifteen functionals has zero variance.


Verdict: PASS.


8. GRAM AND SCHUR CONSEQUENCES


The full joint Gram is strictly positive definite. Its six-pin principal block is therefore strictly positive definite, and the conditional nine-jet covariance is its positive-definite Schur complement.


The resulting conditional Gaussian law has an everywhere-positive continuous density on R⁹, so every positive-width subbox has positive probability.


Verdict: PASS.


9. ADVERSARIAL CHECKS


Potential failure mode A — phase convention reversed.
Effect: swaps z^m and z^{-m}; the same separation matrix is obtained up to column permutation. No failure.


Potential failure mode B — only two residue classes used.
Effect: insufficient to separate three spatial-support polynomials. GP-DER-161 correctly uses three classes.


Potential failure mode C — z has order 1 or 2.
Effect: determinant vanishes. At the exact L and r, z has order 960. No failure.


Potential failure mode D — a midpoint coordinate contains an unrecorded lower-order component overlapping span{1,X,Y}.
Effect: would require a convention amendment. GP-DER-044’s declared target multi-indices and GP-DER-161’s crosswalk show the nine coordinates are the stated derivative functionals up to nonzero diagonal scaling/sign. No such overlap found.


Potential failure mode E — a torus spectral weight vanishes.
Effect: symbol vanishing only on supported frequencies might not force zero polynomial. The periodized Gaussian weights are strictly positive on every lattice frequency. No failure.


Potential failure mode F — polynomial-grid lemma misapplied because X is restricted to a residue class.
Effect: after m=c+960k, X is an invertible affine function of k; an identically zero polynomial in k,n is equivalent to an identically zero polynomial in X,Y. No failure.
