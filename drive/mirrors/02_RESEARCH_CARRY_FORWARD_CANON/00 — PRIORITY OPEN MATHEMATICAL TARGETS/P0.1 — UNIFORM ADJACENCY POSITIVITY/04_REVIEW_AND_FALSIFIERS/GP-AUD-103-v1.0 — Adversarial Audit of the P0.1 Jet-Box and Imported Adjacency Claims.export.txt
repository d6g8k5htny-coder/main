3. FINDING F1 — THE REPORTED a_r VALUES ARE IMPORTED, NOT MEASURED BY THIS SOURCE


CL-DATA-051 hard-codes


PNC={0.05:8.449730814727972e-08, 0.025:8.157025792561156e-09, 0.0125:1.082671331099101e-09}


and labels their source CL-AUD-049. It does not recompute the corresponding populations or trajectories.


The inference that CL-AUD-050 “confirmed genuine” those original noncapture masses is not admissible. GP-AUD-088 previously established that CL-AUD-050 used a different random-vector dimension/draw stream and therefore was a fresh sample, not a replay of the original CL-AUD-056/049 population. GP-AUD-087/088 also recorded missing solver-success/finite-state gates and unavailable event-source support in the earlier V3 lineage.


Therefore the three imported values may be preserved as historical exploratory numbers, but they cannot support a current claim that a_r is measured, uniformly bounded, or tending to one.


Disposition of the headline “a_r >= 0.99999992 uniformly across accessible rungs”:
- REJECT as a self-contained result of CL-DATA-051;
- QUARANTINE pending configuration-identical, failure-complete recomputation;
- do not cite toward P0.1 or P0.2 proof status.


4. FINDING F2 — 72 TEST POINTS DO NOT VERIFY EVERY CONFIGURATION IN A 9D BOX


The published driver sets


relev=[0,1,2,4,6]


and tests 2^5=32 corners only in those selected coordinates, holding the other four coordinates at the mean, plus 40 random interior points. It does not test all 2^9=512 vertices, much less the continuum interior.


The source comment “Every configuration in B is verified to capture” and the report’s statement that the box is a concrete open event on which every configuration is adjacent are unsupported by that finite design.


GP-DATA-104 strengthens the evidence by testing every one of the 512 vertices at all three rungs with a fail-closed classifier. Results:
- r=0.05: 512 CAPTURE_EVENT, zero other classes;
- r=0.025: 512 CAPTURE_EVENT, zero other classes;
- r=0.0125: 512 CAPTURE_EVENT, zero other classes.


This still is not a continuum proof. A nonlinear flow property need not attain its worst case at a box vertex. Vertex success cannot replace interval, analytic, validated-integrator, or subdivision certification of the interior.


5. FINDING F3 — THE PUBLISHED BOX CLASSIFIER IS NOT FAILURE-COMPLETE


The CL captures function:
- does not require sol.success;
- does not reject NaN or infinite state values;
- has an escape event but no capture event;
- determines capture from final distance at a fixed horizon;
- checks the S saddle but does not explicitly require the M maximum inside the trajectory function.


These are instrument defects even if the reported runs happened to be numerically benign.


The GP audit classifier adds:
- strict S-saddle and M-maximum gates;
- explicit branch orientation;
- solver-success and all-finite gates;
- explicit capture and four-sided escape events;
- FINITE_UNRESOLVED, SOLVER_FAIL, NUMERICAL_INVALID, NOT_FLOW_SADDLE, NOT_MAXIMUM, and DEGENERATE_BRANCH classes.


The all-vertex replay passes under that corrected classifier. This improves the evidence but does not certify the continuum box or exact field.


6. FINDING F4 — THE MASS IS BOX-MEMBERSHIP MASS UNTIL CAPTURE INCLUSION IS PROVED


The Monte Carlo computation estimates the determinant-weighted typed-law mass of the declared coordinate box. It does not independently determine the mass of B intersect A_r. The identity


a_r >= P_r^MS(B)


requires a proved inclusion B subset A_r. Finite test points do not supply that inclusion.


Admissible numerical statement:


Under the declared planar finite-r Gaussian sampler and determinant/type weighting, the coordinate box has estimated mass approximately 0.71 at each of three accessible rungs.


Required qualifications:
- single seed;
- 400,000 draws per rung;
- no reported confidence interval or weight-effective sample size;
- planar kernel with torus images omitted;
- local degree-four dynamics;
- capture inclusion unproved.


7. FINDING F5 — “EXACT-LAW,” “UNIFORM,” AND LIMIT LANGUAGE REQUIRE CORRECTION


The source explicitly drops side-24 torus image terms. Their claimed numerical size may be negligible, but the executed law is a planar approximation, not literally the exact normalized-periodized torus law.


Three discrete rungs do not establish uniformity over 0<r<=r0. They also do not imply a_r -> 1. The data are compatible with such claims but do not prove them.


The dynamics are degree-four local-model dynamics, not the exact conditioned field. Exact-field transfer remains governed by GP-SCHEMA-095, GP-DER-095, GP-DER-100, GP-DATA-098-v1.1, HB-024, and HB-025.
