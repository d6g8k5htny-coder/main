4. B-N1 — PARTLY ANTICIPATED, AND THE WAY IT WAS ANTICIPATED IS ITSELF THE FINDING


This is the most interesting result of the comparison and I want to state it carefully, because LCR-AUD-064 deserves credit for seeing the dependency and because what it then did with it illustrates exactly why organizationally distinct review exists.


LCR-AUD-064 saw it. Its check BG12 is graded "PASS CONDITIONAL ON NAMED INPUTS" and its list of those inputs ends "...P02-LM-006 supplies finite positive Z_r on its domain." Its clarification C4 says "P02-LM-006 is used only for finite positive normalizer on the common small-r domain; the abstract Palm lemma itself is independent of its proof." So the same-family audit identified the normalizer dependency, graded the six-pin application conditional on it, and correctly separated the abstract lemma from the application. That is good work and I do not want to overstate my own contribution.


What it did not do is charge any of the three defects I charged. It did not observe that the citation carries no version and no hash. It did not observe that "on its domain" is never cashed out — and here is the striking part: C4 supplies the missing domain itself, calling it "the common small-r domain", a phrase that appears nowhere in Carrier B's frozen body. The audit repaired the gap in its own prose rather than charging it against the object. That is precisely the same-line reflex the Independent-Eyes Rule is designed to catch: a reviewer who shares the authoring line fills in what the author meant instead of reading what the author wrote.


And no document in the set observed the third defect at all, which is the one I consider decisive: that GP-DER-197, the parent from which this companion was extracted, states the same result CONDITIONALLY — "is a well-defined exact Palm probability whenever 0<Z_r^{exact}<infinity" — and never cites P02-LM-006, while the companion discharges the condition and asserts "Consequently ... is a legitimate exact Palm probability." The companion claims more than its parent on the strength of an import the parent declined to make. The string "P02-LM-006" does not occur in S2-AUD-009, S2-AUD-008 or S2-REQ-004 at all.


So the honest accounting on B-N1 is: dependency seen by LCR-AUD-064, defects not charged by anyone, parent-versus-companion strengthening new to this review.
