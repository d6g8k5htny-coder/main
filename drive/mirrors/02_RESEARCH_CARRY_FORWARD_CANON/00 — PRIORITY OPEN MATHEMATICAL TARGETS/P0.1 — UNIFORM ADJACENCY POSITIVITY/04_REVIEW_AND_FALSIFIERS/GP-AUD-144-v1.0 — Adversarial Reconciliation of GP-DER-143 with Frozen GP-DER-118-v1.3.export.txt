3. LEMMA 3.1 — AMEND


Claim:


Let K be compact, E a separable Banach space, k↦nu_k weakly continuous, k↦p_k continuous, and epsilon>0. If


nu_k(B(p_k,epsilon))>0


for every k∈K, then


inf_{k∈K}nu_k(B(p_k,epsilon))>0.


The conclusion is correct.


Correct proof of lower semicontinuity:


Set F(k)=nu_k(B(p_k,epsilon)). Let k_n→k. Fix any delta with 0<delta<epsilon. For all sufficiently large n,


||p_{k_n}-p_k||<delta,


hence


B(p_k,epsilon-delta) subset B(p_{k_n},epsilon).


By Portmanteau for the fixed open ball B(p_k,epsilon-delta),


liminf_n F(k_n)
>= liminf_n nu_{k_n}(B(p_k,epsilon-delta))
>= nu_k(B(p_k,epsilon-delta)).


As delta↓0, the inner balls increase to B(p_k,epsilon), so continuity from below gives


sup_{delta>0}nu_k(B(p_k,epsilon-delta))=nu_k(B(p_k,epsilon))=F(k).


Therefore


liminf_n F(k_n)>=F(k),


and F is lower semicontinuous. Since K is compact, F attains its minimum. Pointwise positivity implies that attained minimum is strictly positive.


The v1.0 proof’s inner-ball argument is sufficient to prove local positivity and could alternatively be completed by a finite subcover, but it does not as written establish the displayed full lower-semicontinuity inequality. Classification: REPAIRABLE / NO CHANGE TO CONCLUSION.
