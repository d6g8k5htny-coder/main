5. B3 — LIMITING SPECTRAL WITNESSES — FAILS AS WRITTEN; CLAIMS TRUE; CL CERTIFICATES SUPPLIED


5.1 What the artifact certifies. exact_spectral_witnesses() extracts


    block = sigma0.extract([0, 6, 8], [0, 6, 8]) = [[2,-2,-12],[-2,6,12],[-12,12,96]]


and tests Sylvester minors of block - (43/100) I and of 100 I - block. Those two tests are the entire spectral evidence. GP-DER-204 section 3 says of this block only that "Only the (q,c22,c04) block is nontrivial for the extremal spectral bounds", and then concludes lambda_min(Sigma_0) > 0.43 and lambda_max(Sigma_0) < 100.


5.2 Why it does not follow. For a symmetric A and a principal submatrix B, Cauchy interlacing gives


    lambda_min(A) <= lambda_min(B)  and  lambda_max(B) <= lambda_max(A).


Both run the wrong way: proving lambda_min(B) > 0.43 bounds nothing below on A, and proving lambda_max(B) < 100 bounds nothing above on A. The assertion that only that block "is nontrivial for the extremal spectral bounds" is exactly the claim that needs proof and is the thing not proved. Sigma_0 is not block-diagonal with respect to {0,6,8} and its complement — row 0 alone couples index 0 to 6 and 8 — so the reduction is not immediate from the matrix's shape either.


5.3 CL computation on the full 9x9. Exact rational LDL definiteness tests on Sigma_0 - tI and tI - Sigma_0, bisected:


    lambda_min(Sigma_0) in [0.433285274926709708907812679257, 0.433285274926709752275032216939]
    lambda_max(Sigma_0) =  99.094984180889...


Hence, TRUE and now certified:

    lambda_min(Sigma_0) > 0.42   (margin 0.013285...)

6.2 CL uniform lemma. Every row of the transformed pin frame T is a divided-difference functional over the two pin abscissae; every nine-jet coordinate is a pure derivative evaluation at the midpoint. For each, CL computed the least non-annihilated x-degree D and the Peano constant kappa symbolically in the Laurent ring, with a runtime assertion that the accumulated power of r is exactly zero, so every kappa is r-FREE:


    T rows:  D = 0, 1, 2, 0, 1, 3   kappa = 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2
