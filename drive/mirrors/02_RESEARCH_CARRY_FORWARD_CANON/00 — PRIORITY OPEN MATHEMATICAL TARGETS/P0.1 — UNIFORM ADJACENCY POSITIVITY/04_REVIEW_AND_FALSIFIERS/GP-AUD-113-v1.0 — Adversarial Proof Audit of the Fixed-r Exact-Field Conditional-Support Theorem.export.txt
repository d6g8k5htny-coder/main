3. CHECK A — TOPOLOGY AND CONTINUITY


PASS after v1.1 correction.


The constraint list contains derivatives through order four, so C^4 is the natural Banach realization. Evaluation of every pin and midpoint derivative is continuous on C^4. The exact analytic field is a C^4-valued Gaussian random element.


The scaled-field map


S_r:C^4(T^2)->C^2(D^+)


is continuous at fixed r>0. Therefore the capture-neighborhood event is C^4-open.


Mutation M1 — C^2 ambient space with fourth-derivative conditioning:


FAIL. The nine-jet constraint map is not continuous on C^2. v1.0 is correctly superseded.


4. CHECK B — FIFTEEN-CONSTRAINT NONDEGENERACY


PASS modulo source identity review.


Let P be the six-pin vector and J the nine-jet vector. The joint covariance has block form


Sigma_{P,J}=[[Sigma_PP,Sigma_PJ],[Sigma_JP,Sigma_JJ]].


The pin covariance Sigma_PP is invertible. The conditional covariance


Sigma_{J|P}=Sigma_JJ-Sigma_JP Sigma_PP^{-1} Sigma_PJ


is positive definite at r=1/20 by the exact-torus nine-jet reconstruction and GP-AUD-109. Therefore the full joint covariance is positive definite by the Schur-complement criterion.


This simultaneously proves:


- gamma, the J marginal under Q_6, has positive density;
- the fifteen RKHS constraint representers have an invertible Gram matrix;
- regular conditional laws given all fifteen constraints are nondegenerate on the residual directions.


Required outside check: exact ordering and normalization of all fifteen functionals.


5. CHECK C — AFFINE RKHS SUPPORT WITH EXACT CONSTRAINTS


PASS.


Every torus Fourier weight is strictly positive, so every trigonometric polynomial belongs to the exact-torus RKHS. Trigonometric polynomials approximate smooth periodic targets in C^4.


Approximation alone is insufficient because it perturbs the fifteen constraints. GP-DER-112-v1.1 correctly repairs each approximant using the finite set of RKHS Riesz representers and the inverse fifteen-constraint Gram.


Because the constraint mismatch tends to zero and the representers are fixed analytic functions, the correction tends to zero in C^4. Thus each smooth target g_u belongs to the affine constrained support.


Mutation M2 — omit the finite-constraint correction:


FAIL. Dense unconstrained RKHS approximation does not place g_u in the conditional affine support.


6. CHECK D — CONDITIONAL GAUSSIAN SUPPORT


PASS at cited-standard grade.


For the conditional Gaussian law Q_u on C^4, the centered Cameron–Martin space is H_0=H intersect ker L. The support is the closure of m_u+H_0. Since g_u belongs to this closure, every C^4-open neighborhood of g_u has positive conditional probability.


The event uses only a C^2 seminorm on the scaled chart, but it is open in C^4 because the restriction/scaling map is continuous.


This is the same standard support mechanism used in Q0_MASTER's Lemma LB-ARCH package, with fifteen constraints instead of nine.


Required outside check: Banach-space conditional support theorem citation and separability/Radon hypotheses.


7. CHECK E — POSITIVE BASE SET AND DISINTEGRATION


PASS.


A single conditioned jet vector u would have zero gamma mass. GP-DER-112 instead integrates over a positive-volume subbox B'.


For every u in B', p(u)=Q_u(N_u)>0. The kernel u->Q_u is regular conditional and the event definition is Borel, so p is measurable. Since gamma(B')>0,


int_{B'}p(u)gamma(du)>0.


No uniform lower bound on p(u) is required. The elementary measurable-function argument is valid: B' is the countable union of sets {p>=1/n}; if all had zero measure, p would vanish almost everywhere, contradicting positivity at every point.


Mutation M3 — condition on one central u and infer positive unconditional mass:


FAIL. One point has zero nondegenerate Gaussian marginal mass.


Mutation M4 — require an unproved uniform small-ball constant:


UNNECESSARY. The qualitative fixed-r integral is positive without it.


8. CHECK F — UNIFORM ROBUST CAPTURE NEIGHBORHOOD


PASS as a project-specific candidate dependency; outside review remains load-bearing.


GP-DER-106 gives explicit positive margins uniformly over the full compact box B. GP-DER-045 gives robust transfer of endpoint type, eigendirection, cone, strip, transit, and capture inequalities under sufficiently small C^2 potential perturbations of the scaled field.


A finite collection of strict continuous inequalities with positive uniform lower bounds admits a common epsilon_*>0. Therefore the same epsilon_* works on the smaller compact B'.


The exact pins prevent endpoint drift. The local certificate keeps the exact branch in the chart until it enters an invariant exact M-neighborhood, so local capture is the exact global branch-specific adjacency event.

9. CHECK G — EXACT ENDPOINT WEIGHT


PASS.


On E_*, endpoint Hessians are exact Hessians of f, not quartic substitutes. Exact type is preserved by the robust neighborhood. Hence W_exact(f)>0 pointwise on E_*.


The proof does not need a uniform positive determinant lower bound. A measurable nonnegative function which is strictly positive on a positive-measure set has strictly positive integral.


Mutation M5 — insert the finite-Q4 determinant weight in the numerator:


FAIL OBJECT ID. This is exactly the error identified by GP-AUD-112 and is absent from v1.1.


10. CHECK H — EXACT NORMALIZER


PASS.


Under the six-pin conditional full-field law, the endpoint Hessian entries form a finite-dimensional Gaussian vector. The determinant product is a polynomial of degree four. Gaussian polynomial moments are finite, so Z_exact<infinity.


The event E_* has positive Q_6 probability and W_exact>0 on E_*, so Z_exact>0.


Mutation M6 — reuse the finite-Q4 normalizer Z_{Q4}:


FAIL OBJECT ID. v1.1 derives exact normalizer finiteness and positivity directly from the full-field endpoint Hessians.


11. CHECK I — PHYSICAL/SCALED TARGET EXTENSION


PASS as an existence construction, with one presentation improvement requested.


At fixed r, inverse scaling from P_u^(4) to a physical local target is a continuous linear operation. A fixed periodic cutoff equal to one on a larger neighborhood preserves every local constraint and certificate domain. The family depends continuously on u.


The proof does not require the extended target to equal a global quartic polynomial, only to be smooth, periodic, constraint-compatible, and locally equal to the degree-four reference.


Outside reviewers should verify the precise additive/affine normalization in the selected GP-DER-045 convention. A future v1.2 may display the extension formula explicitly, but this does not presently break the existence proof.
