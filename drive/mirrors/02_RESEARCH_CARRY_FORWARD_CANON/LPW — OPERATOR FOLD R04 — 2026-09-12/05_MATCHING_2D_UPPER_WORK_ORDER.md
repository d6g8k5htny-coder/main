# LPW-UPPER-R04 — source-grounded audit of the missing 2D upper half

Status: OPEN work order. No upper theorem is proved by this note. Its observations below are source-based review targets and elementary deductions, not silently corrected historical sources.

## Exact target

For the same fixed 2D normalized periodized side-24 field, b=6/5, six exact value/gradient pins separated by r, Gaussian regression version, endpoint determinant/type weight and elder-pairing event as LPW, prove or refute:

    exists C<infinity, R>0: 1-q(r,6/5)<=C*r^3 for all 0<r<=R.

Only after this and a valid lower theorem are admitted at the same scope may a two-sided Theta(r^3) conclusion be composed. The radius is the minimum of the two theorem radii. A lower positive liminf follows from a lower inequality; an exact limiting coefficient does not.

## U1 — cover every failure, not just one sufficient mechanism

LPW proves a local path event is a subset of pairing failure. That is a lower-bound inclusion. An upper theorem requires a covering inclusion in the other direction. Enumerate preemption, nonconnection, younger-component merging, self-attachment/nonessential issues, and permitted degeneracies under the actual estimand. UB0's branch language and its 'younger-looking but actually older-by-value' phrase need exact event reconstruction, not automatic acceptance. Do not replace a direct adjacency event with connectivity in the merge tree.

## U2 — enforce exact pair-Palm weighting

P_r(E)=E_Q[W_r 1_E]/E_Q[W_r]. Ordinary six-pin conditioning plus an unweighted P(typed) divisor is not in general this quotient. C038's numerical P_typed and its assembled denominator must be mapped to the exact weighted measure or replaced. An unweighted rare event may be magnified by W_r; pointwise or numerical resemblance does not establish the transfer.

## U3 — resolve the fixed-error obstruction

C038 displays 4.3*r^3 + exp(-92) + collar. For any fixed finite C, exp(-92)/r^3 -> infinity. Thus this display alone does not yield an all-small-r Cr^3 bound. A fixed positive residual being tiny at two rungs is not enough. Prove exact exclusion, a genuine O(r^3) or smaller bound, or a different covering. Retain the failed display as history.

## U4 — write the exact height integral

UB0 calls a representative-height formula exact on the basis of small Hessian height sensitivity. The correct generic structure is an integral across v in the full window of its joint value/gradient density times the conditional weighted determinant expectation. Small sensitivity supplies, at most, a remainder after a proved uniform estimate. It is not exact constancy. For an upper bound, a true supremum over height can replace the conditional factor, but its error and law must be controlled.

## U5 — continuum and singular strata

Two rungs do not give a uniform radius interval. Derive bounds for the regular region, collision neighborhoods, collars and the full torus exterior, including the singular normalization powers. Spatial mesh convergence is not a continuum enclosure. For a first-moment upper bound, Bonferroni subtraction need not be imported unless the chosen argument actually uses it.
