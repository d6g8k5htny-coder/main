## What this does not establish

These six files are reading copies, not the objects. A text export of a native
Google Doc loses the object's own bytes, carries no declared digest, and cannot
be checked against one; the digests recorded in the manifest describe the export
this repository holds and nothing else. Mirroring is not review, replay,
endorsement or promotion, and none of the status words above was decided here.
Whether OP-PROT-011, OP-PROT-012 or a later record is the live governance rule is
what these documents say about themselves and each other; this repository
transcribes the chain and adjudicates no part of it. Nothing here grades, closes
or discharges any claim, premise or obligation, and nothing stored in this
directory is imported, executed or tested by anything in this repository.
