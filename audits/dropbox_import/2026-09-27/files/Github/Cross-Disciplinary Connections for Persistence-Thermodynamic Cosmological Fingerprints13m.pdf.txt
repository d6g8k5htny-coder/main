
Cross-disciplinary connections for persistence-

thermodynamic cosmological fingerprints

The canonical ensemble Z(β) = Ʃ exp(β·ℓᵢ) over persistence lifetimes is not merely
analogous to established mathematical frameworks — it is an instance of several deep

structures simultaneously: an exponential family in information geometry, a Laplace

transform with Lee-Yang phase structure, an entropic optimal transport dual, and a

Wilsonian RG observable. Seven cross-disciplinary investigations reveal that every major

extension proposed has genuine mathematical grounding, several have exact results

available, and at least three directions (Rényi persistence entropy, topological RG flow

equations, the (σ,β) bifiltration) represent clear gaps in the literature where original

contributions are immediately possible.

A. The Fisher metric is an exact exponential family identity, not

an analogy

The Gibbs measure pᵢ(β) = exp(β·ℓᵢ)/Z(β) over persistence lifetimes is a textbook 1-
parameter exponential family with natural parameter θ = β, sufficient statistic t(ℓ) = ℓ, and
log-partition function ψ(β) = log Z(β). The fundamental theorem of information geometry

(Amari & Nagaoka 2000; Ay, Jost, Lê & Schwachhöfer 2017) then gives g(β) = ψ″(β) =

Var_β(ℓ), confirming the stated identity g(β) = C(β)/β² exactly. By Chentsov’s uniqueness
theorem, this Fisher metric is the only Riemannian metric on the persistence parameter

space invariant under sufficient statistics PubMed Central  — it is canonical, not a choice.

arXiv

The exponential family structure yields a dually flat manifold with e-connection

(exponential, flat in natural coordinate β) and m-connection (mixture, flat in expectation

coordinate η = ⟨ℓ⟩_β). The Legendre transform ψ*(η) = sup_β{βη − ψ(β)} gives the dual
potential (the entropy), and the Bregman divergence D_ψ(β₁‖β₂) equals the KL divergence

between the corresponding Gibbs measures. arXiv  The geodesic distance ∫√Var_β(ℓ) dβ
is the thermodynamic length in the sense of Crooks (arXiv:0706.0559) and Ruppeiner

geometry, measuring total statistical distinguishability between persistence measures at

different temperatures.

The natural multi-dimensional generalization uses full birth-death coordinates: Z(β_b,

β_d) = Ʃ exp(β_b·bᵢ + β_d·dᵢ), yielding a 2×2 Fisher metric G with components Var(b),



Var(d), and Cov(b,d). For this 2D manifold, the scalar curvature R becomes nontrivial and

physically meaningful. Following Ruppeiner’s program, |R| diverges at phase transitions,

positive R indicates repulsive interactions among topological features, and near critical

points |R| ~ ξ^d where ξ is the correlation length. The 1D manifold is intrinsically flat (all 1D

Riemannian manifolds are), so curvature diagnostics require at minimum the (β_b, β_d) or

(β_birth, β_lifetime) extension. Recent work by Le & Yamada (arXiv:1802.03569) defined a

“Persistence Fisher Kernel” using Fisher geometry on persistence diagram distributions,

ResearchGate  while Edelsbrunner, Virk & Wagner (arXiv:1903.08510) developed persistent

homology using Fisher information distance directly as the ambient metric. arXiv  Biagetti,

Cole & Shiu (arXiv:2403.13985) applied Fisher matrix analysis to persistence-based

cosmological parameter estimation, achieving 13–50% tighter constraints than joint

power spectrum + bispectrum. arXiv

B. Baez’s theorem gives the exact Rényi–free energy bridge

No published paper defines Rényi or Tsallis persistent entropy. This is a confirmed gap

in the literature. Standard persistent entropy (Chintakunta et al. 2015; Scikit-tda  Atienza,

Gonzalez-Diaz & Rucco, arXiv:1701.07857; arXiv  Atienza et al. arXiv:1803.08304) uses

exclusively Shannon entropy on normalized lifetimes pᵢ = ℓᵢ/L. ScienceDirect  The stability of
Shannon persistent entropy under Wasserstein perturbations is proven, ScienceDirect +2  but

no stability analysis exists for Rényi generalizations.

The most important theoretical connection is Baez’s theorem (Entropy 24:706, 2022;

arXiv:1102.2098): for the canonical ensemble with energies Eᵢ = −ℓᵢ, the Rényi entropy of
order q equals minus the Jackson q-derivative of the free energy:

S_q(β) = (1/(1−q)) log(Z(qβ)/Z(β)^q)

This provides the natural two-parameter family indexed by (β, q) where β controls energy-

scale weighting and q controls moment-order sensitivity. At q = 1, one recovers Shannon

entropy at each β. The physical interpretation is precise: S_q measures the maximum work

extractable when the system temperature is suddenly divided by q and it relaxes to new

equilibrium. The (β, q) plane forms a diagnostic surface where the Rényi profile’s slope

between q = 0 and q = 1 directly quantifies evenness — if two fields’ profiles cross, their

diversity ordering is ambiguous, which Shannon entropy alone cannot detect.

The ecological framework of Hill numbers (Hill 1973; Jost 2006, 2010; Chao & Jost 2015)

provides mature theory for interpreting the q-profile. The natural Rényi generalization of

Pielou evenness is J_q = H_q/log(n) = log(Ʃpᵢ^q)/((1−q)log(n)), preserving [0,1]
normalization. Different q values sweep from counting all topological features equally (q →

0) to being dominated by the single most persistent feature (q → ∞). Jost (2010)



recommends the Hill evenness ratio E_{1,0} = exp(H)/n as superior to Pielou’s J.

For the Tsallis q-exponential replacement Z_q(β) = Ʃ exp_q(β·ℓᵢ) with exp_q(x) = [1+
(1−q)x]^{1/(1−q)}: Wikipedia  when q < 1, the q-exponential has compact support providing

a natural UV cutoff on lifetime contributions; when q > 1, power-law (fat) tails emerge,

modeling scale-free topological structure. The Rényi spectrum H_q is mathematically

equivalent to the generalized dimension spectrum D_q used in multifractal analysis,

arXiv  connecting the (β, q) framework to multiscale self-similarity in cosmic topology.

C. Phase transitions require the thermodynamic limit but have a

clear mechanism

For finite n persistence points, Z(β) = Ʃ exp(β·ℓᵢ) is an entire function — analytic
everywhere for real β. The heat capacity peaks are therefore crossovers, not genuine

singularities. This is the standard finite-size argument from statistical mechanics. MIT  A

genuine phase transition requires the thermodynamic limit n → ∞, naturally achieved as

the random field volume V → ∞.

The mechanism for a genuine transition is concrete. Hiraoka, Shirai & Trinh (Ann. Appl.

Probab. 28:2740, 2018) proved that for stationary point processes, persistence diagrams

satisfy a strong law of large numbers Springer  — the persistence measure converges to a

deterministic limit µ as V → ∞. Project Euclid  The limiting free energy density f(β) = lim (1/V)

log Z_V(β) becomes the log-moment generating function of the limiting persistence lifetime

distribution ρ(ℓ). If ρ(ℓ) has power-law tails ρ(ℓ) ~ ℓ^{−α} (expected for gravitationally
evolved fields due to hierarchical merging), then Z(β) = ∫ exp(βℓ)ρ(ℓ)dℓ converges only for β
< α, making β = α a genuine phase transition* where the partition function diverges.

Lee-Yang zeros analysis supports this picture. For finite n, Z(β) in the complex β-plane has

zeros at quasi-periodic imaginary spacing Physics LibreTexts  ~2π/(nΔℓ_eff). As n → ∞, this
spacing vanishes and zeros accumulate on the line Re(β) = β*, approaching the real axis to

produce a genuine singularity. Wikipedia  The density of zeros near the real axis determines

the transition order. Unicamp

Cole, Loges & Shiu (Phys. Rev. B 104:104426, 2021; arXiv:2009.14231) defined

“persistence critical exponents” for phase transitions in Ising and XY models, showing

they relate to conventional critical exponents. The three-regime structure of random

geometric complexes identified by Yogeshwaran, Subag & Adler (Probab. Theory Relat.

Fields 167:107, 2017) — subcritical (dust), critical/thermodynamic (rich topology, percolation

threshold), supercritical (connectivity) arXiv  — maps directly to the C(β) behavior, with

β* corresponding to the boundary between topological regimes. Donato et al. (Phys. Rev. E



93:052138, 2016) showed that persistence distributions change qualitatively across phase

transitions in XY and φ⁴ models, arXiv  while a 2025 paper (arXiv:2602.09058) rigorously

proves that Shannon persistent entropy exhibits asymptotically non-vanishing gaps across

phase boundaries. arXiv

D. Free energy F(β) is structurally the entropic optimal

transport dual

The p-Wasserstein distance between persistence diagrams is formally an optimal partial

transport problem ResearchGate  (Divol & Lacombe, J. Appl. Comput. Topol. 5:1, 2021;

arXiv:1901.03048), where the diagonal acts as a mass reservoir arXiv  Springer  —

analogous to a particle reservoir in the grand canonical ensemble. Bubenik & Elchesen

(Comput. Geom. 105:101882, 2022; arXiv:1912.02563) proved that W₁ on persistence

diagrams satisfies Kantorovich-Rubinstein duality and that persistence diagrams form the

universal p-subadditive commutative metric monoid. NSF PAGES  ScienceDirect

The connection to the thermodynamic formalism is structural and precise. The free energy

F(β) = −(1/β) ln Ʃ exp(β·ℓᵢ) is the softmin functional, which is identically the value function
of the entropic optimal transport dual with regularization ε = 1/β and cost c = −ℓ. As β → ∞
(zero temperature), F(β) → −max(ℓᵢ), recovering the bottleneck-like metric; at finite β, the
Gibbs reweighting provides the soft version, exactly paralleling the entropic regularization

limit. The parameter β interpolates between maximum-lifetime selection (β → ∞,

bottleneck) and uniform weighting (β → 0, entropy), mirroring how ε in entropic OT

interpolates between sharp transport and maximum entropy.

The Gibbs measure πᵢ(β) = exp(β·ℓᵢ)/Z(β) is a single-marginal Sinkhorn scaling — the
diagonal of the Gibbs kernel normalization performed in each Sinkhorn iteration. Lacombe,

Cuturi & Oudot (NeurIPS 2018; arXiv:1805.08331) directly reformulated persistence diagram

metrics as OT problems and applied Sinkhorn iterations for scalable computation of

Wasserstein barycenters of persistence diagrams. NeurIPS  arXiv

For cosmological applications, Tsizh et al. (MNRAS 522:2697, 2023) used W₁ between

persistence diagrams as a cosmological discriminant for σ₈. Oxford Academic  Wang &

Chen (arXiv:2412.03806, 2024) published the first paper on Wasserstein gradient flows in

persistence diagram space, using the JKO scheme adapted to PD space for topological

optimization. arXiv  arXiv  This provides the mechanism for studying how Z(β) evolves

under Wasserstein gradient flow, connecting the thermodynamic formalism to dynamical

optimal transport.



E. No exact persistence lifetime distribution exists, but the

pairing problem is constrained

The Feldbrugge-Pranav symmetry (arXiv:1908.01619) states that for D-dimensional

Gaussian random fields, β_k(ν) = β_{D−1−k}(−ν): the Betti number of superlevel sets at

threshold ν equals the complementary Betti number of sublevel sets at −ν. arXiv

Perimeterinstitute  This symmetry extends to persistence diagrams, implying that the

superlevel H₀ persistence diagram (peaks) is statistically the mirror image of the sublevel H₀

diagram (troughs) under ν → −ν. arXiv  Since Pielou evenness depends only on the lifetime

distribution, E[J₀] = E[J₁] for Gaussian fields, confirming the Gaussian null E[ΔJ] = 0 with

fluctuations governed by the CLT.

However, no exact closed-form distribution exists for persistence lifetimes ℓ = death −
birth of H₀ persistence in Gaussian random fields. The persistence pairing is non-local (it

depends on the Morse-Smale complex connectivity, not just individual critical point

statistics), Oxford Academic  and Pranav et al. (MNRAS 485:4167, 2019) explicitly note that

“there is no indication that an analytical description for Betti numbers and other homological

concepts is feasible” along the lines of the Gaussian Kinematic Formula. Oxford Academic

arXiv  Feldbrugge et al. developed path-integral/graph-theoretic integral expressions

for Betti numbers that achieve excellent numerical agreement, but these are approximate,

not exact. arXiv +2

BBKS (Bardeen, Bond, Kaiser, Szalay 1986) constrains the picture significantly: for

superlevel H₀ persistence, birth values are maxima values with exact distribution n_pk(ν)

from BBKS, and the total number n of H₀ persistence pairs equals the number of maxima —

meaning n in J = H/log(n) is directly given by BBKS peak counts. The death values

(saddle points) require the joint distribution of (maximum height, connecting saddle height),

which remains only partially characterized.

The Dirichlet baseline J_base(n) = 1 − 1/(2n·log(n)) lacks a Morse-theoretic derivation.

It likely represents the expected evenness of a maximally random Morse function, but this

has not been proven. A major recent advance is the Bobrowski-Skraba universal law (Sci.

Rep. 13:12274, 2023; arXiv:2406.05553, 2024): normalized persistence diagrams from

random point clouds obey a universal left-skewed Gumbel distribution for log(death/birth)

ratios, independent of the underlying point process. Extending this universality to scalar

field sublevel/superlevel filtrations would provide the null distribution needed for analytical

predictions of J₀ and J₁. Kanazawa & Hiraoka (J. Appl. Comput. Topol. 8:1649, 2024) proved

LLN and large deviation principles for cubical persistence diagrams ScienceDirect  Springer

— the natural setting for gridded random fields — though explicit limiting distributions

remain unknown. arXiv



F. Persistent entropy and ecological Shannon diversity are

mathematically identical operations

The cosmological framework’s J₀ and J₁ are persistent entropies normalized by log(n) —

the identical mathematical operation to Pielou’s ecological evenness index applied to

species abundance distributions instead of persistence lifetime distributions. Both compute

H = −Ʃpᵢ log pᵢ on a distribution of feature “sizes” (abundances vs. lifetimes), both normalize
by log(n) to obtain [0,1] evenness, and both are sensitive to dominance by a few large

features.

The most direct topological analogue to ΔJ comes from Angulo et al. (Nature Ecol. Evol.

5:1091, 2021), who used homology theory on species coexistence hypergraphs to identify

“coexistence holes” — topological discontinuities where assembly and disassembly

processes have different homological structure. PubMed  Nature  Assembly holes

(adding species triggers breakdown) and disassembly holes (removing species causes

cascades) have different frequencies and dimensions, creating a structural asymmetry

directly paralleling J₀ ≠ J₁. NSF PAGES

The “topological second law” (monotonic ΔJ decrease under structure formation) has a

genuine thermodynamic parallel in Odum’s successional theory (1969) augmented by the

Maximum Entropy Production Principle. Skene (Ecol. Modelling 250:287, 2013) showed

succession follows MEPP with entropy production following a logistic curve toward climax.

Annila & Salthe (BioSystems, 2010) cast succession explicitly through the second law:

systems evolve to maximize energy dispersal, creating increasingly structured

configurations. The directionality is analogous — both gravitational structure formation and

ecological succession are irreversible self-organizing processes with monotonically evolving

topological signatures.

Landscape ecology’s threshold-based habitat patch framework is operationally identical

to superlevel persistence without the mathematical formalization. Varying a tree-cover

threshold to define habitat patches is exactly a superlevel filtration. Sena et al. (2021)

explicitly applied H₀ persistence to MODIS satellite NDVI data ScienceDirect  — the same

mathematical operation as the cosmological framework applied to vegetation density rather

than matter density. The rasterdiv  R package (Rocchini et al. 2021) computes Pielou’s J

on raster satellite imagery, making it a direct computational parallel. Pielou’s J remains

widely used in ecology but faces criticism (Alroy 2025 argues evenness indices are “overly

sensitive to dominant species”); the Hill numbers framework is now preferred, reinforcing

the case for Rényi generalization. ADS



G. Gaussian smoothing is Wilsonian RG — exactly, not by

analogy

The smoothed field X̃(σ,p) = exp(−σ²p²)φ̃(p) is identical to applying a Gaussian UV regulator

with cutoff Λ = 1/σ. The smoothing flow ∂X/∂τ = ∇²X (with τ = σ²/2) is the heat equation, and

Sonoda & Suzuki (PTEP 2019; arXiv:1901.05169) proved that correlation functions of

diffused fields with respect to the bare action exactly equal those of bare fields with respect

to the Wilsonian effective action Oxford Academic  at cutoff Λ² = 1/2τ. Rosten (Phys. Rept.

511:177, 2012) showed that the Polchinski exact RG equation in the local potential

approximation reduces to the heat equation arXiv  ScienceDirect  for free fields;

University of Cambridge  Rabambi (arXiv:2406.12523, 2024) confirmed this explicitly for both

bosonic and fermionic cases. arXiv  This is not an analogy — it is a mathematical identity

for Gaussian fields.

Persistence has been used as a phase transition diagnostic in lattice gauge theory,

though not yet formally as an RG flow observable. Sale & Lucini (Phys. Rev. D 107:034501,

2023; arXiv:2211.16273) used persistent homology to detect center vortices in SU(2)

configurations, extracting the critical coupling β_c and critical exponent ν via finite-size

scaling of persistence diagrams. arXiv  APS Journals  Spitz et al. (Phys. Rev. D 107:034506,

2023; arXiv:2208.03955) applied persistence to cooled (smoothed) lattice configurations

— the closest existing study of persistence under RG-like transformations — finding that

cooling reveals instanton-dyons obscured by UV fluctuations. APS Journals

No published flow equations exist for Betti numbers or persistence diagrams under

functional RG. The Euler characteristic has a known flow (its expectation is given by the

GKF as a Hermite polynomial in the threshold, with amplitude depending on spectral

moments that evolve under smoothing), but it is power-spectrum-independent — a

universality property. Oxford Academic  arXiv  Betti numbers are power-spectrum-sensitive

HAL  arXiv  (Pranav 2021; arXiv:2109.08721), carrying non-universal information.

arXiv +2  The Bobrowski-Skraba universality (arXiv:2406.05553) for persistence ratios may

be the topological analogue of a universality class, but the connection to RG fixed points is

unexplored.

The (σ, β) parameter space defines a 2D flow where σ controls Wilsonian coarse-graining

and β controls thermal weighting of persistence lifetimes. The function β*(σ) — the locus of

C(β) peaks at each smoothing scale — traces a crossover line in this space analogous to a

critical line in a phase diagram. The RG flow of the persistence partition function is ∂(ln Z)/

∂σ = β Ʃᵢ (∂ℓᵢ/∂σ)(exp(βℓᵢ)/Z), where features killed by smoothing contribute negatively and
enhanced features contribute positively. This 2D (σ, β) bifiltration has not been studied

anywhere — it represents a novel contribution combining multiparameter persistence theory

with exact RG formalism.



Conclusion: a hierarchy of testable extensions with decreasing

speculative content

Three findings carry the force of mathematical identity rather than analogy. The Fisher

metric g(β) = Var(ℓ) is an exact consequence of exponential family structure, inheriting all of
Amari’s dually flat geometry, Chentsov uniqueness, PubMed Central  and Ruppeiner

thermodynamic interpretation. arXiv  Baez’s theorem S_q = log(Z(qβ)/Z(β)^q)/(1−q)

provides the exact Rényi–free energy bridge, making the (β, q) diagnostic surface a well-

defined mathematical object. And the Sonoda-Suzuki gradient flow correspondence makes

Gaussian smoothing literally identical to Wilsonian RG Oxford Academic  for free fields.

Three directions have strong but incomplete theoretical grounding. Phase transitions in Z(β)

require the thermodynamic limit n → ∞, but the Hiraoka-Shirai-Trinh convergence theorem

Project Euclid  plus expected power-law tails in gravitationally evolved ρ(ℓ) provide a
concrete mechanism for genuine singularities at β* — testable by computing Lee-Yang

zeros from N-body simulation persistence data. The optimal transport duality is

structural (F(β) is the softmin/entropic dual), though a full Kantorovich-dual formulation

relating F(β) to a Wasserstein distance between specific measures remains to be

constructed. Tlacombe  The Morse-theoretic derivation of the Dirichlet baseline and exact

predictions for J₀, J₁ remain open problems, but BBKS constrains n and the Bobrowski-

Skraba universality provides a promising null distribution.

The most immediately actionable extension is the Rényi persistent entropy J_q — confirmed

as a gap in the literature, with exact machinery available from both Baez’s theorem and the

Hill numbers framework, and stability analysis templates from Atienza et al.’s Shannon case.

ScienceDirect  arXiv  The most speculative but highest-ceiling direction is the (σ, β) RG

bifiltration, where establishing flow equations for persistence-based observables under

exact RG would connect topological data analysis to quantum field theory in a

fundamentally new way.
