The single authoritative carrier of the machine language: words, syntax,

registers, and counting. Consolidates and supersedes MPV3_LEXICON

(r3.1.0) and MPV3_GRAMMATICA (Edition 2.0), both retained authority-free

CHANGE RECORD — ADJUDICATION OF THE SRM REVIEW,

AND ONE CAESUM OF OUR OWN

An external review (the “SRM” proposal) read the lexicon and the first edition of the

grammar — it says so itself, and correctly declined to pretend it held the Edition-2 carrier —

and proposed the language’s next layer. Verdicts, in the house style: adoptions named,

corrections argued, and the review’s own errors caught by the program’s own rules. The

review’s input arrived truncated mid-section; this adjudication covers what arrived.

ADOPTED, with sources credited. R1 — The ratiomen. The unit of the language is not a

sentence; it is the smallest syntactically complete binding act whose meaning is fixed

entirely by its lexemes, delimiters, junctors, references, fields, and position. Named per the

review’s coinage, in the lexicon’s own -men artifact morphology: ratiomen (pl. ratiomina).

§4. R2 — LEX RELATIONIS. No relation between load-bearing objects may be inferred from

prose proximity; every relation is written by a declared operator. The junctor doctrine,

universalized into a foundational law. §1-A12. R3 — NODUM. A parenthesized junctor group

is a constructed typed object — a bounded semantic graph whose single internal junctor

determines its class — not decoration. With the review’s nesting insight taken to its

conclusion in the counting algebra (§6), including the ruling the review left open: (A~

(B+C))  is not “possibly illegal”; it is a type error, because constirpal operands must be

atoms or constirpal noda — constirpal absorbs, distirpal composes (§6.4). R4 — CANON.

A normalization law giving every expression one canonical serialization — same-junctor



flattening, commutative-operand sorting, field ordering, spaceless nodum bytes — so that

CANON(R₁)=CANON(R₂) is syntactic-semantic equality: hashing, deduplication, semantic

diffing, and cross-agent comparison become mechanical. §7. The review’s single best

proposal. R5 — COHAESUM, corrected in mechanism: spaceless nodum interiors are

adopted as canonical serialization inside CANON, not as meaning — whitespace remains

semantically insignificant (authoring may space; the canonical bytes do not), because the

win is eliminating a hashing degree of freedom, and pretending the win is model

comprehension would be claiming more than is known. R6 — ADHAESIO. Bare adjacency

between payload atoms is illegal; A B  is a parse error; the relation must be written ( A:B ,

A->B , a junctor, @ ). Strengthened beyond the review into the atom-closure law: ratiomen

payloads consist only of mentions, identifiers, numbers, strings, math spans, data blocks,

noda, and lacunae — free multi-token prose in a binding line lives inside "…"  or does not

live there at all. §4. R7 — VINCULUM. The colon formally named as the language’s one

binding operator: X:Y  binds Y as the declared value of X, and means nothing else anywhere

in binding syntax. §4. R8 — FINGIUM. A counterfactual enclosure distinct from hypothesis:

FINGE{…}  reasons under posits that may contradict the authoritative ledger — which makes

proof by contradiction first-class syntax: FINGE{NON{#X}} … FINIS-FINGE ->

testimen(contradiction)  is reductio ad absurdum, wearing a wall. §8. R9 — CALOR.

Semantic temperature: declared inference-permission classes 0–3, from exact binding

syntax (calor:0 — no paraphrase, no inferred fields) through structured development,

exploration, and free generation, controlled syntactically per block rather than globally per

agent. §3. R10 — PERMEA. The typed export operator between calor levels and registers —

the only crossing; content moves toward authority exclusively through PERMEA source ->

target ; via:<ritual> , and the SI and FINGE discharges are revealed as specializations of

it. §3, §8. R11 — The echo law. Repetition carries no evidentiary weight — n·ECHO(X) ≡ X —

and evidence plurality is encoded by routes (R1+R2) , never by repeated conclusions

(T,T) . Adopted, and unified: under CANON, identical canonical forms are one object, so

the echo law is a corollary of R4 rather than a separate mechanism (§6.6). The review

proposed two rules; the language needs one. R12 — Positional roles. ^HEAD ^BODY ^FOOT

line-initial role sigils, with FOOT parity strengthened from enumeration to semantic parity:

the FOOT’s declared deltas must equal the BODY’s computed deltas, and inequality is a

compilation failure, not a formatting nit. §13. R13 — REFERUM, the naming of the existing

law: reference is ID- or hash-addressed only; “this result,” “the former,” “above” are

unwritable in binding lines.

CORRECTED — the review’s errors, caught by the program’s own registry. C1 — The

CLAUSTRA / ONERUM  collisions. The review named the bracket doctrine CLAUSTRA and the

mandatory slot ONERUM — colliding, respectively, with claustrum (the peer-review

capsule, already in the lexicon) and with onus/onerarium (the obligation system). The

collision registry (design axiom P6) rejects both: the bracket doctrine is SAEPTA (saeptum,



enclosure-fence), and the slot is LACUNA (the missing piece — the scholarly prior of a

manuscript lacuna is exactly the meaning). The reviewer’s instrument, applied to the

reviewer. C2 — The ::  field separator. The review correctly caught a real overload in

Edition 2 — ;  served as both sequence junctor and field separator — but its repair adds a

digraph with mixed priors. The adopted repair moves the other piece: sequence inside noda

becomes >>  (the pipeline prior: “then, into”), and ;  becomes exclusively the field/peer

boundary, where its statement-separator prior is strongest. One symbol reassigned, none

added; the alphabet stays the same size.

CAESUM — against our own Edition 2 (filed at full parity). D6 — The semicolon

overload. Edition 2 defended the double duty of ;  by position and was wrong to: two

meanings on one token across nesting depths is exactly the micro-ambiguity this language

exists to kill, and the defense was convenience wearing rigor. Killed by the review’s witness;

repaired per C2. The failure archive gains its eighth entry-class instance: definition defects

now lead the motif count by two.

Everything that survives from the lexicon and both grammar editions is restated here in full

— this document is self-contained, says “as before” nowhere, and is the only language

carrier with authority from this release forward.

§0. WHAT THIS LANGUAGE IS AND WHAT IT IS TRYING TO

ACHIEVE

Every protocol this program has written is made of words optimized by centuries of human

use, and every such word arrives inside a language model carrying a massive pretrained

prior for its colloquial meaning — “verified,” “independent,” “matches,” “and,” “not,” “this.”

Definition drift is, in large measure, the prior leaking through the word; binding failure is the

prior leaking through the syntax. This language attacks both, at both levels, with one design:

At the word level: coined tokens with near-zero pretrained prior, so the in-context

definition faces no competition; Latin-derived so a naive reader decomposes toward the

meaning instead of away from it (graceful degradation); morphologically compositional so

the suffix announces a term’s protocol role — and, for pathologies, its propagation behavior

— before the definition is even consulted; performative so that writing a word, with its slots,

is the record.

At the sentence level: the unit is the ratiomen — an epistemic instruction, not prose — in

which separators are typed, brackets are typed, adjacency is illegal without a declared

relation, position confers legal force, hypotheticals and counterfactuals have walls,

mathematics has an inviolate register, references are addresses, numbers carry provenance



or cannot be written, repetition counts as one, and independence lives in the punctuation

where it can be counted but not faked.

At the document level: registers with surface-decidable membership, declared inference-

temperature, one typed crossing operator toward authority, blocks whose footers must

equal their bodies’ effects, and a canonicalization law under which semantic identity is byte

identity.

And the constraint that all of it remain humanly readable is not a tax — it is the instrument:

every exported binding block carries a gloss that must expand from it with zero semantic

residue, so divergence between the registers is machine-detectable drift. The human

channel is the checksum on the machine channel. Two audiences; each keeps the other

honest.

§1. DESIGN AXIOMS

A1 — Code-prior riding. Wherever a structural choice exists, borrow the shape whose

semantics models have already absorbed from billions of tokens of code and LaTeX:

operator-first application, key:value , arrows, $…$ , pipelines, comment markers. Reliability

already paid for beats novelty. A2 — Prior displacement. Where a construct must be novel,

make it rare enough that no pretrained usage competes, and Latin-adjacent enough to

degrade toward its meaning. A3 — Line-initial authority. The first token of every line

decides its legal force, with no exceptions and no judgment. A4 — Closed sets; mandatory

nesting; no precedence (MONOJUNCTUM). The junctor and sigil inventories are closed

and tiny; one junctor family per nodum; mixing requires explicit nesting — (a&b|c)  is

unwritable, ((a&b)|c)  is the only form. Ambiguity is not resolved; it is denied a syntax. A5

— Graceful degradation by design. Every token is chosen so a grammar-naive reading

lands near the definition, under-claims safely, or constitutes a named checkable defect. No

token fails open (§2 table). A6 — Register partition. LEX (binding), GLOSS (speculum),

MATH ( $…$ , inert) — membership decided by surface marks only. A7 — REFERUM. No

anaphora, no deixis, no tense in LEX: identifiers, hashes, and epoch fields. A8 —

Whitespace canon. Whitespace is semantically insignificant except the newline (clause

boundary outside open saepta) and byte-faithful interiors ( "…" , [ ] ); adjacency carries

meaning in exactly one construct — the space-free key:value  VINCULUM pair — because

it is the one adjacency convention with a universal code prior; and CANON serializes nodum

interiors spaceless for hashing (COHAESUM), which is canonicalization, not meaning. A9 —

ASCII canon. Canonical forms are ASCII; Unicode display forms are specula; hashes, grep,

and stability bind to the ASCII. A10 — Gloss as checksum. Exported LEX travels with

GLOSS; the gloss must expand from the LEX with no residue; divergence is lapsura and

machine-detectable. Neither register exports alone. A11 — No assertion outside ledger-



shaped lines. No inline assertion exists. The only doors into the record are CLAIM, EDGE,

STATE, ONUS, and the performatives — lines whose first token confesses what they are.

Claims cannot be laundered through discussion, glosses, quotation, mathematics, or

beauty. A12 — LEX RELATIONIS. No relation between load-bearing objects may be inferred

from proximity, order-of-mention, or rhetoric; every relation is a written operator. The

generalization of everything above it. A13 — CANON. Semantically equal expressions have

one canonical byte form; canonical equality is the language’s identity relation; all counting,

hashing, deduplication, and diffing run over canonical forms.

§3. REGISTERS, CALOR, AND PERMEABILITY

The three registers. LEX: any line whose first token is a performative or declarative

operator (§4 table); enters the record; obeys every law here. GLOSS: any line beginning

// ; natural language; zero authority; LEX may never cite it (DEF-DRIFT — the anti-

laundering wall), while GLOSS may freely mention #ids . MATH: $…$ , legal in LEX payloads

and GLOSS; every symbol inside means mathematics; the grammar reads it byte-faithfully

and never into it; a math span asserts nothing — an equation becomes a commitment only

as a CLAIM payload.

CALOR — declared inference temperature. Blocks carry @calor(n) :

calor:0 — binding. Exact syntax, no paraphrase, no inferred fields, no unstated

assumptions. LEX is calor:0 by definition.

calor:1 — development. Structured derivation; limited inference; no state changes;

SI/FINGE walls available.

calor:2 — exploration. Analogies, candidate bridges, pattern-hunting. GLOSS defaults

here.

calor:3 — generation. Maximum freedom; the idea nursery’s native temperature; nothing

here is even a candidate yet. The point is to stop choosing one behavior globally: the

model’s inferential power is the asset at calor 2–3 and the liability at calor 0, and the

block header says which mode the next lines are in — so “answer at calor:0” is now a

writable instruction with defined meaning.

PERMEA — the one crossing. Content moves toward authority only through the typed

export operator:

There is no other door: no calor-3 fragment becomes a candidate, no exploratory number

becomes a ledger number, no gloss observation becomes a claim, except through a

PERMEA <source-ref> -> <target-state> ; via:<ritual> ; carrying:<posits/residuals>



PERMEA whose via:  names the ritual actually performed (triprobium, rescrutinium,

VERIFONS batch, definition-compiler run) — and a PERMEA whose ritual has no receipts is

fictura, exactly as any macro is. A direct calor:3 → FROZEN is unwritable. Enforcement is by

review and the consistency checker, not by software that does not exist — the K11

confession, standing. The discharges FINIS-SI and FINIS-FINGE (§8) are PERMEA

specializations: exports out of walled worlds, carrying their posits.

§4. VERBA — THE COMPLETE WORD INVENTORY (LEXICON,

CONSOLIDATED)

The generative morphology (minting runs only through this, prospectively, as DEF-

records): roots carry domain — VER truth · FALS falsity · PROB test · TEST witness · FIN

boundary · QUANT quantifier · STIRP lineage · NEX dependency · CAED/CID kill · FONS

source · PACT/PANG fix-commit · OBIC throw-against · SCI/NESC know/not-know · RES the-

thing-itself · POND weight · SPEC view · VULN weak point. Suffixes carry protocol class — -

men/-mentum artifact · -ura structured record · -ium bounded object or ritual · -osis

chronic pathology · -itis spreading pathology (contaminates transitively) · -oma accreting
pathology (grows with downstream mass) · participles -atum/-itum claim-states · first-

person verbs performatives. The pathology suffixes are load-bearing: the suffix encodes

the blast behavior, so three suffixes teach a reader the propagation semantics of the whole

defect taxonomy. Rules: one word, one meaning, bijectively; no synonym minting; ASCII

canonical; collision registry enforced (aligned priors welcome — stirps rides “per stirpes,”

onus rides “onus of proof,” lacuna rides the manuscript lacuna); retirement applies to

unused words.

4.1 Performatives (writing the word with its slots constitutes the act)

NESCIO — the honest-ignorance operator; the most important token in the language. The

only lawful empty; satisfies fail-closed in one token; never a demerit. Filling a NESCIO-field

with generated content is fictura; writing NESCIO where the cited record determines the

value is theatrosis. It exists because pattern-completion is the model’s deepest instinct and

the countermeasure must be one cheap, high-status token. FATEOR ⟨capabilities⟩ — the

session-opening capability confession (exec/web/cas/proof/exact/file/db, Y/N), ceilings

auto-applied; a text-only system can derive, design, decompose, and attack, and cannot

claim to have run or read — saying otherwise is fictura. OBICIO ⟨target : method ->

outcome⟩ — the adversarial pass; mandatory once per session; an OBICIO whose method

could not have failed is theatrosis. OCCIDO ⟨target : defect ; testimen ; first-step ; blast ;

repair ; successor⟩ — files a kill; state allegatum until the testimen re-runs, then caesum;

slots filled or NESCIO. PANGO ⟨block⟩ — commits a pre-registration, producing a pactum;



failed pacta spawn successors by new PANGO, never edits (the rescue restriction as

grammar). FIGO ⟨id : predicates⟩ — the freeze act; void without verified gate predicates, and

the void is filed. SUPERSEDO ⟨old -> new : reason⟩ — supersession, transactional: edge,

reason, and descendant flags commit together or not at all. VERIFONS ⟨tag : record⟩ — full

citation discharge (resolve, access via named tool, locator, ≤15-word anchor, SupportType,

S_cited ⇒ S_used, level, residual); without tooling it auto-degrades to NESCIO[V0-BLOCKED]

and cannot be faked past. RECONSTRUO — blind reconstruction from definitions and

statement only (B3+), I_seen recorded. COMPRIMO — reduce a proof to its dependency-

minimal skeleton, every arrow cited. EXPANDO — unfold every “it follows easily / similarly /

by symmetry / therefore uniformly” into explicit derivation. CLAUSTRO ⟨id⟩ — assemble the

claustrum, the minimal peer-review capsule (claim + stirps + proof + receipts + known

attacks, nothing else). REDDO ⟨dG dS dO⟩ — the closing transaction receipt; empty deltas

must name the blocking onus. PERMEA ⟨source -> target ; via:ritual⟩ — §3; the only

crossing toward authority.

4.2 Structures (record and object types)

ratiomen — the unit of the language: the smallest syntactically complete binding act (§5).

testimen — the minimal re-runnable witness, first failing step identified. stirps — the load-

bearing ancestor set; adjectives constirpal (shared ancestry or result contact —

independence-killing) and distirpal (provenance-disjoint); independence ⇔ distirpal ∧

class-separate ∧ no result contact. concursus — an agreement event between distirpal

routes: the only agreement that earns corroboration; the designed antonym of consensitis.

confinium — the formal scope object (object class, parameters, dimension, structure,

moment order, regime); every claim lives intra confinium. quantisignum — the quantifier

signature as structured metadata, diffed between statement, proof, dependencies, and use

before any freeze. pactum — the committed registration block. vestigium — the execution

receipt (artifact hash, environment fingerprint, seed, timestamp, stdout hash): no vestigium,

no execution. speculum — any derived zero-authority view (summary, manuscript,

dashboard, scalar grade, the §17 card); citing one as evidence is DEF-DRIFT. vetitura — the

auto-generated forbidden-extrapolation list every frozen claim carries. onus / onerarium —

an open obligation / the frontier ledger of them. dominamen — the named domination

witness every limit interchange must cite, or NESCIO and no interchange. saltus — the

certified numerical margin against the total error budget; strict-inequality credit requires

saltus > ε_total. pondus — descendant mass: debt interest, systemic-importance

thresholds, refuter targeting. vulnerarium — a frozen claim’s published attack surface: a

new system is handed the wound-book, not asked to “verify.” nodum, datum, regnum,

lacuna — the four saepta (§6). condicium / fingium — the hypothetical and counterfactual

enclosures (§9).

4.3 States



intactum — internally strong, never struck from a distirpal route (high F, low I/A): the honest

name for an untested strong claim, kept permanently distinct from a verified one. allegatum

/ caesum — kill pending witness / kill verified; only caesum binds. nescitum — a component

in declared-UNKNOWN state. vagamen — work outside any pactum: welcome, preserved,

permanently non-confirmatory.

4.4 Pathologies (the nosology)

fictura — the fabrication family, critical: claiming to have run, read, checked, discharged, or

performed what was not; certificate-less + ; receipt-less rituals; content in NESCIO’s place.

theatrosis — chronic ceremonial compliance: unfailable probes, condition-less falsifiers,

decorative NESCIO, fields filled to fill fields. consensitis — spreading correlated agreement

mistaken for corroboration; constirpal routes agreeing, contamination transitive through

summaries, prompts, and result contact; quarantine the contact chain and recount from

exhibited distirpal sets. quantiflexio — the silent quantifier bend (∀∃→∃∀,

pointwise→uniform, a.s.→expectation), caught by quantisignum diffs. ultrafinium — use

beyond the confinium: scope and theorem creep. lapsura — statement drift under

paraphrase toward the prettier, broader, wronger version; caught by canonical-form diffs.

geminosis — reproduction mistaken for certification: byte-identical replay of a wrong

formula; twin agreement is not truth. falsobiectum — the wrong-object defect: flawless

evaluation of the wrong event, measure, conditioning, Jacobian, normalization, or

multiplicity; the antidote is rescrutinium. pulchrosis — credence elevated by beauty;

indicated treatment is OBICIO, never grade. nexoma — an accreting mass of unverified

dependency whose risk weight grows with pondus.

4.5 Rituals (macro-lexemes; utterance claims completion; receipts or

fictura)

triprobium — the full g₀→g₁ ritual: written refutation target first, then generic + boundary +

adversarial probes at ≥E1, CANNOT-PROBE recorded as a finding. rescrutinium — the

Stage-A object audit before expensive computation: event, conditioning, measure,

Jacobian, normalization, domain, multiplicity — rem tene, verba sequentur. gradascensus

— precision escalation with its named triggers: float → extended → arbitrary → interval →

exact. stratonus — the singular-stratum ritual: decompose Ω into regular/boundary/singular

strata, each with coordinates, dominant scaling, bound, contribution, plus the partition-

completeness certificate.

§5. THE RATIOMEN

Shape. OPERATOR target : payload ; field:value ; field:value  — operator-first; one



ratiomen, one act; the newline is the boundary except inside open saepta (the continuation

law: long records wrap only inside their own brackets, as code does).

Operators (closed set): the performatives of §4.1, plus the declaratives — UTOR

⟨file@hash,…⟩ the language/version pin, the document’s mandatory first LEX line; CLAIM —

the full schema record and the only door for asserting content, mathematical or otherwise;

EDGE #A =>type #B ; via:<ref>  — the dependency graph, writable line by line; STATE

#id -> STATUS ; trigger:<…> ; predicate:<…> ; ONUS — opens or annotates an

obligation; DIFF (#X.vM , #X.vN) -> {dScope,dStatement,dAssumptions,dStatus,dDeps}

— semantic deltas, never line diffs; QUAERO ⟨kind : question⟩; SI / FINGE with their FINIS

closers (§9); ABEST (§10).

VINCULUM (the colon law). The first spaced :  after the operator separates target from

payload; every space-free key:value  is an atomic binding pair — bind-by-adjacency, the

language’s single meaningful adjacency, adopted because k:v  carries a universal code

prior and refused everywhere else because whitespace semantics are tokenizer-fragile. One

symbol, one relation: no colon in binding syntax is ever punctuation.

ADHAESIO and the atom-closure law. Bare adjacency between payload atoms is a parse

error; every relation is written. Payload atoms are exactly: mentions ( #id ), identifiers,

microformatted numbers, strings ( "…" ), math spans ( $…$ ), data ( […] ), noda, claim-sets,

and lacunae. Free multi-token prose cannot occur in a ratiomen outside quotes — thinking

lives in GLOSS; commitments live here; and the boundary is decidable from surface tokens

alone.

Fields. ; -separated, space-free k:v , order-free (CANON sorts them by key), drawn from

the schema vocabulary; unknown values are NESCIO, never blanks, never guesses.

§6. SAEPTA AND NODA

6.1 The four saepta (each enclosure creates a different attention regime)

NODUM ( … )  — a typed relational graph: one junctor family per depth (MONOJUNCTUM),

class fixed by that junctor; the language’s composition mechanism (§6.3–6.5). DATUM [ …

]  — inert, byte-faithful, mentioned material: quotes, logs, search results, other agents’

outputs. Nesting adds mention depth — [A reports: [B claims: X]]  puts X at depth 2 —

and assertion never crosses a datum boundary in either direction: an instruction inside

is data about an instruction (the injection rule as syntax), and nothing becomes believed by

being legible. Use requires a LEX line asserting it with its V-level, whereupon the assertion is

that line’s, on that line’s evidence, forever. REGNUM { … }  — exact operator scope (NON,



SI, FINGE) and claim-sets {#A,#B}  of mentions. LACUNA < … >  — the mandatory slot:

filled, or filled with NESCIO; empty is a parse error, making fail-closed a property of syntax

rather than a policy to remember. Lint: NESCIO in a lacuna the cited record determines is

theatrosis — ignorance is a right, performing it is a defect, and the difference is checkable.

6.2 The six junctors

&  coniunctum — joint bundle, one package: components may not be detached and cited

alone at the bundle’s strength (the Frankenstein ban as punctuation); a bundle binds at its

weakest link, so &  is never an upgrade path. |  alternatum — any one suffices; one

member’s failure says nothing of the others; the shape of closure-route menus. >>

seriatum — order is semantic: steps, causal chains, escalation ladders —

(toy>>small>>targeted>>certify) ; reordering changes meaning and therefore hash. ,

collectum — order explicitly meaningless; inferring priority from position is the reader’s

defect, not the writer’s license. ~  constirpum — the members share load-bearing ancestry

or result contact; the group is ONE source in every independence computation; the naive

reading under-counts, which is the direction the doctrine prefers to fail. +  distirpum —

certified provenance separation; members add; cert:  field mandatory; writing +  is itself

a claim, and a certificate-less +  is fictura — the strongest symbol is the most dangerous to

write, by design, because independence is the one thing this program never gives away,

including typographically.

6.3 Nesting is composition

((claude~gpt)+interval-route)  — one correlated compound source, plus one certified-

independent route: the correlation structure of an entire verification campaign on one line,

uncountable wrongly because the count is the punctuation. Noda nest freely subject to

MONOJUNCTUM and the typing rule:

6.4 The typing rule: constirpal absorbs, distirpal composes

Operands of ~  must be atoms or constirpal noda — a correlation class is an equivalence-

class-like object, and it absorbs: (A~(B~C))  flattens under CANON to (A~B~C) . A distirpal

nodum may not appear inside a constirpal one: (A~(B+C))  would assert shared ancestry

between A and an independence-certified compound without saying with which member —

§7. CANON — THE NORMALIZATION LAW

Every expression has exactly one canonical byte serialization, computed by:

1. Resolve display equivalents to ASCII (⟨⟩→ <> , ∥→paired // , etc.).

2. Flatten same-junctor nesting where the junctor is associative: ((A&B)&C)  → (A&B&C) ;

likewise , , ~ , + , | . Never >>  — sequence does not flatten across intent boundaries

and never reorders.

3. Sort operand lists of commutative junctors ( & , , , ~ , + , | ) lexicographically by each

operand’s own canonical form. >>  keeps author order absolutely.

4. Sort ratiomen fields by key.

5. Serialize nodum interiors spaceless (COHAESUM); collapse all other insignificant

whitespace to single spaces; strip trailing whitespace.

6. Hash the result (SHA-256).

CANON(R₁) = CANON(R₂) is the language’s identity relation. Consequences, each

mechanical where it used to be judgment: duplicate-claim detection (two agents proving

one lemma under different names — merge by canonical equality); theorem fingerprints (the

claim-record hash is over canonical bytes, so cosmetic rewording cannot mint a “new”

theorem and substantive rewording cannot hide as cosmetic); semantic diffing (DIFF runs

canonical-form to canonical-form, so a reorder of commutative operands is invisible and a

junctor change is glaring); the echo law (§6.5); and cross-agent comparison (two systems

disagree about content or they do not — never about formatting). Authoring may be spaced,

ordered, and human; the canonical bytes are the identity; both are true at once because

CANON is serialization, not meaning.



§8. CONDICIUM AND FINGIUM — THE TWO WALLED WORLDS

SI (condicium): the hypothesis wall. SI{<posits, comma-collected>} … FINIS-SI ->

<export> . Everything inside is conditional on the posited set; posits are consistent-
unknowns — things that may be true. Nothing inside may be cited outside except

through the discharge, which is a PERMEA specialization: FINIS-SI -> #NEW-CLAIM  only

into a record whose ASSUMPTIONS field contains the posits (a discharge into a claim

missing them fails lint with a one-line witness), or FINIS-SI -> nihil . A reference from

outside to an undischarged interior line is a parse error: the classic long-context failure —

“suppose X” quietly becoming “since X” two hundred tokens later — is not forbidden; it is

unwritable. SI blocks nest; each FINIS closes the innermost.

FINGE (fingium): the counterfactual wall. FINGE{<posits>} … FINIS-FINGE -> <export> .

Posits here may contradict the authoritative ledger — inside a fingium, contradiction with

a FROZEN claim triggers nothing, because contradiction is the point. This gives

mathematics its native move as first-class syntax: reductio ad absurdum is

FINGE{NON{#X}} … derive contradiction … FINIS-FINGE -> testimen(contradiction) ,

and that testimen supports #X through an =>log  edge tagged reductio . Fingia are also

the lawful home of adversarial modeling (“assume the mocks were unmatched; what

follows?”) and of exploring a rival theory’s world without contaminating one’s own. Exports

out of a fingium are the strictest PERMEA in the language: nothing inside becomes evidence

about the real ledger except a contradiction witness or an explicitly conditional claim,

independently validated on the way out. SI asks “what if this unknown is true?”; FINGE asks

“what if the ledger is wrong here?” — different questions, different walls, never one

construct, which is why the review’s instinct to separate them was adopted whole.

§9. NEGATION, ABSENCE, QUESTIONS

NON{…}. Bare “not,” “no,” “never,” “without” are illegal in LEX; negation is NON{…}  and the

braces are the scope — never context, never inference. NON{(A&B)}  negates the bundle

jointly (at least one fails); (NON{A}&NON{B})  negates each — and preserving that distinction,

which prose renders identically, is the construct’s whole job. Nested double negation is

legal, flagged for rewrite, and disliked. ABEST(x ; in:<sources> ; per:<protocol/pactum

ref> ; on:<date>). The only grammatical form for negative existence. “X does not exist”

and “X has never been done” have no production in this language — the novelty doctrine

enforced by the parser rather than the conscience. An ABEST is updated, not disgraced, by

a later find. QUAERO <kind> : <question> ; blocking:<#onus|NESCIO>. Typed questions,

so interrogation cannot masquerade as assertion or dissolve into prose. Kinds: decide

(yes/no, with its decision procedure named or NESCIO — and “kind:decide,



procedure:NESCIO” is the honest form of we do not yet know how we would know, worth

more than most answers), value , status , define  (requests a definition-compiler run),

route  (requests closure-route proposals).

§10. NUMBERS, UNITS, ENCLOSURES

Microformat, full form: name:value±err[errtype]{unit}@source . gamma:1.48±0.13[stat]

{1}@#COS-MEA-001  · A:576[exact]{len^2}@#Q0-THM-001  · an uncertainty may be NESCIO; a

source may not. A bare numeral in a ratiomen is illegal: a number without provenance and

uncertainty is a rumor with digits, and the parser refuses rumors. Error types (closed, DEF-

extendable): [stat] [sys] [boot:N] [encl] [model] [exact] [fit] . Enclosures in

function form — encl(lo,hi)  — the pinch’s native datum; strict inequalities between

overlapping enclosures are parse-visible STATISTICS defects; separations carry saltus:

against eps_total: , and rigorous separation means inf−sup > 0. Units: where a capsule

declares dimensions, {unit}  is mandatory ( {1}  for dimensionless, never omitted), and

equating unequal declared dimensions fails lint — the cheapest wall against a whole family

of physics errors. Display rounding never appears in LEX: canonical precision in the

record, rounding in specula, and no proof-adjacent inequality ever flips on a formatting

choice.

§13. BLOCKS AND DOCUMENTS

Roles by sigil: ^HEAD  — identity, UTOR, scope, calor; the binding acts open here (high-

§15. ADVERSARIAL ANALYSIS — TEN ATTACKS, TEN VERDICTS

(a) Junctor laundering — +  between correlated routes: the cert:  field is mandatory;

absence is fictura; the attack converts soft inflation into a written, named, prosecutable act

— the language’s preferred trade everywhere. (b) Gloss laundering — assert in GLOSS, cite

the gloss: LEX may not cite GLOSS; the citation is the visible crime. (c) Math smuggling —

a claim hidden in $…$ : math asserts nothing; the only door is a CLAIM line whose first token

confesses itself. (d) Datum drift — quoting an authority and sliding into reliance: assertion

never crosses [ ] ; reliance requires a LEX line with a V-level, and until then the quote is

decor. (e) NESCIO flooding — lawful ignorance everywhere: NESCIO where the record

determines the value is theatrosis, and the minimum viable session is still owed. (f) SI

leakage — discharging posits into an unconditional claim: the target’s ASSUMPTIONS must

contain them; the lint witness is one line. (g) FINGE laundering — exporting counterfactual

“results” as real: the fingium’s PERMEA admits only contradiction witnesses and explicitly

conditional claims, independently validated. (h) Calor laundering — writing calor:0 syntax

around calor:3 content: form does not launder substance; a binding line’s evidence fields
still demand receipts, and receipt-less precision is fictura in a nicer font. (i) PERMEA

skipping — quietly promoting a nursery idea by restating it in a CLAIM: the claim’s stirps

names its source; a calor-3 ancestor with no PERMEA in the chain fails the consistency

sweep. (j) Pinning games — unpinned #X  inheriting a later, stronger version: unpinned

resolution announces itself; FROZEN claims must pin. The pattern across all ten: the

language rarely prevents bad faith — it converts every soft, deniable inflation into a discrete

token with a name, so that fluency stops being a weapon the moment every escalation costs

a written symbol.
