1. **The sharp-bin injection (1).** The hypothesis `a > d` means every bar of either diagram with lifetime in `[a, b)` is matched to a finite bar. The diagonal can take only lifetimes `≤ d`.    - `ℓ_P ∈ [a, a+d)`, when `ℓ_Q < a ≤ ℓ_P`;
   - `ℓ_P ∈ [b−d, b)`, when `ℓ_P < b ≤ ℓ_Q`;
   - `ℓ_P ∈ [a−d, a)`, when `ℓ_P < a ≤ ℓ_Q`;
   - `ℓ_P ∈ [b, b+d)`, when `ℓ_Q < b ≤ ℓ_P`.

   Injectivity of the matching gives (1). If the strips overlap, they count a union, which is still a valid bound, and under the schedule they are eventually disjoint.
