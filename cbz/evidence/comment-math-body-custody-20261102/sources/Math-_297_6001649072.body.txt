### Required correction

`jet_data` represents A by the unscaled upper-triangle entries `[H(w_i,w_j), i<=j]` and defines `tr_AgV` as the raw sum of their conditional variances. NOTE Method5 says all listed quantities are independent of the transverse frame. This trace is **not** invariant for d>=3: off-diagonal coordinates need Frobenius weight2.

At fixed contact direction u=e1 in d=3, put a=m2>0, b=m4 and k4=b-3a^2. In transverse coordinates (H22,H23,H33), after conditioning V=(H11,H12,H13)=0,

`Sigma = [[v,0,c],[0,a^2,0],[c,0,v]]`,
`v=b-a^4/b`, `c=a^2-a^4/b`.

For a transverse rotation through theta,

`trace_raw(Sigma_theta)-trace_raw(Sigma_0) = -2*k4*sin(theta)^2*cos(theta)^2`.

Thus for cos(theta)=3/5 and sin(theta)=4/5 the difference is **-288*k4/625**, while u and the field law are unchanged. The periodic product law has nonzero k4 in the packet's examples, so this is directly relevant, not merely a roundoff concern.

Exact finite product-spectrum control (iid uniform on {-2,-1,1,2}): k4=-41/4; raw trace is239/17 before rotation and199559/10625 after, difference **2952/625**. This finite spectrum is an algebraic control, not a claim to realize the packet's infinite Gaussian spectrum.
