### Authored proof of Q4

Put R=P+|Z|≥1. First define the conditional positive measure

    μ_n(E)=E[(1+||A||)^n1{A<0,λ∈E}|Z].

Its small-layer mass is at most C R^(n+v)s, and its total mass is at most C R^n. For 0<a≤1, the identity x^(−2)=1+2∫_x^1t^(−3)dt on[a,1] and Tonelli give

    ∫_[a,1]x^(−2)dμ_n(x)
      ≤ μ_n((0,1])+2∫_a^1μ_n((0,t])t^(−3)dt
      ≤ C R^(n+v)[1+2(a^(−1)−1)].

The x>1 tail is bounded by total mass. If a≥1, use x^(−2)≤a^(−2)≤a^(−1) on x≥a. Thus for every a>0,

    E[(1+||A||)^nλ^(−2)1{A<0,λ≥a}|Z]
      ≤ C R^(n+v)/a.                                      (T2)

This argument is valid for positive measures with atoms; it does not presume differentiability of the eigenvalue CDF or an untruncated inverse moment.

Let E={A<0,t₁≤λ<t₁|f₄|/κ}. On E,

    λ≥t₁≥c₀r,       t₁|f₄|/(κλ)>1.

Separating the least eigenvalue of −A gives Δ²≤λ²||A||^(2m−2), with ||A||⁰=1 for m=1. Apply the fourth power of the event ratio:

    w_κ1_E
      ≤36κ^(−2)t₁⁴|f₄|⁴λ^(−2)||A||^(2m−2)
           1{A<0,λ≥c₀r}
      =36c₀⁴κ^(−2)r⁴N⁴H⁴|f₄|⁴λ^(−2)||A||^(2m−2)
           1{A<0,λ≥c₀r}.                                  (1)

The branch event is enlarged only after it supplies this pointwise bound and the deterministic cutoff. Every factor except N⁴ is J′-measurable. Condition first on J′ and apply F4 with p=4.

Then condition on(A,Z). Equivalent finite-dimensional norms give P+|J′|≤C(R+||A||+|f₄|). Expanding the fourth power and using conditional f₄ moments of orders4–8 gives

    E[(P+|J′|)⁴|f₄|⁴|A,Z] ≤ C(R+||A||)⁸.                  (2)

No norm event is treated as independent of f₄, and f₄ is not assumed independent of A. Its possibly nonzero conditional mean is included in(2).
