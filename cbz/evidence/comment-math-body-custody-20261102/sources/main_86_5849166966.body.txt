## 1. R2: confirmed arithmetic discrepancy; proposed repair still needs justification

Exact arithmetic gives

\[
0.76\frac{25-6.25}{9-6.25}=\frac{57}{11},\qquad
0.76\frac{25-6.25}{9-2.25}=\frac{19}{9}.
\]

Thus the printed expression is about 5.181818, while the proposed alternative is about 2.111111. Matching an approximate 2.1 does not prove which collar was intended. The retrieved C030 package supplies the B3 budget, but does not supply the proposed B3-to-B5 annular intensity comparison. C031 prints the inconsistent expression directly. For a nonconstant conditioned intensity, an area ratio alone does not justify transferring an integral bound between annuli.

Even if the proposed denominator is established, an upper coefficient 2.1 rounds downward from 19/9. With ell/2 = r^3/12, the proposed exact coefficient is 19/108 = 0.175925925..., which is also greater than 0.1759. Retain the exact coefficient or a justified outward bound, conditional on proving the underlying spatial comparison. This arithmetic issue alone does not disprove existence of some positive asymptotic lower constant.

## 2. WP: arithmetic consistency is not discharge

At r = 0.025,

\[
1.95\times10^{-6}+1.37\times10^{-6}=0.21248r^3,
\qquad 0.213r^3=3.328125\times10^{-6}.
\]

The slack is 8.125e-9. This checks a sum; it does not verify the omitted rigidity region, pin collars, Kac–Rice applicability or a uniform O(r^3) estimate.

The supplied `wp_rho` code contains two concrete defects:

- With t1=(b-mu)/s and t2=(b-ell-mu)/s, t1>t2. Therefore Q(t1)-Q(t2)<0: its window-probability subtraction has the wrong sign and always selects the fallback in exact arithmetic. The correct difference is Q(t2)-Q(t1). The fallback peak-density formula is an upper estimate for the window probability, not the exact probability.
- Cauchy–Schwarz requires sqrt(P(A)), not P(A). For D=det(H) and the type-and-window event A, the bound is

\[
\rho_{WP}\le p_{\nabla f}(0)\sqrt{\mathbb E[D^2\mid\nabla f=0]}\sqrt{\min(P_{type},P_{window})}.
\]

The missing square root was already explicitly rejected in GP-LB-STAT-003 and reaffirmed in GP-LB-STAT-004. Accordingly AUD-023 v1.1's 1.30399e-5 upper-bound claim is also quarantined. Do not replace the August 4 claim with an unqualified acceptance of v1.1.

A large computed upper estimate cannot refute a smaller claimed upper bound on the true count. The later direct estimate near 4.7602e-6 is diagnostic unless supplied with a rigorous lower enclosure. This review did not reproduce that spatial integral.

## 3. LB-1: a deterministic pinned point contradicts the displayed count

LB-1 Section 1 fixes f(M)=b and grad f(M)=0. Section 4 counts all critical points with f>=b in B(3r,M). Hence every sample satisfying those pins obeys

\[
N_{crit}(f\ge b,B_{3r}(M))\ge1,\qquad
\mathbb E N_{crit}(f\ge b,B_{3r}(M))\ge1.
\]

This directly contradicts the displayed bound by E_zone(r), including 2.1e-30 at r=0.05. The same issue defeats Section 2's claim for every Borel set A when A contains this pin. The off-pin Kac–Rice density does not account for the deterministic atom at M.

This is a falsifier of the literal untyped surrogate statement. It does not establish a large count of additional strictly-above-b saddles. A successor must define the additional/punctured count, specify its strict threshold and typing, treat deterministic atoms separately, and prove the resulting estimate. Merely changing the count notation will not supply the missing continuum estimates.

## 4. Certificate distinctions that matter

The pasted `log_phi_bar` uses -1035/t^10 where the fifth Mills-series coefficient is -945/t^10. Repeated integration by parts for Q(t) shows that the series truncated after the negative fifth term is a lower bound for t>0. The supplied polynomial is smaller still, by exactly 90/t^10. Therefore it cannot serve as an upper bound on the Gaussian tail. This direction error is exact, even though its numerical size at t=30 is small. See [NIST DLMF 7.12(i)](https://dlmf.nist.gov/7.12#i) for the expansion and signed remainder rule.

The `rho_bar` fourth-moment calculation applies centered Wick pairing to raw second moments. For Hessian mean entries nu=(nu_xx,nu_xy,nu_yy), its D2 equals the true second determinant moment plus 2(nu_xx*nu_yy-nu_xy^2)^2. The printed exact identity is wrong, but this excess is conservative for that particular upper moment. It is not by itself a refutation of an upper bound.

Finite grids, sampled circles and the declared factor exp(min(25,0.3*abs(L2))) do not provide the missing analytic between-sample bound. Six rungs also do not establish an estimate for all 0<r<=r0. Analyticity requires quantitative remainder control here; high precision and identical optimized transcripts do not provide it.

## 5. Downstream assembly

C031 itself labels 0.0334 as a measured far probability and 0.946 as the Lambda value at r=0.025. It separately gives a derived-on-grid asymptotic estimate 0.9091 with errors. Reusing the finite-rung 0.946 as a proved all-small-r floor needs a new argument. The theorem-tier far factor is a conditional barrier constant, not automatically 1-0.0334.

The exact product 0.9666*0.946 is 0.9144036. If a rigorous asymptotic lower estimate with that leading coefficient were established, the smaller coefficient 0.9144 could eventually be valid. The failure here is unproved inputs, law transfer and uniform remainders—not merely this rounding.

Measured exit zero, deterministic mean connectivity at two rungs, unresolved gamma-LOC and grid extensions do not yield the advertised theorem. The two-dimensional lower campaign also cannot inherit a two-sided rate merely from a ratified three-dimensional upper statement without a matching law/domain theorem.

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Small mathematical audit probes of a user-pasted LB1 source, 2026-09-26.

This is an independently written extraction/reproduction, not the raw historical
certificate. It does not authenticate the pasted file's advertised SHA256 and
does not execute its full gridded certificate or change any research status.
Source snippets consumed: wp_rho probability subtraction and final factor;
log_phi_bar asymptotic polynomial; rho_bar's noncentral Wick construction.
"""
from decimal import Decimal as D, getcontext
from fractions import Fraction

getcontext().prec = 90
PI = D('3.14159265358979323846264338327950288419716939937510582097494459230781640628620899862803482534')

class MP:
    mpf = staticmethod(D)
    sqrt = staticmethod(lambda x: D(x).sqrt())
    exp = staticmethod(lambda x: D(x).exp())
    pi = PI
    nstr = staticmethod(lambda x, n: format(x, '.29g'))

mp = MP()

def emit(name, value):
    print(name + ': ' + mp.nstr(value, 30))

def Phi_bar(t):
    # Entire erf power series, only used here for |t|<=2.1.
    # This numerical probe is not directed interval arithmetic.
    x = t/mp.sqrt(2)
    term, total, k = x, x, 0
    while True:
        k += 1
        term *= -x*x/k
        add = term/(2*k+1)
        total += add
        if abs(add) < D('1e-85'):
            break
    return (1-2*total/mp.sqrt(PI))/2
phi = lambda t: mp.exp(-t*t/2) / mp.sqrt(2*mp.pi)

# The exact line order of the pasted wp_rho uses t1 > t2 and Q(t1)-Q(t2).
b = mp.mpf('1.2')
ell = mp.mpf('0.025')**3 / 6
mu = mp.mpf('1.187')
sigma = mp.sqrt(mp.mpf('5.94e-5'))
t1, t2 = (b-mu)/sigma, (b-ell-mu)/sigma
source_pw = Phi_bar(t1) - Phi_bar(t2)
true_pw = Phi_bar(t2) - Phi_bar(t1)
emit('wp_rho source subtraction (negative)', source_pw)
emit('correct window probability (positive)', true_pw)
if not (source_pw < 0 < true_pw):
    raise SystemExit('unexpected probability ordering')

# Missing sqrt counterexample to the proposed generic determinant/event bound.
# Z ~ N(0,1), Hessian diag(Z,-Z): always saddle off Z=0, |det H|=Z^2.
# E = {2 <= Z <= 2.1}; E[D^2]=E[Z^4]=3, P(type)=1.
lo, hi = mp.mpf(2), mp.mpf('2.1')
p_event = Phi_bar(lo)-Phi_bar(hi)
actual = lo*phi(lo)-hi*phi(hi)+p_event
source_bound = mp.sqrt(3)*p_event
cs_bound = mp.sqrt(3*p_event)
emit('window probability in saddle counterexample', p_event)
emit('true E[|det H| 1_E]', actual)
emit('invalid sqrt(E[D^2])*P(E)', source_bound)
emit('valid Cauchy-Schwarz upper bound', cs_bound)
if not (source_bound < actual <= cs_bound):
    raise SystemExit('counterexample not established')

# The pasted log_phi_bar uses -1035 u^5; fifth Mills coefficient is -945.
# Integration by parts gives Q(t)>phi(t)/t*S5(t) for t>0, where S5
# terminates in -945u^5. Exact Fraction arithmetic proves source<S5,
# so source<Q. This does not rely on evaluating the tiny exact tail.
for t in (30, 40):
    u = Fraction(1,t*t)
    source_S = 1-u+3*u**2-15*u**3+105*u**4-1035*u**5
    lower_S = 1-u+3*u**2-15*u**3+105*u**4-945*u**5
    deficit = lower_S-source_S
    print('tail t='+str(t)+' exact lower-polynomial deficit: '+str(deficit))
    emit('tail t='+str(t)+' lower-polynomial relative deficit',
         D(deficit.numerator)/D(deficit.denominator)/(D(lower_S.numerator)/D(lower_S.denominator)))
    if not (source_S < lower_S and deficit == 90*u**5):
        raise SystemExit('tail underestimate not established')

# Independent exact noncentral determinant moment: Hxx=1+X, Hyy=1+Y,
# Hxy=Z with X,Y,Z independent standard normal.
# E[(Hxx*Hyy-Hxy^2)^2] = 2*2 - 2*1*1*1 + 3 = 5.
# Wick on raw second moments C=Sigma+mu*mu^T gives 7 instead.
mu = [mp.mpf(1), mp.mpf(0), mp.mpf(1)]
S = [[mp.mpf(int(i == j)) for j in range(3)] for i in range(3)]
C = [[S[i][j]+mu[i]*mu[j] for j in range(3)] for i in range(3)]
def source_em(a,b,c,d):
    return C[a][b]*C[c][d]+C[a][c]*C[b][d]+C[a][d]*C[b][c]
code_d2 = source_em(0,0,2,2)-2*source_em(0,1,1,2)+source_em(1,1,1,1)
exact_d2 = mp.mpf(5)
det_mean_hessian = mu[0]*mu[2]-mu[1]**2
emit('raw-second-moment Wick D2', code_d2)
emit('true noncentral D2', exact_d2)
emit('conservative excess 2*(det mean H)^2', 2*det_mean_hessian**2)
if code_d2-exact_d2 != 2*det_mean_hessian**2:
    raise SystemExit('noncentral moment relation failed')

print('PROBES_PASS: explicit defects and conservative-moment distinction reproduced')
