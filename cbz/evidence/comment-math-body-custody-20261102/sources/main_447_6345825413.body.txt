### General conditional argument

Let z=Z_T>0 be the exact retained spectral mass, delta=Z−Z_T>=0 the omitted positive mass, and N the complete numerator. The old padded interval I contains N/z, not necessarily N/Z. The actual `spectral_tail_1d(0)` computes an upper bound D for the omitted mass and divides by the lower endpoint L of the interval enclosing z. Since 0<L<=z, its result B satisfies **B>=D/L>=delta/z**. This bound is stronger than merely delta/Z and is the one needed here.

Therefore

`N/Z = (N/z)/(1+delta/z) belongs to I / [1,1+B]`.

The denominator interval is strictly positive. Interval division handles positive, negative and sign-crossing numerators without assuming a sign; dependence loss only enlarges the enclosure. Replacing the denominator interval with its upper endpoint is invalid because delta may be smaller than B*z, including zero.

For the cosine/sine sums, the existing numerator padding bounds the omitted absolute-moment sum divided by L, so it indeed contains the complete numerator/z. For the image path, incorporate the positive 24/sqrt(2*pi) prefactor in N; the existing image-tail bound also divides by L and covers its complete numerator/z. Wrapping the whole padded result by the factor above corrects both cases. Parity signs are already applied; no second correction is needed in `k1` or `moment_a`. `cov` multiplies two independently corrected one-dimensional factors.

**Imports remain explicit:** original numerator-tail bounds and field/kernel identification, inclusion-preserving interval arithmetic/exp/pi/sqrt, valid configured cutoffs and matched-precision endpoint conversion. This is not a proof of mpmath itself, arbitrary malformed/nonfinite inputs, externally changed precision, whole H3 constants, or actual downstream consumer acceptance. Preserving the finite cache avoids changing the separate majorant consumers' denominator convention.
