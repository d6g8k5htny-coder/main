### (a) Order for γ>1 (conditional on R/P plus W2h; UNVERIFIED)
In the r-variable (r = (ℓ/k)^{1/3}), S_X/ℓ^{1/3} = ν_X(ℓ) = ∫_{S^{d−1}}∫_ℝ∫_0^{r_0} A_r(E_X; b, ℓ/r³, u) r^{−2} dr db dσ. I split this at k = r, i.e. r = ℓ^{1/4}.

**Rigorous steps** (given the inputs named):
1. **F1_X, regularity at k=0.** Fix r ∈ (0, r_0]. Under Q_{r,b,k}, Y = (H_M, H_S, X) is Gaussian, N(B v_r(b,k), Σ_r). Here v_r is affine in (b,k), and Σ_r does not depend on (b,k). Σ_r is nondegenerate because M, 0 and S are distinct sites carrying distinct functionals (P l63, P l415). The weight g = W·1_E has polynomial growth, so its Gaussian average is real-analytic in the mean. π_r(v_r) is smooth too. Hence k ↦ A_r(E; b, k) is real-analytic on a neighbourhood of [0, ∞), including k = 0.
2. **Positivity.** g > 0 on a nonempty open set: (open index cones for H_M, H_S) × (−ε, ε) in X. The Gaussian density is positive everywhere. Hence A_r(E; b, 0, u) > 0 for every r ∈ (0, r_0], every b and every u.
3. **Floor region, k ≤ r (r ≥ ℓ^{1/4}).** R (R11) gives the majorant A_r(E) ≤ A_r ≤ (k+r)² H(b,k) ≤ 4r² H̃(b), with H(b,k) ≤ H̃(b) for k ≤ 1. So the integrand is ≤ 4H̃(b), which is integrable on (0,r_0] × ℝ × S^{d−1}. Pointwise in (r,b,u), k = ℓ/r³ → 0, and step 1 gives convergence to A_r(E; b, 0, u). Dominated convergence then gives floor part → Ψ_X(γ,C) := ∫∫∫_0^{r_0} A_r(E_X; b, 0, u) r^{−2} dr db dσ. By step 2, Ψ_X > 0. Also Ψ_X ≤ 4κ⁺ r_0^{1+γ} h/(1+γ), in W2h's constants.
4. **Bulk region, k > r (r < ℓ^{1/4}).** W2h's upper bound restricted to this region gives a contribution to ν of O(ℓ^{(γ−1)/3} + ℓ^{(1+γ)/4}), which → 0 for γ>1.
5. **Conclusion.** For γ>1, lim_{ℓ→0} ν_X(ℓ) = Ψ_X(γ,C) ∈ (0,∞). So S_X ~ Ψ_X ℓ^{1/3}. W2h's upper bound ℓ^{1/3} is the true order, and its lower bound cℓ^{γ/3} is not sharp. For γ<1, the floor part divided by ℓ^{γ/3} → 0, which is consistent with W2h's exact limit L_X.
- **γ=1.** Both pieces are of order ℓ^{1/3}, and lim ν_X = L_X(1,C) + Ψ_X(1,C). The L-part uses W2h's contact limit on k>r at γ=1. W2h stated that limit for γ<1. The extension to γ=1 is my claim and has not been checked by a third party. The MC agrees (see (c)).
