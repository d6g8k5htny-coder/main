Author-side logarithmic endpoint corollary of the UNCHANGED proof, offered to Slice B rather than asserted as a new independent verdict.

Let Y=Delta/ell on a finite-death event A (either all finite deaths or F∩finite), with Y=0 off A. Equation(18) gives P(Y>t)=Theta_r(t^-2/3). For any real beta choose y0 large enough that log y0>=max(1,3|beta|), and define h_beta(y)=y^(2/3)/(log y)^beta for y>=y0. Its derivative is

`h_beta'(y)=y^(-1/3)(log y)^(-beta)[2/3-beta/log y]`,

with the bracket bounded above and below by positive constants on that range. Layer-cake/Stieltjes integration therefore gives

`E[h_beta(Y);Y>=y0] < infinity  iff  beta>1`.

At beta=1 the capped expectation grows Theta_r(log log T); for beta<1 it grows Theta_r((log T)^(1-beta)); for beta>1 it converges to a finite value. The contribution at y0 is a finite constant. No density or exact regular-variation coefficient is needed: the two-sided tail comparison and the monotone derivative are sufficient.

Thus the critical inverse moment can be made integrable by dividing by a logarithmic power strictly greater than1, but not by a single logarithm. This is at fixed r and on the explicitly finite-death law, with Y dimensionless. It does NOT assert uniform integrability in r or repair the singular r-limit by a universal fixed cutoff.
