### 4. NEW_AUTHOR_PROOF — finite-radius transverse-sector bound

**(A)**
```
E_Q[(W_r/r^4) N^a ; lambda_p(B_M)<=epsilon]
 <= C_a (1+|b|+k)^N_a epsilon^[p(p+4)/2] (epsilon+r)^p.
```

No remaining eigenvalue has a positive floor; no isotropy, eigenvalue independence or compact positive lower bound on k is used. This is an upper bound, not a sharp asymptotic.

**Proof.** E3 Lemma H at blob `c8767ddecf1041d234f685e95af582073a7be393` proves that Htilde_M is a uniformly nondegenerate full Gaussian on Sym(d), with mean O(1+|b|+k), and `E[N^s|Htilde_M]<=C_s(1+|b|+k+|Htilde_M|)^s`. This uses the full averaged pin/jet Gram, not an almost-pinned unscaled derivative.

In the eigenbasis of B_M write `Htilde_M=[[a_M,b_M^T],[b_M,-diag(lambda)]]`. Negative definiteness implies

```
a_M<0,  sum_i b_M,i^2/lambda_i < -a_M,
|det Htilde_M| <= (-a_M) prod_i lambda_i.
```

In particular `|b_M,i|<=sqrt((-a_M)lambda_i)`.

The exact pins and Taylor identities give, with T=C N>=1, `|a_M|+|a_S|<=CT`, `||A_S-A_M||<=CrT`, and `|b_S+b_M|<=CrT`. Put `D=diag(lambda_i+rT)`. The transverse block of `diag(1,D^-1/2) Htilde_S diag(1,D^-1/2)` has bounded operator norm, its mixed block is O(sqrt(T)) by the maximum-cone constraint and the O(rT) error, and its axial entry is O(T). **(B)** `W_r/r^4 <= CT^(2d) prod_i lambda_i(lambda_i+r) 1{Htilde_M<0}`.
