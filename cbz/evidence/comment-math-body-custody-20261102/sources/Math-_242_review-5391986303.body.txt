## 3. Root-authored support D.1: direct model identity in every fixed dimension

**Precise statement.** Fix d≥2, m=d−1, L>0, b, k>0 and an orthonormal frame. Assume the common nondegenerate contact Gaussian jet law and the deterministic typed cubic classifier of #170 Theorem E(1), with the raw Lebesgue/Weyl convention in [SC](8).     F(k;b,u) = k A_*(b,k,u) a_fail(b,k,u).                 (D.1)

Also the compact-window coefficients agree wherever the stated source integration hypotheses apply:

    (1/3) ∫ k^(-8/3) F db dk dσ
      = ∫ A_* a_fail /(3k^(5/3)) db dk dσ = C_fail.       (D.2)

The proof below does not require #243 Theorem FL or #175 Theorem F. Adding a retained actual-field failure theorem afterwards yields a conditional fixed-mark field limit by the preceding elementary identity. This does not independently verify that field theorem, prove its rate, establish local uniform convergence, or handle k endpoints/intermediate regions.

**Step 1: use one raw measure convention.** Let A be the midpoint transverse Hessian and T the complete nonrepeated third-derivative list excluding the pinned f_xxx. Its contact density is h_0(A,T). Use upper-triangular Lebesgue coordinates for A and the original raw derivative coordinates for T. Write

    A = -O diag(x,h_2,...,h_m) O^T,
    0<x<h_2<...<h_m,  dO = normalized Haar probability.

For m=1 the hard coordinates are absent. [SC](8) fixes

    c_m = (2π)^(m/2) π^(m(m-1)/4) / H_m,
    H_m = ∫_(l_1<...<l_m) exp(-Σl_i²/2) Π_(i<j)(l_j-l_i) dl.

Indeed the Gaussian test integral over the raw entries of A is the numerator: diagonal entries contribute (2π)^(m/2), off-diagonal entries contribute π^(m(m−1)/4). Reflection A↦−A changes no Lebesgue factor, and using ordered eigenvalues of −A gives the same positive Vandermonde. PR242's c_m in (1.1a) is fixed by this same measure identity. There is no free multiplicity constant left to match and no extra m!, 2^m or factor 1/2 to add.

Rotate T by diag(1,O), obtaining τ. This representation preserves the subspace excluding f_xxx. Its absolute determinant is one: it is a continuous group homomorphism from the compact group O(m) into the positive multiplicative reals. The matrix-plus-tensor change is block triangular, so O-dependence of the tensor coordinates contributes no further factor. Denote the inverse raw rotation by Rot_O τ and retain h_0(A,Rot_O τ). The density need not be isotropic; it is never replaced by a GOE law.

Write γ=τ_xxe1, B=τ_xe1e1 and C=τ_e1e1e1. All remaining entries of τ remain integrated. Reflection e_1↦−e_1 sends (γ,B,C) to (−γ,B,−C), preserving both t=12kB/γ² and χ_0=576k²C/γ³ and the cubic decision. Thus the Haar sign frames introduce no additional count of physical pairs.

**Step 2: justify the boundary limit directly in the joint density.** Put

    A_0(h,O) = -O diag(0,h_2,...,h_m) O^T,
    J(h) = Π_(j≥2) h_j³ · Π_(2≤i<j≤m)(h_j-h_i).

All empty products equal one. The product J is exactly the boundary Vandermonde times the two hard determinant factors:

    V(0,h) Π_(j≥2)h_j² = J(h).

Expand the ε-average in PR242(3.1) using the joint raw density. Its inner x-integral runs from 0 to min(ε,h_2), not past the eigenvalue ordering boundary. For each fixed h_2>0 it is eventually an average over 0<x<ε. The factor γ⁶ I(t,χ_0) is a function of the fixed rotated tensor τ, not x. In particular no continuity theorem for I as an eigenframe moves is needed for this argument.

By Lemma 3 of PR242,

    0 ≤ γ⁶ I(t,χ_0)
      ≤ C[γ⁶ + 1728 k³ |B|³ + 331776 k⁴ C²].           (D.3)

The exceptional set γ=0 is a null set under the nondegenerate joint density, and can be assigned value zero. For 0≤x≤1, the joint Gaussian density is bounded by a constant times exp[-c(|h|²+|τ|²)], after absorbing the fixed affine mean. Norm equivalence under the compact family of tensor rotations is uniform in O. The Vandermonde and hard weight are polynomials and (D.3) has polynomial growth.     F/(12π_0)
      = c_m ∫_(0<h_2<...<h_m) ∫_O ∫_τ
          J(h) h_0(A_0(h,O),Rot_O τ)

This statement is about the cubic, not convergence of the original field. The null sets remain null under the continuous joint Gaussian density; the typed boundary has zero weight. Since dλ̃=−k ds, reversing the limits gives a factor k, not 1/k and not a signed measure. Inserting (D.5) into (D.4) yields
