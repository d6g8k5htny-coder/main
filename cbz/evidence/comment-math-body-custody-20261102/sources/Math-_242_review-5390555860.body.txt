## 1. An explicit reconstruction of Lemma 5

Write θ=(b,u), with measure db dσ(u), for fixed d and model parameters. Set Aθ(κ)=𝒜^rej(b,κ,u), Fθ=F₀(b,u). The needed measurable, integrable interfaces can be written using a nonnegative envelope Pθ with M=∫Pθ dθ<∞:

- 0≤Aθ(κ)≤a₀Pθ κ² for 0<κ≤1;
- 0≤Fθ≤f₀Pθ and |Aθ(κ)−Fθ/κ|≤a₁Pθ/κ² for κ≥1;
- Hθ≥0 is jointly measurable and |Hθ(k)−1|≤h min(1,k²).

All constants are finite and uniform in θ; they may depend on the fixed model and dimension. The packet supplies the first two interfaces through the contact kernel, Gaussian height decay and Theorem 1. Theorem 1 was separately reviewed in C67 at exactly these bytes. The third is the stated hypothesis of Lemma 5; Proposition 4 is one proposed application. This reconstruction does not assert that hypothesis for every covariance or finite torus.

Define J=∫∫₀∞ Aθ(s⁻⁴) ds dθ. At s≤1 the integrand is at most Pθ(f₀s⁴+a₁s⁸); at s≥1 it is at most a₀Pθs⁻⁸. Thus

    0≤J≤M(f₀/5+a₁/9+a₀/7)<∞.

The identity J=I^cand−c₁ is the difference of the two cusp integrals in the packet's §0, after their common contact subtraction cancels. It does not identify a field persistence coefficient.

The coefficient R is absolutely integrable, since

    |v⁴ Fθ(Hθ(v⁻³)−1)|≤h f₀Pθ min(v⁴,v⁻²),
    |R|≤(6/5)h f₀M,
    R=(1/3)∫∫₀∞ Fθ[Hθ(k)−1] k⁻⁸ᐟ³ dk dθ.             (D.1)

The last formula uses v=k⁻¹ᐟ³, including its 1/3 Jacobian. Absolute integrability justifies Fubini and the changes of variables. The composite itself is finite because H≤1+h and J is finite.

For 0<ℓ≤1, put r_c=ℓ¹ᐟ⁴, r_f=ℓ¹ᐟ³, V=ℓ⁻¹ᐟ¹². Subtract the H=1 part Jℓ¹ᐟ⁴. On r>r_c,

    ∫∫ Aθ(ℓ/r⁴)|Hθ(ℓ/r³)−1| dr dθ
    ≤a₀hM ℓ⁴∫[r_c,∞)r⁻¹⁴dr
    =(a₀hM/13)ℓ³ᐟ⁴.                                 (D.2)

On 0<r≤r_c use Aθ=Fθr⁴/ℓ+Eθ, |Eθ|≤a₁Pθr⁸/ℓ², and r=vℓ¹ᐟ³. The main term is

    ℓ²ᐟ³∫∫[0,V] v⁴Fθ(Hθ(v⁻³)−1)dv dθ.

Its missing tail is bounded by h f₀M ℓ²ᐟ³/V=h f₀Mℓ³ᐟ⁴. The error from Eθ is at most

    a₁hMℓ [∫₀¹v⁸dv+∫₁ⱽv²dv]
    =a₁hMℓ [V³/3−2/9]≤(a₁hM/3)ℓ³ᐟ⁴.                (D.3)

Consequently the claimed expansion has the explicit bound

    |νᶜ(ℓ)−Jℓ¹ᐟ⁴−Rℓ²ᐟ³|
    ≤hM(a₀/13+f₀+a₁/3)ℓ³ᐟ⁴.                         (D.4)

No differentiation of a cumulative estimate, unweighted probability, unproved interchange of limits, or field-to-model approximation is used in this proof. The statement is about the defined composite. The sign of R is not determined by these hypotheses.

## 2. Exact adversarial examples

These are scalar kernel examples that test the inference, not counterexamples to a Gaussian-field theorem. They deliberately satisfy the relevant analytic hypotheses without being claimed to arise from Gaussian persistence.

**Sharpness of the remainder.** On a one-point θ-space let F=1, A(κ)=κ³ for κ≤1 and A(κ)=κ⁻¹ for κ≥1. Let H(k)=1+a min(1,k²), with −1≤a≤1. Then J=1/5+1/11=16/55 and R=6a/5. Direct integration at r_f and r_c gives exactly

    νᶜ(ℓ)=(16/55)ℓ¹ᐟ⁴+(6a/5)ℓ²ᐟ³−(16a/17)ℓ³ᐟ⁴,
    0<ℓ≤1.                                             (D.5)

This both checks the coefficient/Jacobian and rules out silently strengthening Lemma 5's remainder to o(ℓ³ᐟ⁴). Positive and negative a also show that its assumptions do not certify a numerical sign for R.

**Small-k control is essential.** The same A with H(k)=1+min(1,k) has H→1 and bounded H, but violates the quadratic hypothesis. Its correction is exactly

    νᶜ(ℓ)−Jℓ¹ᐟ⁴=(4/7)ℓ¹ᐟ²−(3/10)ℓ²ᐟ³.              (D.6)

The proposed R integral diverges at v=∞. In general a bound |H−1|≤C min(1,k^α) makes that end behave as v^(4−3α); α>5/3 is sufficient for integrability. At α=5/3 this example yields

    νᶜ(ℓ)−Jℓ¹ᐟ⁴=ℓ²ᐟ³[21/80+(1/12)log(1/ℓ)].        (D.7)

Thus merely proving H→1 would not justify the ℓ²ᐟ³ coefficient. The original α=2 hypothesis is strong enough; this is an inference control, not a defect in Lemma 5.

**Fixed windows miss intermediate mass.** Let ψ(s)=30(s−1)²(2−s)² on [1,2], zero otherwise. It is nonnegative, continuously differentiable after extension, and ∫ψ=1. For 0<ℓ≤1 define

    Eℓ(r)=ℓ¹⁷ᐟ⁴⁵ ψ(r/ℓ²ᐟ⁹).

Then ∫Eℓ(r)dr=ℓ³ᐟ⁵. Its support [ℓ²ᐟ⁹,2ℓ²ᐟ⁹] escapes every fixed cusp window r=sℓ¹ᐟ⁴ and every fixed fold window r=vℓ¹ᐟ³. On either fixed compact window it is eventually identically zero, including all changes of amplitude used in the fold limit. It is also eventually zero at every fixed r>0.

On this support κ=ℓ/r⁴=ℓ¹ᐟ⁹s⁻⁴, and

    Eℓ(r)/κ³=ℓ²ᐟ⁴⁵s¹²ψ(s)≤7680.

It therefore even obeys an O(κ³) intermediate envelope, which is no stronger than the packet's stated typed-window majorant there. Its integrated mass is O(ℓ³ᐟ⁵), hence within the quoted weaker O(ℓ³ᐟ⁷) and O(ℓ⁴ᐟ⁹ log(1/ℓ)) budgets for small ℓ. Yet it dominates both ℓ²ᐟ³ and ℓ³ᐟ⁴. Adding Eℓ to a model kernel preserves all these fixed-window limits and still destroys Conjecture 7's claimed next coefficient.

This is a counterexample to deriving the integrated claim from those selected bounds alone. It does not assert compatibility with every Gaussian regression identity or topological constraint, nor refute Conjecture 7. It proves why its intermediate matching input cannot be deleted.

**The far term must stay.** A nonnegative function f(ℓ)=qℓ²ᐟ³, q>0, meets #187's upper bound but changes the coefficient when moved to the other side of (4.3). An oscillating nonnegative coefficient, e.g. ℓ²ᐟ³(1+sin(log(1/ℓ)))/2, need not even have a normalized limit. Consequently the O(ℓ²ᐟ³) upper bound cannot justify either deleting the far term or assigning it a coefficient. No assertion about its actual sharp order is made.

## 3. A precise conditional target for the next field calculation

The following is **root-authored support derived during review**, not a separately reviewed new field theorem. It sharpens the form of the already open input in §4 without purporting to discharge it.

    Uℓ=eℓ+∫∫₀∞ gℓ(r,θ)dr dθ,

    ε_f(ℓ,v,θ)=ℓ⁻¹ᐟ³[gℓ(ℓ¹ᐟ³v,θ)−Cℓ(ℓ¹ᐟ³v,θ)], 0<v≤V,
    ε_i(ℓ,s,θ)=gℓ(ℓ¹ᐟ⁴s,θ)−Cℓ(ℓ¹ᐟ⁴s,θ),         s≥1.

A sufficient, explicitly weighted transfer target is

    |eℓ|≤K₀ℓ³ᐟ⁴,
    ∫∫[0,V]|ε_f|dv dθ≤K_fℓ¹ᐟ¹²,
    ∫∫[1,∞)|ε_i|ds dθ≤K_iℓ¹ᐟ².                     (D.8)

Indeed, the first integral in original units is ℓ²ᐟ³∫|ε_f| and the second is ℓ¹ᐟ⁴∫|ε_i|. The disjoint split r≤r_c, r>r_c gives

Combining with (D.4) proves precisely (4.3), if and only insofar as the application supplies these sufficient hypotheses. Signed cancellation could support a weaker sufficient interface; (D.8) is deliberately an absolute-integral criterion, not claimed necessary. For merely identifying an ℓ²ᐟ³ coefficient with an o(ℓ²ᐟ³) remainder, replace the three rates respectively by o(ℓ²ᐟ³), o(1), and o(ℓ⁵ᐟ¹²). This weaker target still excludes the moving-mass example. Neither version is presently proved for the field in this review.
