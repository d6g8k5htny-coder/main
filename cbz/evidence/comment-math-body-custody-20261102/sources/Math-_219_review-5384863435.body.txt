## Reconstructed analytic argument

Write a box as a closed rectangle in the complex plane. For the safe kernels,

\[
 E_1(z)=\int_0^1 e^{\tau z}\,d\tau,\quad
 L(z)=\int_0^1\frac{d\tau}{1+\tau z},\quad
 A(z)=\int_0^1\frac{d\tau}{1+\tau^2z^2},\quad
 \operatorname{Sinc}(z)=\int_0^1\cos(\tau z)\,d\tau.
\]

The first and fourth are entire. The other two are holomorphic wherever their denominators avoid zero for every real `tau` in `[0,1]`. Replacing `tau` or `tau^2` by `[0,1]` gives a containing box, even though it discards correlations. Averaging a curve in a rectangle stays in that rectangle, so the integral-hull return is an enclosure. In particular, `_Ac_hull`'s use of `TAU * z^2` is valid: the range of `tau^2` is exactly `[0,1]`. It does not need the pointwise relation between the two appearances of `z` for an enclosure.

`cdiv` forms `|denominator|^2` with nonnegative square ranges and refuses a lower bound at or below zero. A successful finite calculation therefore gives a positive denominator margin on the whole box, including all `tau`. By compactness that margin persists on a neighborhood. Thus a successful safe-form expression is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the input rectangle. Conservative wrapping can make a nonsingular rectangle fail; it cannot turn an enclosed zero into a certified positive lower bound under the stated finite IEEE-754 arithmetic assumptions.

The closed forms are consistent with that same continuation:

* `Lc` uses `Log(1+z)/z` only when the lower modulus bound is at least one and `Re z > -1/2`. Hence `1+z` is in the right half-plane, its principal argument is `atan(Im/Re)`, and no logarithm cut is crossed.
* `Ac` uses `(pi/2 - atan(1/z))/z` only for `Re z > 0` and lower modulus at least one. The exact reciprocal is also in the right half-plane. The identity follows there from analytic differentiation and its value on the positive real axis. The reciprocal's integral-hull evaluation must separately pass its denominator check.
* The closed `E1c` and `Sincc` forms divide only on boxes with a certified positive lower modulus. Their apparent singularities at zero are removed by the hull forms.

For the direct integrands, `_off_cut` requires a principal-power base rectangle to avoid the closed negative real axis: positive real lower endpoint, or an imaginary interval strictly above or below zero. Each condition is sufficient; it is intentionally conservative. If `a` is real and a principal power is defined, `|z^a|=|z|^a`. `g2_bound_direct` and `f3_bound_direct` use outward lower moduli for negative powers, outward upper exponentials, triangle inequalities for `1-(Phi+Phi*)/2`, and a separately checked nonzero denominator.
