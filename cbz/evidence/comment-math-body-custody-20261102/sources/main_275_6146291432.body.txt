## Statement being compared, not certified

Fix d≥2, L>0, and one ensemble-centered stationary real Gaussian law on the flat torus of side L, with symmetric nonnegative Fourier weights summing to one. The concrete sufficient source route uses positivity on the finite K5 carrier and A4: the sum of sqrt(q_n)(1+|2πn/L|)^4 is finite. The source also states a zero-mode variant and an alternative actual-C4 route with the specified moment and rank premises. These alternatives must retain their own hypotheses; no minimality or law-uniform assertion is inferred.

The observable is the once-counted actual ordinary finite superlevel H0 barcode with global elder pairing and the essential class removed. For one fixed law, as ℓ↓0 and t↓0, the source claims a canonical per-volume Borel density representative ν_bar(ℓ)~c_q ℓ^(-1/3) and E[N_t]/L^d~(3/2)c_q t^(2/3). A change of density on a null set need not preserve its pointwise asymptotic.

For precision, source equation (27) retains the full coefficient

c_q = Γ(7/6)/(24^(1/3)√π) ∫_(S^(d−1)) p_G(0) p_Vu(0) τ_u^(4/3) D_u dσ(u),

where p_G(0)=(2π)^(-d/2)(det M_G)^(-1/2), p_Vu(0)=(2π)^(-d/2)(det M_Vu)^(-1/2), V_u=Hu is the full d-vector, A_u is the transverse Hessian, D_u=E[(det A_u)^2 1{A_u<0}|V_u=0], and τ_u² is the residual variance of F_uuu given G. The sphere uses ordinary full area and ordered maximum-to-saddle direction, without an extra half. The coefficient depends on the law.

The separate marked limit uses iid whole-field copies and n t_n^(2/3)→λ∈(0,∞), after establishing the within-copy estimate E[N_(Tt)1{N_(Tt)≥2}]=o(t^(2/3)) for fixed T. Its source marks are (a=ℓ/t, birth level b, k=ℓ/dist(M,S)^3, birth point x, ordered direction u), with intensity λη_q and

dη_q = 4 a^(-1/3) k^(-2/3) π_0(v_0) z_0(b,k,u) da db dk dx dσ(u),
η_q{a≤T} = (3/2)L^d c_q T^(2/3).

The source's π_0,z_0 and all conditioning conventions remain as defined there. This is neither a growing-domain theorem nor a same-field spatial Poisson theorem. A global factorial bound does not settle a regional shrinking multiple-witness collision estimate.

## Exact negative controls for interpretation

1. **Contact does not select a bar.** Formally r dr and ℓ=k r³ yield (1/3)k^(-2/3)ℓ^(-1/3)dℓ for fixed k>0. That substitution alone supplies neither the all-mark integrable normalizer nor the actual elder selection factor, an all-small-bars converse, or once-counting. The corresponding mathematical nodes retain their own reviews.

2. **A first moment does not imply Poisson.** Put e_t=t^(2/3) and Z_t=2 Bernoulli(e_t/2). Then E[Z_t]=e_t. For n independent copies with n e_t→λ, their probability-generating function is [1+(e_t/2)(z²−1)]^n→exp[(λ/2)(z²−1)], the law of 2 Poisson(λ/2), not Poisson(λ). Here E[Z_t 1{Z_t≥2}]=e_t, not o(e_t). This is an abstract count counterexample, not a counterexample to the Gaussian candidate. It identifies why the separate source multiple-event estimate matters.

3. **The sampling order matters.** One realized Morse field on a compact domain has finitely many positive finite lifetimes; its process of bars with ℓ≤Tt eventually empties as t↓0. The proposed nonzero Poisson limit instead superposes independent fields while increasing their number. Neither statement permits replacing iid fields with a growing spatial region or a regional shrinking witness process.
