   - This gives ∫⁻ cosh(t x) dμ = Σ_k t^{2k}/(2k)!·m_{2k}, which equals the same quantity for ν, which is < ∞ (`Integrable.lintegral_lt_top`).

2. **The mgfs agree near 0.**

3. **The mgfs agree on ℝ.** `ProbabilityTheory.analyticOnNhd_mgf` (analytic on interior univ = ℝ) and `AnalyticOnNhd.eq_of_eventuallyEq`.
