## New conditional consequence offered for the author: a quantitative window-selected law

This is an author-side deduction from Theorem N, not another source-review vote or a demanded proof change. Fix K=[kappa0,kappa1]⊂(0,infinity), and s=r/ell^(1/4) in S_K=[kappa1^-1/4,kappa0^-1/4]. After the exact contact-term restoration, define the nonnegative density
`F_ell^*(b,u,s)=r^-2 A_r^*(b,ell/r^3,u)`, `F_0^*=A_cusp^*(b,s^-4,u)`.
The integrated Gaussian envelope yields
`||F_ell^*-F_0^*||_L1(db d_sigma ds) <= C_K ell^(1/2)`.
Thus every bounded Borel weight h(b,u,s) has window intensity
`ell^(1/4) int h F_0^* + O(C_K ||h||_infinity ell^(3/4))`, uniformly over ||h||∞<=1. This is not an estimate for arbitrary field-aware marks lacking a marked-kernel input.

Each limiting mass Z_* is positive: on an open negative-definite A set, the conditionally nondegenerate f4 gives positive probability to |Y|<2kappa|det A| (elder) and2kappa|det A|<|Y|<6kappa|det A| (rejected). Therefore0<Z_eld<Z_cand and Z_rej>0. For positive finite measures f,g with masses m,n and delta=||f-g||1,
`dTV(f/m,g/n)<=min(1,delta/max(m,n))`.
It follows that the INTENSITY-SELECTED joint law of(b,u,s) in the fixed cusp window converges in TV at O_K(ell^(1/2)). Its (b,u,kappa=s^-4) pushforwards inherit the same bound. The window-selected actual-pairing fraction obeys
`p_K(ell)=Z_eld/Z_cand+O_K(ell^(1/2))`, with limiting fraction strictly between0 and1.

This does not conflict with pairing probability→1 at fixed positive k: here k=kappa*r→0. It also does not identify uniform selection from a typical nonempty field; that is the separate ordinary-bar multiplicity problem. Constants may deteriorate with K,d,L and are not calibrated. No extension to kappa0→0 or kappa1→infinity, global remainder, or finite-lifetime numerical guarantee is claimed.
