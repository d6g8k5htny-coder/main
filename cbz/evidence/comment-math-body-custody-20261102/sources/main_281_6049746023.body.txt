\[
H_0(X_t)=\bigoplus_{C\in\pi_0(X_t)} \mathbf F e_C.
\]

For \(s\ge t\), the inclusion sends \(e_C\) to the basis vector of the component of \(X_t\) containing \(C\).

1. **The deterministic component process is finite and binary.**  
   Crossing a maximum downward adds a superlevel zero-handle and creates one component. Crossing an index-\((d-1)\) saddle adds a superlevel one-handle, whose attaching region has exactly two feet.

   If the feet lie in different components \(C_1,C_2\), they merge into \(D\), and the \(H_0\) map has exactly the kernel
   \[
   \operatorname{span}\{e_{C_1}-e_{C_2}\}.
   \]
   Thus one saddle kills **at most one** \(H_0\) class.

   If both feet lie in the same component, the attachment gives no \(H_0\) death. Higher-index superlevel handles have connected attaching spheres and cannot merge distinct components. Distinct critical values ensure that each crossing contains a single critical event.

2. **The elder representative is a maximum of the current component.**  
   Label every component \(C\) by its unique highest maximum
   \[
   E(C)=\arg\max_{M\in C\cap\operatorname{Max}(f)} f(M).
   \]
   Existence follows from compactness of each component of the closed superlevel. If its maximum value is strictly above the threshold, a small connected neighborhood of the maximizer lies in the same component, so the maximizer is an ambient local maximum. The equality case is a singleton component born at a maximum. In particular, a component containing a point older than M contains an ambient local maximum with value at least that point's value.
