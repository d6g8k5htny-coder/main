## 6. An explicit compact overdetermined-zero lemma

Here is the elementary probability argument needed instead of importing a
genericity assertion. Let Y be an a.s. C1 random map on a neighborhood of a
compact q-dimensional parameter set K in finitely many smooth bounded charts,
with values in R^p, p>q. Suppose its point laws have densities bounded by one
constant M on a fixed compact chart neighborhood. Its random chart derivative
supremum is finite. On the event that all these derivative norms are≤A,
a zero in K implies that at a mesh point of spacing ε in a fixed enclosing
chart box, |Y|≤C A ε. There are≤C_K ε^-q mesh points, all in the neighborhood.
The probability at one point is bounded by M times the volume of that ball,
so a union bound gives

    P(Y has a zero in K and derivative supremum≤A)
                                  ≤C M A^p ε^(p-q).

Let ε tend to0 for fixed A, then A tend to∞. This proves absence of zeros a.s.
Boundary mesh points are covered by the same fixed chart neighborhood; the
Lipschitz bound is taken there as well. No independence of derivative bounds
and point values, or of different mesh points, is assumed. A finite atlas
absorbs fixed chart distortions. Countably many compact sets suffice for a
noncompact open parameter domain. For q=0 the same density argument directly
gives no zero at each of the finitely/countably many parameter points.

Under each fixed Q_(O,v), every field map used below is C1 a.s., by its C4
support. Its centered point covariance is the positive Schur covariance(3),
continuous in the indicated parameter and uniformly positive on compact
separated sets. A p-dimensional Gaussian density is bounded by
