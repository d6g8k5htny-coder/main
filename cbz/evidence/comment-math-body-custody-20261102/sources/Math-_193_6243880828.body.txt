## Contract and scope

One standard-library Python producer accepts explicit rational 0<L_lo<=L_hi, positive integer cutoff M, bounded precision/series settings and optional rational common absolute width goal. It returns machine-readable rational enclosures for z_L and mu2,mu4,mu6 valid FOR EVERY L in the closed input interval. It never chooses an input midpoint or claims to shrink input uncertainty. The source law is h=2pi/L, alpha=h^2/2, S_k=sum_(m in Z)(hm)^(2k)exp(-alpha m^2), z=S_0, mu_(2k)=S_k/S_0. The m=0 contribution is exactly1 for S_0 and0 for k>0. Both +/-m contribute. ## Arithmetic and analytic trusted base

Python integers/Fraction provide exact rational operations. Each forward interval operation rounds outward to a dyadic denominator2^p; the checker verifies containment against exact rational operations, including intermediate h/alpha/finite sums/tail/raw totals/normalization. Nonnegative factors use endpoint monotonicity; all division denominators have verified positive lower bounds. No floats/Decimal/transcendental library are a proof source.

Pi: Machin's identity pi=16atan(1/5)-4atan(1/239). Each atan alternating series is bracketed by N-term partial sum and its next term. The identity is justified by tan(2atan(1/5))=5/12, tan(4atan(1/5))=120/119, and tan(4atan(1/5)-atan(1/239))=1, with the angle in(0,pi/2). The checker recomputes the rational series brackets.

Exp: for rational x>=0 choose bounded s with y=x/2^s<=1. The alternating exp(-y) Taylor polynomial through odd degree2n+1 is a lower bound and through even degree2n an upper bound. Terms decrease. Round outward, then square s times with nonnegative endpoints and outward rounding. The checker recomputes the exact Taylor brackets and verifies every recorded squaring interval; it does not import the producer. For an x interval, use lower exp(-x_hi) and upper exp(-x_lo). Witnesses retain x,y,s and the squaring levels.

Tail: M must be a positive INTEGER and alpha_lo*M^2>=3, sufficient for all k=0,1,2,3. Let E>=exp(-alpha_lo*M^2), I0<=E/(2alpha_lo M), and I_k<=M^(2k-1)E/(2alpha_lo)+(2k-1)I_(k-1)/(2alpha_lo). Tail_k<=2h_hi^(2k)I_k. Refuse an uncertified threshold. Add [0,Tail_k] to the finite intervals, then divide by the SAME full S_0 interval. Normalizer lower bound is at least1.

Certificate fields include canonical rational input/settings, source hashes, pi/h/alpha bounds, exponential witnesses for all finite modes and the tail, finite/tail/raw/normalized bounds, dyadic rounding provenance, and tolerance disposition. Checker validates source IDs, schema, primitive proofs, physical-frequency formula, zero mode, integer-M guard, tail recurrences and final divisions. A valid certificate is not a scientific acceptance; trusted base includes checker correctness and the stated elementary lemmas. Producer/checker share the mathematical specification but no Python imports; this is not a formal proof of the checker or organizationally independent review.
