# Shrinking-window Gaussian comparison lemma — removes the N3/N6 coarse loss

Continuation of the intermediate pickup5957277321, after the corrected baseline5959794872. This is a new author-side support lemma, not a source edit or Conjecture7 closure.

### Lemma GW (generic Gaussian form)

Let (X\in\mathbb R^m), (z\in\mathbb R), and let (\mu_0,\mu_r) be Gaussian laws with uniformly nondegenerate covariance matrices and means/covariances satisfying
[
|m_r-m_0|+\|\Sigma_r-\Sigma_0\|\le C\varepsilon .
]
Then their joint densities satisfy, for some fixed (c,C,N),
[
|p_r(X,z)-p_0(X,z)|
 \le C\varepsilon(1+|X|+|z|)^N e^{-c(|X|^2+z^2)}.             \tag{GW.1}
]
This is immediate by differentiating the explicit Gaussian density on the compact set of uniformly positive covariance matrices.

If (h_\kappa) is supported on (|z-a(X)|\le C_0\kappa) and
[
|h_\kappa(X,z)|\le C_1\kappa^2(1+|X|)^M,
]
then
[
|E_{\mu_r}h_\kappa-E_{\mu_0}h_\kappa|
 \le C\varepsilon\kappa^3.                                  \tag{GW.2}
]
Proof: use (GW.1), integrate the (z)-variable only over an interval of length (2C_0\kappa), take (|z|\le |a(X)|+C_0\kappa), absorb the polynomial center into the Gaussian (X)-envelope, and integrate (X). The factors are exactly (\varepsilon\times\kappa^2\times\kappa).

### Exact scope/boundary

GW does not solve the regime where the finite-(r) decision boundary displacement is larger than the (O(\kappa)) window; that is precisely the unresolved (\kappa\lesssim r) geometry. It also does not replace #240's N2 exceptional-event estimates. It only shows that **once the support is localized, N3/N6 can be sharpened automatically** by Gaussian density smoothness.
