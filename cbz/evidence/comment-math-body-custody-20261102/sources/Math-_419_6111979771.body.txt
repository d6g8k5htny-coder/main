## 3. What one attached cell does to the actual inclusion on H0

Apply the attachment result to f=-g at any critical point p. Write c=-g(p),
A=F_{g(p)+epsilon}, B=F_{g(p)-epsilon}. These spaces have finitely many path
components by C257. Let j:A->Y and i:A->B be inclusions and r:B->Y the
retraction. Since r fixes A, r composed with i=j. The retraction and its
homotopy give a bijection on path components: every point of B has a path to
its retraction, and retracting a path gives a path in Y; inclusion gives the
inverse. Therefore the natural component comparison identifies the kernels
of H0(i) and H0(j). This conclusion concerns the actual inclusion, rather
than only the dimensions of H0(A) and H0(B).

Here is the complete component calculation for Y. Components of A are clopen
in A, because A is locally path connected. For lambda=0, e is a disjoint
point. All old components remain distinct and one component is added, so
H0(j) is injective.

For lambda>=2, the boundary sphere is nonempty and path connected, so its
image lies in one component C of A. The disk and C form one path-connected
set. No other components become identified: label each other component
separately, give C and the disk a common label, and map to the finite discrete
set of labels. This map is continuous on A and on the disk, agrees on their
identified boundary, and hence is continuous on their attachment. Every path
has constant label. Thus these are exactly the components of Y and H0(j) is
an isomorphism, even allowing paths that enter and leave the disk repeatedly.

For lambda=1 the boundary has two points, lying in components C_1,C_2 of A.
The same labeling argument proves that only those components can be joined.
If C_1=C_2, all component labels survive and H0(j) is an isomorphism. If they
differ, their union with the interval is path connected, precisely those two
labels become one, and all other components survive separately. In the
component bases the map sends e_1 and e_2 to the same basis vector and every
other basis vector to a different one. Its kernel is exactly

    k (e_1-e_2),                                            (2)

because a vector in the kernel has zero coefficients on the other components
and its two remaining coefficients sum to zero. This also holds in
characteristic two. In all dimensions, the kernel dimension is at most one;
it can be one only for lambda=1 with two different attaching components.

Since the Hessian of -g is the negative of the Hessian of g and both are
nondegenerate, their indices add to d. Thus the only possible death index
for g is d-1. The two local attaching ends of a 1-cell are not necessarily
different global components: the same-component case has zero kernel. That
case is the indispensable nonmerging-saddle negative control.

## 4. Isolating a death: index, one label, and injectivity

Fix a critical value a of g, with its unique point p. Choose epsilon>0 small

b_M>a+epsilon. This is possible because P is finite and delta_M<b_M. Births
and finite deaths in (1) are critical values, so there are no other interval
endpoints in this band. For the actual map

    V_{-a-epsilon} -> V_{-a+epsilon} = H0(A)->H0(B),

formula (1) shows its nullity is exactly

    # {M in P\{M_*} : delta_M=a}.                           (3)

Indeed each summand present at the source maps identically if still present
at the target and to zero if it dies in between. Every summand with death a
is present at the source by the chosen gap. A summand born in this band has
zero source and contributes no kernel; the essential summand never dies.
Naturality of (1), not an objectwise dimension difference, justifies (3).

For a=delta_M this number is positive. Section 3 forces lambda=1, two distinct
attaching components, and nullity one. Consequently index_g(S_M)=d-1 and no
other maximum has the same death value. If S_M=S_N their values coincide,

Conversely a critical point whose attaching ends enter two different

label counted in (3), and p=S_M is its actual merger point. C257 supplies the

The resulting bijections are

    finite intervals of V <-> nonglobal maxima M

The last arrow uses injectivity and the converse just proved; it does not

## 5. Concrete periodic torus and Field predicates

We now justify that these concrete assumptions give the intrinsic hypotheses.

At a lift x, the chain rule gives Dg_tilde_x=dg_{pi(x)} composed with Dpi_x.
Since Dpi_x is invertible, one derivative vanishes exactly when the other
does. At a critical point, the second derivative chain rule has no term
involving the first derivative of g; hence the Hessian transforms by
congruence under Dpi_x. Smoothness and (D) identify `fderiv` and
`iteratedFDeriv 2` with these genuine derivatives; their default values for
nondifferentiable functions play no role. In particular the source Hessian
is symmetric. Differentiating lattice-translation invariance also makes the

The source's `HessianHasInertia j` uses complementary subspaces V,W of
dimensions j,d-j on which the quadratic form is respectively negative and
positive definite; it does not require Hessian-orthogonality. Here is why it
nevertheless has the intended meaning. For a symmetric form, use (S) to
split E into negative, zero and positive eigenspaces, of dimensions n_-,n_0,n_+.

dim W<=n_+. Because V,W are complementary, dim V+dim W=d. Consequently

    d <= n_-+n_+ <= d,

so n_0=0 and both bounds are equalities. Also j<=d since V is a subspace;
the natural-number subtraction d-j causes no exceptional case. Conversely
the negative/positive eigenspaces of a nondegenerate form of index j supply
the source witnesses. Congruence transports definite subspaces and their
dimensions, giving precisely the intrinsic index. This proves the concrete
Morse clause implies intrinsic nondegeneracy. The Morse normal form identifies
local maxima exactly with index d; thus `IsNondegMax` means an actual local
maximum, and `IsIndexSaddle` means index d-1, including d=1.

The definition `torusDist` is the real infimum of
q_n=||x-y+Ln||, n in Z^d. This infimum is attained. Set R=q_0. The nonempty
set of n with q_n<=R is finite, because

    L |n_i| <= R+|x_i-y_i|

for every coordinate. Choose its minimizer; every excluded n has q_n>R and
therefore cannot improve it. The entire family is nonempty and bounded
below by zero, so its conditional real infimum has the usual infimum meaning
and equals this minimum. It is zero exactly when x-y+Ln=0 for some n, exactly
when pi(x)=pi(y). Otherwise its attained minimum is strictly positive.
Accordingly the source distinct-value clause gives distinct critical values

Each quotient point has exactly one representative in `FundDomain=[0,L)^d`.

Finally a Euclidean path starting at x projects to a quotient path with the
