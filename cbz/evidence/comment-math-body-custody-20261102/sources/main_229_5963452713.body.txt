No division by theta occurs. A critical point on the line with theta positive and Phi locally maximal is consequently a saddle or degenerate point, not a local minimum. The imported tangency calculation places the degenerate extra point below `-1-m_S`.

## 4. Theorem QS-E′: boundary, compact maximum and both maximin inequalities

Keep QS's inherited setting explicit: e is **globally continuous** and C2 on the two ellipses, not merely Hessian-bounded there.

- extra saddles and tangencies are below ell;
- an extra point of positive determinant has `c>sigma psi`. If sigma were nonnegative, the conic would force sigma>1 and hence c>psi, contradicting typing. Thus sigma is negative, `P_uu=-6sigma>0`, and positive determinant makes it a strict minimum.

This covers R=0, c=0, the degree drop and the tangency. Hence `P<=-epsilon_M` on K. QS's M-local lemma and `eta<epsilon_M` imply `g<=0` throughout the trap closure, with equality only at M.

Any continuous path from M to a point with g>0 must cross the trap boundary. At a level boundary `g<ell+eta=-1`; at L0 the exact S pins and QS local lemma give `g<=-1`. Therefore `d_g(M)<=-1`.
