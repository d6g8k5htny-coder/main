## 1. Reconstructed exact physical identity

Let h(t)=f(M+tu),0<=t<=r, and w(t)=t(r-t). Its boundary values vanish, and h'(0)=h'(r)=0. Two integrations by parts give

    ∫_0^r w h''' = [w h'']_0^r - [w' h']_0^r + ∫_0^r w'' h'
                   = -2(h(r)-h(0)) = 2kr^3.

Consequently k=(2r^3)^(-1)∫_0^r t(r-t)h'''(t)dt. Since ∫w=r^3/6, the physical gap coefficient is a3=h'''(0)/12. It is half the ordinary cubic Taylor coefficient h'''(0)/6. The pin orientation and sign are both essential.
