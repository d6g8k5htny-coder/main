## 1. Pair field, charts and regression

Choose the flat coordinate charts on open neighbourhoods whose closures stay

are quotient charts given by translations of the periodic lift, so location

distribution argument makes Cov G(x,y) positive definite at every distinct

not merely on D. The Gaussian density is continuous in location and target;
its supremum over all target values is bounded there by the covariance floor.
This supplies the explicit Theorem 2.1(ii) hypothesis as well as Theorem 2.2.
Compactness of D gives the particular floor used in regression below. P's Fourier
summability supplies almost-sure smooth paths, so G has C1 paths. Its location

The torus has no boundary; chart changes only re-express the same zero count.

Use E=C2(X). For t=(x,y), put C_t=Cov G(t) and the linear map
