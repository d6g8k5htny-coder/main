### Derivation / independently checkable steps
1. Work with dimensionless g(Y)=F(p+sRY) and the exact ten-row operator T of C322 (6). Let J3 g be its degree-three Taylor polynomial at 0, and I_T g the unique cubic reproducing all ten actual rows. C322's uniformly invertible row matrix on P3, bounded row operators in C³ (including q=0,eta=0 extension), and smoothness give, for every |alpha|<=3 on the fixed dimensionless chart, ||D_Y^alpha (g-I_T g)||_(L²(sup)) <= C s⁴. Indeed g-J3g has C³ L² remainder O(s⁴), T(g-J3g)=O_L²(s⁴), and I_T g-J3g = B^(-1)T(g-J3g) has cubic coefficients O_L²(s⁴). Its fourth derivative is simply D_Y⁴g, also O_L²(s⁴). This step uses exact polynomial reproduction, NOT an unqualified conditional covariance-floor claim.
2. Put R=g-I_Tg. Because T R=0, its original pin values and both gradients vanish at u=±q/2,v=0. The standard one-dimensional cubic-Hermite remainder gives |R_uu(±q/2,0)| <= C q² sup|R_uuuu|. With h(u)=R_v(u,0), h(±q/2)=0 and Rolle gives |R_uv(±q/2,0)| <= q sup|R_uuv|. Thus for physical derivatives (divide by s²): ||Huu(R)_pin||_L² <= C r²; ||Huv(R)_pin||_L² <= C r s; ||Hvv(R)_pin||_L² <= C s². At both witnesses, each Hessian-residual entry has L² norm <= C s². No independence of Hessian entries is assumed.
3. Under T=nu, I_Tg becomes the deterministic cubic P_nu. The target minus constant b has dimensionless row coordinates O(k s³): bbar-b= -kr³/2, Hq'(0)= -3kr²s/2, Hq'''(0)=12ks³, all other rows zero. Uniform invertibility on P3 implies every nonconstant monomial coefficient of P_nu is O(k s³). Moreover the *exact* pin rows impose C20=C11=0 and C30=2ks³. Hence physical Huu(P_nu)_pin and Huv(P_nu)_pin are O(k r) (their second derivatives vanish at u=v=0 and are cubic-linear in u=±q/2), while Hvv(P_nu)_pin and all witness Hessians are O(k s).
4. For any scalar residual Hessian A(R), Gaussian regression bounds |E[A(R)|T=nu]| <= sqrt(Var A(R))*sqrt(E_T), and sd(A(R)|T) <= sqrt(Var A(R)). The reviewed compact-sector energy bound E_T<=C(b²+k²) and compact b,k therefore convert the unconditional L² residual bounds in step 2 into conditional mean+SD bounds with the same powers. Since r<=s<=1, r²<=r and rs<=r, step 3 gives the displayed entrywise scales.
5. Each pin 2x2 determinant is Huu*Hvv-Huv²: every monomial envelope is O(rs) (r²<=rs). Each witness determinant envelope is O(s²). The expansion of their product has 2^4 determinant terms; multiplying the four entrywise bounds gives Env8 <= C (rs)²(s²)² = C r² s⁶. Correlated Gaussian entries do not matter for this *coefficientwise* envelope bound.
