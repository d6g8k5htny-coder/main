Further progress on pickup5957277321, now in the correct kappa→0 regime.

At the limiting cusp/contact law used by #240, condition on all free jets except f4 and write
`z=f4-3q`.
The candidate window is `|z|<72kappa`; the elder window is `|z|<24kappa`; hence the rejected window is
`24kappa<|z|<72kappa`.
The zeroth-order typed weight is
`w0=(Delta²/144)(5184kappa²-z²)`.

[
J(kappa)=\frac{Delta^2}{144}\int_{24kappa<|z|<72kappa}(5184kappa^2-z^2)p(3q+z)\,dz.

[
J(kappa)=\frac{Delta^2 kappa^3}{144}\int_D(5184-s^2)p(3q+kappa s)ds.

+\frac{8183808}{5}\,Delta^2 p''(3q)\,kappa^5
+O(Delta^2\sup|p^{(4)}|\,kappa^7).
