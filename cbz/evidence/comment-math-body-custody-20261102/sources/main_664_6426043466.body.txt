## 5. Endpoint bound, including floor and rounding

Define the proposed normalized endpoint allowance

    eta = (M2p/2)*hp^2 + MZp*dZ + 1/(S*Q0^4).

Every term is derived here. The first is a root-bracket Taylor error; the second is fixed-node Z variation; the third is the actual center integer-floor error after normalization. There is no MQ*dQ term because no comparison of endpoint heights at two Q values is made.

Fix any Z in [Za,Zb], a branch i, and either fixed endpoint e in {a_i,b_i}. Both e and p_i(Z) lie in R_i. On the segment between them,

    |partial_p^2 h_Z(p)|=|3-3p^2| <= 3+3Xp^2=M2p.

Taylor's theorem at the actual critical root, where `partial_p h_Z(p_i(Z))=0`, therefore gives

    |h_Z(p_i(Z))-h_Z(e)| <= (M2p/2)*hp^2.

At the same fixed endpoint e,

    |partial_Z h_Z(e)| = 16 Z^7 |e| <= 16 Zb^7 Xp=MZp,

so

    |h_Z(e)-h_Z0(e)| <= MZp*dZ.

These two estimates use a common actual root and a fixed endpoint inside its certified interval. They preserve the needed correlations without an implicit-function derivative or independently varied Q^2 and Q^3 terms.

The endpoint samples are unclamped, as a separate checkable consequence of the hypotheses. At any normalized critical root, substituting f_Z(p)=0 gives the identity

    h_Z(p)=(3/4)(p^2-1)^2-3/4 >= -3/4.

    h_Z0(e) >= -3/4-15 = -63/4.

As 0<Q0^4<=1, `Q0^4*h_Z0(e)>=-63/4>-16`. Thus each of the six root-endpoint axis samples uses the actual field value, not the computational floor. In fact S is divisible by 4, so their integer values are at least -63S/4 and strictly exceed -16S. The actual D/floor/path checks still bind the specific observation and must not be omitted.

Combining the three estimates proves, for every Z and either endpoint of R_i,

    |h_Z(p_i(Z)) - V(e,0)/(S Q0^4)| <= eta.
