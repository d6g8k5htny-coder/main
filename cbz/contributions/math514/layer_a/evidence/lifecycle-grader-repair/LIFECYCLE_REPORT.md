A KILLED result requires real selected-body AssertionError evidence and full selected population execution. Direct assertion callbacks must carry the exact exception escaping the selected body. Required `assertion_events` distinguish direct and body-subtest assertions, admit at most one direct failure per parent method, and match both the total assertion count and subtest assertion events exactly. Body-scoped successful and assertion-failing subtests are supported with parent/phase/outcome events; lifecycle subtests and subtest runtime errors fail closed. Errors, skips, expected failures, unexpected successes, noncanonical/synthetic/duplicate identities, incomplete populations, missing event metadata, boolean counts, and inconsistent summaries are ERROR. `parse_test_result` remains a diagnostic compatibility helper; outcome grading no longer trusts text summaries.

Complete fabricated JSON from an attacker-controlled process remains outside that custody assumption; missing or inconsistent receipt fields fail closed. Historical summaries cannot be fed into the new classifier as though they contained the new events.
